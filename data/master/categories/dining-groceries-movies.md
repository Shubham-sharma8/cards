# Dining, Groceries & Movies Credit Cards in India (2026)

- **Total Matching Cards:** 273
- **Best For Whom:** Urban families & couples spending Rs. 5k–Rs. 25k/mo across Swiggy/Zomato/Instamart/Blinkit/BigBasket, supermarkets, restaurants & BookMyShow BOGO.
- **Avoid This Category If:** You buy groceries primarily from local unorganized kirana stores via UPI (use RuPay UPI card instead).

## Top 5 Recommended Cards in This Category

### 1. ICICI Bank Sapphiro Credit Card (ICICI Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 4.08/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 3,500 (Waiver: Rs. 6L/yr) | **Forex:** 3.5% | **Domestic Lounge:** 4 domestic lounges/qtr subject to Rs. 75,000 quarterly spend criteria
- **Rewards:** Reward Points (Reward Points)
- **Pros:** Up to 16 domestic and 2 international airport lounge access every year; Welcome benefits worth Rs. 13,000 exceeding the joining fee of Rs. 6,500; Unlimited bonus reward points as milestone benefit depending on the cardholder’s annual spending capacity
- **Cons:** Low rewards earning rate on card spends; Spend-based condition on domestic airport lounge and golf benefits; High forex mark-up fee of 3.5% despite offering accelerated rewards on international spends
- **Ideal For:** Alumni qualifying for LTF Sapphiro solely for BookMyShow BOGO (Rs. 1,000/mo free movie tickets) and ICICI bank sale discounts
- **Verdict:** Get it ONLY if Lifetime Free via Alumni/Salary offer for BookMyShow BOGO; never pay the Rs. 6,500 + GST fee.

### 2. HSBC Live+ Credit Card (HSBC Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 3.96/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 999 (Waiver: Rs. 2L/yr) | **Forex:** 1.99% | **Domestic Lounge:** 2 complimentary domestic lounges/year (max 1 per 6 months, updated Nov 2026)
- **Rewards:** Direct Statement Cashback (1.5% – 10%)
- **Pros:** 10% cashback on food, dining, grocery, shopping and utility bill payments; Low forex markup fee of 1.99%; Complimentary domestic and international airport lounge visits
- **Cons:** 10% cashback is capped at Rs. 1,200 per month; Low frequency of complimentary international lounge visits; Limited value to consumers who do not spend significantly on the accelerated cashback categories
- **Ideal For:** Households spending Rs. 10,000/mo on dining, groceries (incl. offline D-Mart/supermarkets!), Zomato, Swiggy, Blinkit plus everyday offline 1.5%
- **Verdict:** Still India's #1 offline + online Dining & Grocery cashback card (10% up to Rs. 1k/mo + 1.5% unlimited offline) even after Nov 2026 trims.

### 3. Airtel Axis Bank Credit Card (Axis Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 3.7/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 500 (Waiver: Rs. 2L/yr) | **Forex:** 3.5% | **Domestic Lounge:** Domestic lounge access discontinued in 2026
- **Rewards:** Cashback / Store Credit (1% – 25%)
- **Pros:** Up to 25% cashback; No capping on base cashback; Benefits on everyday brands: Zomato, Blinkit and District Movies
- **Cons:** Bill payment benefits are limited to Airtel Thanks App; Monthly capping linked to base category spends to all accelerated cashback category; No complimentary lounge access
- **Ideal For:** Airtel subscribers spending Rs. 20k–25k+/mo on general 1% spends alongside Airtel & utility bills
- **Verdict:** Severely nerfed in 2026 by the base-spend unlock rule and lounge cut; PhonePe SBI Select Black or Tata Neu Infinity now beat it for pure utilities.

### 4. Swiggy BLCK HDFC Bank Credit Card (HDFC Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 3.67/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 1,000 (Waiver: Rs. 2L/yr) | **Forex:** 3.5% | **Domestic Lounge:** No domestic airport lounge access

- **Pros:** Complimentary Swiggy BLCK Membership; 10% cashback on Swiggy spends; 5% cashback on select online spends
- **Cons:** No complimentary lounge access benefits; Monthly capping on both accelerated and base cashback; No cashback on spends via HDFC SmartBuy portal
- **Ideal For:** Regular Swiggy Food + Instamart + Dineout users spending Rs. 5,000–Rs. 15,000/mo on Swiggy plus Rs. 30k online
- **Verdict:** Best-in-class 10% food delivery + quick commerce card in India — just apply ONLY after holding an HDFC core card!
