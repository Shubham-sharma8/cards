# Airport & Railway Lounge Access Credit Cards in India (2026)

- **Total Matching Cards:** 213
- **Best For Whom:** Flyers & rail passengers wanting guaranteed entry — prioritizing ZERO-spend-criteria cards (Tiger, Infinia, TravelOne, Marriott Bonvoy) over Rs. 50k–Rs. 1L/qtr gated traps.
- **Avoid This Category If:** You fly less than once a year (paid entry or Rupay Select debit card may be cheaper).

## Top 5 Recommended Cards in This Category

### 1. HSBC Premier Credit Card (HSBC Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 4.28/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 20,000 (Waiver: None / N/A) | **Forex:** 0.99% | **Domestic Lounge:** Unlimited complimentary domestic lounge access (no spend criteria)
- **Rewards:** HSBC Reward Points (3% – 18%)
- **Pros:** 0.99% low forex markup + 3% base 1:1 transfer value yields net positive ~2% on foreign spends; Rs. 20k renewal fee waived for maintaining HSBC Premier status without spend stress; Unlimited domestic & international lounge access with zero spend threshold
- **Cons:** Gated behind HSBC Premier relationship (Rs. 40L TRV or Rs. 3L+ net salary); HSBC's city footprint is limited to select metros in India
- **Ideal For:** HNIs and senior executives eligible for HSBC Premier who travel internationally and want Infinia-grade perks with low forex
- **Verdict:** Top-tier super-premium challenger to HDFC Infinia & ICICI Emeralde Private Metal for HSBC Premier clients.

### 2. HDFC BizBlack Metal Edition Credit Card (HDFC Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 4.12/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 10,000 (Waiver: Rs. 7.5L/yr) | **Forex:** 2% | **Domestic Lounge:** Unlimited complimentary domestic lounge visits
- **Rewards:** HDFC Reward Points (3.33% – 16.6%)
- **Pros:** Up to 5X Reward Points on card spends, including business expenses; Extensive travel benefits like unlimited lounge access, accelerated rewards, discount offers and vouchers; Lifestyle benefits like dining discounts, golf access and rewards and discounts on popular brands/platforms
- **Cons:** Spend-based condition on rewards on business spends and capping on monthly rewards earnings; Low redemption value of reward points if redeemed against non-travel options
- **Ideal For:** Business owners, CAs, doctors, and self-employed professionals paying quarterly advance tax & GST bills
- **Verdict:** Unrivalled #1 business and tax-payment card in India — turns Income Tax & GST outflows into 16.6% SmartBuy travel value.

### 3. American Express Platinum Charge Card (American Express)
- **Composite Score:** 4.08/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 66,000 (Waiver: None / N/A) | **Forex:** 3.5% | **Domestic Lounge:** Unlimited domestic lounge access incl. Amex Centurion Lounges (Delhi T3, Mumbai T2) + guests
- **Rewards:** Membership Rewards Points (1.25% – 6.25%)
- **Pros:** Unmatched hotel elite status bundle (Marriott Gold, Hilton Gold, Taj Epicure) & FHR $100 hotel credits; Best global lounge access in India (Centurion, Delta, Lufthansa, Priority Pass); Metal prestige & world-class Platinum Concierge service
- **Cons:** Steep Rs. 66,000 + GST (Rs. 77,880/yr) fee with NO spend-based waiver; Base reward rate (~1.25%) lags Infinia/EPM/Magnus Burgundy; high 3.5% forex fee; Lower domestic offline merchant acceptance than Visa/Mastercard
- **Ideal For:** Luxury international travelers staying 10+ nights/year at Marriott/Hilton/Taj & Fine Hotels + Resorts who maximize status perks & Centurion lounges
- **Verdict:** Lifestyle and luxury-travel status card rather than a points-earning workhorse — worth it only if you extract >Rs. 80k/yr from FHR, lounges, and hotel statuses.

### 4. Axis Bank Magnus Credit Card for Burgundy (Axis Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 4.06/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 30,000 (Waiver: Rs. 30L/yr) | **Forex:** 2% | **Domestic Lounge:** Unlimited complimentary domestic airport lounge visits
- **Rewards:** Axis EDGE Reward Points (5:4 partner miles transfer) (4.8% – 24%)
- **Pros:** Unlimited complimentary lounge access worldwide; Reward redemption against partner airlines and hotel loyalty points at a 5:4 ratio; Complimentary airport meet and greet services every year
- **Cons:** High spending milestone of Rs. 30 Lakh for annual fee waiver; Capping on reward points redemption for airmiles and hotel loyalty points; Limited welcome benefits of Rs. 5,000, especially considering the Rs. 30,000 joining fee
- **Ideal For:** Ultra-high spenders putting Rs. 3L–Rs. 15L/month (weddings, luxury, corporate travel) unlocking the unbeatable 35 RP/Rs. 200 tier above Rs. 1.5L/mo
- **Verdict:** Unbeatable beast ONLY when monthly spend crosses Rs. 2.5L+/mo reliably — nothing touches its uncapped 14%+ incremental earn rate above Rs. 1.5L.

### 5. HDFC Infinia Metal Edition Credit Card (HDFC Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 4.01/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 12,500 (Waiver: Rs. 10L/yr) | **Forex:** 2% | **Domestic Lounge:** Unlimited complimentary domestic lounge access for BOTH Primary & Add-on holders (Rs. 2 Visa validation per hdfC Sep 2025 PDF)
- **Rewards:** HDFC Reward Points (3.33% – 33.3%)
- **Pros:** 3.33% value-back across all spending categories, including insurance, utilities and education; Unlimited domestic and international lounge access for primary and add-on users; Unlimited complimentary golf games at select golf courses of India and abroad
- **Cons:** Forex mark-up fee could have been lower as several premium and mid-range cards charge lower fee; Complimentary Club Marriott membership is limited to the first year
- **Ideal For:** High-spending HNIs (Rs. 10L+/year) buying flights, hotels, and brand vouchers via SmartBuy with unlimited family lounge access
- **Verdict:** Still the undisputed king of Indian super-premium cards in 2026 — unmatched 3.33% base + up to 33% SmartBuy + unlimited Primary & Add-on lounges.
