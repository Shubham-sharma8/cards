# Cashback & Online Shopping Credit Cards in India (2026)

- **Total Matching Cards:** 357
- **Best For Whom:** Salaried & everyday spenders doing Rs. 10k–Rs. 1L/mo on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Swiggy, D2C websites, and utilities who want hard Rupee returns with zero miles math.
- **Avoid This Category If:** You spend >Rs. 8L/yr and want Business-Class flights / luxury hotel stays (Air Miles / Infinia yield 1.5x–2x more value).

## Top 5 Recommended Cards in This Category

### 1. Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card (ICICI Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 4.5/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 0 (LTF) (Waiver: None / N/A) | **Forex:** 1.99% | **Domestic Lounge:** No domestic airport lounge access
- **Rewards:** Amazon Pay Balance Cashback (1% – 5%)
- **Pros:** Lifetime Free with uncapped 5% return on Amazon for Prime users; Immune to monthly caps that plague competing cashback cards; Great starter/keeper card with 1.99% forex markup as backup
- **Cons:** Zero domestic or international airport lounge access; Excluded from most ICICI bank instant discounts during Big Billion/Great Indian Festival sales (outside Amazon Prime 5%); Cashback locked into Amazon Pay wallet ecosystem (though widely usable)
- **Ideal For:** First-time earners, Prime shoppers, and anyone wanting a zero-fee forever keeper card with uncapped 5% Amazon return
- **Verdict:** Unquestioned GOAT starter and LTF keeper card in India — keep it forever even after upgrading to super-premium cards.

### 2. Cashback SBI Card (SBI Card)
- **Composite Score:** 4.34/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 999 (Waiver: Rs. 2L/yr) | **Forex:** 3.5% | **Domestic Lounge:** No domestic airport lounge access
- **Rewards:** Direct Statement Cashback (1% – 5%)
- **Pros:** Still India's #1 universal 5% online cashback card across almost any e-commerce site; Zero redemption hassle or fees — auto-adjusted in statement within 2 days; Easy Rs. 2L/year fee waiver makes effective annual cost zero for online shoppers
- **Cons:** April 2026 cap merges 5% online + 1% offline into a single Rs. 5,000/month ceiling; Zero airport lounge access and excluded from SBI instant-discount e-commerce sales; Aggressive exclusions (utilities, insurance, education, rent, fuel, gift vouchers)
- **Ideal For:** Online shoppers spending Rs. 15,000–Rs. 1,00,000/month across D2C sites, Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, food/travel portals
- **Verdict:** Must-have core online cashback card for 80% of Indian cardholders despite the April 2026 combined Rs. 5k/month cap.

### 3. HDFC Millennia Credit Card (HDFC Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 4.11/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 1,000 (Waiver: Rs. 1L/yr) | **Forex:** 3.5% | **Domestic Lounge:** Direct airport lounge access discontinued; Rs. 1,000 voucher @ Rs. 1L/qtr milestone
- **Rewards:** CashPoints (1 CP = Rs. 1 statement cashback; Rs. 50+GST redemption fee) (1% – 5%)
- **Pros:** Up to 5% cashback on major lifestyle-related online spends; Bonus points worth the joining fee on activation; Multiple redemption options with redemption value of up to 1:1
- **Cons:** Monthly capping on cashback earning; Low cashback rate for non-accelerated category; Exclusion of major everyday spends like utilities and rent from cashback earning
- **Ideal For:** Young salaried users wanting an easy Rs. 1L waiver core HDFC card for Amazon/Flipkart/Swiggy/Zomato/Uber (up to Rs. 20k/mo) & HDFC sales
- **Verdict:** Great core HDFC starter card for building HDFC upgrade history and top-10 merchant 5% cashback, even after losing direct lounge access.

### 4. BOBCARD Eterna Credit Card (BOBCARD (Bank of Baroda))
- **Composite Score:** 3.88/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 0 (LTF) (Waiver: Rs. 2.5L/yr) | **Forex:** 2% | **Domestic Lounge:** Unlimited domestic lounges subject to Rs. 40k/quarter spend
- **Rewards:** BOB Reward Points (1 RP = Rs. 0.25 cashback) (0.75% – 3.75%)
- **Pros:** Up to 10X extra rewards on travel spends; 5X rewards, i.e. 3.75% back on dining, online shopping, travel and international spends; Unlimited domestic airport lounge access
- **Cons:** Low reward redemption value; No complimentary international airport lounge access; Monthly capping on 5X reward benefits
- **Ideal For:** Online/dining/movie spenders grabbing Eterna LTF for uncapped-merchant 3.75% rewards + 2% forex
- **Verdict:** Sleeper hit of 2026 — 3.75% on online/dining/travel with LTF promos and Fitpass/BOGO extras.

### 5. Airtel Axis Bank Credit Card (Axis Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 3.7/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 500 (Waiver: Rs. 2L/yr) | **Forex:** 3.5% | **Domestic Lounge:** Domestic lounge access discontinued in 2026
- **Rewards:** Cashback / Store Credit (1% – 25%)
- **Pros:** Up to 25% cashback; No capping on base cashback; Benefits on everyday brands: Zomato, Blinkit and District Movies
- **Cons:** Bill payment benefits are limited to Airtel Thanks App; Monthly capping linked to base category spends to all accelerated cashback category; No complimentary lounge access
- **Ideal For:** Airtel subscribers spending Rs. 20k–25k+/mo on general 1% spends alongside Airtel & utility bills
- **Verdict:** Severely nerfed in 2026 by the base-spend unlock rule and lounge cut; PhonePe SBI Select Black or Tata Neu Infinity now beat it for pure utilities.
