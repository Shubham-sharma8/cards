# Travel, Hotels & Air Miles Credit Cards in India (2026)

- **Total Matching Cards:** 347
- **Best For Whom:** Frequent domestic & international flyers spending Rs. 4L+/yr who redeem points 1:1 / 1:2 into Accor, KrisFlyer, Qatar Avios, Air India, or Marriott Bonvoy.
- **Avoid This Category If:** You travel <2 times a year or hate tracking transfer ratios and award seat availability.

## Top 5 Recommended Cards in This Category

### 1. Axis Bank Atlas Credit Card (Axis Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 4.25/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 5,000 (Waiver: None / N/A) | **Forex:** 3.5% | **Domestic Lounge:** 8 domestic lounges/yr (Silver tier) incl. guest access, NO spend criteria
- **Rewards:** Axis EDGE Miles (4% – 10%)
- **Pros:** 1:2 redemption value against partner airline and hotels; Accelerated reward benefits (2.5X EDGE Miles) on travel spends; Milestone benefits of up to 10,000 EDGE Miles every year
- **Cons:** High forex markup fee of 3.5%; Complicated tier-based benefit structure; High annual fee of Rs. 5,000
- **Ideal For:** Airline flyers redeeming SQ KrisFlyer / United / Air India directly from airline & hotel websites with guest lounge access
- **Verdict:** Still strong for airline miles & guest lounges, but 2026 Accor exit means HSBC TravelOne now wins head-to-head for flexible travel miles.

### 2. IndusInd Bank Avios Credit Card (IndusInd Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 4.15/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 5,000 (Waiver: None / N/A) | **Forex:** 1.5% | **Domestic Lounge:** 2 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per quarter (8/year)
- **Rewards:** Avios Air Miles (Direct 1:1 Qatar / British Airways) (1% – 5%)
- **Pros:** 20,000 Avios joining & 5,000 Avios renewal cover annual fees immediately; Direct 1:1 Avios earning with Qatar Airways & British Airways (Oneworld sweet spots); Low 1.5% forex markup + Qatar Airways flight discount from India
- **Cons:** Lower domestic base earn rate unless maximizing preferred international cities; Strict Avios ecosystem lock-in (no Star Alliance/Accor transfers)
- **Ideal For:** Frequent flyers to Europe/US/Middle East via Qatar Airways or British Airways who value Oneworld Avios business-class redemptions
- **Verdict:** Best dedicated Oneworld Avios co-brand card in India, strong specifically for Qatar Airways/BA international flyers.

### 3. American Express Platinum Travel Credit Card (American Express)
- **Composite Score:** 4.09/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 5,000 (Waiver: None / N/A) | **Forex:** 3.5% | **Domestic Lounge:** 8 domestic airport lounges/yr (2/quarter), Priority Pass membership fee waived
- **Rewards:** Membership Rewards Points (1% – 8%)
- **Pros:** Targeted rewards structure backed by American Express; 8 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits/year
- **Cons:** High joining/annual fee (Rs. 5,000 + GST) if spend thresholds aren't met; High 3.5% (+GST) foreign currency markup — poor for overseas spends; Check latest issuer exclusions on rent, utilities, education & wallet loads before high spends
- **Ideal For:** Users with exactly ~Rs. 4,00,000/year in non-rewarding spends (tax, insurance, jewelry, education, rent) turning dead spends into 48k MR + Rs. 10k Taj voucher
- **Verdict:** Unbeatable Rs. 4 Lakh/yr milestone card — park all jewelry/insurance/tax/general spends to hit Rs. 4L cleanly, then switch to another card.

### 4. HDFC Regalia Gold Credit Card (HDFC Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 3.99/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 2,500 (Waiver: Rs. 4L/yr) | **Forex:** 2% | **Domestic Lounge:** Up to 12 domestic lounge visits/yr subject to Rs. 60,000 quarterly spend requirement (updated 2026)
- **Rewards:** HDFC Reward Points (1.33% – 6.66%)
- **Pros:** 5X rewards on Nykaa, Myntra, Marks & Spencer, and Reliance Digital; Rewards on generally-excluded categories like insurance, utilities and education; Multiple redemption options with good redemption value
- **Cons:** Accelerated earning is limited to select brands only; Low redemption value on product/voucher purchase and cash conversion; High spending threshold to make the most of milestone benefits=
- **Ideal For:** HDFC Imperia/Preferred customers climbing toward Infinia/DCB who spend Rs. 4L–8L/year incl. Myntra/Nykaa/Reliance Digital/M&S
- **Verdict:** Solid core HDFC travel stepper toward Infinia, though HSBC TravelOne now beats it for pure miles & spend-free lounges.

### 5. HSBC TravelOne Credit Card (HSBC Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 3.81/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 4,999 (Waiver: Rs. 8L/yr) | **Forex:** 3.5% | **Domestic Lounge:** 6 complimentary domestic lounge visits/yr with ZERO spend criteria
- **Rewards:** HSBC Reward Points (1:1 Airline & Hotel Miles incl. Accor!) (2% – 12%)
- **Pros:** Accelerated rewards on travel spends with up to 12% value-back; 1:1 conversion ratio of rewards to airmiles or hotel points; Up to 15% discount across some of the most popular travel platforms
- **Cons:** High forex mark-up fee of 3.5%; Welcome benefits come with spend-based conditions; Redemption of the accrued reward points is limited to just travel partner points or miles
- **Ideal For:** Travelers booking flights/hotels/OTAs wanting 1:1 Accor & airline transfers + spend-free lounges (dethroned Axis Atlas in 2026!)
- **Verdict:** 2026 #1 Mid-Tier Travel Credit Card in India — 1:1 Accor + 4%–12% travel rewards + zero-spend lounges beat post-devaluation Axis Atlas.
