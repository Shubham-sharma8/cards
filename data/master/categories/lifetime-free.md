# Lifetime Free (LTF) Cards Credit Cards in India (2026)

- **Total Matching Cards:** 120
- **Best For Whom:** Zero-commitment keeper cards for Amazon (Amazon Pay ICICI), UPI (Kiwi/super.money), Lounge (IndusInd Tiger), 0% Forex (IDFC Wealth/Scapia/Ixigo), & Bank Sales.
- **Avoid This Category If:** You spend >Rs. 5L/yr on concentrated categories where paying Rs. 1k–Rs. 12.5k fee yields 3x–10x net profit.

## Top 5 Recommended Cards in This Category

### 1. Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card (ICICI Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 4.5/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 0 (LTF) (Waiver: None / N/A) | **Forex:** 1.99% | **Domestic Lounge:** No domestic airport lounge access
- **Rewards:** Amazon Pay Balance Cashback (1% – 5%)
- **Pros:** Lifetime Free with uncapped 5% return on Amazon for Prime users; Immune to monthly caps that plague competing cashback cards; Great starter/keeper card with 1.99% forex markup as backup
- **Cons:** Zero domestic or international airport lounge access; Excluded from most ICICI bank instant discounts during Big Billion/Great Indian Festival sales (outside Amazon Prime 5%); Cashback locked into Amazon Pay wallet ecosystem (though widely usable)
- **Ideal For:** First-time earners, Prime shoppers, and anyone wanting a zero-fee forever keeper card with uncapped 5% Amazon return
- **Verdict:** Unquestioned GOAT starter and LTF keeper card in India — keep it forever even after upgrading to super-premium cards.

### 2. Federal Bank Scapia Credit Card (Federal Bank / Scapia)
- **Composite Score:** 4.01/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 0 (LTF) (Waiver: None / N/A) | **Forex:** 0% | **Domestic Lounge:** Unlimited domestic lounge access + Rs. 5,000/cycle airport dining/spa/shopping credits when spending Rs. 20k in prev cycle
- **Rewards:** Scapia Coins (10% Visa / 5% RuPay on online/offline -> 2% Visa / 1% RuPay value @ 5 coins = Rs. 1 on Scapia travel) (2% – 4%)
- **Pros:** Zero forex mark-up fee on international spends; Up to 20% Scapia coins on card transactions without any capping; Unlimited domestic airport lounge access with discount on airport spends
- **Cons:** Scapia coins redemption limited to just travel bookings on Scapia app; Low Scapia coins redemption ratio of 5:1; No reward points on international spends
- **Ideal For:** International travelers wanting pure 0% forex markup on LTF card, plus domestic flyers meeting Rs. 20k/cycle spend
- **Verdict:** Leading 0% forex LTF travel card alongside AU Ixigo & IDFC WOW/Wealth — note Rs. 20k/cycle spend needed for lounges in 2026.

### 3. IDFC FIRST Wealth Credit Card (IDFC FIRST Bank)
- **Composite Score:** 3.96/5 | **Annual Fee:** Rs. 0 (LTF) (Waiver: None / N/A) | **Forex:** 0% | **Domestic Lounge:** 2 domestic lounge visits/qtr on Rs. 20,000/mo spend

- **Cons:** Low reward redemption value of Rs. 0.25 and limited redemption options; Base reward rate is quite low for a premium card; Lounge access is subject to meeting monthly spending thresholds
- **Ideal For:** International travelers wanting LTF + 0% forex WITH rewards + low APR + roadside assistance
- **Verdict:** 2026 upgrade to 0% forex WITH full rewards makes IDFC Wealth one of the best LTF travel backups in India.

- **Ideal For:** Online/dining/movie spenders grabbing Eterna LTF for uncapped-merchant 3.75% rewards + 2% forex
