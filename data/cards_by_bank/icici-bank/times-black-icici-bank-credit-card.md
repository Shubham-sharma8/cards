## About the Card
Times Black ICICI Bank Credit Card is a metal card co-branded with Times Internet Ltd. Catering to the lifestyle needs of the premium segment, the card lets the users elevate their lifestyle and travel experiences while availing exclusive services. At an annual fee of Rs. 20,000, it offers some high-end benefits like unlimited lounge access, milestone rewards worth up to Rs. 70,000, and other lifestyle benefits, including, but not limited to, the lifestyle management service called CribLife, exclusive access to events, dining discounts, and offers on travel and shopping. Though these features are expected from a high annual fee card, the major value back of this card comes from its annual and milestone benefits. Every year, card users can earn benefits up to Rs. 70,000 on meeting the spend threshold of up to Rs. 25 lakh. Though a decent value, the good part is the benefits are scattered around spend milestones ranging from Rs. 2 lakh to up to Rs. 25 lakh. That way, the card has something to offer to both moderate and high spenders. Even the joining and renewal perks on the card are good, covering some popular brands like Zomato and EaseMyTrip.
