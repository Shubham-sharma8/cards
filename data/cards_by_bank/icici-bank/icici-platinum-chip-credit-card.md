## About the Card
ICICI Platinum Chip is a **lifetime-free entry-level credit card** , ideal for individuals who are beginning their credit journey. With **no joining or annual fee** , this card can be used to build up your credit profile while earning reward points on every card spend, including utility bills and insurance payments. Cardholders can earn **2X rewards on retail purchases** , making it a suitable choice to save on everyday purchases. Moreover, the card provides a **1% fuel surcharge waiver** and **exclusive dining discounts** which add to the overall value-back.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for ICICI Platinum Chip Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card through both online and offline modes. To apply online through Issuer / Market, you can simply enter your mobile number in the form available on this page and carry out the application process, or you can also visit the official bank website. Apart from this, you can visit any of the nearest branches of ICICI bank to apply offline.

### I don’t have a credit score, so can I apply for ICICI Platinum Chip Credit Card?
Yes, people who do not have a credit score or are new to the credit journey can apply for this credit card. This is an entry-level credit card that is designed for people who want to avail basic benefits without any annual fees.

### I have a salary account with ICICI Bank. Can I get ICICI Platinum Chip Credit Card?
Yes, anyone can apply for this credit card regardless of whether you have an account with the bank or not.

### I have already applied for ICICI Platinum Chip Credit Card. How can I check the application status?
You can check the application status through online and offline modes. You can visit the official website of the bank and check the application status by entering your application number. Apart from this, you can also call on the ICICI Credit Card customer care number and ask the representative about the application status.

### What is the cash advance fee charged on ICICI Platinum Chip Credit Card?
The bank charges a cash advance fee of 2.5% of the transacted amount or a minimum of Rs. 500 on every such transaction made using ICICI Platinum Chip Credit Card. In addition to this fee, an interest rate of 3.75% per month (45% per annum) is also applicable on the withdrawn amount from the date of cash withdrawal till the full repayment of card dues. Therefore, it is advisable not to withdraw cash using a credit card as it incurs high charges.

### What is the forex markup on ICICI Platinum Chip Credit Card?
ICICI Platinum Chip Credit Card charges a 3.5% forex markup fee on foreign currency transactions. Applicable taxes are charged over and above the forex markup.

### Does ICICI Platinum Chip Credit Card offer a fuel surcharge waiver?
Yes. ICICI Platinum Chip Credit Card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on eligible fuel transactions at HPCL petrol pumps. The eligible transaction amount is Rs. 400 to Rs. 4,000 per transaction.

### Does ICICI Platinum Chip Credit Card give rewards on UPI payments?
No, currently ICICI Plaitnum Chip Credit Card is not available on the RuPay network. Therefore, no reward points will be offered on UPI transactions.
