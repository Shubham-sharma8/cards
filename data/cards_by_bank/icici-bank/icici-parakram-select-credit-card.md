## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the reward rate on ICICI Parakram Select Credit Card?
The card offers 10 reward points per Rs. 100 spent at eligible grocery stores, departmental stores and canteen stores, subject to a monthly cap of 1,000 reward points. Other eligible domestic spends earn 2 reward points per Rs. 100.

### Does ICICI Parakram Select Credit Card offer international lounge access?
Yes. Cardholders can avail 2 complimentary international airport lounge visits per year through Priority Pass Membership, subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

### What are the golf benefits offered on ICICI Parakram Select Credit Card?
Cardholders can avail 1 complimentary golf round on spending Rs. 50,000 in a month. The benefit is capped at 4 complimentary rounds in a month.

### Does ICICI Parakram Select Credit Card offer a fuel surcharge waiver?
Yes. The card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on eligible fuel transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000 across fuel stations in India. The maximum waiver is capped at Rs. 250 per month.

### Is there a monthly cap on accelerated reward points?
Yes. The accelerated reward rate of 10 reward points per Rs. 100 on grocery, departmental and canteen store spends is capped at 1,000 reward points per month.
