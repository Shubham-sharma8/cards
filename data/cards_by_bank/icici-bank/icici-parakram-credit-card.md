## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who is eligible for ICICI Parakram Credit Card?
ICICI Parakram Credit Card is designed for eligible personnel of the Indian Defence Services. Applicants should check the latest eligibility criteria with ICICI Bank before applying.

### How many reward points can I earn on grocery and departmental store spends?
Cardholders can earn 5 reward points per Rs. 100 spent at eligible grocery stores, departmental stores and canteen stores. The accelerated reward earning is capped at 1,000 points per month.

### What is the reward rate on other domestic spends?
The card offers 1 reward point per Rs. 100 spent on eligible domestic transactions outside the accelerated reward categories.

### Does ICICI Parakram Credit Card offer airport lounge access?
Yes. Cardholders can avail 1 complimentary domestic airport lounge access per quarter by spending Rs. 75,000 or more in the previous quarter.

### Is the accelerated reward rate applicable to all grocery and shopping spends?
5 reward points per Rs. 100 spend is applicable to eligible spends at grocery stores, departmental stores and canteen stores. The accelerated reward earning is subject to a monthly cap of 1,000 reward points.

### What is special about the ICICI Parakram Credit Card?
The card is specifically designed for Defence Services personnel and features an embossing of the Defence Services on the card. It also offers accelerated rewards on select everyday spends with lounge access and insurance benefits.

### Who should get ICICI Parakram Credit Card?
Eligible Defence Services personnel seeking a credit card with accelerated rewards on grocery, departmental and canteen store spends, along with airport lounge access and insurance benefits can consider this card.
