## About the Card
Loyal Adani One customers can save on their spends by redeeming accumulated reward points against spends within the Adani One ecosystem. Frequent travellers who prefer to book through Adani One can also avail premium travel benefits, including **complimentary lounge access worldwide, porter services, premium car parking** , and more—making it a suitable option for frequent travellers. While the card focuses on travel and lifestyle categories, it offers limited lounge access benefits and charges a high forex markup fee of 3.5%, which may impact its overall value. However, if you frequently travel and shop within the Adani One ecosystem, this card can help you save significantly.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How much can I save through Adani One ICICI Signature Credit Card?
Adani One ICICI Signature Credit Card offers 7% value back on spends within the Adani One ecosystem, along with 2% on international and 1.5% on domestic spends. You can also earn 0.5% value back on utility and insurance spends.

### How can I apply for Adani One ICICI Signature Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online by visiting the official website of ICICI Bank and submitting a duly filled application form along with the required documents. You can also apply offline by visiting any of the nearest ICICI Bank branches and submitting the form in person.

### Does Adani One ICICI Signature Credit Card offer an annual fee waiver?
Yes, Adani One ICICI Signature Credit Card charges an annual fee of Rs. 5,000. You can avail a fee waiver on spending Rs. 6 Lakh in a membership year.

### How can I activate Adani One ICICI Signature Credit Card?
To activate your card online, log in to ICICI Bank’s mobile banking or internet banking platform and generate the PIN. Once the PIN is successfully generated, you can start using your card for transactions.

### Does Adani One ICICI Signature Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, the card offers 2 complimentary international lounge visits per year and 4 complimentary domestic lounge visits per quarter.

### Can I avail fuel surcharge waiver on all fuel spends via Adani One ICICI Signature Credit Card?
No, you can avail 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel transactions of up to Rs. 4,000 across all petrol pumps.
