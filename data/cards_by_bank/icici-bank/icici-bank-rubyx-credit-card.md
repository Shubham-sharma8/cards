## About the Card
ICICI Bank Rubyx Credit Card is an all-rounder card offering benefits like rewards, joining and milestone bonus, movie and dining discount, airport and railway lounge access, golf benefits, and others. These benefits cover some of the major spending categories, like shopping, entertainment, dining and travel. However, the overall value-back is quite dependent on the spending capacity of the cardholder as many of these features come with spend-based conditions.****

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I track my ICICI credit card status?
You can check the application status of your ICICI Bank Rubyx credit card by either visiting the official bank website or by calling teh credit card customer care number.

### Will I be charged any fee on making international purchases using the ICICI Bank Rubyx credit card?
Yes, a forex markup fee of 3.50% will be charged on all international retail and online transactions. It will also be applied when the card is used at those POS machines which are registered in the international territory; like the POS machines at duty-free shops.

### What are the criteria to avail the complimentary access to domestic airport lounges?
In order to avail the free domestic airport lounge access, you must spend at least Rs. 75,000 during the previous calendar quarter.
