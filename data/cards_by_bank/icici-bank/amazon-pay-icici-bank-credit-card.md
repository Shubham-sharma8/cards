## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Unquestioned GOAT starter and LTF keeper card in India — keep it forever even after upgrading to super-premium cards.

**Best For:** First-time earners, Prime shoppers, and anyone wanting a zero-fee forever keeper card with uncapped 5% Amazon return

**Not Ideal For:** Flyers needing airport lounge access or Flipkart/brick-and-mortar heavy spenders

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Any (zero fee means zero minimum spend needed)

### Pros (Good)
- Lifetime Free with uncapped 5% return on Amazon for Prime users
- Immune to monthly caps that plague competing cashback cards
- Great starter/keeper card with 1.99% forex markup as backup

### Cons (Bad)
- Zero domestic or international airport lounge access
- Excluded from most ICICI bank instant discounts during Big Billion/Great Indian Festival sales (outside Amazon Prime 5%)
- Cashback locked into Amazon Pay wallet ecosystem (though widely usable)
