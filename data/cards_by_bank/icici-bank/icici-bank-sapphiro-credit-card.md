## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for ICICI Bank Sapphiro Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card through both online and offline modes. To apply online, you can visit the official website of the bank and fill out the application form. If you want to apply offline, you need to visit the nearest bank branch.

### How much time will it take for the delivery of items I purchased by redeeming my reward points?
The redeemed items generally take up to 10 working days to reach the cardholder.

### Can I make purchases online using my ICICI Bank Sapphiro Credit Card?
Yes, ICICI Bank Sapphiro Credit Card is enabled with the online purchase option and you can use it to make purchases on the website of all domestic online retailers as well as a few international retailers.

### How can I avoid paying the annual fee of ICICI Sapphiro Credit Card?
You can avail the annual card fee waiver facility by spending a minimum of Rs. 6 lakh in a year.
