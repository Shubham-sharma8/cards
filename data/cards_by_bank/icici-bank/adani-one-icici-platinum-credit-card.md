## About the Card
The Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card is the entry-level offering from the co-branded partnership between Adani One and ICICI Bank. It offers benefits around card activation, renewal, airport-linked services, lounge access, reward earnings, and redemption. Cardholders can earn accelerated **(3%) Adani reward points on spends within the Adani ecosystem** and redeem them for travel services and bookings via the Adani One portal. Besides this, the card also offers **1.5% value back on international spends** , 1% on other spends, and 0.5% on utility and insurance spends. For frequent travellers, the card offers basic domestic lounge access benefits along with premium airport-linked services like **lounge upgrades, premium car parking, porter services** , and more.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card by visiting the official ICICI Bank website and submitting a duly filled application form along with the required documents. Alternatively, you can apply offline by visiting the nearest ICICI Bank branch and submitting the form in person.

### How to redeem reward points earned via Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card?
Cardholders can redeem reward points on spends within the Adani ecosystem. The redemption can be done via the Adani One portal or mobile app, based on your preferences.

### Does Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card offer any exclusive airport benefits?
Yes, the card offers 2 complimentary domestic lounge visits per quarter. Additional airport benefits include lounge upgrades, valet parking, premium car parking, porter services, and more.

### Is Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card good for travel?
As a base variant, the Platinum Card offers limited travel benefits such as 2 complimentary domestic lounge visits per quarter, 2 premium lounge upgrades per quarter, 2 valet parking and 2 premium car parking benefits per year, and up to 4 porter services. For better travel benefits, your can apply for the Signature variant.

### How soon do reward points reflect after a transaction with Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card?
Reward points usually reflect within 24 to 48 hours after the transaction. You can view them via internet/ mobile banking or through the monthly credit card statement.

### What is the eligibility criteria to apply for Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card?
To apply for Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card, you must be at least 21 years old. For salaried individuals, the minimum income required is Rs. 20,000 per month, and for self-employed individuals, it is Rs. 2.40 LPA.

### How to activate Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card?
You can activate your Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card online through mobile banking or internet banking. Log in using your credentials, go to the credit cards section, and generate the PIN to activate your card. After activation, you can use it for both online and offline transactions.

### What are the charges for Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card?
Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card has a joining and annual fee of Rs. 750. However, the annual fee is waived off on spending Rs. 3 Lakh or more in a year.

### Is Adani One ICICI Platinum Credit Card worth it?
Adani One ICICI Platinum Card is a good option for consumers who frequently spend within the Adani ecosystem and want to earn rewards without paying a high annual fee.

### What is the difference between Adani One ICICI Signature and Platinum Credit Cards?
Both are co-branded credit cards. The Platinum variant is the base card with limited benefits and a Rs. 750 annual fee. On the other hand, Signature variant is the premium card offering a higher reward rate and other travel benefits, with an annual fee of Rs. 5,000.
