## About the Card
ICICI Bank Emeralde Private Metal credit card is an **invite-only premium credit card** catering to high-income individuals. Offering **3% value-back across all spends** , unlimited lounge access, unlimited golf rounds, movie and dining benefits,this card compliments the diverse needs of high-spenders. While the card’s base reward rate may not be the most competitive among premium cards, consumers can leverage the reward acceleration offered on the iShop Platform to maximize travel value. Benefits like **unlimited domestic and international lounge access** , **EaseMyTrip vouchers** as milestone benefits, **lower forex mark-up** and **trip cancellation insurance cover** make it particularly appealing for frequent travellers. Notably, the unlimited lounge access extends to both primary and add-on members. Note: This is an invite-only credit card. Content on this page is for information purpose only._

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What benefits are offered via the Taj Epicure membership?
Taj Epicure membership is feature-loaded with perks like complimentary night stays, 25% off on F&B, 20% off on Qmin App orders, hotel spa & salon usage and discounted stays among other benefits.

### How many add-on credit cards can I avail with the Emeralde Private Card?
You can avail up to 3 complimentary add-on cards with your ICICI Bank Emeralde Private Credit Card.

### What are the redemption options on this card?
The card offers a redemption ratio of 1:1. You can redeem your accumulated rewards against flight and hotel vouchers for 1 reward point = Rs. 1. Additionally, you can get selected brand vouchers from Apple, Tanishq, Tumi, Coach, Brooks Brother, Emporio Armani, Jimmy Choo, G-Star Raw, Canali & Mother Care for 1 reward point = Rs. 1. Beyond these, you can redeem against the reward catalog for 1 reward point = Rs. 0.6 and Rs. 0.4 for redemption against statement balance.

### Does the card offer complimentary concierge to all customers?
The complimentary concierge service on the card is currently available only in selected cities- New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru and Chennai.

### What is ICICI Credit card customer care number?
In case of any queries or disputes, you can raise your concerns to ICICI Bank customer care at 1800 1080.
