## About the Card
ICICI Bank HPCL Coral is a co-branded fuel credit card that largely **caters to consumers who prefer HPCL fuel pumps over others**. At a very low annual fee of just Rs. 199, the card offers a good **3.5% savings on fuel** (including a 1% fuel surcharge waiver). Additionally, it allows rewards on all transactions, including the often restricted ones like **utility bills and insurance payments** —a too-good feature for an entry-level card. For lifestyle benefits, cardholders can avail benefits on **movie tickets via BookMyShow and dining discounts via the issuer’s Culinary Treats Programme**. HPCL offers more co-branded cards with other issuers—IDFC FIRST Bank, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India, and one more variant with ICICI—ICICI HPCL Super Saver. However, the HPCL Coral is **closely comparable with IDFC’s base variant—IDFC FIRST Power**. Both cards come at a similar fee of Rs. 199, but the **value back on the IDFC card is higher at 5%** , due to an additional 1.5% value back via HappyCoins—a benefit offered on all HPCL variants except ICICI’s Coral. Additionally, the Power card also offers incremental rewards on non-fuel categories like grocery, utility, and FASTag, and welcome benefits that are worth up to Rs. 2,250 that justify the fee by a wide margin. Over Power, **Coral has an edge only for offering the movie benefits**.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for an ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card through ICICI Bank’s website. Alternatively, you may visit your nearest ICICI Bank branch.

### Can I avail a fuel surcharge waiver on every transaction via the ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card?
You can avail a fuel surcharge waiver of 1% on transactions ranging between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000 only. Also, this waiver is applicable only at HPCL pumps.

### How can I check my ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card application status?
The bank keeps you updated regarding your application status via text on your registered mobile number. Additionally, you can also track your application status online by visiting the official website of the bank. Enter your application number, and the status will be shown on your screen. You can also contact the ICICI Bank Credit Card customer care regarding this query.

### Why did my application for the ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card get rejected?
The bank rejects your application if you don’t meet the required eligibility criteria. In order to apply for the ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card, your age must be above 21 years, with a minimum monthly income of Rs. 20,000. Additionally, the issuer may consider factors like a good credit score and credit profile, among others.

### What is the cash advance fee charged on the ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card?
The cash advance fee charged on the ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card is 2.5% on the advance amount or a minimum of Rs. 500. This fee is charged along with a finance charge of 3.75%. In general, withdrawing cash using a credit card is not advisable.
