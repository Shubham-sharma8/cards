## About the Card
ICICI Bank Expressions Credit Card is an entry-level card that allows cardholders to**personalize their card** from the given designs. With this card, cardholders can earn**3 points per Rs. 100 spent across all categories** , making it a suitable option for day-to-day spends. Besides this, it also offers a base reward rate of 1 point per Rs. 100 spent on utilities and insurance, categories that are usually excluded from the rewards program. The card also offers other lifestyle benefits, such as **exclusive hotel deals** (with the VISA variant), complimentary lounge access, and **free movie tickets**. Overall, consumers looking for a credit card with basic benefits across shopping, travel, dining, and entertainment can consider this credit card.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does ICICI Bank Expressions Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, the card offers 1 complimentary domestic airport lounge visit per quarter on spending Rs. 75,000 in the preceding quarter. However, this benefit is only available with the VISA variant.

### Can I avail an annual fee waiver on ICICI Bank Expressions Credit Card?
Yes, the card charges an annual fee of Rs. 499 plus applicable taxes. However, you can avail an annual fee waiver by spending Rs. 1.5 Lakh in a year.

### What is different about ICICI Bank Expressions Credit Card?
ICICI Expressions is an entry-level credit card that allows you to personalize your card design from over 175 available designs. You can choose designs from different categories, including sports, adventure, travel, music, food, fashion, nature, and more.

### How can I apply for ICICI Bank Expressions Credit Card?
To apply for this credit card online, you can submit the duly filled application form through the official website of ICICI Bank. Besides this, you can also visit any of the nearest bank branches and enquire about the offline application process.

### How much can I save on fuel spends with ICICI Bank Expressions Credit Card?
You can save on your fuel spends with this credit card by availing a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions of up to Rs. 4,000 per month at HPCL pumps.
