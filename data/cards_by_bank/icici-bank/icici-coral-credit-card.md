- 1 domestic airport lounge visit per quarter on spending min. Rs. 75,000 in a quarter

- You can unlock and maximise the milestone bonus rewards by spending Rs. 2 lakh or more in a year

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is there any movie offer on ICICI Coral Credit Card?
Yes, you can get 2 movie tickets per month across BookMyShow and INOX with this ICICI credit card.

### What are ICICI Coral Credit Card cash withdrawal charges?
ICICI Bank charges 2.50% on the withdrawn amount, subject to a minimum of Rs. 500 as credit card cash withdrawal fee. Moreover, until repaid, an interest rate of 3.75% per month is also applicable on the cash amount.

### Is it safe to make offline transactions through ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card?
Yes, it\'s absolutely safe to make offline transactions through ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card as it contains an embedded chip which provides additional security for transactions at stores or ATMs.

### Is there any complimentary access to international lounges for ICICI Coral credit cardholders?
No, ICICI Coral only offers complimentary access to domestic airport and railway lounges.

### What are the ICICI Coral Credit Card airport lounge access benefits?
This credit card offers 1 complimentary domestic airport lounge visit every quarter on spending at least Rs. 75,000 in the previous quarter.
