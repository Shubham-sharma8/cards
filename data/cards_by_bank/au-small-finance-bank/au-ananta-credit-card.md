## About the Card
Consumers who frequently travel and prefer to make bookings via AU Rewardz can make the most of this card with a **15% discount on hotel bookings** and**4 complimentary lounge visits per quarter** on booking flight tickets above Rs. 5,000 through AU Rewardz. Apart from these categories, cardholders who can spend Rs. 10 Lakh in a year can earn **up to 50,000 bonus reward points** annually.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the annual fee for AU Ananta Credit Card?
AU Ananta Credit Card charges a joining fee of Rs. 2,000 + applicable taxes and an annual fee of Rs. 2,000 + applicable taxes. The annual fee can be waived on spending Rs. 3 Lakh in a year.

### What reward rate does AU Ananta Credit Card offer?
The card offers 5 Reward Points per Rs. 100 spent on shopping, dining, and travel, 2 Reward Points per Rs. 100 on other eligible spends, and 1 Reward Point per Rs. 100 on insurance, utility, and telecom spends.

### Does AU Ananta Credit Card offer lounge access?
Yes, the card offers 4 complimentary airport lounge visits per quarter on booking flight tickets worth Rs. 5,000 or more through AU Rewardz.

### Can I get milestone benefits on AU Ananta Credit Card?
Yes, cardholders can earn up to 50,000 bonus Reward Points annually on achieving spending milestones of up to Rs. 10 Lakh in a year.

### Is AU Ananta Credit Card good for travel benefits?
Yes, frequent travellers can benefit from accelerated rewards, hotel booking discounts via AU Rewardz, complimentary lounge access, and travel-related insurance coverage.

### Who should choose AU Ananta Credit Card?
This card is suitable for consumers who frequently shop online, dine out, and travel regularly, especially those who prefer to make bookings through AU Rewardz and District to maximize benefits.
