## About the Card
AU Prathama Credit Card is an entry-level card designed for spenders looking to earn rewards on their everyday purchases. The card stands out primarily due to its very **low annual fee of Rs. 100** , along with benefits on day-to-day spends. The card comes with a base reward rate of **1 point per Rs. 100 spent**. However, consumers can earn **2X reward points on incremental spends** above Rs. 5,000 in a month, along with contactless payments. Cardholders can redeem the accumulated reward points at a value of **Rs. 0.25 per point**. Apart from the rewards program, the card also offers other basic perks, such as a fuel surcharge waiver and **500 bonus reward points** on the setup of a standing instruction or recurring transaction. Here is all you need to know about the AU Prathama Credit Card.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does the card offer any welcome benefits or cashback?
No. AU Prathama Credit Card does not offer any welcome benefit upon joining, and there is no cashback on any spend category.

### I am a beginner, can I apply for AU Prathama Credit Card?
Yes, this card is an entry-level credit card and is well-suited for first-time credit card users.

### How can I get annual fee waived on the AU Prathama Credit Card?
If your **total spends cross Rs. 75,000 in a year** , the annual fee of Rs. 100 + GST will be waived off automatically.

### What is the value of 1 reward point on the AU Prathama Credit Card?
Each reward point on the card is worth **Rs.0.25** which means **100 reward points = Rs. 25**.

### What are the insurance benefits on offered with the AU Prathama Credit Card?
The card comes offers card liability cover, credit shield, and device protection plan.
