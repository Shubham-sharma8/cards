## About the Card
AU Xcite Credit Card is the entry-level card in the Xcite credit card range, designed for individuals looking for a rewards credit card with a low annual fee. The card offers 15 Reward Points per Rs. 100 spent on dining, grocery, departmental stores, and movies, 3 Reward Points per Rs. 100 on online and offline spends, and 2 Reward Points per Rs. 100 on other retail transactions. It also offers 8 complimentary railway lounge visits per year, making it suitable for those who frequently travel by train. Also, consumers can opt for the AU Xcite ACE Credit Card as it is suitable for individuals who regularly spend across everyday categories and want to earn more reward points while availing additional travel-related benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Which spending categories earn the highest reward points on the AU Xcite Credit Card?
Consumers can earn 15 Reward Points for every Rs. 100 spent on dining, grocery shopping, movies, and departmental stores. These are the cards highest reward categories, making it a good option for everyday spends.

### How can I get the annual fee waived on AU Xcite Credit Card?
The annual fee of Rs. 249 + taxes is waived if you spend Rs. 1 lakh or more in a calendar year using the card.

### Does the AU Xcite Credit Card offer airport lounge access?
No. The card does not offer complimentary airport lounge access. Instead, it offers 8 complimentary railway lounge visits per year, with a maximum of 2 visits per quarter.

### Are insurance and utility bill payments eligible for reward points?
Yes. Consumers can earn 1 Reward Point per Rs. 100 spent on eligible insurance premium payments, utility bills, and telecom transactions.

### Can I convert large purchases into EMIs with AU Xcite Credit Card?
Yes. Transactions worth Rs. 2,000 or more can be converted into EMIs. You can also club up to 20 billed or unbilled transactions for EMI conversion, subject to the banks terms and conditions.

### Is AU Xcite Credit Card a good option for first-time credit card users?
Yes. With its low annual fee, accelerated rewards on daily spending categories, railway lounge access, fuel surcharge waiver, and fee waiver milestone, AU Xcite Credit Card is a suitable option for first-time cardholders looking for value without paying a high annual fee.
