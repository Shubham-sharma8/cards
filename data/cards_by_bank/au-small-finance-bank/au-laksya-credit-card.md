## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance
- **Welcome Benefit:** Welcome bonus worth the joining fee

### Milestone Benefits
- 1 Reward Point = Rs. 0.25, redeemable against e-vouchers, merchandise, flight or hotel bookings and other such options on AU Rewardz platform.
- 4,000 reward points or brand voucher worth Rs. 1,000 on card activation and 1st transaction made within 30 days of card issuance.
- 2 complimentary domestic lounge visits per calendar quarter on spending Rs. 50,000 in the same quarter
- Annual fee is waived off on spending Rs. 2 lakh or more in the previous year

- Discount on popular platforms like Amazon Fresh/Now, Zomato and District

### Insurance Cover

- Insurance Coverage: Card liability cover, credit shield and device protection plan offered under insurance benefits

- You are looking for a card with accelerated rewards on grocery, departmental store and contactless spends
