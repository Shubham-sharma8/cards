## About the Card
The Paytm AU Credit Card, offered in partnership between Paytm and AU Small Finance Bank, is a lifetime-free card that rewards everyday UPI, Paytm Gold and travel spends made through the Paytm app. Read on to know about the features and benefits of this card, followed by a detailed review.

## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Lifetime Free option from AU Small Finance Bank suited for travel & miles users with 12 domestic lounge visits/yr.

**Best For:** Domestic & international flyers value-optimizing airline/hotel loyalty points and lounge access

**Not Ideal For:** Low-travel users preferring straight monthly statement cashback

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 25,000+

### Pros (Good)
- Accelerated rewards on travel bookings made via Paytm Travel
- Good value-back on UPI transactions made via Paytm App
- Utility bill payments are not excluded and earn rewards when made via Paytm App
- Up to 12 free domestic lounge visits in a year
- 1:1 reward redemption value against Paytm Gold

### Cons (Bad)
- Lounge visits are tied to the condition of booking return tickets via Paytm Travel
- Low reward rate on transactions beyond Paytm App, and even lower value on making UPI transactions through other apps
- Lower reward redemption value when redeeming through the AU Rewardz Platform

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is the Paytm AU Credit Card lifetime free?
Yes, the card does not carry any joining or annual fee.

### What are the benefits of the Paytm AU Credit Card?
Paytm AU Credit Card offers up to 5% value-back on Paytm Travel bookings and up to 2% value-back on Paytm UPI payments as reward points. It also offers up to 12 complimentary domestic lounge visits every year.

### Does the Paytm AU Credit Card offer rewards on UPI payments made through other apps?
Yes, you will get rewards on UPI spends made through other apps but at a much lower rate of 1 reward point for every Rs. 400 spent.

### How can I redeem Paytm AU Credit Card reward points?
You can redeem the reward points earned on the Paytm AU Credit Card through the AU Rewardz portal where 1 reward point = Rs. 0.5. However, if you redeem them against Paytm Gold purchase, you get 1:1 redemption value.

### Is the Paytm AU Credit Card good for everyday UPI spends?
Yes, if you prefer Paytm for all your UPI spends, this credit card can be a good choice.
