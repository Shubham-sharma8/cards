## About the Card
AU Xcite Ace Credit Card is an entry-level cashback credit card that offers up to 3% cashback on eligible monthly spends, along with 2 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits every quarter (subject to spending conditions). The card also provides 8 complimentary railway lounge visits per year, a fuel surcharge waiver, and travel insurance benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I earn the maximum cashback with the AU Xcite Ace Credit Card?
You can earn up to 3% cashback by spending Rs. 20,000 or more in a calendar month on eligible retail transactions. The maximum cashback is capped at Rs. 600 per month, and certain transactions are excluded.** ** ** **

### Does the AU Xcite Ace Credit Card offer airport lounge access?
Yes. The card offers two complimentary domestic airport lounge visits every quarter if you spend at least Rs. 50,000 in the previous quarter. It does not provide international airport lounge access.

### Is there any annual fee waiver on the AU Xcite Ace Credit Card?
Yes. The annual fee of Rs. 749 plus applicable taxes is waived if you spend Rs. 2 lakh or more in an anniversary year, helping frequent users save on renewal charges.

### Which transactions are not eligible for cashback or milestone benefits?
Transactions such as wallet reloads, rent payments, fuel purchases, education fees, government payments, EMI conversions, BBPS transactions, cash withdrawals, and quasi-cash transactions are not considered for cashback eligibility.

### What travel and insurance benefits are available with this credit card?
The card offers railway lounge access, domestic airport lounge access, air accident insurance of up to Rs. 50 lakh, credit shield cover, purchase protection, and travel-related covers for baggage, flight delays, passport loss, and hijacking.
