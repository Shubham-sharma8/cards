## Frequently Asked Questions
### How are reward points earned on the AU Tejas credit card?
You earn 5 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on the AU Rewardz portal and 1 point per Rs. 100 on other purchases. Points can be redeemed at Rs. 0.25 per point, maximizing value for portal usage.

### Are there discounts or offers available with the AU Tejas credit card?
Yes, the card offers up to 10% discount at select merchants including Amazon Now/Fresh, Ola, Uber, Zomato, etc.

### What is the milestone benefit on this card?
Consumers can earn 500 reward points by completing just five transactions of Rs. 500 or more.

### How can I get the annual fee waived?
AU Tejas credit card’s annual fee can be waived by spending Rs. 1.5 lakh in a year. Regular use and strategic spending ensure that the card remains free of charges while maximizing rewards and benefits.

### How do I redeem reward points?
Reward points can be redeemed at Rs. 0.25 per point. For example, 1,000 points give Rs. 250. Using the AU Rewardz portal for purchases earns higher points, enhancing redemption value compared to regular spending.
