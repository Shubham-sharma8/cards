## About the Card
AU LIT Credit Card is a lifetime-free card that allows cardholders to choose benefits based on their lifestyle and spending behaviour. Cardholders can log in to the AU 0101 app and select the features they wish to avail for the next 90 days. However, cardholders have to pay a quarterly fee ranging from Rs. 49 to Rs. 399, according to the benefit they choose. The card offers up to 10X reward points on both online and offline spends. Additionally, cardholders can avail 5% cashback on day-to-day categories such as groceries and apparel. On top of this, the card offers complimentary memberships to ZEE5 and Amazon Prime, along with other basic benefits. Although the card is marketed as lifetime-free, it charges a quarterly fee between Rs. 49 and Rs. 399, which puts its lifetime-free status into question. Moreover, the price-wise complicated cashback and reward structure makes it challenging for cardholders to maximize benefits even on a single category.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is AU LIT Credit Card and how does it work?
AU LIT Credit Card is a lifetime-free card that allows cardholders to customize benefits based on their lifestyle and spending habits. Cardholders can activate features through the AU 0101 app for a 90-day period, with quarterly fees applicable depending on the selected benefits.

### What are the benefits of AU LIT Card?
The benefits offered by AU LIT Credit Card include up to 10X reward points on card spends, up to 5% cashback with additional value-back as milestone benefit, and memberships related to various lifestyle categories like fitness and entertainment.

### What is the eligibility for AU LIT Credit Card?
To be eligible for AU LIT Card, the applicants must be between the age of 25 and 65 years if they are self-employed and between 21 and 60 years old if they are salaried employees. They should also be an Indian resident.

### Are there any charges on AU LIT Credit Card?
While the card is marketed as lifetime free, most benefits require a quarterly activation fee ranging from Rs. 49 to Rs. 399. These charges are applicable every 90 days based on the selected features.

### Who should apply for AU LIT Credit Card?
This card suits consumers who want customized benefits and are comfortable paying quarterly fees for selected features. Those preferring a simple, low-maintenance rewards card may find better alternatives.
