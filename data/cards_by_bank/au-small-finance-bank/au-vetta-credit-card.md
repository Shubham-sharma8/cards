## About the Card
AU Vetta Credit Card is a suitable option for consumers who want to earn accelerated rewards on everyday spends. Cardholders can earn **4 reward points per Rs. 100 on grocery and departmental store** purchases, 2 points on other retail spends, and **1 point on utility and telecom payments**. The accumulated rewards can be redeemed for e-vouchers, merchandise, mobile/DTH recharges, or flight/hotel bookings. Apart from rewards, the card offers several benefits for frequent travellers, including **complimentary domestic & international lounge visits**, railway lounge access, and 24×7 concierge services. Cardholders can also earn up to **1,500 bonus rewards** through milestone benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for AU Vetta Credit Card?
You can apply for AU Small Finance Bank Vetta Credit Card online via official website of AU Small Finance Bank. The application process involves filling out a form, submitting necessary KYC documents, and completing identity verification.

### How can I earn reward points with AU Vetta Credit Card?
You can earn 4 reward points per Rs. 100 on grocery and departmental store purchases, 2 reward points per Rs. 100 on other retail spends and 1 reward point per Rs. 100 spent on utility & telecom categories.

### What are the milestone benefits associated with AU Vetta Credit Card?
AU Vetta Credit Card offers milestone rewards to encourage regular usage. Cardholders can earn 500 bonus points on spending Rs. 50,000 per quarter, an additional 1,000 bonus points for spending Rs. 1 Lakh per quarter, and Rs. 1,000 worth of vouchers on annual spends of Rs. 2.5 Lakh.

### Does AU Vetta Credit Card offer lounge access?
Yes, the card offers complimentary domestic & international lounge visits. You get 1 complimentary domestic lounge visit per quarter, 2 complimentary railway lounge visits per quarter, and 1 international lounge visit per quarter through the Priority Pass program.

### Is there a fuel surcharge waiver with this card?
Yes, the card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel transactions ranging between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000. The waiver is capped at Rs. 250 per statement cycle, making it a useful benefit for regular fuel spenders.

### How can I redeem reward points earned via AU Vetta Credit Card?
Reward points earned on AU Vetta Credit Card can be redeemed through the AU 0101 app or the net banking portal. The points can be used to avail e-gift vouchers, merchandise, or other redemption options offered by the bank, depending on the number of points accumulated.
