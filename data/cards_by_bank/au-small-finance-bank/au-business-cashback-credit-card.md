## About the Card
AU Business Cashback Credit Card is an ideal choice for consumers who are looking for a basic credit card as per their business requirements. This credit card offers **1% cashback on the repayment of total amount due** , capped at Rs. 1,000 per month. Besides this, it also offers **8 complimentary railway lounge visits** annually, making it a preferable option for frequent train travellers. It also offers **30% discount at over 300 partner restaurants** under the Silver Spoon Dining Program, which adds to its value. However, this credit card does not offer any airport lounge visits and welcome/renewal benefits. Overall, this credit card is a suitable option for small business owners seeking a credit card with basic benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What cashback does AU Business Cashback Credit Card offer?
AU Business Cashback Credit Card offers 1% cashback on repayment of the total amount due every month. However, this cashback is capped at Rs. 1,000 per month.

### What lounge access benefits are available with this card?
Cardholders get 8 complimentary railway lounge visits per year, limited to 2 visits per quarter. Lounge access is available at select railway stations using the VISA or RuPay variant of the card.

### What dining benefits come with AU Business Cashback Credit Card?
The card offers up to 30% discount at over 300 partner restaurants across 30 cities under the Silver Spoon Dining Program, making it beneficial for frequent dining and business meals.

### Is there any fee waiver on AU Business Cashback Credit Card?
Yes, the monthly fee of Rs. 99 plus taxes is waived if retail spends on the card exceed Rs. 10,000 in a billing cycle, helping reduce overall card cost.

### What fuel benefits does this card offer?
The card offers 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000, capped at Rs. 200 per month, providing modest savings on regular fuel expenses.
