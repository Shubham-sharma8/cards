## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are CheQ Points?
CheQ Points are the reward currency for the CheQ AU Credit Card. You earn 24 CheQ Points per Rs. 200 on Amazon, Flipkart, Zomato, CheQ Travel, and utility bill payments; 10 points per Rs. 200 on several everyday categories; 5 points per Rs. 200 on UPI spends; and 2 points per Rs. 200 on other spends.

### How can I redeem CheQ Points?
CheQ Points can be redeemed on the CheQ App for flights and hotels (2:1), shopping across 30+ brands (2:1), and utility bill payments (4:1).

### What is the LED Card feature?
CheQ App is India’s first LED credit card that glows green when you tap the card for payment. To get a card with LED feature, you have to pay a one-time additional fee of Rs. 999.

### I don’t make bill payments via CheQ App. Should I get this card?
Since value-back is offered in the form of CheQ Points which can only be redeemed on the CheQ App and utiltiy bill payments accrue accelearted rewards on this app, those who do not use the app will not be able to maximise the benefits. However, CheQ app allows you to make your bill payments, travel bookings and shop from a wide range of popular brands and platforms, thus not limiting you to only a few features or categories. So, you can use this app as a complete solution to your spending needs and maximise your savings. In case, you don’t wish to use CheQ, you can explore other cards that align better with your needs.

### Do I get any benefits on fuel transactions using this card
While fuel spends do not earn any rewards, you can save up to Rs. 150 per month in the form of fuel surcharge waiver.
