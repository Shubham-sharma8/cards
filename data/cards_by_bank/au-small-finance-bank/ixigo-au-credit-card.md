## About the Card
Ixigo AU is a co-branded travel credit card which focuses on offering benefits across train, bus, flight and hotel bookings. After MakeMyTrip, Yatra.com and EaseMyTrip, Ixigo stepped into the credit card market to offer co-branded and other travel benefits to users. The card offers direct discount to Ixigo users on flight, hotel and bus bookings, and accelerated rewards on railway ticket bookings. Besides, you also get 5 rewards per Rs. 200 on all transactions, including UPI. This enables savings potential via travel benefits beyond Ixigo, when you make your travel bookings offline or online through third-party portals or directly from hotel or airline websites. If you are an Ixigo loyalist and a beginner, this card can be a great choice, even when you do not own any other credit card since you get a decent reward rate on general spends. Besides, it can be a best suited choice for domestic travellers who prefer railways over airways due to accelerated savings on railway bookings and good number of free railway lounge visits in a year. However, the card lacks significantly in offering overall international travel benefits even when it charges zero forex markup fee. Read on to find out more.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for Ixigo AU Credit Card?
You can apply for Ixigo AU Credit Card by visiting the official website of AU Small Finance Bank. You need to fill out the application form and submit the required documents. Alternatively, you can visit the nearest AU Bank branch and apply manually by submitting a duly filled application form.

### Is Ixigo AU Credit Card lifetime free?
Yes, Ixigo AU Credit Card is a lifetime free credit card. AU bank is offering it without any joining or annual fee.

### What is the reward rate of Ixigo AU Credit Card?
With Ixigo AU Credit Card, you earn 2.5% reward points per Rs. 200 spent, that is 5 reward points on every spend of Rs. 200. This reward rate is applicable on all online, offline and UPI spends. However, no reward points are earned on spends related to fuel, rent, insurance, jewellery, international transactions, and wallets.

### Does Ixigo AU Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, this card offers extensive lounge access benefits. Cardholders can avail 16 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year, along with 1 complimentary international airport lounge visit in a year. Additionally, the card offers 16 complimentary railway lounge visits annually, making it ideal for frequent domestic travellers.

### How much can I save on travel bookings with Ixigo AU Credit Card?
With this credit card, you can save 10% on bus bookings, with a maximum discount of Rs. 300 per month. On flight and hotel bookings, you can save 10% per transaction, with a capping of Rs. 3,000 per month on each category.

### Is Ixigo AU Credit Card good for international travel?
Ixigo AU Credit Card charges zero forex markup fee and offers 1 complimentary international airport lounge access per year. However, it does not offer any additional benefits for international travel, such as discounts on foreign transactions or international hotel stays, or even any reward points. If you frequently travel abroad, you may want to consider other credit cards that offer better savings on international spends.

### How much is the interest free credit period with ixigo AU credit card?
AU ixigo credit card offers up to 48 days of interest-free credit period to its cardholders.

### Are there any charges for the add-on cards of the ixigo AU credit card?
The add-on cards of AU ixigo credit cards do not come with any additional annual fee.
