## About the Card
In addition to cashback benefits, the card allows eligible transactions of Rs. 500 and above to be converted into EMI, subject to the minimum total value requirement of Rs. 2,000. Overall, AU Kosmo Credit Card can be a suitable option for consumers looking for a lifetime free card with cashback on UPI, and other online/offline spends.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is AU Kosmo Credit Card?
AU Kosmo Credit Card is a lifetime free credit card offered by AU Small Finance Bank in partnership with Kiwi. It offers cashback on eligible UPI Scan & Pay, online payment, and shopping transactions, along with fuel and EMI-related benefits.

### How much cashback can I earn on UPI payments with AU Kosmo Credit Card?
Cardholders can earn 1.5% cashback on eligible UPI Scan & Pay transactions made through the Kiwi app, and 0.5% cashback on online payments via Kiwi app, along with other offline & online shopping spends.

### Is the 1.5% cashback available on all UPI transactions?
No. The 1.5% cashback rate is applicable to eligible UPI Scan & Pay transactions made through the Kiwi app. The cashback rate and eligibility may vary for other types of transactions.

### Is AU Kosmo Credit Card suitable for regular UPI users?
Yes. The card can be particularly useful for consumers who frequently make UPI Scan & Pay payments through the Kiwi app, as these transactions offer the highest cashback rate of 1.5% among the card\'s listed cashback categories.

### Can I convert AU Kosmo Credit Card transactions into EMI?
Yes. Eligible transactions of Rs. 500 and above can be converted into EMI, subject to a minimum total transaction value of Rs. 2,000 and applicable terms and conditions.

### Who should get AU Kosmo Credit Card?
AU Kosmo Credit Card is best suited for consumers seeking a lifetime free credit card that offers cashback on UPI, online and shopping spends, along with basic benefits such as fuel surcharge waiver and EMI conversion.
