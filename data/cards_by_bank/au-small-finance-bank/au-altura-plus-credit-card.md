## Frequently Asked Questions
### What rewards does the AU Altura+ Credit Card offer?
The card provides 1.5% cashback on offline retail spends, 2 Reward Points per Rs. 100 on online transactions, and 1 Reward Point per Rs. 100 on utility, telecom, and insurance payments.

### Are there any milestone benefits?
Yes, cardholders receive 500 bonus Reward Points for monthly retail spends of Rs. 20,000 or more. Milestone rewards are applied automatically after meeting the minimum spend requirement each month.

### What are the lounge visits offered by AU Altura+ Credit Card?
The card includes eight complimentary railway lounge visits per year. Access is limited to railway lounges and is not applicable to airport lounges or other travel facilities.

### What are the fees and charges of AU Altura+ Credit Card?
Joining and annual fees are Rs. 499 each, plus taxes. Finance charges are 3.75% per month, and late payment fees range from Rs. 10 to Rs. 1,300 depending on the outstanding amount.

### Are there any fee waivers?
The joining fee is waived for Rs. 20,000 spent within 90 days of activation, and the annual fee is waived if Rs. 80,000 was spent in the previous year.

### Who is the AU Altura+ Credit Card suitable for?
AU Altura+ Credit Card suits consumers who shop both online and offline, travel by train occasionally, and want basic cashback and reward points. It requires regular usage to fully utilize milestone benefits.
