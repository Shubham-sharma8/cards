## About the Card
AU Fi MagniFi is a lifetime free credit card that offers accelerated benefits on weekend spends. It offers 5% value-back on all online and in-store weekend spends as well as 20% discounts on popular brands like Zomato, BookMyShow, Amazon, and Zepto during weekends. Besides this, it offers a low reward rate of 0.5% weekday spends.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Are there any special discounts on AU Fi MagniFi Credit Card?
Yes, AU Fi MagniFi Credit Card offers 20% off (up to Rs. 100 per month) on BookMyShow, Amazon, Zepto, and Zomato food orders, with a minimum spend of Rs. 500, applicable only on weekends, making it ideal for weekend entertainment, dining, and online shopping.

### How can I redeem my Fi-Points?
With the AU Fi MagniFi Credit Card, 1 Fi-Point equals Rs. 0.25. Fi-Points can be redeemed to pay your card bill, convert into air miles, or get up to 50% off on gift vouchers via the Fi Money app, giving you flexible ways to use your rewards for shopping, travel, or everyday expenses.

### Can I convert purchases into EMI?
Yes, transactions of Rs. 500 or more can be converted into EMI by clubbing up to 20 billed or unbilled transactions, with a total EMI value up to Rs. 2,000, making it easier to manage larger or multiple purchases.

### Does AU Fi MagniFi Credit Card offer lounge access or premium travel benefits?
No, this card does not provide airport lounge access or premium travel perks. It is primarily designed for everyday spends.

### Are there any exclusions on rewards?
Yes, you cannot earn rewards on EMI, fuel, rent, insurance, government, and educational spends.

### Who should get the AU Fi MagniFi Credit Card?
AU Fi MagniFi Credit Card is a lifetime-free card ideal for weekend spenders and frequent Zepto or Amazon shoppers, offering high weekend rewards and flexible Fi-Point redemptions, making it a practical choice for everyday spending, dining, entertainment, and online shopping.
