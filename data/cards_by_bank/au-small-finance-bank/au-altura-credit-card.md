## About the Card
AU Altura Credit Card offers basic cashback benefits and some travel perks. It has a joining and **annual fee of Rs. 199 + taxes.** The card offers **2% cashback on groceries, departmental store, and utility bill payments** , capped at Rs. 50 per month, and **1% cashback on other retail spends,** also capped at Rs. 50 per month. Besides this, the card also includes 8 complimentary railway lounge visits per year and a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel transactions, saving up to Rs. 1,200 annually. Additionally, this card also offers milestone benefits that offer cashback when a minimum spend of Rs. 10,000 is met every month. Overall, this card is a suitable option for customers who want a card that offers cashback on day-to-day spends.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the joining and annual fee of AU Altura Credit Card?
AU Altura Credit Card has a joining fee and an annual fee of Rs. 199 plus applicable taxes. Fees can be waived if the minimum spending conditions of Rs. 10,000 for joining and Rs. 40,000 annually are met.

### How does the cashback system work?
AU Altura Credit Card offers 2% cashback on groceries, departmental stores, and utility bill payments, capped at Rs. 50 per month, and 1% cashback on other retail spends, also capped at Rs. 50 per month. Additional cashback is available on milestone spending.

### What are the railway lounge benefits?
Cardholders receive 8 complimentary railway lounge visits per year. Add-on cardholders share these visits. There is no airport lounge access included with this card.

### Are there any fuel-related benefits?
The card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000, with a maximum annual benefit of Rs. 1,200, applicable at all fuel stations.

### Who should consider this card?
This card is suitable for individuals with frequent grocery and utility expenses and railway travellers seeking lounge access. It is also beneficial for those seeking low joining and annual fees with basic cashback benefits.
