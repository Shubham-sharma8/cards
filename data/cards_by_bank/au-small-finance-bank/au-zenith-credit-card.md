## About the Card
AU Zenith+ Credit Card is a premium metal card that caters to the needs of frequent travellers as well as rewards high-spenders. A competitive addition to the list of premium cards, such as SBI Card Elite and HDFC Regalia Gold, this card offers benefits across multiple categories, like international travel, movies, golf, dining and others. Additional benefits, including complimentary airport lounge access and low forex mark-up fee further add to the card’s value.
