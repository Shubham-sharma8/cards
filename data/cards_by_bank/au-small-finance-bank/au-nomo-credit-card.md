## About the Card
Cardholders can earn **2 Reward Points for every Rs. 100 spent on retail** purchases and 1 Reward Point per Rs. 100 on utility and insurance payments. These points can be redeemed through the AU Rewardz catalogue at a value of**Rs. 0.25 per point**. Additionally, the card comes with 500 welcome points, quarterly milestone rewards, and 1% fuel surcharge waiver on eligible fuel transactions. However, the card does not offer any benefits on international airport lounges and is primarily designed for domestic travel and everyday spending, making it a decent option for first-time credit users.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the key benefits of AU Nomo Credit Card?
The card offers 2 Reward Points per Rs. 100 on retail spends, 1 point per Rs. 100 on utilities and insurance. It also offers 8 domestic airport & 8 railway lounge visits per year and charges a low forex markup of 0.99%.

### What travel benefits are included?
The card offers 8 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits and 8 railway lounge visits annually. The card also charges a low forex markup fee of 0.99%, making it a preferable option for international transactions.

### Are there any welcome or milestone rewards?
Yes, cardholders can earn 500 welcome points on the first transaction within 30 days of activation. Additionally, 500 points for quarterly retail spends of Rs. 25,000, and another 500 points on quarterly spends of Rs. 50,000.

### Is AU Nomo Credit Card suitable for frequent travellers?
Yes, with travel benefits like a low forex markup fee of 0.99%, 8 complimentary domestic airport and 8 railway lounge visits yearly, this credit card is a suitable option for frequent travellers. Besides this, cardhodlers can also redeem the accumulated points against travel spends via rewards catalogue.

### How does the reward program work?
Cardholders can earn 2 Reward Points per Rs. 100 on retail purchases and 1 point per Rs. 100 on utility or insurance payments. Accumulated points can be redeemed via AU Rewardz catalogue for flights, hotels, recharges, merchandise at a value of Rs. 0.25/point.
