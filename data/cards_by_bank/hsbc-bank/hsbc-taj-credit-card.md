## About the Card
HSBC Taj Credit Card, offered in partnership with the Indian Hotels Company Ltd (IHCL), is a co-branded hotel credit card **tailored to the needs of affluent consumers who value luxury travel and hospitality experiences.** While the Marriott Bonvoy HDFC Credit Card introduced India to hotel co-branded credit cards in the mid-range segment last year, the HSBC Taj Credit Card is India’s first hotel co-branded card in the super-premium category. With an annual fee of Rs. 1.10 Lakh, it also becomes one of the costliest credit cards in India. Cardholders get**exclusive benefits not just with Taj Properties but also across other hotel chains under the IHCL Group, including SeleQtions, Gateway, Vivanta, Ginger Hotels and amã Stays & Trails.** A complimentary membership to Taj InnerCircle Platinum NeuPass gives access to exclusive privileges at participating hotels, further enhancing consumer experience. Beyond hotel benefits, the card also caters to travellers’ needs with benefits such as unlimited lounge access, discount on flight ticket bookings, discount on duty-free spends and complimentary concierge service. Direct discount on some popular brands like Starbucks, Tira, PVR Cinemas, Zomato, and Swiggy provide cardholders with more opportunities to save.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the documents required to apply for this card?
To apply for an HSBC Taj Credit Card, cardholders need to submit proof of residence, identity proof and their permanent account number (PAN) card. Additionally, income proofs such as the latest salary slip may be required if you\'re a salaried employee.

### What benefits does the NeuPass membership offer?
The complimentary Platinum NeuPass membership offered on the card offers benefits like additional NeuCoins/points; early/priority check-in and late check-out; unlimited room upgrades to the next category, happy hours and a dedicated customer care service among other benefits.

### What is the forex markup fee on the card?
The card charges a forex markup fee of 3.5% of the transaction amount. However, the fee is waived off for India’s executive banking clients.

### How do I contact HSBC for more details on the card?
You can contact the bank at their toll-free number 1800 569 1111.

### Does the card offer a fuel surcharge waiver?
Yes, the card offers fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000 on all fuel pumps.
