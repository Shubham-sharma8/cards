## Frequently Asked Questions
### Can I use HSBC RuPay Cashback Credit Card internationally?
Yes, you can use HSBC RuPay credit cards for international transactions in approximately 160 countries and regions worldwide under the JCB network.

### What is HSBC SimplyPay?
HSBC SimplyPay is a mobile application that allows you to make UPI payments and pay bills. You can link your resident account and HSBC RuPay credit card to the app to make the payments.

### Can I earn cashback on international spends using HSBC RuPay Cashback Credit Card?
All foreign currency transactions are excluded from cashback benefits offered by HSBC Cashback RuPay Credit Card. However, this card currently does not charge any forex mark-up fee on such transactions.

### Will I earn any cashback on UPI transactions on HSBC RuPay Cashback Credit Card?
Yes, HSBC Cashback RuPay credit cardholders will earn cashback on all their UPI transactions, except on the excluded spending categories.

### What are the benefits of EazyDiner Prime membership?
With EazyDiner Prime membership, you can get up to Rs. 3,000 savings with 25% to 50% off at premium bars/restaurants. You can also get priority reservations at premium restaurants, earn 2X EazyPoints redeemable for dining bills, and get access to dedicated VIP EazyDiner Prime Concierge.
