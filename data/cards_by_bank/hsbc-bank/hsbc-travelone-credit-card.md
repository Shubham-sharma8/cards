## About the Card
HSBC TravelOne is an airline-agnostic travel credit card. Cardholders get accelerated reward points on travel expenses, which can be redeemed instantly against leading airlines and hotel partners including Air India, British Airways, Etihad, Qatar Airways, Accor, Marriott, and many others. A 1:1 conversion ratio for most of these partners ensures a good value for travellers. Moreover, payments made for travel bookings on HSBC Rewards Marketplace come with up to 12% rewards using TravelOne card. Additional discount offers on major travel platforms such as Yatra, EaseMyTrip, Cleartrip, PayTM, and StayVista Hotels, along with complimentary lounge access, enhance its appeal as a travel card.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How to activate the complimentary EazyDiner Prime membership?
The eligible cardholders can activate the 3 months EazyDiner Prime membership by first downloading the EazyDiner app and signing up or logging in. Then select the 3 months membership plan and click on the ‘Pay Now’ option. Enter the coupon code received and complete your purchase to activate the membership.

### How do I redeem my reward points earned through HSBC TravelOne Credit Card?
To redeem your points, log in to the HSBC India app and select your card. Click on ‘’ and select the ‘Redeem points’ option. Then go to the rewards marketplace after tapping on ‘Redeem points now’. Select the preferred category and enter the details, as required. Enter the number of points you wish to redeem and continue. Read and accept the terms and conditions and place your order.

### For which airline partners can I use my HSBC reward points?
HSBC TravelOne credit card users can convert their reward points in 1:1 ratio into airmiles for Air India, Air Canada, Air France–KLM, British Airways, Etihad Airways, EVA Air, Hainan Airlines, Japan Airlines, Singapore Airlines, Qantas Airlines, Qatar Airlines, Thai Airways, Turkish Airlines, United Airlines and Vietnam Airlines. They can also convert reward points into miles for Air Asia, where 1 point = 3 miles.

### What are the hotel partners for HSBC TravelOne Credit Card reward points redemption?
You can convert your accrued reward points into hotel loyalty points of Accor, IHG® Hotels & Resorts, Marriott International, and Wyndham Hotels & Resorts in the ratio of 1:1. You can also redeem them for hotel points at Shangri-La where 5 reward points = 1 hotel loyalty point.
