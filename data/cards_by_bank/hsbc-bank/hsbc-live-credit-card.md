## About the Card
Apart from cashback, HSBC Live+ Credit Card offers several lifestyle and travel benefits, including complimentary domestic & international airport lounge visits, low forex markup fee of 1.99%, dining discounts at 200+ partner restaurants, movie offers and exclusive Visa Infinite privilege. Overall, it can be a suitable option for consumers who want a card offering cashback on everyday spends, along with decent travel and lifestyle privileges.

## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Still India's #1 offline + online Dining, Grocery, Shopping & Utility cashback card (10% up to Rs. 1,200/mo + 1.5% unlimited base) even after Nov 2026 lounge trims.

**Best For:** Households spending up to Rs. 12,000/mo on dining, groceries (incl. offline D-Mart/supermarkets), Zomato, Swiggy, Blinkit, utility bills & eligible shopping plus everyday 1.5% base spends

**Not Ideal For:** Users living in non-HSBC serviceable pincodes, heavy Amazon/Flipkart shoppers wanting >1.5%, or frequent lounge flyers

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 15,000 – Rs. 40,000

### Pros (Good)
- 10% cashback on food, dining, grocery, shopping and utility bill payments

- Complimentary domestic and international airport lounge visits
- Multiple welcome benefits worth more than Rs. 3,000

### Cons (Bad)

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the cashback rate offered by HSBC Live+ Credit Card?
HSBC Live+ Credit Card offers 10% cashback on eligible food, dining, grocery, shopping and utility bill payments, up to Rs. 1,200 per month. It also offers unlimited 1.5% cashback on other eligible spends.

### What is the maximum cashback I can earn on HSBC Live+ Credit Card?
You can earn up to Rs. 1,200 cashback per month on the eligible 10% cashback categories. In addition, you can earn unlimited 1.5% cashback on other eligible spends.

### Does HSBC Live+ Credit Card offer airport lounge access?
Yes. The card offers up to 2 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year, with a maximum of 1 visit in six months. It also offers 1 complimentary international airport lounge visit every year.

### Does HSBC Live+ Credit Card offer movie discounts?
Yes. Cardholders can avail Buy 1 Get 1 offers on movie and live event tickets through District and BookMyShow as per the applicable terms and conditions.

### Is HSBC Live+ Credit Card suitable for international travel?
It can be a suitable option for international travellers as it offers a 1.99% forex markup fee, 1 complimentary international airport lounge visit annually and a complimentary 1GB Global eSIM with discounted top-ups.
