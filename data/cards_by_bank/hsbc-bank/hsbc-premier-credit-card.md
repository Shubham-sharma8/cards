## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Top-tier super-premium challenger to HDFC Infinia & ICICI Emeralde Private Metal for HSBC Premier clients.

**Best For:** HNIs and senior executives eligible for HSBC Premier who travel internationally and want Infinia-grade perks with low forex

**Not Ideal For:** Non-metro residents or users unable to qualify for HSBC Premier banking

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 75,000+

### Pros (Good)
- 0.99% low forex markup + 3% base 1:1 transfer value yields net positive ~2% on foreign spends
- Rs. 20k renewal fee waived for maintaining HSBC Premier status without spend stress
- Unlimited domestic & international lounge access with zero spend threshold

### Cons (Bad)
- Gated behind HSBC Premier relationship (Rs. 40L TRV or Rs. 3L+ net salary)
- HSBC's city footprint is limited to select metros in India
