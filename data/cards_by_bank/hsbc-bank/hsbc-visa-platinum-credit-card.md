## About the Card
Despite having no joining fee, the card comes with decent welcome benefits, including an **Amazon voucher worth Rs. 250** , 3-month **Swiggy One Membership** , and 2,000 bonus reward points. In addition, cardholders can avail exclusive travel and lifestyle benefits through Visa Signature platform, such as a complimentary **12-month Times Prime subscription** and cashback on travel bookings through MakeMyTrip.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is HSBC Platinum Credit Card good?
HSBC Visa Platinum is an entry-level credit card, ideal for individuals who are new to credit. You can earn reward points on every spend and redeem them for airmiles, vouchers, and more, as per your preferences.

### How can the HSBC Platinum Credit Card annual fee be waived?
HSBC does not charge any joining or annual fee from HSBC Platinum credit card users.

### Is the HSBC Platinum Credit Card lifetime free?
Yes, HSBC Visa Platinum credit card is a lifetime free credit card without any joining or annual fee.

### Does the HSBC Platinum Credit Card have lounge access?
No, HSBC Platinum credit card does not offer any complimentary airport lounge access to its users.

### What is the credit limit on the HSBC Platinum Credit Card?
Credit card limit varies depending on the individual and as per their credit score, credit history, income and other related factors. So, there is no fixed credit limit that is assigned to all the cardholders.

### What is the movie offer on HSBC Platinum Credit Card?
Currently, HSBC Visa Platinum Credit Card does not offer any exclusive benefits on movie spends. However, you can earn reward points on your online as well as offline transactions.

### How to redeem points earned on HSBC Platinum Credit Card?
You can redeem your accumulated reward points through the HSBC rewards catalogue for products or vouchers. Additionally, you can convert your points into air miles at a 2:1 ratio.

### How to apply for HSBC Platinum Credit Card?
To apply for HSBC Visa Platinum Credit Card online, visit the official HSBC website and apply through the card’s page. Alternatively, you can visit any of the nearest bank branches and apply offline by submitting the credit card application form.
