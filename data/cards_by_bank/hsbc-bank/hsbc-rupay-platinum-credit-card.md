## About the Card
The HSBC RuPay Platinum Credit Card is a **lifetime-free rewards credit card with UPI-linked benefits**. At zero annual fee, the card offers rewards on all transactions. Plus, the Amazon gift voucher, cashback benefits, and EazyDiner membership on activation offer a good start to maximize benefits on everyday purchases. In addition to these features, **what gives this card an edge over other lifetime free RuPay credit cards is the miles conversion feature**. Unlike most RuPay cards that come from the entry-level category, with the HSBC variant, consumers can utilize the accumulated reward points not just across gift cards but across 15+ airline and hotel partners such as Air India, Air Asia, British Airways, Accor, Marriott International, and Shangri-La, among others. To add more to the overall value-back, it **also offers some co-branded benefits** (limited period offer) with Swiggy, Tira, Paytm, and Cleatrip. This card is a good choice for users seeking their first credit card with zero annual fee at an easy income eligibility. With this launch, HSBC puts forth a decent addition to the list of RuPay credit cards in India, adding more to its continuous climb in the cards market. Read on to know more.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How do I use the HSBC RuPay Platinum Credit Card for UPI payments?
To make UPI payments, you need to first add the HSBC RuPay card to the HSBC Simply Pay or other eligible UPI app. On successful addition, you can make seamless payments via scanning the QR code.

### Can this card be used for international transactions?
Yes, you can use the HSBC RuPay Platinum Credit Card for international transactions, but a forex transaction fee of 3.5% is applicable on such spends.

### What is the interest-free period offered on this credit card?
The card offers an interest free period of 48 days.

### What is the validity of the complimentary Amazon gift voucher?
The Amazon voucher is valid for a period of six months from the date of issuance.

### Does the card offer rewards on fuel transactions?
No, the card does not offer rewards on fuel transactions.
