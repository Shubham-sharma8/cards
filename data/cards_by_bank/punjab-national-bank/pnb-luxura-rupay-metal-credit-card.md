## About the Card
PNB LUXURA Credit Card is a premium offering on RuPay EKAA network which comes with luxury-oriented benefits. Suitable for high net worth individuals, this card comes with a credit limit ranging from Rs. 1 lakh to Rs. 15 lakh (approx.). Its benefits majorly cater to the travel needs of the cardholders through 3% value-back as rewards, complimentary HoteLux membership and premium hospitality benefits at ITC Hotels. In addition to these travel savings, this PNB credit card also attempts to provide an effortless travel experience through a variety of services. Furthermore, this card also offers discounts on movie tickets and fast track immigration, accelerated rewards on dining spends, golf access, and others, thus covering a wide range of lifestyle categories. Read on to know more about the fees, features and benefits of PNB LUXURA RuPay Metal Credit Card.

## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- 40,000 rewards points on spending Rs. 50,000 within 90 days
- Joining Fee: On spending more than Rs. 2.5 lakh within 3 billing cycles from the card activation date
- Annual Fee: On spending more than Rs. 8 lakh in a year
- 10,000 reward points on Rs. 5 lakh annual spends

### Dining Benefits
- 3X Rewards: 12 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on travel and dining
- Hotel dining and spa credits worth up to USD 200
- 1+1 dining with set menu at Signature ITC restaurants, max. 1 table per bill

### Movie / Entertainment
- Buy 1 get up to Rs. 500 off on 2nd movie ticket4 on BookMyShow or similar platforms, once a month
- Up to Rs. 6,000 annual savings on movies via buy 1 get 1 offer

### Golf Privileges
- 4 complimentary golf rounds/lessons per year, max. 1 per quarter
- 24/7 Concierge Services: Domestic services for card rentals, golf reservations, travel reservation assistance, etc. and international services for lost passport/luggage assistance, embassy and hospital assistance, etc.

### Insurance Cover
- Insurance Coverage: Personal accident cover of Rs. 50 lakh, air accident cover of Rs. 1 crore, purchase protection cover of Rs. 2 lakh and travel insurance for baggage loss of Rs. 20,000.

### Get This Card If
- You are a frequent traveller and want a credit card with premium travel benefits
- You often stay at luxury hotels, including ITC Hotels, and wish to save on this expense
- You are a high spender and can make the most of the card benefits by availing the spend-based milestone bonuses

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I waive off the annual fee of PNB LUXURA RuPay Credit Card?
The annual fee of PNB RuPay LUXURA can be waived off on spending Rs. 8 lakh in a year.

### On which network is PNB LUXURA Credit Card offered?
Currently, PNB offers its premium card LUXURA on RuPay and Visa network.

### Who can get PNB LUXURA RuPay Credit Card?
Those who are between the age of 21 and 70 years and earn a minimum of Rs. 1.5 lakh per month, if salaried, or Rs. 18 lakh per year, if self-employed.

### Can I get PNB LUXURA RuPay Credit Card against an FD?
Yes, PNB LUXURA RuPay Credit Card is also offered against an FD of minimum Rs. 1.5 lakh, subject to other eligibility criteria as per the bank’s policy.

### Is PNB LUXURA a metal credit card?
Yes, PNB LUXURA RuPay is a metal credit card, however, you can also opt for a non-metal variant that comes at a joining fee of Rs. 6,000 + GST and an annual fee of Rs. 4,500 + GST.
