## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the welcome benefits of the PNB EMT RuPay Platinum Credit Card?
On activation, this card offers a welcome voucher worth Rs. 3,000 split across flights (Rs. 500), hotels (Rs. 1,000), and holidays (Rs. 1,500), along with 300+ reward points credited to your account immediately after card issuance.

### How can I save on flight and hotel bookings using this card?
You get 10% off up to Rs. 1,000 on domestic flights and 10% off up to Rs. 5,000 on international flights via EaseMyTrip. Hotel discounts include 20% off up to Rs. 5,000 domestically and 20% off up to Rs. 10,000 internationally.

### Does the PNB EMT RuPay Platinum Credit Card provide lounge access?
Yes, the card offers complimentary access to both domestic and international airport lounges, making it ideal for frequent travellers.

### How can the annual fee be waived for this card?
The annual fee of Rs. 2,000 plus taxes can be waived if you spend a minimum of Rs. 1 Lakh in the previous year, making it cost-effective for frequent travelers with high annual spending.

### Are there any insurance benefits with this credit card?
Yes, cardholders receive insurance coverage of up to Rs. 2 Lakh for accidental death and personal total disability, providing financial protection alongside travel perks and discounts.

### Who should consider getting the PNB EMT RuPay Platinum Credit Card?
This card is best suited for consumers who frequently travel, book flights, hotels, or holidays through EaseMyTrip, want airport lounge access, and can meet the spending requirement to avail fee waivers.
