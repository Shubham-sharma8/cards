## Frequently Asked Questions
### I have Indian citizenship but I travel a lot overseas. Can I still apply for Paytm SBI Card SELECT?
Yes, as long as you are a resident of India, you can apply for this credit card. However, it is advisable to contact SBI Card customer service to understand the detailed eligibility criteria for this card.

### Are all kinds of transactions rewarded with 5% cashback on Paytm SBI Card SELECT?
No, only travel, movie, and Paytm Mall purchases made through the Paytm app are eligible for 5% cashback. Other purchases through the Paytm app are eligible for 2% cashback, while all other non-Paytm spends will earn 1% cashback.

### Can I make utility bill payments via Paytm SBI Card SELECT?
Yes, you can pay your electricity, telephone, mobile, and other utility bills via Paytm and earn 2% value-back on these spends. Besides this, you can also make utility bill payments using the Easy Bill Pay facility.

### Does Paytm SBI Card SELECT offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, Paytm SBI Card SELECT offers 4 complimentary domestic lounge visits per year (maximum 1 per quarter). The card also offers a complimentary Priority Pass Membership worth $99 for the first two years. However, it does not include international lounge visits.

### How can I apply for Paytm SBI Card SELECT?
You can apply for this credit card online through the Paytm app or the official website of SBI Card. You will need to submit a duly filled application form along with the required documents. Alternatively, you can also apply offline by visiting your nearest SBI branch and submitting the application form in person.

### Does Paytm SBI Card SELECT offer rewards on wallet loads?
No, you cannot earn reward points on certain categories including wallet loads, fuel, rent, and government transactions.
