## Frequently Asked Questions
### Can I get an add-on card for Yatra SBI Credit Card?
Yes, you can get an add-on card for your parents, spouse, children or siblings above the age of 18, on your primary Yatra SBI Card.

### Is SBI Yatra credit card internationally acceptable?
Yatra SBI Card is accepted at over 24 million Visa outlets worldwide, including 3,25,000 outlets in India. However, the card charges a forex mark-up fee of 3.5% on international spends.

### Can I transfer my outstanding balance from any other bank’s credit card to Yatra SBI Card?
Yes, Yatra SBI Card provides the facility of ‘Balance Transfer’ via which you can transfer your outstanding balance from any other bank’s credit card. However, this is subject to the available credit limit on your card and other internal policies of the issuer.

### Do SBI Credit Card Reward Points expire?
Yes, Reward points expire after a period of 2 years. You need a minimum of 1,000 reward points to redeem your reward points for discount vouchers.

### Do I get fuel surcharge waiver on all fuel transactions?
No. Only fuel transactions ranging from Rs. 300 to Rs. 5,000 will be eligible for surcharge waiver. The maximum waiver amount in a month is Rs. 100.
