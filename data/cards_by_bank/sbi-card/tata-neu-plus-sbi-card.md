## About the Card
With an annual fee of Rs. 499 – compensated for in the first year by 499 bonus NeuCoins and waived from second year onwards on Rs. 1 Lakh annual spends – the **Plus variant can be an ideal choice for beginners seeking multi-brand benefits at low fee**. However, it is important to consider that earning as well as redemption options are limited to brands within the Tata Neu ecosystem. Hence, consumers who frequently shop with the associated brands can make the most of its benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### When do I earn the bonus points on the card?
Cardholders earn bonus 499 NeuCoins post the payment of the joining fee. These are credited to the Tata NeuPass account.

### Do I earn a fuel surcharge waiver on this card?
Yes, you can earn a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on the card on transactions ranging between Rs. 500 up to Rs. 4,000, capped at Rs. 250 per month.

### Is the Tata Neu Plus SBI Card similar to Tata Neu Plus HDFC?
Yes, the Tata Neu Plus variant is similar across both SBI and HDFC Bank. Both cards offer the same valueback benefits across UPI, partner Tata brands, and other spends. To choose the right card among these, you can make a decision based on your issuer preference and chances of approval based on your existing relationship with the bank.

### What is the forex markup fee on the card?
Unlike the Infinity variant, the Tata Neu Plus Infinity credit card does not offer a low forex markup fee. A forex markup fee of 3.5% is applicable on all international transactions.

### How do I apply for this credit card?
To apply for this credit card you can contact SBI Card at their customer care number 1860 180 1290. Alternatively, you can visit the issuer’s website.
