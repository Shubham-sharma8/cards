## Frequently Asked Questions
### How to get the SBI pulse credit card welcome gift voucher?
SBI Pulse credit card comes with a Noise ColorFit Pulse 4 Pro Smart Watch voucher worth Rs. 7,999 as a welcome gift. You can get this voucher within 21 working days of eligibility calculation, i.e. card joining fee payment, through email or SMS. You can click on the link shared and redeem the voucher by entering its code and other details where required.

### What is Pulse credit card maximum limit per month?
The credit limit assigned to the SBI Pulse cardholders can vary as per the individual eligibility, income, credit history and credit score, along with other internal policies. The cardholders can check their limit in the card welcome kit or by logging in to the card account.

### What are the benefits of SBI Pulse credit card?
SBI Card Pulse comes with complimentary memberships to FITPASS PRO and Netmeds, along with Noise ColorFit Pulse 4 Pro Smart Watch as joining benefit. It offers up to 20 reward points per Rs. 10 spent and a Rs. 1,500 voucher on reaching Rs. 4 lakh annual spends. It also provides Priority Pass membership and up to 8 free domestic airport lounge visits every year. Refer to the page above for detailed card benefits.

### Can we withdraw money from SBI Pulse credit card?
Yes, you can withdraw cash using SBI Pulse credit card, however credit card cash withdrawals come with high interest charges applicable from the date of withdrawal itself, along with a one-time withdrawal fee. These charges can pile up quickly and make it difficult to repay on time, thus increasing the risk of getting debt trapped. So, it is advisable to avoid credit card cash withdrawals.

### How to activate SBI pulse credit card?
You can activate your card online by logging into your SBI Card account through the official SBI Card website. Enter your username and password to log in and select the card activation option under the ‘Requests’ section. Then enter your card details and click on the ‘Activate’ button. You may also call SBI Card helpline number for assistance.

### Can I use SBI Card Pulse internationally?
Yes, SBI Card Pulse can be used in over 24 million outlets across the world.

### What happens if I already have an existing Netmeds First Membership?
In this case, you can redeem the voucher after your existing membership expires.

### How many FITPASS PRO sessions am I eligible for?
With SBI Pulse Card, you get a maximum of 12 sessions per month.

### How can I access my FITPASS PRO membership using the SBI Card Pulse?
You will have to make a prior reservation using the FITPASS mobile application.

### How to close SBI Pulse credit card?
You can close your SBI Pulse credit card account either by calling the SBi Card helpline on 1860 500 1290 , 1860 180 1290 or 1800 180 1290 (Toll Free) or by sending a written request online on customercare@sbicard.com. However, cardholders must make sure to pay all the card dues beforehand to avoid any negative impact on their credit profile.
