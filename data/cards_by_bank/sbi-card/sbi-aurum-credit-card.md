## About the Card
SBI Aurum is the most premium offering of SBI Card, designed for the ultra-elites. The card offers some exclusive privileges like **AURUM’s Secretarial Access** , complimentary **membership to Inflection Point Venture (IPV)** , and digital subscriptions to The Wall Street Journal and Mint. Such perks complement the high-end lifestyle and are comparably distinct as offered by other premium cards like HDFC Infinia and ICICI Emeralde Private. Beyond these, Aurum also offers some expected premium features like unlimited international airport lounge access, low forex markup fees, and air miles conversion, among others. However, despite these advantages, Aurum fails to match up to its competitors due to two basic yet important features. 1. Reward Rate: For a premium credit card, the card offers a very low reward rate of just 1%, in comparison to other cards like Infinia (3.33%-33%) and Emeralde Private (3%). 2. Lounge Access: While the lounge access benefits are unlimited for international lounges, domestic lounge access is limited to 16 in a year (4 per quarter).

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is Aurum credit card limit?
The credit limit offered on the SBI Aurum credit card is subjective, based on the cardholder’s profile on factors like credit history, existing loans, monthly income, etc.

### Which is better, HDFC Infinia or SBI Aurum?
Both HDFC Infinia and SBI Aurum have their own shares of pros and cons. While Infinia is a good choice for its reward rate, Aurum is suitable for users seeking premium memberships. However, most of the privileges on SBI Aurum are valid for the first year only, marking Infinia as a better choice for overall savings in the long run.

### How to avail free movie tickets with an SBI Aurum credit card?
You can avail free movie tickets on the SBI Aurum Credit Card by using it for booking tickets via BookMyShow.

### What is the minimum income requirement for this credit card?
SBI Aurum is offered to high net-worth individuals only. To be eligible for this card, you must have an annual gross income of Rs. 40 lakh or more.

### Will I get an Amazon Prime membership with the SBI Aurum Credit Card?
No, post feature update, the card does not offer Amazon Prime membership.

### What is the cash advance fee charged on the SBI Aurum Credit Card?
The cash advance fee for the SBI Aurum Credit Card is 2.5% of the transaction amount or Rs. 500, whichever is higher.
