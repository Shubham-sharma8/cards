## About the Card
Unlike other similar cards – Paytm HDFC Credit Card, this card offers a **complimentary Paytm First membership** as a welcome benefit. Cardholders can renew this membership annually by spending Rs. 1 Lakh, an easily achievable milestone even for moderate spenders.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Will the cashback be directly credited into my credit card account with Paytm SBI Card?
No, the card offers value-back in the form of reward points, which are redeemable at a value of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 1

### What is the credit limit offered by Paytm SBI Card?
The credit limit is determined by SBI Card based on various factors such as credit score, repayment history, monthly income, and more. Therefore, you will get to know your assigned credit limit once your application is approved.

### What is the annual fee for Paytm SBI Card?
The card charges a joining and annual fee of Rs. 500. However, it offers a complimentary Paytm First Membership as a welcome benefit. You can also renew this membership every year by spending Rs. 1 Lakh annually.

### Does Paytm SBI Card offer lounge access benefits?
No, Paytm SBI Credit Card does not offer complimentary lounge access. However, you can still save on your travel spends by earning 3% reward points on travel bookings made via Paytm.

### How can I apply for Paytm SBI Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online either by visiting the official SBI Card website and submitting a duly filled application form, or through the Paytm app. If you prefer to apply offline, you can visit any of the nearest SBI branches and submit the application form in person.

### Does Paytm SBI Card offer milestone benefits?
Yes, the card offers renewal of the complimentary Paytm First Membership on achieving annual spends of Rs. 1 Lakh.
