## About the Card
SBI Card Miles is a travel credit card designed to make your travels more rewarding. It offers Travel Credits as rewards on every card spend with a higher earning rate on travel spends. Though the reward rate of up to 1% struggles to compete with other similar cards, the travel-related redemption options make up for it by offering up to 1:1 points-to miles conversion ratios for 20+ top airline and hotel partners such as Air India, British Airways, Etihad Airways, IHG Hotels & Resorts, Radisson Hotels and many more. Other travel benefits include complimentary airport lounge access and spend-based bonus Travel credits and card fee reversal. Entirely focused on travel, this card is suitable for those who wish to get an agnostic travel credit card for domestic travels and is the base variant among other such SBI Card offerings – SBI Card Miles Prime and Miles Elite.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is there any expiration date for the reward points accrued through SBI Card Miles?
Yes, the earned reward points (Travel Credits) are only valid for 24 months from the date of their accrual.

### When will I get my earned Travel Credits using SBI Miles Credit Card?
Regular Travel Credits will reflect in your account within 10 days of the spend date and those earned as welcome bonus will reflect within 15 working days from the date of reaching the spending milestone.

### Is there any capping on reward earnings and redemptions on Miles SBI Card?
No, there is no capping on reward earnings or redemptions. However, the cardholders must use at least 2,000 Travel Credits for every redemption and must redeem their Travel Credits in the multiples of 2,000.

### Is there any free access to international lounges using SBI Miles Card?
Despite offering a complimentary Priority Pass to the cardholders, SBI Card Miles does not offer any free international lounge visit. Cardholders can access the lounges under this program without paying membership fee, but will have to pay the visit charges nonetheless.
