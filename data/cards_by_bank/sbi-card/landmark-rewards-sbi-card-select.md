## About the Card
Landmark Rewards SBI Card SELECT is one of the three co-branded credit cards offered by SBI Card in association with the Landmark Group, with the other variants being Landmark Rewards SBI Card and Landmark Rewards SBI Card PRIME. It comes with 3.75% savings as rewards on spends made at Landmark stores, like Lifestyle, Max, Spar, Home Centre and others. In addition to these co-branded spends, dining, movies and international purchases come with 2.5% value-back as rewards. However, this card comes with a reward redemption ratio of 4:1 which stands in contrast with the general trend of co-branded cards offering 1:1 ratio on redemption against partner brands. In addition to the core reward program, this credit card also offers joining and renewal benefits as reward points worth the card fee amount, thus helping minimising the card cost. Furthermore, 12,000 bonus reward points offered on reaching the annual spend-based milestone also add to the overall value-back of this card. Read on to know more about the features, benefits and associated charges of Landmark Rewards SBI Card SELECT.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is there any reward redemption fee on Landmark Rewards SBI Card SELECT?
Yes, cardholders are charged with a redemption fee of Rs. 99 + GST for redemptions below 1,000 reward points.

### What are the cards launched by SBI Card in partnership with Landmark Group?
SBI Card offers three credit cards co-branded with Landmark Group, namely Landmark Rewards, Landmark Rewards SELECT and Landmark Rewards PRIME.

### What brand stores are included in Landmark Group?
Landmark Rewards SBI credit cards offer accelerated value-back on online and offline retail spends made at six Landmark stores which include Max, Lifestyle, Spar, Home Centre, Baby Shop and EasyBuy.

### Is there any annual fee waiver benefit on Landmark Rewards SBI Card SELECT?
No, Landmark Rewards SBI Card SELECT does not come with any fee waiver condition, but it offers reward points worth the fee amount on joining and renewal fee payments.
