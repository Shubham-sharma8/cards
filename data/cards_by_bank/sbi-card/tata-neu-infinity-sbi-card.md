## About the Card
The card offers**5% value back on spends at Tata Neu** and its partner brands and 1.5% value back on other spends. A key highlight is that the card is available on the RuPay network, allowing cardholders to earn **1.5% value back on UPI transactions** as well. Besides rewards, the card also offers decent travel benefits, including a**low forex markup fee of 1.99%** along with complimentary international and domestic lounge access, enhancing the card’s overall value for frequent travellers. Overall, Tata Neu Infinity SBI Card is an ideal option for consumers who frequently shop within the Tata Neu ecosystem and are looking for a credit card with co-branded rewards and decent travel benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the difference between Tata Neu Infinity HDFC and Tata Neu Infinity SBI Card?
Tata Neu Infinity HDFC and Tata Neu Infinity SBI are both co-branded credit cards offered in collaboration with Tata Neu and HDFC Bank or SBI Card, respectively. Both cards offer similar benefits such as up to 10% value back on select Tata Neu spends, complimentary domestic & international lounge access, welcome benefits, and more.

### How much can I save with Tata Neu Infinity SBI Card?
With Tata Neu Infinity SBI Card, you can earn up to 10% value back on select Tata Neu spends. The card offers 5% value back on spends at Tata Neu and its partner brands, and 1.5% value back on other spends, including UPI transactions. Additionally, you can earn an extra 5% value back on select Tata Neu spends, enhancing the total value back to 10%.

### How can I apply for Tata Neu Infinity SBI Card?
To apply for Tata Neu Infinity SBI Credit Card online, you can visit the official SBI Card website and navigate to the card’s page. Click on \"Apply Now\" and submit the duly filled application form along with relevant documents. Alternatively, you can visit the nearest SBI branch and apply for the card in person.

### Does Tata Neu Infinity SBI Card offer an annual fee waiver?
Yes, Tata Neu Infinity SBI Card charges an annual fee of Rs. 1,499. However, you can avail annual fee waiver on spending Rs. 3 Lakh or more in a year.

### Can I earn rewards on UPI spends with Tata Neu Infinity SBI Card?
Yes, Tata Neu Infinity SBI Card is also available on the RuPay variant, which allows cardholders to earn a base reward rate of 1.5% on UPI spends, in addition to other regular spends.

### Can I earn reward points on fuel spends with Tata Neu Infinity SBI Card?
No, you cannot earn reward points on fuel spends. However, you can save up to Rs. 500 per month on fuel transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000 through a 1% fuel surcharge waiver.
