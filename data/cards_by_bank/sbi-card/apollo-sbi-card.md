## About the Card
Apollo SBI Card is designed for consumers who frequently spend on healthcare, pharmacy, wellness, and lifestyle categories. Along with accelerated rewards on Apollo services, the card also offers benefits on dining, movies, entertainment, and beauty & wellness spends. Cardholders can also enhance savings through 10% instant discount on select Apollo services. Overall, this credit card is a suitable option for consumers looking for a low annual fee card to save on Apollo services.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the welcome benefits offered on Apollo SBI Card?
The card offers 500 reward points worth Rs. 500 upon payment of the joining fee. The card also comes with complimentary Apollo Gold Tier Membership benefits.

### What rewards can I earn on Apollo SBI Card?
You can earn 3 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on Apollo services, 2 reward points per Rs. 100 on dining, movies, and entertainment, and 1 reward point on eligible spends across other categories.

### Does Apollo SBI Card offer annual fee waiver?
Yes, the annual fee can be waived on spending Rs. 1 Lakh or more in a card anniversary year.

### Is there any fuel benefit on Apollo SBI Card?
Yes, the card offers 1% fuel surcharge waiver across fuel stations in India, subject to a maximum waiver of Rs. 100 per month.

### Who should apply for Apollo SBI Card?
This card is suitable for consumers who frequently spend on healthcare, pharmacy, wellness, and Apollo-related services and want rewards on everyday lifestyle categories as well.

### Does Apollo SBI Card offer discounts on Apollo services?
Yes, cardholders can avail up to 10% instant discount on select Apollo services as per applicable offers.
