## About the Card
While both cards offer similar benefits, such as **4% cashback on partner merchants** and **unlimited 1% cashback** on other spends, Flipkart SBI Card differs when it comes to partner merchants. It offers 4% cashback on spends at Netmeds, Zomato and more but with a quarterly capping of Rs. 4,000. Whereas, Flipkart Axis Card offers unlimited cashback on partner merchants.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for Flipkart SBI Card?
You can apply for this credit card online through the official SBI Card website by filling out the application form and submitting the required documents. Alternatively, you can also apply offline by visiting your nearest SBI branch and submitting the application form in person.

### Does Flipkart SBI Card offer renewal benefits?
No, this credit card does not offer any renewal benefits. However, you can get the annual fee reversed if you spend Rs. 3.5 Lakh in a year.

### What is the difference between Flipkart SBI Card and Flipkart Axis Bank Credit Card?
Both are co-branded credit cards that offer similar benefits, including 7.5% cashback on Myntra, 5% cashback on Flipkart & Cleartrip, and unlimited 1% cashback on other spends. Besides this, Flipkart SBI Card offers 4% cashback on Netmeds, PVR, Uber, and Zomato, but this comes with a quarterly capping of Rs. 4,000. Where, Flipkart Axis Bank Credit Card offers unlimited 4% cashback on partner merchants, including Cult.fit, PVR, Swiggy, and Uber.

### Can I use Flipkart SBI Card for international spends?
Yes, you can use this credit card for overseas transactions. However, it comes with a high forex markup fee of 3.5%. Therefore, if you want to save on international spends, you should use a credit card with a low or zero forex markup fee.

### Can I earn unlimited cashback on partner merchants using Flipkart SBI Card?
No, this credit card offers 4% cashback on spends at partner merchants like Netmeds, PVR, Uber, and Zomato, but you can earn a maximum cashback of Rs. 4,000 in a quarter.

### Is Flipkart SBI Card lifetime free?
No, this credit card comes with a joining and annual fee of Rs. 500 plus applicable taxes. However, from the second year onward, the annual fee can be reversed if you spend Rs. 3.5 Lakh in a year.
