## Frequently Asked Questions
### Do I need a savings account with SBI to get an SBI Unnati Credit Card?
No, you don’t need a savings account with SBI to avail this card. SBI Unnati is issued against fixed deposit of Rs. 25,000 or more. Hence, even if you don’t have a savings account with SBI, you can open an FD with SBI and apply for this card.

### Is it possible to increase my credit limit on SBI Unnati Credit Card?
Yes, you can increase your credit limit by increasing your fixed deposit amount. Visit your nearest SBI branch, top up or create a new FD at a higher amount and request for credit enhancement against the revised FD.

### What all documents do I need to apply for SBI Unnati Credit Card?
You need ID proof such as PAN, Aadhaar, or Passport and address proof. Additionally, you will need a fixed deposit receipt or certificate of Rs. 25,000 or more with SBI. You do not require any income proof since this card is backed by a fixed deposit.

### Do I need a credit score to apply for SBI Unnati Credit Card?
No, you do not need credit score to apply for the SBI Unnati Credit Card as it is specifically designed for individuals with no credit history or a low credit score. The FD acts as a security, which eliminates the need for a credit check making it an ideal credit-building tool for those who are new to credit.

### If I close my fixed deposit account, will my SBI Unnati Credit Card also stop functioning?
Since this is a secured card that is offered against a fixed deposit. Hence, if you close your FD, you will no longer be able to use your SBI Unnati Credit Card.
