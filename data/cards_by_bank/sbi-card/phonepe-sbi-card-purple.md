## About the Card
Another notable benefit is that the card is also offered on the RuPay network, which allows cardholders to link their card with the UPI apps and earn benefits on UPI spends made via PhonePe app as well. The card also compensates for its joining fee by offering a **PhonePe gift card worth Rs. 500** at the time of joining. Additionally, the card also offers a **Yatra voucher worth Rs. 3,000** as a milestone benefit, allowing frequent travellers to maximize their savings. Overall, when compared to PhonePe HDFC Uno Card, SBI PURPLE offers better value at the same annual fee, due to its higher reward rate, rewards on offline & in-store spends, and direct welcome benefits. This card is a suitable option for consumers looking to maximize savings on their PhonePe spends.

## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance
- **Welcome Benefit:** Welcome benefits worth the joining fee without any spend-based condition

### Milestone Benefits
- 3 reward points for every Rs. 100 spent on eligible PhonePe spends, including recharges, utilities, bill payments, insurance and travel, capped at 250 points per month on insurance spends via PhonePe app and 750 points per month on other PhonePe spends
- 2 reward points for every Rs. 100 on eligible online spends, capped at 750 points per month
- Redeem reward points against cashback or vouchers at a value of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 1
- Yatra voucher worth Rs. 3,000 on spending Rs. 3 Lakh in a year

### Insurance Cover
- 3 reward points for every Rs. 100 spent on eligible PhonePe spends, including recharges, utilities, bill payments, insurance and travel, capped at 250 points per month on insurance spends via PhonePe app and 750 points per month on other PhonePe spends
- No reward benefits on utility & insurance payments made outside PhonePe app

- You are looking for a low annual fee card offering benefits on UPI spends

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Which one is better- PhonePe SBI Card PURPLE or PhonePe HDFC Uno Card?
PhonePe SBI Card PURPLE is a better option if you want to maximize your savings on PhonePe spends. At the same annual fee, the SBI card offers a higher reward rate of 3% on PhonePe spends, along with 1% reward rate on offline and in-store spends. Also, unlike HDFC Uno Card, where the welcome benefits are split across 10+ transactions, this card offers a direct PhonePe gift card of Rs. 500 after the payment of joining fee.

### How can I apply for PhonePe SBI Card PURPLE online?
You can apply for this credit card online by visiting the official SBI Card website and navigating to the card’s page. You are required to fill out the application form and upload the required documents. Besides this, you can also apply offline by visiting the nearest SBI branch.

### Can I earn reward points on utility spends outside PhonePe with PhonePe SBI Card PURPLE?
No, you cannot earn reward points on utility and insurance spends made outside of PhonePe.

### Does PhonePe SBI Card PURPLE offer any travel benefits?
This credit card does not offer exclusive travel benefits like airport lounge access or hotel memberships. However, you can earn 3% reward points on travel bookings made via PhonePe. Additionally, the card offers a Yatra voucher worth Rs. 3,000 as a milestone benefit on spending Rs. 3 Lakh in a year.

### Can I redeem accumulated reward points against cashback through PhonePe SBI Card PURPLE?
Yes, you can redeem the accumulated reward points against cashback and vouchers at a value of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 1.
