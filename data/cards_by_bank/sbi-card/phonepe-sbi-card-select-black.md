**Summary:** Strong 2026 utility + RuPay hybrid challenger to Airtel Axis, though monitor caps carefully.

**Best For:** Users paying monthly electricity/broadband/insurance bills via PhonePe wanting a combined utility + online 5% + RuPay UPI card

- Up to 10% back on spends made through PhonePe
- 1:1 reward points redemption ratio
- Complimentary domestic airport lounge access
- Rs. 5,000 Yatra voucher as milestone bonus

### Cons (Bad)
- Capping on rewards earnings
- Priority Pass membership only offered for the first 2 years
- No complimentary access to international airport lounges

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is PhonePe SBI SELECT BLACK Credit Card a RuPay credit card?
PhonePe SBI Card SELECT BLACK is offered on both Visa and RuPay networks.

### What is the UPI benefit offered by PhonePe SBI Card SELECT BLACK Credit Card?
Since PhonePe SBI SELECT BLACK Credit Card is also offered on RuPay network, it offers 1% value-back as rewards on UPI Scan & Pay transactions made via PhonePe app.

### Do I get reward points on utilities and insurance spends made outside PhonePe app?
No, PhonePe SBI SELECT BLACK Credit Card does not offer any value-back on utilities and insurance payments made outside PhonePe app.

### Does PhonePe SBI SELECT BLACK Credit Card offer international lounge benefits?
PhonePe SBI Card SELECT BLACK offers Priority Pass membership for the first 2 years of card membership, but no complimentary access to international airport lounges.

### Are the reward points on offline spends made using PhonePe SBI SELECT BLACK Credit Card capped?
Yes, offline spends made using PhonePe SBI Card SELECT BLACK offer 1% reward points which are capped at 2,000 points per month.
