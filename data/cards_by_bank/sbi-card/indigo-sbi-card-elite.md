## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is there any annual fee waiver option for IndiGo ELITE SBI Credit Card?
No, IndiGo SBI Card ELITE does not offer any option to waive off its annual fee.

### How to activate the IndiGo BluChip account?
Sign-up into your account on IndiGo website or app, validate mobile number and email through OTP and set your password to activate your IndiGo BluChip account.

### Do I get IndiGo BluChip membership with IndiGo SBI Card ELITE?
No, IndiGo SBI Card ELITE does not come with any complimentary IndiGo BluChip membership. But rather the card applicant must already have an active IndiGo BluChip account to be eligible for this credit card.

### Do IndiGo BluChips expire after their accrual through IndiGo ELITE SBI Card?
No, IndiGo BluChips do not expire after accrual using IndiGo SBI Card ELITE.

### How do I redeem IndiGo BluChips to book flights?
The IndiGo BluChips earned using IndiGo SBI Card ELITE can be used to book IndiGo flights by logging into your IndiGo BluChip account. You can go to the booking page and select the Pay with points option. Select passenger names, but note that only primary IndiGo BluChip member or nominees can redeem the points for their flights. Then choose the payment method between IndiGo BluChips and their voucher. Redeem the selected option and verify with an OTP sent to the registered mobile number.
