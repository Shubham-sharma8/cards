## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the main benefits of the SBI Tata Card?
SBI Tata Card offers reward benefits on Tata outlets, Croma purchases, departmental stores, and grocery spends. Cardholders can earn Empower Points on eligible transactions.

### What is the annual fee of the SBI Tata Card?
SBI Tata Card has an annual fee of Rs. 499 plus applicable taxes. The fee can be waived if the cardholder spends Rs. 1,00,000 in a year.

### How can consumers earn welcome benefits on the SBI Tata Card?
Cardholders can earn 500 Empower Points by spending Rs. 2,000 within 60 days of card activation. Additional gift vouchers may also be provided.

### What is the reward redemption value for SBI Tata Card points?
The accumulated reward points earned through the SBI Tata Card can be redeemed at a value of 1 Empower Point equal to Rs. 1.

### Does the SBI Tata Card provide fuel surcharge waiver benefits?
Yes, the card provides a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on eligible transactions between Rs. 500 and Rs. 3,000, capped at Rs. 100 per month.

### Does the SBI Tata Card offer airport lounge access?
No, the SBI Tata Card does not provide airport lounge access benefits. Consumers looking for travel-related features may need to consider other credit card options.
