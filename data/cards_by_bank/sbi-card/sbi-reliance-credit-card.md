## About the Card
SBI Card and Reliance Group offer co-branded cards in two variants: Reliance SBI Card and Reliance SBI Card PRIME. The Reliance Group covers varied segments like clothing, footwear, furniture, etc., and **cardholders can save a significant amount on their daily spends using the co-branded benefits.** SBI Card is already in partnership with Landmark Group offering Spar, Max and Home Center credit cards and this new collaboration will specifically cater to Reliance Group loyalists.**The reward rate of the base variant is just decent at 1.25%, but still a good option for loyalists seeking a low annual fee card.** Reliance Retail consists of brands like AJIO (online shopping), Reliance Trends (lifestyle spends), Jio Mart (groceries), Reliance Jewels (jewellery), Netmeds (online pharmacy), and several other lifestyle brands that come under the Reliance Retail umbrella, including Centro, Fashion Factory, Zivame, and Coverstory.

- Cashback Offer: 5% cashback, up to Rs. 250 per month on Jio Mart spends.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does the card offer any rewards on UPI payments?
Yes, with the RuPay variant of your Reliance SBI Card, you get 1 reward point per Rs. 100 spent at participating Reliance Retail stores.

### Do I earn rewards on rent payments?
No, rental transactions are excluded from the rewards program.

### How to close a Reliance SBI credit card?
To close your Reliance SBI Credit Card, you can contact SBI Credit Card Customer Care at 1860 180 1290/1860 500 1290.

### Does the SBI Reliance credit card have lounge access?
No, the Reliance SBI credit card does not offer any complimentary lounge access. However, the premium variant - Reliance SBI Card Prime offers 8 domestic and 4 international airport lounge access per calendar year.

### Is there any capping on earning fuel surcharge benefits?
Yes, the 1% fuel surcharge waiver is capped at Rs. 100 per statement cycle.

### Is Reliance SBI Prime credit card good?
Reliance SBI Prime credit card is a good choice for people who like shopping on multiple brands covered under Reliance Group’s umbrella, such as Jio Mart, Ajio, Reliance Trends, Zivame, and Reliance Jewels among others.

### What are the benefits of SBI Reliance welcome card?
On activation, the card offers Reliance retail voucher worth Rs. 500 and additional discount vouchers worth Rs. 3,200 of participating Reliance stores such as Jio Mart. AJIO, Reliance Trends, Azorte and Zivame among others.

### What is the movie ticket offer on Reliance SBI Prime credit card?
The card offers one movie ticket every month worth Rs. 250 via BookMyShow.

### How to redeem Reliance SBI credit card voucher?
Reliance SBI credit card voucher is redeemable across general merchandise & fashion categories.

### How to apply for Reliance SBI credit card?
You can apply for Reliance SBI credit card online on the SBI Card’s official website.
