## About the Card
Doctor’s SBI Card is designed for medical professionals who spend regularly on clinic needs, travel, and international purchases. It offers **5X reward points on medical supplies, travel bookings, and overseas spends** , plus the same**5X rewards on all transactions made on Doctor’s Day** (1 July). For other spends, consumers can earn 1 reward point per Rs. 100, with **each point valued at Rs. 0.25**. Travel perks include a complimentary Priority Pass membership, **4 free international lounge visits** (max 2 per quarter), and 4 domestic lounge visits yearly. You can also get a Rs. 5,000 e-gift voucher on annual spends of Rs. 5 Lakh.
