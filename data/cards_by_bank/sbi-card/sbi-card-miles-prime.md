## About the Card
SBI Card Miles Prime is one of the 3 agnostic travel credit cards offered by SBI Card, namely Miles, Miles Prime and Miles Elite. It comes with accelerated travel savings in the form of Travel Credits on every travel spend, irrespective of the merchant. These Travel Credits can later be redeemed as air miles and hotel loyalty points for future travel bookings made across 20+ travel partners in a conversion ratio of up to 1:1. Besides these rewards, the card also offers free airport lounge access across the globe, along with milestone benefits which include fee reversal, bonus Travel Credits, and additional domestic lounge visits. Though it also comes with Priority Pass membership, it is only available for the first 2 years. Another point to take note is that this card does not offer any significant benefit targeting non-travel spending categories.
