## About the Card
BPCL SBI Card Octane is a **co-branded fuel credit card** and an upgraded version of the BPCL SBI Card. It offers **up to 7.25% value-back** on fuel spends at BPCL and remained the best fuel credit cards in India—until the launch of the **Indian Oil RBL XTRA Credit Card** , which provides an **8.5% value-back** on Indian Oil fuel spends. Designed for commuters who spend a significant amount on fuel, the **BPCL** **Octane Card** offers **25X reward points** (25 points per ₹100) on BPCL fuel purchases, along with a **fuel surcharge waiver of up to ₹100 per month**. Beyond fuel, it provides **10X reward points** on groceries, departmental stores, movie tickets, and dining—covering key everyday spending categories. Reward points can be redeemed for fuel at BPCL outlets at a rate of **1 RP = Rs. 0.25**. While BPCL SBI Card Octane is an excellent choice for BPCL loyalists, its **base reward rate of just 0.25%** on other spends may not appeal to users seeking broader benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does SBI BPCL Octane credit card have lounge access?
BPCL SBI Card Octane offers 4 complimentary visits to domestic airport lounges every year, capped at 1 visit in every calendar quarter.

### What is the milestone benefit in BPCL SBI Octane card?
BPCL SBI Octane offers an annual card fee reversal benefit on reaching Rs. 2 lakh annual card spends and an e-gift voucher worth Rs. 2,000 from Aditya Birla Fashion/ Yatra/ Hush Puppies/ Bata on reaching the annual spends threshold of Rs. 3 lakh.

### What is the limit of a BPCL Octane card?
The assigned credit limit of credit cards can vary for each cardholder as it is dependent on the card user\\\\\'s income, credit score, credit history and other factors.

### How to redeem SBI BPCL Octane credit card reward points?
You can redeem your accrued reward points against future fuel purchases at eligible BPCL fuel outlets, where the value of each reward point will be equal to Rs. 0.25.

### How can I apply for BPCL SBI Card Octane?
You can apply for this credit card online by visiting the official website of SBI Card and navigating to the credit card page. You are required to submit a duly filled application form along with the relevant documents. Besides this, you can also apply offline by visiting the nearest SBI branch and submitting the application form in person.

### Is there any capping on the fuel surcharge waiver on BPCL SBI Card Octane?
Yes, you can avail 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel spends of up to Rs. 4,000 per transaction. However, you can earn a maximum fuel surcharge waiver of Rs. 100 in a month.
