## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I redeem reward points on my SBI SimplySAVE Credit Card?
You can redeem SBI Credit Card reward points through online and offline modes. To redeem your reward points online, you can download the SBI Card mobile app or visit the internet banking portal of the bank. Apart from this, you can also call on the customer care number and the executive will help you with the redemption process.

### Can I avail fuel surcharge waiver on every transaction via SBI Simply SAVE Credit Card?
No, you can avail fuel surcharge waiver on your transactions between Rs. 500 to Rs 3,000 across any petrol pump in India. Also, you can avail a maximum waiver of Rs. 100 per statement cycle.

### What is SBI Simply Save credit card reward points value in rupees?
Every reward point earned using SBI Simply Save credit card holds the value of Rs. 0.25.

### Is SBI Simply Save credit card good?
SBI SimplySAVE credit card is a good option for those looking for a low annual fee rewards credit card. It is suitable for those who want accelerated savings on dining, movies, grocery and departmental store spends.

### What is the limit of SBI Simply Save credit card?
The credit limit of SBI SimplySAVE credit card depends on the individual’s credit history, income, and credit score, among other factors as per the card provider’s internal policies. So, there is no common fixed credit limit associated with any credit card.

### Does SBI Simply SAVE Credit Card offer airport lounge access?
No, SBI SimplySAVE Credit Card does not offer any airport lounge access facility to the cardholders.

### What is SBI SimplySAVE Credit Card interest rate?
The current finance charges are up to 3.50% p.m i.e 42% annually. The charges are applicable from the transaction date and are subject to change as per the discretion of SBI Card.

### How can I check my SBI Simply SAVE Credit Card limit?
Both total credit limit and available credit limit can be seen via the SBI Card app and on your monthly credit card statement.

### How to upgrade SBI Simply Save credit card?
You can upgrade your existing SBI credit card by first logging in to your SBI Card account and then select the ‘Upgrade Now’ option to upgrade to the card offered to you.

### How can I check the status of my application after applying?
You can check your application status for SBI Simply Save Credit Card on SBI Card’s official website by entering your application reference number.
