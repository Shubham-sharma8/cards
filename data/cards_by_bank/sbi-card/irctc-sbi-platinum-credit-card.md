## Frequently Asked Questions
### How to redeem SBI IRCTC credit card reward points in cash?
You cannot redeem the accrued reward points as cash or statement balance. However, you can use the reward points earned via SBI IRCTC Platinum card for IRCTC bookings in 1:1 redemption ratio. To redeem, you have to first link the IRCTC loyalty number as per your card to your IRCTC login ID and then proceed with the bookings.

### Will I get reward points for tickets booked through the mobile application?
Yes, SBI IRCTC Platinum credit cardholders can earn up to 10% value back as reward points on IRCTC ticket bookings made via the IRCTC mobile app.

### What are the annual charges of the SBI IRCTC credit card?
You have to pay a joining fee of Rs. 500 + GST in the first card membership year and an annual fee of Rs. 300 + GST from 2nd year onwards.

### Does the SBI IRCTC credit card have airport lounge access?
No, SBI IRCTC Platinum credit card does not offer any complimentary airport lounge access.

### Should I opt for SBI IRCTC Platinum Card or SBI IRCTC Premier Card?
IRCTC Platinum Card is the base variant whereas IRCTC SBI Premier Card is the premium variant, available at a higher annual fee of Rs. 1,499. While the savings on train tickets is the same on both variants, the Premier Variant offers accelerated rewards on dining, bonus reward points and some other perks which are not available on the base variant. You should assess your spending patterns and choose a card that better aligns with your needs.
