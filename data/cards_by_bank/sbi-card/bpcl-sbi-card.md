## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply online for SBI BPCL Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online via issuer channels by sharing your details in the application form or through the official website of SBI Card. However, if you want to apply offline, then you can visit any of the nearest branches of SBI Bank and contact an executive.

### What is the maximum fuel surcharge waiver offered by SBI BPCL Credit Card?
You can avail a fuel surcharge waiver of 1% of your transactions of up to Rs. 4,000 at any BPCL petrol pumps across India. However, you can avail a maximum waiver of Rs. 100 in a billing cycle, which is equivalent to an annual savings of Rs. 1,200.

### How can I check the application status of my SBI BPCL Credit Card?
Usually, the bank informs you about the updated application status via SMS on your registered mobile number. However, you can also track your application status through the official website of SBI Card by entering your application number. Besides this, you can also call on the SBI Credit Card customer care number and ask the representative about the application status.

### Can I transfer my outstanding balance from any other bank’s credit card to SBI BPCL Credit Card?
Yes, BPCL SBI Card offers a ‘Balance Transfer’ facility through which you can transfer your outstanding balance from any other bank’s credit card to your SBI Card. For this, you can contact the bank and ask the representative about your query.

### What are SBI BPCL credit card benefits for lounge access?
BPCL SBI card does not offer any complimentary airport lounge access to the cardholders.

### What are the annual charges for BPCL card?
SBI BPCL credit card charges an annual fee of Rs. 499 + applicable taxes every year.

### Does BPCL SBI Credit Card have any add-on facility for family members?
Yes, the primary BPCL SBI cardholders can get add-on cards for their family members, including their parents, spouse, siblings or children who are above the age of 18 years. SBI Card does not charge any annual fee for this add-on card facility.

### What is the maximum limit per month for SBI BPCL credit card?
The card provider assigns the credit limit to SBI BPCL credit card users based on the internal policies and this limit is communicated at the time of card delivery itself. Based on your usage, the available credit limit is also mentioned in every monthly card statement.

### How do the standard and Octane variants of the SBI BPCL Credit Card differ?
The standard and Octane variants of SBI BPCL Credit Cards differ in terms of their annual fee and the extent of benefits. The base variants charges Rs. 499 and the Octane variant charges Rs. 1,499 as the annual fee. With the base variant, you get 4.25% value-back on BPCL spends whereas on the Octane variant you will get 7.25% back.

### Are there any insurance benefits included with the BPCL SBI Credit Card?
No. BPCL SBI Card does not offer any insurance benefit. However, on the BPCL Octane Card, you get fraud liability cover of Rs. 1 Lakh.
