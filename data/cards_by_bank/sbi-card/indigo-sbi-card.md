## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- 4 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits in a year on spending Rs. 50,000 in the previous quarter, capped at 1 visit per quarter

- 1% fuel surcharge across all petrol pumps in India, on fuel spends between Rs. 500 and Rs. 4,000, capped at Rs. 200 per month

- You are comfortable redeeming the accumulated rewards for IndiGo flights & other activities
- You want to earn accelerated rewards on hotel, lodging, and select travel spends other than IndiGo
- You can spend Rs. 6 Lakh in a year to maximize milestone benefits

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I earn rewards with IndiGo SBI Card?
You earn 3 BluChips per Rs. 100 spent on IndiGo, 2 BluChips per Rs. 100 on hotels, lodging, and select travel MCCs, and 1 BluChip per Rs. 100 on other spends. The accumulated BluChips can be redeemed for IndiGo flights or other activities through the IndiGo app or website.

### Who should get IndiGo SBI Card?
This card is best for frequent travellers who prefer IndiGo flights and can spend around Rs. 6 Lakh per year to maximize milestone benefits. It is also suitable for those who want rewards on hotel, lodging, and select travel spends beyond IndiGo flights.

### Does IndiGo SBI Card offer lounge access?
Yes, the card offers up to 4 complimentary domestic lounge visits per year. However, you can avail complimentary lounge access benefits on spending Rs. 50,000 in the previous quarter.

### What are the welcome and milestone benefits offered by IndiGo SBI Card?
The card offers 2,500 IndiGo BluChips as a welcome benefit after joining and completing the first transaction with 60 days of account opening. Additionally, you can earn up to 7,500 BluChips as milestone benefits on spends of Rs. 2 Lakh, Rs. 4 Lakh, and Rs. 6 Lakh in a year.

### How does IndiGo SBI Card compare with IndiGo Kotak Card?
While both cards offer similar welcome, renewal, and milestone benefits, IndiGo Kotak offers higher rewards on IndiGo spends (up to 19 BluChips per Rs. 100). However, IndiGo SBI Card offers domestic lounge access, making it more suitable for travellers seeking additional travel perks.

### What is the annual fee of IndiGo SBI Card?
The card charges an annual fee of Rs. 1,499 plus applicable taxes. On renewal, you can earn 2,500 bonus BluChips, which helps offset the annual fee and offers additional travel savings.
