## About the Card
Cardholders can redeem accumulated reward points against cashback or through the rewards catalogue at a value of **1 Reward Point = Rs. 0.25**. Apart from the accelerated categories, the card offers a **low value-back of 0.25% on other spends**. Overall, Shaurya SBI Card is a low annual fee credit card with basic benefits. Apart from reward points, it offers benefits such as fuel surcharge waiver, **1,000 reward points as a welcome benefit** , and an annual fee waiver. This card is a suitable option for defence personnel seeking a low annual fee card to save on canteen and everyday spends.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is Shaurya SBI Card?
Shaurya SBI credit card is designed especially for the defence personnel. Cardholders can get the card embossed with the Navy, Army, Air force or Paramilitary’s insignia. The card offers accelerated reward points on everyday spends and even on CSD spends.

### Does SBI Shaurya Card have lounge access?
No, SBI Shaurya Credit Card does not offer any domestic or international airport lounge access.

### What reward points does the Shaurya SBI Card offer?
You earn 5 reward points per Rs. 100 on CSD, dining, departmental store/grocery, and movie spends, and 1 reward point per Rs. 100 on all other eligible purchases.

### Does Shaurya SBI Credit Card offer a fuel surcharge waiver?
Yes, you get a 1% fuel surcharge waiver for fuel transactions between Rs. 500 and Rs. 3,000, with a maximum waiver of Rs. 100 per statement cycle.

### What insurance benefits come with Shaurya SBI Credit Card?
The card offers a complimentary personal accident insurance cover of Rs. 2 Lakh.

### What are the interest rates on outstanding balances for Shaurya SBI Credit Card?
The interest (finance) charge is up to 2.75% per month, which translates to about 33% p.a.

### Are there fees for cash advances on Shaurya SBI Credit Card?
Yes, the cash advance fee is 2.5% of the amount withdrawn, or a minimum of Rs. 500, whichever is higher.

### Does Shaurya SBI Credit Card charge foreign currency transaction fees?
Yes, this credit card charges a foreign currency markup fee of 3.5% on transactions made in non-INR currency.

### Can purchases made on Shaurya SBI Credit Card be converted into EMIs?
Yes, you can convert purchases into EMIs (“Flexipay”), though cash advance, balance transfers, and fuel transactions are excluded from earning reward points, per T&Cs.
