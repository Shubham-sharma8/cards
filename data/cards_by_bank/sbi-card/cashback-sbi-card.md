## About the Card
Must-have core online cashback card for 80% of Indian cardholders despite the April 2026 combined Rs. 5k/month cap.

## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Must-have core online cashback card for 80% of Indian cardholders despite the April 2026 combined Rs. 5k/month cap.

**Best For:** Online shoppers spending Rs. 15,000–Rs. 1,00,000/month across D2C sites, Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, food/travel portals

**Not Ideal For:** Travelers seeking lounge access, utility/insurance billers, or shoppers relying on SBI instant sale discounts

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 20,000 – Rs. 1,00,000 online

### Pros (Good)
- Still India's #1 universal 5% online cashback card across almost any e-commerce site
- Zero redemption hassle or fees — auto-adjusted in statement within 2 days
- Easy Rs. 2L/year fee waiver makes effective annual cost zero for online shoppers

### Cons (Bad)
- April 2026 cap merges 5% online + 1% offline into a single Rs. 5,000/month ceiling
- Zero airport lounge access and excluded from SBI instant-discount e-commerce sales
- Aggressive exclusions (utilities, insurance, education, rent, fuel, gift vouchers)
