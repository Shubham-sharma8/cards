## About the Card
Landmark Rewards SBI Card is a shopping credit card intended for consumers who are loyal customers of Landmark Group brands like Lifestyle, Max, Home Centre, Spar, Baby Shop, and EasyBuy. With this card, consumers can earn **10 reward points per Rs. 100 spent at Landmark stores**. Additionally, consumers can also earn 5 reward points for each Rs. 100 spent on dining, movies, and international transactions, plus a base reward rate (1 point per Rs. 100) on all other categories. At a redemption rate of **1 RP = Rs. 0.25** , the reward points can be redeemed at Landmark stores. Besides rewards, the card also offers a**welcome benefit of 2,000 reward points**(worth Rs. 500) on the payment of joining fee, plus a milestone benefit of 8,000 bonus reward points on annual spends of Rs. 2 lakh. Landmark Rewards SBI Card also offers a fuel surcharge waiver and renewal bonus points, enhancing the overall value at an annual fee of Rs. 499. In total, this card is a good option for consumers who want to start their credit journey with a low-cost card that offers high value mainly on Landmark store purchases. It may not provide travel benefits or premium privileges, but it is an everyday shopping card that is rewarding for consumers who are regular shoppers at Landmark brands.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What rewards do I earn with the Landmark Rewards SBI Card?
Landmark Rewards SBI Card offers up to 10 reward points per Rs. 100 spent at Landmark stores, 5 reward points on dining, movies, and international spends. Besides this, it offers 1 reward point per Rs. 100 spent on other retail purchases, making it a decent choice for everyday spends.

### Does Landmark Rewards SBI Card offer lounge access?
No, Landmark Rewards SBI Card does not offer any travel benefits, such as domestic or international lounge access. This card is a shopping-focused card that provides accelerated reward points on shopping and everyday categories.

### What welcome benefits come with this card?
Landmark Rewards SBI Card offers 2,000 bonus reward points worth Rs. 500 on the payment of joining fee.

### Are there any milestone rewards on this card?
Yes, Landmark Rewards SBI Card offers 8,000 bonus reward points on spending a minimum of Rs. 2 Lakh in a year, which makes the card more rewarding for consumers with regular retail expenses.

### Who should get the Landmark Rewards SBI Card?
Landmark Rewards SBI Card is a great choice for consumers who frequently shop at Landmark Group brands such as Lifestyle, Max, Home Centre, Spar, Baby Shop, or EasyBuy.
