## About the Card
Since the highest benefits are limited to a few partner merchants, SBI Simply CLICK Credit Card is ideal for those who frequently shop at these platforms, enabling them to maximize the card’s benefits. However, those who seek substantial returns across a broader range of online transactions, without specific merchant restrictions, could consider cards that offer broader benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is SimplyCLICK a Visa or RuPay card?
SBI SimplyCLICK credit card is offered only on the Visa network.

### What is the limit of the SBI Simply Click credit card?
The credit limit on SBI SimplyCLICK credit card varies as per the issuer’s discretion. It is based on the consumer’s profile on factors like credit score, income, and other internal parameters.

### What is the annual fee for a Simply Click SBI card?
SBI SimplyCLICK credit card charges a low annual fee of Rs. 499, which is waived off on spending Rs. 1 lakh in the previous year.

### What are the benefits of SBI Simply Card?
SBI SimplyClick Card offers accelerated 10x rewards on popular everyday brands—BookMyShow, Apollo24X7, Dominos, IGP, Myntra, Yatra, Netmeds, and Cleartrip. Additionally, cardholders can earn 5x rewards on all online spends. It also offers savings via Cleartrip e-vouchers worth Rs. 2,000 each on reaching annual spending milestones of Rs. 1 lakh and Rs. 2 lakh, respectively.

### Is there a balance transfer facility available on SBI SimplyClick Credit Card?
Yes, SBI SimplyCLICK Credit Card offers the balance transfer on EMI facility, to clear the outstanding dues on other credit cards. The interest rate on credit card balance transfer starts from 0.83% per month.

### What is the SBI SimplyCLICK Credit Card best for?
SBI SimplyCLICK Credit Card is a suitable choice for beginners who frequently spend online at the partner brands- BookMyShow, Apollo24X7, Dominos, IGP, Myntra, Yatra, Netmeds and Cleartrip.

### What is the difference between an SBI SimplySAVE credit card and a SimplyCLICK credit card?
Both SBI SimplySAVE and SimplyCLICK are entry-level cards available at the same annual fee of Rs. 499. However, they differ in terms of features and benefits. While SimplyCLICK offers higher value-back on online transactions, along with 10X rewards with select online brands, SimplySAVE offers accelerated value-back on select transaction categories like dining, movies and departmental store spends.

### What are the eligibility criteria and documents required to apply for SBI SimplyCLICK Credit Card?
The applicant must either be salaried or self-employed with a regular income source and between the age of 21 and 70 years to be eligible for SBI credit cards. In addition, a good credit score is also preferred. Moreover, documents like proof of identity, address and income are required during credit card application for KYC. These can include PAN card, copy of Aadhaar card or other government address proof, salary slip or ITR, etc.

### What is SBI SimplyCLICK Credit Card limit?
Credit card limit is different for different cardholders as it depends on a lot of factors such as income, credit history, current liabilities, limit on existing cards and several other internal policies of the bank. You will get to know your limit only after you receive your card.

### Is Simply CLICK Credit Card good compared to SBI Cashback card or other SBI variants?
SBI SimplyCLICK Credit Card is ideal for online shoppers, offering 10X reward points on select online merchants like Myntra, Yatra, and Cleartrip. In contrast, SBI Cashback Credit Card provides 5% cashback on all online purchases and 1% on offline spends, making it more suitable for users seeking straightforward cashback rewards.
