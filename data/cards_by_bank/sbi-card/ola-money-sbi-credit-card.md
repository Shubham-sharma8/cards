## About the Card
Ola Money SBI Card is co-branded card with the collaboration of SBI Card and Ola. It offers accelerated savings as **7% reward points on Ola ride spends** and a base value-back rate of **1% on other card spends**. While the accelerated rewards come with a monthly capping, you can earn unlimited reward points on other card spends. Further adding to the co-branded benefits, the card allows the card users to utilise the earned reward points in **1:1 ratio** against future Ola ride bookings by paying through Ola Money wallet.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who should get Ola Money SBI card?
Ola Money SBI Card is a beginner-level co-branded credit card that comes with a renewal fee of Rs. 499 and it is ideal for cardholders who are looking for discounts or value-back on Ola rides.

### How can I avail my reward points on Ola Money credit card?
The rewards earned on your credit card are credited to your OlaMoney Wallet. So, it is recommended to complete KYC to avail full benefits of the OlaMoney Wallet.

### How can I redeem the rewards points earned by using Ola Money SBI Credit Card?
The reward points earned through Ola Money SBI Card are credited to your Ola Money wallet in 1:1 redemption ratio from where you can use them for future Ola ride bookings.

### How much can I save on Ola bookings using Ola Money SBI Credit Card?
You can save 7% on every Ola ride you pay for using Ola Money Credit Card. This value-back is capped at Rs. 500 per month.

### How can I apply for Ola Money SBI Credit Card?
You can apply for Ola Money SBI Credit Card online through the official SBI Card website or Ola app. Simply fill in your details, and submit the required documents. If you meet the eligibility criteria, the card will be issued after approval.

### What are the joining and annual fees for Ola Money SBI Credit Card?
Ola Money SBI Credit Card comes with no joining fee. However, second year onwards, the card charges an annual/ renewal fee of Rs. 499 plus applicable taxes.

### How can I contact customer support for issues related to my Ola Money SBI Credit Card?
You can reach SBI Card customer support by calling 1860 180 1290 or 39 02 02 02 (prefix your local STD code). Alternatively, you can raise a query via the SBI Card website or mobile app under the “Contact Us” section for assistance.

### How can I cancel my Ola Money SBI Credit Card?
To cancel your Ola Money SBI Credit Card, you can call SBI Card customer care and request card closure. Ensure that all outstanding dues are cleared before making the cancellation request. Once processed, you’ll receive a confirmation message or email regarding the closure.
