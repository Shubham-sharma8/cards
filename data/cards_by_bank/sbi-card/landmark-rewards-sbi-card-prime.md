## About the Card
Landmark Rewards SBI Card PRIME is the premium variant of other co-branded cards offered by Landmark and SBI Card. The card offers **6.25% value-back on spends at Landmark stores** , including Lifestyle, Home Centre, Max, Spar, Babyshop, and EasyBuy. Besides this, it also offers **2.5% value-back on dining and movies** , making it a suitable option for everyday spends. However, cardholders can redeem accumulated reward points only at Landmark stores, at a value of **1 RP = Rs. 0.25**. Apart from shopping, this credit card also caters to frequent travellers with **5X rewards on international spends** along with complimentary **domestic and international lounge visits**. However, it charges a high forex markup fee of 3.5%, which almost nullifies the value gained from 5X rewards on international spends. In addition to this, it offers welcome and renewal **benefits worth Rs. 3,000 every year** , along with a milestone benefit of **20,000 bonus reward points**. Overall, this credit card is a suitable option for consumers seeking decent value-back at Landmark stores along with reasonable travel and everyday benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the difference between Landmark Rewards SBI Card, Landmark Rewards SBI Card Select, and Landmark Rewards SBI Card PRIME?
Landmark Rewards SBI Card is the entry-level variant offering basic value-back at Landmark stores. The Select variant offers higher rewards on shopping and a few additional benefits. The PRIME variant is the premium option, offering the highest value-back, milestone benefits, lounge access, and accelerated rewards on everyday spends.

### Can I redeem reward points earned through Landmark Rewards SBI Card PRIME against cashback?
No, reward points earned through Landmark Rewards SBI Card PRIME cannot be redeemed against cashback. They can only be redeemed at Landmark partner stores at a value of 1 RP = Rs. 0.25.

### Does Landmark Rewards SBI Card PRIME offer complimentary lounge visits?
Yes, Landmark Rewards SBI Card PRIME offers complimentary domestic and international lounge visits. The card offers 8 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits every year and complimentary Priority Pass Membership with 4 complimentary international lounge visits per year.

### Is Landmark Rewards SBI Card PRIME good for travel?
The card offers lounge access and 5X reward points on international spends, which makes it a decent option for frequent travellers. However, its high forex markup fee of 3.5% reduces the overall benefit, making it a moderate option for frequent international travellers.

### Can I earn reward points on fuel spends through Landmark Rewards SBI Card PRIME?
No, fuel spends do not earn reward points on Landmark Rewards SBI Card PRIME. However, you can save up to Rs. 1,200 every year on fuel through 1% fuel surcharge waiver.

### What are the reward benefits offered by Landmark Rewards SBI Card PRIME?
The card offers 6.25% rewards on Landmark stores and 2.5% rewards on dining, movies, and international spends. It also offers milestone benefits of 20,000 bonus points and additional rewards through welcome and renewal benefits.
