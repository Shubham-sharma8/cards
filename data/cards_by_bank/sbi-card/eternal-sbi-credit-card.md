## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance
- **Welcome Benefit:** Value of the welcome benefit offered may not compensate for the joining fee charged

### Milestone Benefits
- Renewal fee can be waived off on spending more than Rs. 2 lakh in the previous year
- 1% fuel surcharge waiver, up to Rs. 200 per month, on fuel spends of Rs. 500 to Rs. 4,000

### Dining Benefits
- 10% off on all Zomato food orders
- Complimentary 6 month Zomato Gold membership on card activation
- Up to 10% off on popular brands like Zomato, Blinkit, District & Bistro

### Movie / Entertainment
- Buy 1 get 1 free movie ticket of up to Rs. 200 per month via District app
- Buy 1 get 1 offer on movie tickets via District

### Get This Card If
- You prefer shopping online for your everyday and lifestyle expenses, and frequently use platforms within the Eternal ecosystem, i.e. Zomato, District, Blinkit and Bistro
- You want a card with hassle-free value-back system that offers instant discounts and direct cashback, instead of reward points, on spends
- You often go out to watch movies in theatre and want a credit card with good saving options on movie spends
- You are looking for a credit card with low annual fee and easily achievable fee waiver condition
