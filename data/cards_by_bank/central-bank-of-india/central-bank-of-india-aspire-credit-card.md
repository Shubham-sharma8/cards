**Summary:** option from Central Bank Of India suited for travel & miles users.

**Best For:** Students, New-to-Credit applicants, or low-CIBIL users building credit history via a Fixed Deposit

**Not Ideal For:** Established salaried professionals eligible for unsecured cashback/travel cards

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 5,000 – Rs. 20,000 (<30% FD utilization)

### Pros (Good)
- Secured against Fixed Deposit — easy approval without income proof or high CIBIL
- Backed by Central Bank Of India digital card controls, EMI conversion & merchant offers

### Cons (Bad)
- No complimentary domestic or international airport lounge access
- High 3.5% (+GST) foreign currency markup — poor for overseas spends
- Requires locking funds in a Fixed Deposit as collateral
- Check latest issuer exclusions on rent, utilities, education & wallet loads before high spends

**Monthly Capping & Exclusions:**
- The card is issued based on the Cent Aspire Fixed Deposit account. Credit limit is allowed up to 100% of the Cent Aspire Fixed Deposit account. Credit limit can vary from ₹ 20,000 to ₹ 10 Lakhs.
- Cash withdrawal charges – ₹ 100 per ATM withdrawal transaction + 1.5% per month from the date the money is withdrawn till the same is repaid.
- Service charge levied on the amount that is unpaid from the due date is charged at 1.5% per month.
