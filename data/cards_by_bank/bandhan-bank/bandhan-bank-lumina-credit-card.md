## About the Card
Bandhan Bank Lumina Credit Card is a premium card in Bandhan Bank’s portfolio. Priced at Rs. 9,999, the card offers 12% reward points on international spends and 8% reward points across other eligible categories. Like most cards in this fee range, the Lumina Card comes with premium travel benefits such as unlimited international lounge visits, low forex mark-up fee and airport meet and greet service. To offset the first-year membership fee, cardholders get Rs. 10,000 Oberoi e-voucher on joining. While Bandhan Bank has not provided much clarity on the monetary value of its reward points, the high reward-earning rates could translate into significant savings for eligible spends. Here is all you need to know about Bandhan Bank Lumina Credit Card.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the annual fee of the Bandhan Bank Lumina Credit Card?
The Bandhan Bank Lumina Credit Card charges a joining fee and annual fee of Rs. 9,999 + applicable taxes. The card offers a Rs. 10,000 Oberoi e-voucher as a joining benefit, subject to spending Rs. 20,000 within 30 days of card issuance and payment of the joining fee.

### What are the rewards offered by the Bandhan Bank Lumina Credit Card?
Cardholders earn 16 reward points for every Rs. 200 spent on eligible transactions. International spends earn 1.5X rewards, equivalent to 24 reward points per Rs. 200. Cardholders can also earn 10,000 bonus reward points every month on spending Rs. 1 lakh.

### What is the minimum income required for Bandhan Bank Lumina Credit Card?
Applicants must have an annual income of at least Rs. 18 lakh. However, the final approval is subject to the bank\'s internal eligibility and approval criteria.

### Does the Bandhan Bank Lumina Credit Card offer airport lounge access?
Yes. The card offers unlimited international airport lounge access at select airports. It also provides 8 complimentary domestic lounge visits per quarter at select airports. The lounge access benefit is not linked to any minimum spending condition.

### What are the international travel benefits offered by the Bandhan Bank Lumina Credit Card?
The card offers unlimited international lounge access, complimentary airport meet-and-greet services and a low forex mark-up fee of 1%, along with accelerated rewards on international transactions.

### Who should get the Bandhan Bank Lumina Credit Card?
The card is best suited for frequent travellers, particularly those who travel internationally and can benefit from accelerated rewards, low forex mark-up and lounge access. It can also be suitable for consumers who are comfortable paying a premium annual fee for travel and lifestyle benefits.
