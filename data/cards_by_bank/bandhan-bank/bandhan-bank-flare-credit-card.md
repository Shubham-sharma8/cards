## About the Card
Bandhan Bank Flare Credit Card is a mid-range offering among its gamut of credit cards. Unlike most cards that cater to one or two categories, Flare offers a uniform reward rate across all spends. Consumers also get bonus reward points every month as spend-based milestone benefit. In addition to the core rewards, the card also offers up to 4 free domestic airport lounge visits in a quarter – a great perk for frequent travellers. Here is all you need to know about Bandhan Bank Flare Credit Card. Also, read our detailed review of the card along with its pros and cons below.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Rs. 2,000 Amazon Pay voucher on joining What are the rewards offered by the Bandhan Bank Flare Credit Card?
The Bandhan Bank Flare Credit Card offers 10 reward points for every Rs. 200 spent. Cardholders can also earn 5,000 bonus reward points every month on achieving spends of Rs. 75,000. The reward points can be redeemed through the Bandhan Bank Delights Platform and do not expire.

### How many complimentary airport lounge visits does the Bandhan Bank Flare Credit Card offer?
The card offers 4 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per quarter at select airports. It also provides 1 complimentary international lounge visit per quarter.

### What is the joining benefit on the Bandhan Bank Flare Credit Card?
Cardholders can get an Amazon Pay e-voucher worth Rs. 2,000 by spending Rs. 10,000 within 30 days of card issuance.

### What are the annual and joining fees for the Bandhan Bank Flare Credit Card?
The joining fee and annual fee are both Rs. 2,499 plus applicable taxes. The annual fee is waived if the cardholder spends Rs. 6 lakh in the preceding year.

### What is the monthly milestone benefit on the Bandhan Bank Flare Credit Card?
Cardholders can earn 5,000 bonus reward points every month when they spend Rs. 75,000 during that month. This benefit can make the card more rewarding for customers with consistently high monthly spending.

### Who should consider the Bandhan Bank Flare Credit Card?
The card may suit consumers looking for a uniform reward rate across spending categories, frequent domestic travellers who can use the lounge benefits, and those who regularly spend on dining and movies. It may also appeal to customers who can meet the Rs. 75,000 monthly spend requirement to earn bonus reward points.
