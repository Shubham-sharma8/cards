## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the rewards offered by Bandhan Bank Sparks Credit Card?
Bandhan Bank Sparks Credit Card offers 5X reward points on grocery & apparel spends and 2 reward points per Rs. 200 spent on other eligible transactions. The reward points do not expire.

### What is the welcome benefit on Bandhan Bank Sparks Credit Card?
Cardholders can earn 500 bonus reward points on spending Rs. 5,000 within 30 days of card issuance. The card also offers 10% cashback of up to Rs. 500 on the first eligible EMI booking with a minimum tenure of 12 months.

### How can I get the annual fee waived on Bandhan Bank Sparks Credit Card?
The annual fee can be waived if the cardholder spends Rs. 50,000 in a year. However, the card comes with no joining fee.

### Is Bandhan Bank Sparks Credit Card suitable for movie ticket bookings?
It can be useful for consumers who regularly book movie tickets through BookMyShow, as the card offers 20% discount capped at Rs. 100 per month on ticket bookings via BookMyShow.

### Is Bandhan Bank Sparks Credit Card suitable for everyday spending?
Bandhan Bank Sparks Credit Card can be suitable for consumers looking for basic rewards on regular spends along with lounge access, movie discounts and fuel surcharge waiver. However, consumers seeking higher cashback may find other cards more suitable.

### Do Bandhan Bank Sparks Credit Card reward points expire?
No. The reward points earned on Bandhan Bank Sparks Credit Card do not expire.
