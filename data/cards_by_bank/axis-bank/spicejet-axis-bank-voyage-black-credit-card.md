## About the Card
Cardholders can earn up to **28 SC Points for every Rs. 200 spent on SpiceJet** , along with **2X rewards** (12 SC Points per Rs. 200) on **day-to-day spends** , including online bill payments, online food ordering, and online entertainment. The accumulated reward points can be redeemed against SpiceJet flight tickets, meal vouchers, etc., via SpiceJet mobile app or website. The card also compensates for its joining fee of Rs. 2,000 by offering a **flight ticket voucher worth Rs. 4,000** along with complimentary **SpiceClub Gold Membership**. However, one important point to note is that despite being a travel credit card, it **does not offer international lounge visits** and charges a **high forex markup fee of 3.5%.** Also, the **domestic lounge visits are spend-based** , which limits the card’s overall value. This card is a suitable option for consumers who frequently travel and prefer to make bookings via SpiceJet.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the welcome benefits offered by SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Black Credit Card?
The card comes with a complimentary SpiceClub Gold Membership at the time of joining. Besides this, it also offers a SpiceJet voucher worth Rs. 4,000 on completing two transactions within 30 days of card approval.

### Can I redeem the rewards earned via SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Black Credit Card against cashback?
No, you can redeem the accumulated SpiceClub (SC) Points against SpiceJet flight tickets, meal vouchers, etc., via the SpiceJet mobile app or website.

### What is the forex markup fee charged by SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Black Credit Card?
SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Black Credit Card charges a forex markup fee of 3.5%. Therefore, this card is not a suitable option for international spends as it comes with a high forex markup fee. To save on international transactions, you can consider applying for a card with low or zero forex markup fees.

### Can I apply offline for SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Black Credit Card?
Yes, you can apply offline for this credit card by visiting any of the nearest Axis Bank branches and submitting the duly filled application form along with relevant documents in person. You can also apply online by visiting the official website of the bank and submitting the application form there.

### Does SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Black Credit Card offer any benefits on dining?
Yes, you can avail 25% discount, up to Rs. 800 every month, on dining at partner restaurants via Axis Bank Dining Delights Program. However, this benefit is only available on a minimum transaction of Rs. 2,500.
