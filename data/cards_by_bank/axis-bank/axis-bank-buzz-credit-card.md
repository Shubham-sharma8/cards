## About the Card
Axis Bank Buzz Credit Card is primarily a rewards credit card that offers accelerated benefits on online shopping. Cardholders can earn **3X rewards on online spends** and a base reward of **2 points per Rs. 200 on other spends**. Despite being a rewards credit card, it also offers up to **10% instant discount on Flipkart**. Additionally, the card offers a complimentary Flipkart voucher worth Rs. 1,000 as a welcome benefit and **Flipkart vouchers worth up to Rs. 7,000** as milestone benefits. In addition to accelerated rewards, the instant discounts and complimentary vouchers can lead to significant savings for consumers who prefer shopping on Flipkart. Overall, this credit card is a good option for those looking for a low annual fee card with accelerated value-back on online shopping.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Should I choose Axis Buzz Credit Card over Axis Flipkart Credit Card?
Axis Bank Flipkart Credit Card is a co-branded card that offers better benefits at Myntra, Flipkart, Cleartrip, and other partner merchants. However, Axis Bank Buzz Credit Card rewards you for online shopping on other websites as well. So, if you mainly shop on Flipkart, the co-branded card is a better choice, whereas if you shop across multiple platforms, Buzz Credit Card would suit you better.

### Is there any capping on the monthly discount offered by Axis Buzz Credit Card?
Yes, you can earn a maximum discount of Rs. 600 per month. The 10% instant discount on Flipkart spends is capped at Rs. 400 per month, and the 5% discount is capped at Rs. 200 per month.

### Can I get an instant discount on the purchase of any product through Axis Bank Buzz Credit Card?
No, the instant discount is not applicable on the purchase of gold coins and Samsung products.

### How can I redeem the rewards earned on Axis Buzz Credit Card?
You can redeem your rewards directly through Axis Bank internet banking or the mobile app by logging in with your customer ID and password. If you haven’t activated internet banking, you can call 1860-419-5555 or 1860-500-5555 to initiate a rewards redemption.

### What is the minimum salary requirement for Axis Bank Buzz Credit Card?
Axis Bank has not specified a particular income requirement. You can express your interest by filling out the application form on Axis Bank’s website. A bank executive will then contact you to confirm your eligibility.

### Do I get any cashback on Axis Buzz Credit Card?
No, Axis Bank Buzz Credit Card does not offer cashback benefits. With this card, you can earn EDGE REWARD points and redeem them against vouchers and merchandise.

### I want to convert my Axis Bank Buzz Credit Card purchases into EMIs. What is the process?
You can convert purchases above Rs. 2,500 into EMIs directly through net banking or the mobile app.
