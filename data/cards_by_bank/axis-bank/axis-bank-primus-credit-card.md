## About the Card
Axis Primus is an ultra-luxury, invite-only credit card designed for high-income individuals who value both rewards and comfort. It comes packed with premium travel privileges like unlimited lounge access, luxury airport transfers, complimentary nights at the world’s finest hotels, and even companion airfare, elevating consumer’s travel experience. Note: This is an invite-only credit card. Content on this page is for information purpose only._

## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** option from Axis Bank suited for super-premium users with unlimited domestic lounge visits/yr and low 0% forex markup.

**Best For:** International travelers and foreign SaaS/e-commerce buyers avoiding 3.5%+GST markup

**Not Ideal For:** Domestic-only spenders seeking peak local e-commerce cashback

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the benefits offered under the complimentary Epicure membership with Axis Primus Credit Card?
Under the Epicure membership, Primus cardholders get up to 25% discount on food and beverages, spa treatments, salon experiences, stays at partner hotels like Taj Palaces, Taj Safaris, etc. and Qmin food deliveries.

### What is the redemption of each EDGE Mile accrued using Axis Primus Credit Card?
Every EDGE Mile can be converted into 5 partner miles across travel partners of Axis Bank Primus Credit Card.

### Is there an option to waive off Axis Bank Primus Credit Card annual fee?
No, Axis Primus does not come with any fee waiver option for the cardholders. Instead, you get joining and renewal benefits in the form of bonus EDGE Miles and ITC Hotels vouchers.

### Are there any excluded categories for milestone bonus spend calculation?
No, there are no exclusions of spending categories for milestone benefit calculation.

### Are there any complimentary memberships offered on Axis Primus Credit Card?
Yes, Axis Primus offers complimentary memberships to Priority Pass Program, Bloomberg and Epicure program.
