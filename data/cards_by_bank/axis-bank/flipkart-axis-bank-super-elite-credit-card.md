## About the Card
Flipkart Axis Bank Super Elite Credit Card is a co-branded online shopping card suitably designed for Flipkart loyalists. Instead of direct reward points or cashback, the card rewards users in SuperCoins, a rewarding currency that can be used for shopping products and vouchers on Flipkart, Myntra, and Cleartrip. The card comes with benefits ranging from welcome perks and subscription plans to discounts on flight bookings, fuel surcharge waivers, etc. Due to its co-branded association with Flipkart, the card stands in close competition with a similar card, the Flipkart Axis Bank Credit Card. While Axis Flipkart offers direct benefits with Myntra and Cleartrip, the Super Elite variant offers redemption benefits on these. Plus, it lacks the co-branded benefits with other partner brands covered under the 4% umbrella by Flipkart Axis. Overall, the Flipkart Axis Bank Super Elite credit card is a suitable choice for Flipkart loyalists who majorly spend here and can utilize the SuperCoins. On the other hand, Flipkart Axis is a preferable choice with its multi-brand benefits for card users who spend not just on Flipkart but across other partner brands as well.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does this card reward for non-flipkart purchases?
Yes, the Flipkart SuperElite card offers SuperCoins for purchases, on and off Flipkart. For non-flipkart purchases, the card offers 2 SuperCoins on every Rs. 100 spent.

### When are SuperCoins credited to my Flipkart Account?
The SuperCoins are credited to your Flipkart account after the expiry of the return period of the products. In case of multiple products, the highest return window is considered for crediting the SuperCoins. However, for order cancellation, the SuperCoins are reversed. Additionally, for partial cancellation, if the net value of the order drops below Rs. 100, then no SuperCoins are awarded. If you replace a product, SuperCoins are credited after the expiry of the return period of the replaced product. For non-returnable items, SuperCoins are offered at the end of the delivery day.

### What is the validity of SuperCoins?
Flipkart SuperCoins expire after 1 year at the end of the month in which they are credited to your account. For instance, if the SuperCoins are credited on 12 October 2022, they will expire on 31 October 2023.

### Can I Earn SuperCoins on all transactions?
Note that, you can earn SuperCoins on all purchases, on and beyond Flipkart, with a minimum transaction value of Rs. 100 and above. Also, the SuperCoins are offered on the transactions and not on the product level. This means if you order 5 items, you will be eligible to earn SuperCoins on the transaction, but not on 5 items separately.
