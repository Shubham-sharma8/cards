## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is Axis Bank Privilege Easy Credit Card?
Axis Bank Privilege Easy Credit Card is a secured credit card backed by a fixed deposit starting at Rs. 65,000. It offers reward points, dining discounts, domestic lounge access, movie offers, and basic insurance benefits.

### What rewards does Axis Bank Privilege Easy Credit Card offer?
Cardholders earn 10 EDGE reward points for every Rs. 200 spent on eligible transactions. These points can be redeemed for shopping, travel bookings, vouchers, and other rewards through Axis Bank’s EDGE Rewards catalogue.

### What is the welcome benefit on this credit card?
Consumers can avail 12,500 EDGE reward points worth Rs. 2,500 after paying the joining fee and completing their first transaction within 30 days of card activation.

### Does the card offer airport lounge access?
Yes, the card offers 8 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year, capped at 2 visits per quarter, subject to spending Rs. 50,000 in the previous quarter.

### What dining benefits are available on this card?
Cardholders can avail up to 15% discount on dining at over 4,000+ partner restaurants across India through Axis Bank’s Dining Delights Programme with EazyDiner.

### Are there any movie or entertainment benefits?
Yes, the card offers Buy One Get One (BOGO) movie ticket offer on District by Zomato, making it suitable for cardholders who frequently spend on entertainment.
