## About the Card
Miles and More World Axis Bank Credit Card is a travel credit card that offers value-back through Award Miles. Cardholders can earn **4 Award Miles for every Rs. 200 spent** and save on travel spends by redeeming the accumulated miles via Miles and More website. This card also offers **16 complimentary domestic** and **2 international airport lounge visits every year** , enhancing overall value for frequent travellers.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the lounge access benefits of Miles and More World Axis Bank Credit Card?
Miles and More World Axis Bank Credit Card provides 16 domestic airport lounge visits per year (4 per quarter) and 2 international lounge visits annually via Priority Pass.

### What are the key differences between the Miles & More World and Miles & More Select Axis Bank Credit Cards?
Miles & More World Axis Bank Credit Card is the base variant, offering 4 Award Miles per Rs. 200 spent, 16 domestic and 2 international lounge visits per year at an fee of Rs. 3,500. Whereas, Miles & More Select Card is the premium variant, offering 6 Award Miles per Rs. 200 spent, 32 domestic and 4 international lounge visits every year at a comparatively higher joining and annual fee of Rs. 10,000 and Rs. 4,500, respectively.

### What are the welcome and annual benefits of Miles and More World Axis Bank Credit Card?
Cardholders can earn 5,000 Award Miles as a welcome bonus and 3,000 Award Miles annually. These rewards can be redeemed for travel and lifestyle benefits via Miles and More website.

### Are there dining discounts with Miles and More World Axis Bank Credit Card?
Yes, the card offers 25% off (up to Rs. 800) at partner restaurants. This benefit provides extra value for dining, though the discount comes with a monthly capping which makes it ideal for occasional restaurant visits.

### How does the rewards program work?
Cardholders can earn 4 Award Miles for every Rs. 200 spent on eligible transactions. Accumulated miles can be redeemed for flights, flight upgrades, hotels, car rentals, and shopping through the Miles & More website.
