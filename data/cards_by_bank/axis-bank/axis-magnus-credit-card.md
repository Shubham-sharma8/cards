## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the welcome benefit of Magnus Credit Card in Axis Bank?
Axis Magnus Credit Card charges a joining fee of Rs. 12,500. However, the card compensates for this by offering a Luxe gift card or The Postcard Hotels gift voucher worth Rs. 12,500 after the payment of joining fee. Additionally, cardholders can avail renewal fee waiver by spending Rs. 25 Lakh in a year.

### Axis Magnus vs. Atlas vs. Olympus Credit Card? Which one should I prefer?
Choosing between Axis Magnus, Atlas, and Olympus credit cards depends on your lifestyle and spending habits. Axis Magnus Credit Card is ideal for high spenders who value luxury, offering benefits such as unlimited lounge access and luxury hotel stays. If you are a frequent traveller, Axis Atlas Credit Card is better, as it offers Edge Miles, which can be redeemed for air miles, hotel loyalty points, and travel bookings. On the other hand, Axis Olympus Credit Card is a super-premium card designed for those willing to pay a high annual fee of Rs. 20,000. It offers higher reward redemption rates (1:4 against partner air miles or hotel loyalty points), complimentary hotel memberships and vouchers, free night stays at luxury hotels, exclusive concierge services, and more.

### What is the limit of Axis Bank Magnus Credit Card?
Axis Magnus Credit Card is a premium card designed for high-income individuals. The credit limit is determined by the bank based on factors such as the applicant’s credit score, repayment history, job profile, and more. The exact credit limit will be disclosed upon credit card approval.

### Is Axis Magnus a metal card?
Yes, Axis Magnus is a premium metal credit card offering all-around benefits across categories like travel, dining, and lifestyle. However, it comes with a high income eligibility criterion of Rs. 24 Lakh per annum.

### What is the airport lounge benefit on Magnus Credit Card for an add-on member?
Axis Magnus Credit Card offers unlimited complimentary domestic lounge visits for both primary and add-on card members, subject to a minimum spend of Rs. 50,000 in the previous three months. Additionally, cardholders and add-on members can avail unlimited complimentary international lounge visits with Priority Pass, along with four additional guest visits per year.
