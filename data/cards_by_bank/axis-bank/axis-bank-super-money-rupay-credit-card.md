## Frequently Asked Questions
### Can I link Axis SUPERMONEY Credit Card to any UPI app?
Yes, Axis Bank SUPERMONEY card can be linked to any UPI app of your choice like all other RuPay credit cards.

### Will Axis Bank SUPERMONEY Credit Card give 3% cashback on UPI apps like GPay, PhonePe and Paytm?
Axis Bank SUPERMONEY RuPay Credit Card offers 3% cashback only on UPI transactions made through super.money app. UPI payments made using all other UPI apps will only earn 1% cashback.

### Is there any capping on cashback on Axis Bank SUPERMONEY Credit Card?
Yes, you can only earn cashback worth up to Rs. 500 every month through Axis SUPER MONEY Credit Card.

### Are there any fees or charges associated with super.money app usage?
No, you do not have to pay any fee to create your account on super.money or to use the app to make your payments.
