## About the Card
Axis Magnus for Burgundy Credit Card is exclusively available to Axis Bank’s Burgundy clients at an **annual fee of Rs. 30,000**. While the card follows the same spend-based reward structure as Magnus, it offers enhanced travel benefits. Burgundy cardholders can earn up to **24% value-back on travel spends** (through Travel EDGE) and **up to 14% value-back on non-travel spends** , by redeeming reward points against partner airlines or hotel loyalty programs in **5:4 ratio**. In addition to a good reward rate, the card offers several travel benefits, including **unlimited lounge access with complimentary guest visits, a low forex mark-up fee of 2%, and complimentary airport meet-and-greet services,** making it an excellent choice for frequent travellers.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who is eligible for Axis Magnus Burgundy Credit Card?
The card is exclusively available to Axis Burgundy clients. To be eligible, individuals must maintain an Average Quarterly Balance of Rs. 10 Lakh in a savings account or across savings and current accounts. Alternatively, they must have a Total Relationship Value (TRV) of Rs. 30 Lakh across deposits and investments, a TRV of Rs. 1 Crore including Demat holdings, or a net monthly salary of Rs. 3 Lakh or more credited to an Axis Bank salary account.

### How can I apply for Axis Magnus for Burgundy Credit Card?
Axis Magnus for Burgundy Credit Card is offered exclusively to Axis Burgundy customers. If you are an Axis Burgundy customer, you can contact Axis Bank customer service to check your eligibility and apply for the card. Additionally, you can visit any Axis Bank branch, submit a duly filled application form, and provide the necessary documents.

### What is the difference between Axis Magnus and Axis Magnus Burgundy Credit Cards?
Axis Magnus Credit Card is a premium card offered to individuals with a strong credit profile, whereas Axis Magnus for Burgundy Credit Card is exclusively for Burgundy customers. While both cards offer the same reward points, Burgundy cardholders benefit from a better redemption rate, allowing them to convert reward points into partner airline or hotel loyalty points at a 5:4 ratio, compared to a 5:2 ratio for Axis Magnus Card.

### Does Axis Magnus for Burgundy Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, Axis Magnus Burgundy Credit Card offers unlimited complimentary domestic and international lounge visits. Additionally, cardholders can avail 4 complimentary guest visits per year to both domestic and international lounges.

### What are the welcome benefits offered by Axis Magnus Burgundy Credit Card?
The card charges a joining fee of Rs. 30,000 and offers a complimentary voucher worth Rs. 5,000 from Luxe Gift Card, The Postcard Hotels, or Yatra.com after the payment of joining fee.

### Does Axis Magnus for Burgundy Credit Card offer airport meet & greet services?
Yes, Axis Magnus Burgundy Credit Card offers 4 complimentary airport meet & greet services every year.

### What are the Axis Magnus Burgundy credit card fees?
Axis Magnus for Burgundy Credit Card charges an annual fee of Rs. 30,000 which is waived off on annual spends of Rs. 30 Lakh in the previous year.
