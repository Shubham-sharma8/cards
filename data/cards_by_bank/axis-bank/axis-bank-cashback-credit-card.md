## About the Card
Axis Bank Cashback Credit Card offers enhanced benefits of **up to 7% cashback**. It includes perks across multiple categories, such as travel, utility spends and others, but **primarily focuses on online shopping –** making it a suitable choice for those who prefer online brands and portals for their shopping over offline stores. This card is considered to be the successor to the previously popular Citi Cashback Credit Card. However, it works on a different structure – a tier-based cashback structure where users are rewarded based on their monthly spends. Read on to find out more about this card.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How do I activate the Axis Bank Cashback Credit Card?
You can activate your Axis Bank Cashback credit card through mobile banking or internet banking. Alternatively, you can contact Axis Bank credit card customer care at 1860 419 5555/ 1860 500 5555.

### Does the card offer cashback on airline ticket bookings?
The card offers unlimited 0.75% cashback on travel spends, including transactions across airlines, travel agencies, travel rental, railways and others.

### What is the eligibility criteria for Axis Cashback Credit Card?
The card can be availed by resident Indians aged between 18 and 70 years. Other eligibility factors include monthly income, credit score, etc., which are decided based on the issuer’s internal policy.

### What are the documents required to avail the Axis Cashback Credit Card?
Documents required to avail the Axis Cashback Credit Card are identity proofs, income proofs and residence proofs. These include PAN Card, Form 60, Latest Payslip, Form 16, IT Return, Driving License/Electricity Bill/Landline Telephone Bill.

### What is the forex markup fee on this credit card?
The card charges a forex markup fee of 3.5% of the transaction amount.
