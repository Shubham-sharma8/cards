## About the Card
Axis Bank offers two credit cards in co-branded partnership with Samsung, wherein Samsung Axis Bank Signature is the base variant and Samsung Axis Bank Infinite is the premium variant. Available at an annual fee of Rs. 500, the Signature variant offers 10% flat cashback on Samsung product purchases at physical stores, Samsung eStore or App, including transactions made at Samsung Service Centres and Samsung product purchases via Amazon. Besides, cardholders also get accelerated rewards on some popular brands like BigBasket, Zomato, Myntra and more. Read on to know the detailed features and benefits along with a review.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the limit of a Samsung Axis Bank credit card?
Credit limit is determined on the basis of several factors like income, credit history, current debts, etc. and the card issuer’s internal policies. The credit limit on your Samsung Axis Bank Credit Card would be communicated to you on card approval and delivery.

### Is Samsung Axis Bank Signature Card worth it?
Samsung Axis Bank Credit Card can be a good choice for those who frequently purchase Samsung products. However, those who do not want to limit their savings to one or more brands but want solid returns on all online spends should consider other cards.

### Does Samsung Axis Bank Credit Card offer unlimited cashback?
No, 10% cashback on Samsung spends is capped at Rs. 2,500 per month and Rs. 10,000 per year.

### How to activate my Samsung Axis Bank Credit Card?
There are multiple ways to activate your Axis Bank Credit Card including net banking, mobile app, phone banking and ATM. You can also call Axis Bank Customer Care on 1800 209 5577 or 1800 103 5577 to activate your card.

### Is there any limit on the rewards earning on Samsung Axis Bank Credit Card?
No. Only cashback on Samsung spends is capped; there is no capping on reward earning on accelerated categories or other spends.
