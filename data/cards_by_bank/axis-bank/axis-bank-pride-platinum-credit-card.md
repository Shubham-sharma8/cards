## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- Card renewal fee is waived on spending Rs. 20,000 or more during the previous year
- 1% fuel surcharge waiver of up to Rs. 250 per month on fuel spends worth Rs. 400 to Rs. 4,000

### Dining Benefits
- Up to 15% off, capped at Rs. 500 once per month, on dining bills of minimum Rs. 2,500 at partner restaurants in India under the Axis Bank Dining Delights program
- Annual savings of up to Rs. 6,000 on dining at partner restaurants
- No lifestyle benefits on categories like travel, shopping, entertainment, and others, except dining

### Get This Card If
- You are a defence personnel or Govt. of India employee who is beginning their credit journey
- You are looking for an entry-level credit card with low annual fee
- You prefer to earn rewards over cashback on your card spends
- You often dine out and want a card with discount offers to save on this cost

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why can’t I apply the dining discount benefit using my Axis Pride Platinum Credit Card?
The dining discount is applicable only once per month and on a minimum order value of Rs. 1,500. If you have used it once in that month or if you do not meet the order value requirement, you may not be eligible for this benefit.

### Is there any capping on the reward point earnings on Axis Pride Platinum Credit Card?
No, the reward points earnings are not capped and you can earn unlimited points as per your card spends.

### Can I get Axis Pride Platinum Credit Card for free?
Although Axis Pride Platinum comes at zero joining fee, it still charges a renewal fee of Rs. 250 + taxes. However, you can waive off this fee if you spend a min. of Rs. 20,000 in a year.

### Is there any additional fee charged on international spends made using Axis Bank Pride Platinum Credit Card?
Axis Bank Pride Platinum Credit Card charges a forex mark-up fee of 3.50% of the transaction value on international spends.
