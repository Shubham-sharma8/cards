## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is the Axis Neo credit card good?
At a low fee of just Rs. 250, the Axis Bank Neo credit card is a good choice for beginners who spend frequently on popular brands like Zomato, BookMyShow, Myntra, Blinkit, and Paytm.

### What is the annual fee for the Neo Axis Bank credit card?
The Axis Bank Neo Credit Card comes at a low annual fee of Rs. 250. The fee is nil if you apply for the card via issuer channels.

### Is Axis Neo good for online shopping?
The card offers a flat discount on Myntra of Rs. 150 off on minimum spends of Rs. 999 (on selected styles). Thus, for Myntra loyalists, this card can be a good choice. But, for other online spends, users earn 1 reward point for every Rs. 200 spent.

### Is the Axis Bank Neo credit card worth it for beginners?
Yes, for beginners who want a low annual fee card and spend across the partner brands, this credit card can be a good choice.

### What are the benefits of the Neo card?
The Axis Bank Neo credit card offers benefits like 40% off on Zomato, 10% off on Blinkit and BookMyShow, 5% off on utility bills via Paytm, and Rs. 150 off on Myntra.

### Is there any surcharge waiver on fuel payments made via Axis Neo Credit Card?
No, there is no fuel surcharge waiver offered to Axis Neo cardholders. Fuel transactions will be charged with a 1% surcharge.

### How can I increase the credit limit on my Axis Bank Neo Credit Card?
Based on the spending patterns and risk behavior of the user, Axis Bank decides whether or not to increase your credit limit. However, you can request the same by calling on the customer care number 18004190072. You can also use the net banking facility or Axis Bank mobile app for the same.

### How to activate the Axis Neo credit card?
You can activate your Axis Bank Neo credit card through the Axis Bank Mobile banking app or by contacting the bank’s customer care at 1800 103 5577.
