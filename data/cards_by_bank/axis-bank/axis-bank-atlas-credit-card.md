## About the Card
While the card delivers an excellent reward rate on travel, certain limitations exist, such as the categorization of transfer partners into Group A and Group B, along with capping for each. Moreover, accelerated reward earnings on travel are limited to monthly spends of Rs. 2 Lakh. While this may suffice for moderate spenders, travellers with higher monthly expenses might find it restrictive. Moreover, despite being a travel-focused card, Axis Atlas carries a high forex mark-up fee of 3.5%, which can be a drawback for international travellers. Here is all you need to know about Axis Atlas Credit Card.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How many tier memberships are available with the Axis Atlas Credit Card?
Axis Atlas Credit Card offers three tier-based memberships: Silver, Gold, and Platinum, based on the spending criteria. Cardholders can achieve the Silver tier by spending Rs. 3 Lakh, and the Gold tier by spending Rs. 7.5 Lakh, and the Platinum tier by spending Rs. 15 Lakh. With higher tiers, cardholders can access enhanced benefits such as a higher number of complimentary airport lounge visits and increased bonus reward points through milestone and annual benefits.

### What is the difference between Axis Atlas Credit Card and Axis Select Credit Card?
Axis Atlas is a travel-focused credit card that offers EDGE Miles, which can be redeemed against partner air miles or hotel loyalty points at a 1:2 ratio or for travel bookings through the Travel EDGE portal at a 1:1 ratio. On the other hand, the Axis Select Credit Card offers EDGE reward points, redeemable through the rewards catalogue at a value of Rs. 0.20 per point and against partner air miles and hotel loyalty points at a 10:1 ratio.

### Is Axis Atlas Credit Card worth it for a spend of Rs. 4-5 Lakh?
By spending Rs. 5 Lakh in a year, cardholders can achieve the Platinum tier, which offers up to 12 domestic lounge visits and 6 international lounge visits annually. They can also earn up to 5,000 EDGE Miles as milestone and annual benefits, worth Rs. 10,000 when redeemed against partner airlines or hotel loyalty points at a 1:2 ratio. Furthermore, cardholders earn 2 EDGE Miles for every Rs. 100 spent on travel and other categories. This makes the card especially beneficial for frequent travellers who can maximize its features and save on travel spends.

### What are the milestone benefits of Axis Atlas Credit Card?
Axis Atlas Credit Card offers milestone benefits tied to its tier structure. Cardholders who achieve the Silver tier by spending Rs. 3 Lakh receive 2,500 EDGE Miles as milestone benefits. At the Gold tier, achieved by spending Rs. 7.5 Lakh, they receive 2,500 EDGE Miles as milestone benefits and an additional 2,500 EDGE Miles as annual benefits. At the highest level, the Platinum tier, achieved by spending Rs. 15 Lakh, cardholders earn 5,000 EDGE Miles as milestone benefits and 5,000 EDGE Miles as annual benefits.

### What is the eligibility criteria for Axis Atlas Credit Card?
To be eligible for Axis Atlas Credit Card, applicants must be between 18 and 70 years old. Both salaried and self-employed individuals can apply. Salaried individuals must have an annual income of Rs. 12 Lakh, while self-employed individuals must meet the annual income requirement of Rs. 15 Lakh.
