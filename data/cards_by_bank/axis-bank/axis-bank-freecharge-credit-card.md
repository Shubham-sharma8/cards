## About the Card
Axis Bank Freecharge Credit Card is a co-branded card designed to offer benefits on recharges and bill payments. It is an **invite-only card** , and eligible users can see the offer banner on their Freecharge mobile app. With this card, cardholders earn **1 reward point on every spend**. A unique feature of this card is its milestone rewards, allowing cardholders to earn up to 350 reward points on monthly spends above Rs. 5,000. In addition to rewards, the card offers significant savings on popular brands such as**Myntra, Shuttl, FreshMenu, and Eros Now** , covering key categories like shopping, dining, travel, and entertainment. However, despite being a co-branded card, it **does not offer any accelerated rewards on Freecharge spends**. Cardholders earn the same reward rate across all categories, which can be considered as a drawback, especially since most co-branded cards offer accelerated benefits on their partnered brand.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for Axis Bank Freecharge Credit Card?
Axis Bank Freecharge Credit Card is an invite-only card offered to select Axis Bank customers. Eligible users will see a banner on the Freecharge app through which they can apply online. Besides this, you can also contact Axis Bank’s customer care and ask the representative about your eligibility.

### Can I earn rewards on bill payments via Axis Bank Freecharge Credit Card?
Yes, you can earn rewards on recharges and bill payments made via Freecharge app. The card offers 1 EDGE Reward Point on every spend, along with up to 350 reward points as milestone rewards.

### Does Axis Bank Freecharge Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
No, Axis Bank Freecharge Credit Card does not offer any travel or lounge access benefits. However, you can earn 1 EDGE Reward Point on every spend, including the travel category.

### Can I save on online shopping with Axis Bank Freecharge Credit Card?
Yes, besides regular rewards, you can save on online shopping at Myntra. The card offers a 10% discount on your spends at Myntra.

### What are the annual charges for Axis Bank Freecharge Credit Card?
Axis Bank Freecharge Credit Card comes with a joining and annual fee of Rs. 250 plus applicable taxes.
