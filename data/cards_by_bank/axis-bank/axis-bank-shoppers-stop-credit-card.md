## About the Card
Axis Bank Shoppers Stop Credit Card is a migration from the previously popular First Citizen Citi Credit Card. Similar to the Citi variant, this card offers co-branded benefits for Shoppers Stop, but with a revised rewards structure and activation benefits. Available at an annual fee of Rs. 500, this card primarily benefits those who prefer Shoppers Stop for their shopping needs. Cardholders can earn 10X reward points on purchases at Shoppers Stop, along with 6X reward points on beauty products and other select brands at Shoppers Stop. The card offers value back in the form of First Citizen Points, which are redeemable for shopping at Shoppers Stop, making it a great option for loyal customers who spend a considerable amount on shopping each month. While HDFC Bank offers two co-branded credit cards with Shoppers Stop, Axis variant is comparable to the entry-level Shoppers Stop HDFC Bank Credit Card. Although the HDFC variant has an edge due to multiple redemption options and a higher redemption value of 1:1, Axis card stands out with unlimited rewards (no capping) and a higher return on accelerated spend categories. If you frequently shop at Shoppers Stop and are looking for a low annual fee credit card with decent benefits on Shoppers Stop and its partner brands, this card is worth considering.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for Axis Bank Shoppers Stop Credit Card?
To apply for this credit card online, visit the official website of Axis Bank and navigate to the card page. You need to submit a duly filled application form along with the required documents. If you prefer to apply offline, you can visit the nearest Axis Bank branch and submit the application form in person.

### Does Axis Bank Shoppers Stop Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
No, Axis Bank Shoppers Stop Credit Card does not offer any travel benefits, such as lounge access or discounts on travel expenses.

### What are the renewal benefits offered by Axis Bank Shoppers Stop Credit Card?
This credit card does not offer any renewal benefits. However, you can avail renewal fee waiver on annual spends of Rs. 1 Lakh.

### Does Axis Bank Shoppers Stop Credit Card offer a discount on dining bills?
No. You do not get additional dining discount; only base reward rate of 2 First Citizen Points per Rs. 200 spent is applicable.

### Can I earn a fuel surcharge waiver on all fuel spends via Axis Bank Shoppers Stop Credit Card?
No, you can earn 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel spends between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000, up to a maximum of Rs. 400 per month.
