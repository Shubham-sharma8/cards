## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is Indian Oil Axis Bank Credit Card the best Indian Oil co-branded credit card in India?
Indian Oil offers co-branded credit cards with Axis Bank, HDFC Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank and RBL Bank. Among all its co-branded cards, Indian Oil RBL XTRA Credit Card comes with the highest saving potential, albeit at a comparatively higher annual fee of Rs. 1,500.

### How many EDGE Reward Points can I earn in a month through Axis Indian Oil Credit Card?
You can earn maximum 1,000 EDGE Reward Points on Indian Oil Fuel Spends and maximum 250 reward points on online shopping. There is no limit on reward earnings across other eligible categories.

### How can I redeem the EDGE Reward Points earned through this card?
EDGE Reward Points can be redeemed through Axis Bank’s EDGE Reward Portal against vouchers and merchandise.

### How much discount can I avail on BookMyShow through this card?
Maximum discount on movie ticket booking via BookMyShow is capped at Rs. 100 per month.

### Which transactions are excluded from rewards program?
Spends made on rent, wallet, insurance, EMI, cash withdrawals, fee & charges, GST and payment reversals are not eligible for spend-based fee waiver.
