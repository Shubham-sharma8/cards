## About the Card
Axis Bank Rewards Credit Card comes as another addition to the list of the top rewards credit cards with up to 20 EDGE Reward points on every Rs. 125 spent. In addition to the rewards on every card spend, 1,500 bonus EDGE Reward points as monthly milestone benefit confirm its status as a rewards credit card. However, the redemption value of up to Rs. 0.20 per EDGE Reward point is a bit low which this card tries to compensate with multiple redemption options, including cashback, catalogue, partner miles, etc. In addition to the rewards benefits, this card also offers other lifestyle-related benefits in the form of complimentary lounge access, along with discount offers on Swiggy orders and dining at partner restaurants. Read on to know more about this Axis Bank credit card with its fee details, annual benefits, milestone bonus, rewards program and other features.
