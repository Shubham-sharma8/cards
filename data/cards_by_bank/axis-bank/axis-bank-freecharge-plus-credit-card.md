## About the Card
Axis Bank Freecharge Plus Credit Card is an entry-level co-branded credit card, primarily designed to offer accelerated benefits on bill payments and recharges via Freecharge. This is an **invite-only card** , and eligible consumers can see the application banner on their Freecharge app. With this card, cardholders can earn **5% cashback on spends made on Freecharge**. Additionally, it offers **2% cashback on Uber and Ola** rides, making it a suitable option for daily commuters. Apart from these categories, cardholders can earn a base cashback of**1% on other eligible spends**. Additional benefits, such as **up to Rs. 500 discount on dining** and annual fee waiver, further add to the card’s value. Overall, this card is best suited for consumers who prefer making payments via Freecharge, as bill payments made outside the Freecharge app do not earn any cashback.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for Axis Bank Freecharge Plus Credit Card?
This is an invite-only card offered to select Axis Bank customers. The eligible consumers will see an application banner on their Freecharge app to apply online. You can also contact the bank’s customer service and check your eligibility with a representative.

### Does Axis Bank Freecharge Plus Credit Card offer cashback on fuel spends?
No, this card does not offer cashback on certain categories, including fuel spends, wallet loads, cash withdrawals, gold purchases, EMI transactions, rent payments, government spends, insurance and education payments, utility payments outside of Freecharge, and purchases converted to EMI after the transaction.

### What are the renewal benefits offered by Axis Bank Freecharge Plus Credit Card?
Axis Bank Freecharge Plus Credit Card does not offer any specific renewal benefits. However, you can avail an annual fee waiver by spending Rs. 50,000 or more in a year.

### Can I earn cashback on bill payments via Paytm through Axis Bank Freecharge Plus Credit Card?
No, the card offers 5% cashback only on bill payments and recharges made via Freecharge. Bill payments through other platforms, such as Paytm, are not eligible for cashback.

### Is there any minimum transaction value required to earn cashback via Axis Bank Freecharge Plus Credit Card?
Yes, cashback is offered only on transactions of Rs. 100 or above. Transactions below Rs. 100 are not eligible for cashback.
