## About the Card
Axis Bank AURA Credit Card focuses on offering inclined benefits towards a very distinctive category-health and wellness. It comes with free doctor and nutritional consultations, fitness sessions, and discounts on health check-ups. Additionally, cardholders can earn rewards on all retail expenses, with 5X rewards on insurance spends. Note: Axis Bank has discontinued this card. The features and benefits available on this page apply only to the existing credit cardholders.__

## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- 5X EDGE reward points on insurance spends, with maximum transaction capped at Rs. 10,000
- 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000, capped at Rs. 250 per statement cycle

### Dining Benefits
- Up to 15% discount, up to Rs. 500 on a minimum order value of Rs. 2,500 every month, through the Dining Delights Program
- Up to 15% dining discount on partner restaurants

### Insurance Cover
- 5X EDGE reward points on insurance spends, with maximum transaction capped at Rs. 10,000
- Accelerated (5X) rewards on insurance spends
- No medical emergency cover

### Get This Card If

- You don’t’ mind shifting from your regular doctor to the ones on Practo

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How much fuel benefits can I earn via Axis Bank AURA Credit Card?
You can earn a fuel surcharge waiver of 1% on your transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000. However, you can earn a maximum waiver of Rs. 250 in a statement cycle. This fuel surcharge waiver can be availed across all fuel stations in India.

### How can I apply for Axis Bank AURA Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online through the bank’s official website by filling out an application form. If you want to apply offline, you can visit any of the nearest branches of the bank and fill out an application form.

### What should I do if my Axis Bank AURA Credit Card is declined?
The bank rejects your application if you don’t meet the certain eligibility criteria set by the bank. Generally, the bank informs you by sending a text on your registered mobile number stating the reason for your rejection. Apart from this, you can also call on the Axis Bank Credit Card customer care number and ask the representative regarding your query.

### Can I convert my purchases into EMIs via Axis Bank AURA Credit Card?
Yes, you can convert your big-ticket purchases into EMIs via Axis Bank AURA Credit Card. You can simply look on the merchant’s website whether it offers the Axis Bank Credit Card EMI option or not. Additionally, you can also call on the customer care number and ask the representative to convert your selected purchase into EMI.

### Can I withdraw cash using my Axis Bank AURA Credit Card?
Yes, you can withdraw cash using your Axis AURA Credit Card. However, the bank charges a cash advance fee of 3.4% of the withdrawn amount or a minimum of Rs. 500 for withdrawing cash. Therefore, it is advisable not to withdraw cash using your credit card as it incurs high charges.

### How can I redeem the reward points earned on Axis Bank AURA Credit Card?
Axis EDGE reward points can be redeemed through the rewards catalogue. You can visit the catalogue and make the necessary selection and then redeem the points earned on your Axis Bank AURA Credit Card.

### What is the benefit of Aura credit card of Axis Bank?
Axis Aura credit card offers benefits like reward points on every card spend, along with health and wellness related benefits like free doctor consultations, fitness sessions, discount on health check-ups, etc. Refer to the page above to know the crad’s features and benefits in detail.

### Does Axis Aura credit card have lounge access?
No, Axis Aura credit card does not offer any complimentary lounge access to its users.

### What is the difference between Axis Aura credit Card and MyZone credit card?
Both of these credit card variants are offered by Axis Bank and offer rewards on card spends and dining discounts. However, while the benefits offered by Axis Aura lean more towards health and wellness categories, those offered to Axis My Zone card users are more related to lifestyle brands like Swiggy, District, Ajio, etc. Unlike Axis Aura, My Zone also offers free lounge visits.
