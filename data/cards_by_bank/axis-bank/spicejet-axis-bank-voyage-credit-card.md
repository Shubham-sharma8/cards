## About the Card
The card offers value-back through **SpiceClub (SC) Points** , which are redeemable against SpiceJet flight tickets, meal vouchers, and more via SpiceJet mobile app or website. Additionally, **complimentary lounge access** and a **SpiceJet flight voucher worth Rs. 1,500** as a welcome benefit enhance the card’s overall value for frequent travellers. Overall, consumers who frequently travel and are **loyal customers of SpiceJet can make the most out of this card**. However, consumers who are not loyal to a single airline may consider other travel credit cards offering benefits across multiple airlines.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online by visiting the official website of Axis Bank. You need to navigate to the card page and submit a duly filled application form along with the relevant documents. Besides this, if you want to apply offline, you can visit any of the nearest branches of the bank and submit the application form in person.

### How much can I save on flight tickets with SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Credit Card?
With this credit card, you can earn 6 SC Points per Rs. 200 spent on SpiceJet flight tickets, along with 12 SC Points per Rs. 200 spent through the complimentary SpiceClub Silver Membership. Overall, you can earn up to 18 SC Points per Rs. 200 spent on SpiceJet bookings. You can also save on your flight tickets by redeeming the accumulated reward points against SpiceJet flight tickets.

### Does SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, the card offers 4 complimentary domestic lounge visits per year on spending Rs. 50,000 in the previous quarter.

### Is SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Credit Card a good option for international spends?
While this credit card offers 3 SC Points per Rs. 200 spent internationally, it comes with a high forex markup fee of 3.5%, which nullifies the overall value. Therefore, if you want to save on international spends, you should choose a card with zero or low forex markup fees.

### Does SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Credit Card offer renewal fee waiver?
No, SpiceJet Axis Bank Voyage Credit Card does not offer an annual fee waiver. However, it offers 1,000 SpiceClub Points every year on card renewal.
