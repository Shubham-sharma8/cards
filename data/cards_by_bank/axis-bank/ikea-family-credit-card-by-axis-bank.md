## About the Card
IKEA Family Credit Card by Axis Bank is a co-branded card that offers accelerated benefits on IKEA spends. It is a lifetime-free card that primarily offers value back in the form of reward points, and minimal benefits beyond that. Despite reward points being its main feature, this credit card falls short compared to other lifetime-free or shopping cards like IDFC FIRST Select or Amazon ICICI due to its lower reward rate. Overall, this card offers a maximum reward rate of 0.6% on IKEA spends, which can drop to 0.2% on other categories.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How do I activate my IKEA Axis Bank Credit Card?
You can activate your IKEA Axis Bank Credit card via Mobile banking, Internet banking or through the Axis Bank customer care.

### Is IKEA Axis Bank Credit Card lifetime free?
Yes, IKEA Axis Bank Credit Card is lifetime free.

### Does this card offer reward points on utility spends?
No, utility bill payments are excluded from the rewards program.

### Can I convert all transactions into EMIs with this credit card?
Yes, you can convert transactions above Rs. 2,500 to EMIs for a tenure of 3,6,9,12,18 and 24 months.

### What is the EazyDiner offer on this credit card?
The card offers up to 15% off (up to Rs. 500), on dining transactions above Rs. 2,500, once per card, per month.
