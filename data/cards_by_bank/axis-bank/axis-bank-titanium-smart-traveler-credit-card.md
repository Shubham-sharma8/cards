## Frequently Asked Questions
### What rewards does the Axis Bank Titanium Smart Traveler Credit Card offer?
This card offers rewards based on spending categories: 8 points per Rs. 200 on international spends, 4 points per Rs. 200 on travel bookings like flights, hotels, trains, and buses, and 2 points per Rs. 200 on regular domestic transactions.

### How does the dining benefit work on this credit card?
Cardholders can avail up to 15% discount at select partner restaurants. The offer is applicable on a minimum monthly spend of Rs. 2,500, making it useful for frequent diners looking to save on regular dining expenses.

### Is there any fuel benefit available on this card?
Yes, the card provides a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on eligible fuel transactions.

### Who should apply for this credit card?
This card is ideal for frequent travelers and regular spenders who want to earn rewards on travel bookings, international purchases, and everyday expenses. It is especially useful for those who value dining discounts and fuel savings.

### What types of transactions earn the highest rewards?
**International transactions earn the highest rewards at 8 points per Rs. 200 spent. Travel-related bookings like flights and hotels also offer good rewards at 4 points per Rs. 200, encouraging usage for global and domestic travel spending.
