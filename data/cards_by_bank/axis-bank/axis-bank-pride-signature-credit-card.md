## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- Card renewal fee waived off on reaching Rs. 40,000 card spends in the previous year
- 1% fuel surcharge waiver of up to Rs. 400 per month on fuel spends worth Rs. 400 to Rs. 4,000

- 15% off of up to Rs. 500 on minimum dining bill of Rs. 2,500 at partner restaurants, once per month, under Axis Bank Dining Delights program

- You often travel by air within India and want a card with complimentary domestic lounge access

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the value-back offered by Axis Pride Signature Credit Card through reward points?
Since each reward points can be redeemed in 5:1 ratio, you can get a value-back worth 0.80% on your card spends through rewards.

### How often can I apply the dining discount benefit using my Axis Pride Signature Credit Card?
You can use the dining discount once every month using your Axis Pride Signature Credit Card.

### Is there any spending condition associated with the complimentary domestic lounge access using Axis Bank Pride Signature Credit Card?
You can avail the domestic airport lounge visits by spending at least Rs. 50,000 in the previous quarter.

### Is Axis Pride Signature Credit Card lifetime-free?
No, Axis Pride Signature comes at zero joining fee, but it charges a renewal fee of Rs. 500 + taxes. However, you can waive off this renewal fee if you spend a min. of Rs. 40,000 in a year.
