## About the Card
The Fibe Axis Bank Credit Card is a lifetime free RuPay card offered in collaboration with the lending platform Fibe. The card provides cashback across all transactions with accelerated earnings on dining, entertainment, and commuting. Currently offered to Fibe customers only, it claims to be the first numberless card in India, which ensures added security for users, as important details like the CVV are no longer featured on the physical card. Read on to know more details about Fibe Axis Bank credit cards.
