## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for Google Pay Flex Axis Bank Credit Card?
You can apply online through the Google Pay app. The application is paperless, and once approved, your card will be linked directly within the app for instant use.

### Can I earn reward points on rent payments via Google Pay Flex Axis Bank Credit Card?
No, this credit card does not offer reward points on select categories, including rental payments, cash withdrawals, financial institution payments, insurance premium payments, educational institutions and services, government and government institutions payments and fees, payment of card fees and charges, wallet spends, clock, jewellery, watch & silverware stores.

### Does Google Pay Flex Axis Bank Credit Card offer a fuel surcharge waiver?
No, this entry-level credit card does not offer fuel surcharge waiver. However, you can earn reward points on your fuel spends and redeem them against UPI spends via Google Pay.

### What is the flexible repayment option with Google Pay Flex Axis Bank Credit Card?
The card allows you to either pay the full bill or convert your spends into easy EMIs directly within the Google Pay app. This option depends on your eligibility and the bill amount, offering flexibility for managing larger expenses.

### Does Google Pay Flex Axis Bank Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
No, this card does not offer complimentary airport lounge access or other premium travel perks. It focuses on everyday digital spends and rewards rather than luxury benefits.

### How can I redeem accumulated reward points on Google Pay Flex Axis Bank Credit Card?
You can redeem accumulated stars directly via Google Pay for UPI payments. Each star is equivalent to Rs. 1, allowing instant cashback redemption against your future payments.
