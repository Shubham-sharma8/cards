## About the Card
Miles & More Axis Bank Select Credit Card is a premium variant under the Miles & More Axis Bank credit card range. This card is suitable for consumers who are looking for a card with a similar reward rate across multiple categories. It offers **6 Awards Miles for every Rs. 200 spent**. Moreover, these miles can be redeemed via Miles & More website for flight bookings, seat upgrades, hotel stays, car rentals, etc. The card also offers **32 complimentary domestic** and **4 complimentary international** **airport lounge visits per year** , enhancing its value for frequent travellers.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the lounge access benefits of Miles & More Select Axis Bank Credit Card?
The card offers 32 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year and 4 international lounge visits through Priority Pass, making it suitable for frequent travellers who want comfortable airport experiences.

### How do Award Miles work on this credit card?
Cardholders earn 6 Award Miles for every Rs. 200 spent on eligible transactions. These miles do not expire and can mainly be redeemed for travel-related benefits, making them ideal for users who plan long-term travel redemptions.

### What welcome benefits are offered with this card?
This card offers 15,000 Award Miles as a welcome benefit after card activation. These miles have no expiry and can be redeemed against travel spends via Miles and More website.

### What dining benefits are available on this credit card?
Miles & More Select Axis Bank Credit Card offers dining discount of 40% (Up to Rs. 1,000) at select partner restaurants, making it a great choice for those who frequently dine out and want to save on meals.

### Who should consider applying for Miles & More Select Axis Bank Credit Card?
Miles & More Select Axis Bank Credit Card is ideal for consumers seeking a card with decent travel and lifestyle benefits, However, you can only maximize benefits from this card by redeeming the miles via Miles and More website.
