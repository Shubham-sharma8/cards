## About the Card
Samsung Axis Bank Infinite Credit Card is the premium card variant in the co-branded card partnership between Axis Bank and Samsung. On purchasing Samsung products via Samsung physical and eStores and other eligible partners like Amazon, cardholders get flat 10% cashback along with accelerated reward points on some popular everyday brands. In addition to this, the card also extends benefits like domestic and international lounge visits, Priority Pass membership, Eazy Diner Prime membership, etc., enhancing its all-round value. While the card can be a good option for Samsung loyalists, its core rewards program should also be taken into consideration before applying. Read on to know about the card’s features, benefits and a detailed review.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is Samsung Axis Bank Infinite Credit Card better than the Signature variant?
While Samsung Axis Bank Infinite Credit Card offers the same cashback rate on Samsung spend as the Signature variant, it comes with a higher capping, allowing high-spenders to extract maximum value. The reward rate on other preferred categories is also higher on the Infinite variant and it comes with additional benefits like international lounge access and memberships. However, consumers should note that the card comes at a much higher annual fee of Rs. 5,000.

### What is the credit limit I can get on Samsung Axis Bank Infinite Credit Card?
The credit limit will be communicated to you when your card is approved. It is based on your income, existing debts, highest limit on existing cards, and several other internal policies of the bank.

### Is the annual fee of Rs. 5,000 on Samsung Axis Bank Infinite Card worth it?
For Samsung loyalists, it would be easy to extract a value equivalent to the card’s fee. Even if you spend Rs. 50,000 on Samsung products, you will get Rs. 5,000 cashback, compensating for the fee charged. However, before choosing, you must keep other card features in mind.

### How long does it take to process my Axis Bank credit card application?
After you have successfully submitted your credit card application, it may take 7-10 days for the card to reach your registered address.

### What is Axis Bank credit card customer care number?
You can reach Axis Bank credit card customer care on 1800 209 5577 or 1800 103 5577.
