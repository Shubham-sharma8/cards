## About the Card
With the base variant, cardholders earn 3% value-back on IndiGo spends and get other travel benefits such as domestic airport lounge access and up to 2,400 IndiGo BluChips vouchers as milestone bonus. In addition to travel, this card also offers decent savings worth 2% on everyday spends like dining and groceries. While it charges an annual fee of Rs. 799 without any fee waiver facility, the cardholders are still well compensated through joining and renewal vouchers of 1,200 IndiGo BluChips. Read on to know more about this IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card with its feature review, pros and cons, and know who should get this card.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who should apply for IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card?
IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card is best suited for frequent flyers who prefer to travel with IndiGo. It is ideal for consumers who regularly book IndiGo flights and want to save on travel by redeeming the accumulated BluChips.

### How can I earn BluChips on this credit card?
Cardholders can earn 3 BluChips per Rs. 100 spent on IndiGo bookings, 2 BluChips per 100 on dining and grocery spends, and 1 BluChip per Rs. 100 on other eligible spends.

### How can BluChips be redeemed?
Accumulated BluChips can be redeemed directly on the IndiGo website or mobile app. They can be used for flight bookings and upgrades, allowing cardholders to reduce their overall travel costs.

### Are there any milestone benefits on this card?
Yes, the card offers 1,200 BluChips on spending Rs. 1.5 Lakh in a year. An additional 1,200 BluChips are offered on reaching Rs. 3 Lakh annual spends, totaling up to 2,400 BluChips.

### Does the IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card offer lounge access?
Yes, the card offers up to 4 complimentary domestic lounge visits per year (capped at 1 per quarter) on spending Rs. 50,000 in the previous quarter.

### What are the key limitations of this credit card?
The card does not offer complimentary international lounge access. Also, domestic lounge access is spend-based, and the base reward rate on non-bonus categories is relatively low at 1 BluChip per Rs. 100 spent.
