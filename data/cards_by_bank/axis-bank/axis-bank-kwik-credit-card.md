## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Lifetime Free option from Axis Bank suited for lifetime free users.

**Best For:** Everyday UPI Scan & Pay spenders wanting credit-line convenience on merchant QRs

**Not Ideal For:** Travel flyers seeking high-value airline/hotel miles

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 10,000 – Rs. 35,000 on UPI & retail

### Pros (Good)
- Lifetime free card with zero joining and annual fees
- 2 EDGE REWARD Points for every Rs. 200 spent on eligible domestic & international transactions
- Supports UPI payments through the RuPay network, offering rewards on eligible UPI spends
- Instant virtual credit card issued upon approval for immediate usage

### Cons (Bad)
- Reward points are available only on UPI transactions above Rs. 2,000
- Reward earning rate is relatively low for consumers with high monthly spending
- No travel benefits such as airport lounge access or complimentary travel insurance
- Fuel surcharge waiver is capped at Rs. 150 per month

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does Axis Bank Kwik Credit Card support UPI payments?
Yes. Since it is a RuPay credit card, it can be linked to supported UPI apps to make UPI payments. Cardholders can also earn 2 EDGE REWARD Points for every Rs. 200 spent on UPI transactions above Rs. 2,000.

### How can I earn reward points on Axis Bank Kwik Credit Card?
You can earn 2 EDGE REWARD Points for every Rs. 200 spent on eligible domestic and international transactions. The card also offers 2 EDGE REWARD Points for every Rs. 200 spent on eligible UPI transactions above Rs. 2,000.

### Does Axis Bank Kwik Credit Card offer a fuel surcharge waiver?
Yes. The card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000, subject to a maximum waiver of Rs. 150 per month.

### How can I activate Axis Bank Kwik Credit Card?
You can activate this credit card through any supported UPI app or via Axis Bank\'s mobile banking or internet banking platform.
