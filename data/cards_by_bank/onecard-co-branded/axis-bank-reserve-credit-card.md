## About the Card
Having said that**, one of the major drawbacks of this card is its low point-to-mile conversion ratio of 5:2, considering the card’s annual fee.** But it **s****till offers the highest reward-earning rate among all super-premium cards.** The card offers a base reward earning rate of 7.5% across domestic spends, offering 15 points per Rs. 200 and 2X rewards on international spends. Let’s say you spend Rs. 15,000 on the base categories (shopping, travel, utility bills, dining, etc.) on this card you would earn 1,125 reward points.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Do I earn rewards on utility spends made on the card?
No. The card excludes reward earning on utility spends. Some other excluded transactions are spends on insurance, fuel, gold/jewellery, wallet and Govt. institutions.

### I like the previous miles conversion of 5:4 on my existing Reserve credit card. Is there a way to avail it?
One can retain the old redemption ratio of 5:4 by opening and maintaining a burgundy private account (with a minimum TRV of Rs. 5 Crore) and opting for a Burgundy Private Credit Card.

### How much discount can I avail with the EazyDiner membership?
You can avail 25% off up to Rs. 800 at participating restaurants with the complimentary EazyDiner membership.

### Are there any charges on paying rent through the Axis Reserve Credit Card?
Yes, a transaction fee of 1% is applicable on paying rent using this card. This is capped at Rs. 1,500 per transaction.
