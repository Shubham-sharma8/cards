## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Lifetime Free option from OneCard / Co-branded suited for lifetime free users.

**Best For:** Students, New-to-Credit applicants, or low-CIBIL users building credit history via a Fixed Deposit

**Not Ideal For:** Established salaried professionals eligible for unsecured cashback/travel cards

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 5,000 – Rs. 20,000 (<30% FD utilization)

### Pros (Good)
- Lifetime Free card with zero annual fee commitment
- Secured against Fixed Deposit — easy approval without income proof or high CIBIL

### Cons (Bad)
- No complimentary domestic or international airport lounge access
- High 3.5% (+GST) foreign currency markup — poor for overseas spends
- Requires locking funds in a Fixed Deposit as collateral
- Check latest issuer exclusions on rent, utilities, education & wallet loads before high spends

## Frequently Asked Questions

+- OneCard is a lifetime free metal credit card suitable for beginners. It offers up to 5x rewards on selected categories. The card is offered in partnership with several providers, such as SBM Bank, South Indian Bank, Federal Bank, BOB Financial, Indian Bank, and CSB Bank.

### Does OneCard have lounge access?
+- No, OneCard does not offer any complimentary lounge access.

### How much time does OneCard take to activate?
+- You can start your card application via a completely digital onboarding process. It takes less than 5 minutes to activate the card.

### Who is eligible for OneCard?
+- Individuals, whether salaried or self-employed, aged above 18 years can apply for the OneCard.

### Is one card lifetime free?
+- Yes, the OneCard credit card is a lifetime free credit card.

### What are OneCard benefits?
+- OneCard comes with perks like zero joining and annual fee, a low forex markup fee of 1%, and up to 5x rewards on everyday spending.

### How much FD is required for One Credit Card?
+- SBM Bank, in collaboration with One Card, offers a secured variant called SBM Bank OneCard Lite Credit Card, which requires a fixed deposit (FD) as collateral. The minimum FD amount required is not officially disclosed by the bank, but once approved, the credit limit can be up to 110% of the FD amount (Max. Rs. 1.10 Lakh).

### Which bank offers One Credit Card?
