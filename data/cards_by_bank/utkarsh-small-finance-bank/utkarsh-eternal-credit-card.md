## About the Card
Utkarsh Eternal Credit Card is available in both secured and unsecured variants. Cardholders who are not eligible for the unsecured card can apply for the secured version by opening a fixed deposit of Rs. 25,000. The card offers 1.25% cashback on eligible spends and charges a forex markup fee of 3% on international transactions. It also offers fuel surcharge benefits that can help cardholders save on fuel costs over time. However, aside from these features, the card offers few additional benefits or lifestyle perks. Overall, it is a basic credit card suitable for those looking to start their credit journey.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does the card offer cashback on all transactions?
The card offers 1.25% cashback on eligible spends. However, not all transactions may qualify for cashback. Categories such as fuel purchases, cash withdrawals, balance transfers, or other excluded transactions may not earn rewards.

### Is the Utkarsh Eternal Credit Card suitable for international usage?
The card can be used for international transactions and carries a forex markup fee of 3% on foreign currency spends.

### How does the fuel surcharge waiver benefit work?
Cardholders can get a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000. The maximum benefit is capped at Rs. 400. Fuel purchases below or above the eligible transaction range may not qualify for the surcharge waiver benefit.
