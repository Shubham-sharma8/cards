## About the Card
Utkarsh Rhodium Credit Card comes in both secured and unsecured variants backed by an FD amount of Rs. 25,000. The credit card offers basic cashback, travel, and fuel-related benefits. It offers 1.25% cashback on other eligible spends. Cardholders can also get up to 4 domestic airport lounge visits in a calendar year. For international usage, the card has a 3% foreign currency markup fee. On fuel purchases between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000, the card gives a 1% fuel surcharge waiver, with a maximum benefit of Rs. 400. The annual fee is Rs. 4,999 plus taxes, and it can be waived if the cardholder spends at least Rs. 5 Lakh in a card anniversary year. Overall, the card does not have much to offer, making it a suitable option for the cardholders who want to avail a basic credit card to start their credit journey.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is the Utkarsh Rhodium Credit Card available without a fixed deposit?
The card may be offered in both secured and unsecured variants. In the secured version, an FD of Rs. 25,000 is required and acts as collateral for the credit limit. Eligibility for the unsecured variant depends on the bank’s assessment of the applicant’s profile and creditworthiness.

### How does the 1.25% cashback benefit work?
Cardholders can earn 1.25% cashback on eligible spends made using the card. However, certain transactions, such as fuel purchases, cash withdrawals, EMI conversions, or other excluded categories, may not qualify. It is advisable to review the card’s terms and conditions to understand eligible cashback categories.

### Can I use this card for international transactions?
Yes, the card can be used for international transactions. However, a foreign currency markup fee of 3% is charged on overseas purchases and payments made in foreign currencies.

### What are the conditions for the fuel surcharge waiver?
The card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel transactions ranging between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000. The maximum waiver that can be availed is capped at Rs. 400.

### How can I get the annual fee waived?
The annual fee can be waived if the cardholder spends at least Rs. 5 lakh during the card anniversary year. If the spending threshold is not met, the annual fee of Rs. 4,999 plus applicable taxes will be charged as per the card’s fee structure.
