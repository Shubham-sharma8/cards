## About the Card
Utkarsh Wish Credit Card is available in both secured and unsecured variants. The secured version is issued against a fixed deposit and offers a credit limit of up to 90% of the deposit amount, making it a suitable option for consumers who are looking to build or improve their credit history. The card does not charge any joining or annual fee. It offers 3% reward points on eligible UPI transactions made through the PhonePe app, including spends at selected online partner brands and offline merchants using Scan & Pay. Cardholders can also earn an additional 200 reward points on meeting a monthly spending milestone.
