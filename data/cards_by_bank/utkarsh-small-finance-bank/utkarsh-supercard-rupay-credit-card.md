## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the credit limit offered on Utkarsh SuperCard RuPay Card?
The credit limit for the Utkarsh SuperCard RuPay Credit Card is linked to the fixed deposit used as collateral. You can get up to 90% of your FD amount as the credit limit.

### What cashback benefits does the card offer?
Utkarsh SuperCard RuPay offers 1% cashback on UPI and non-UPI transactions, 2% on Flipkart, and 5% on Myntra.

### Are there any fees associated with this card?
Utkarsh SuperCard RuPay Credit Card has no joining or annual fees and is lifetime free. Standard charges include a finance charge of 3% per month on unpaid balances and late payment fees if bills are not cleared by the due date.
