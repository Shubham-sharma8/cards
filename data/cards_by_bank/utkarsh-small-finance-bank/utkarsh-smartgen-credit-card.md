## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is Utkarsh SmartGen Credit Card available as an FD-backed credit card?
Yes, Utkarsh SmartGen Credit Card is available in both secured (FD-backed) and unsecured variants. The secured version can be suitable for individuals with limited credit history or those looking to build their credit profile.

### What cashback does Utkarsh SmartGen Credit Card offer?
The card offers 1% cashback on eligible transactions. Cashback is credited as per the issuer\'s terms and conditions, subject to applicable exclusions.

### Is there any annual fee waiver benefit on this card?
Yes, the annual fee of Rs. 199 is waived if you spend Rs. 50,000 or more during the card anniversary year.

### Does the card offer any fuel benefits?
Yes, cardholders can avail a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel transactions ranging between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000.
