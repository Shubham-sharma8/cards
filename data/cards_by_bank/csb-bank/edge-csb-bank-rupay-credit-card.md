## About the Card
Edge+ CSB Bank RuPay Credit Card is a cashback-focused credit card that offers rewards across shopping, travel, and everyday spends. It offers 10% cashback on select major retail and e-commerce brands like Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Tata Cliq, Ajio, Nykaa, Croma, and Reliance Digital, with monthly caps (category cap of Rs. 1,500 and merchant cap of Rs. 500). It also offers 5% cashback on travel bookings through platforms such as MakeMyTrip, Yatra, EaseMyTrip, and Cleartrip, and 1% cashback on all other online and offline spends, capped at Rs. 1,000 per month.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the cashback on this card, and how does it work?
Edge+ CSB Bank RuPay Credit Card offers 10% cashback on select shopping brands (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Tata Cliq, Ajio, Nykaa, Croma, Reliance Digital, etc.). This is capped at Rs. 1,500 per month for the category and Rs. 500 per merchant. It also offers 5% cashback on travel bookings and 1% cashback on all other eligible spends.

### Is there any annual fee on this card?
No, the annual fee is nil. However, there is a one-time joining fee of Rs. 999 + taxes.

### What are the fuel benefits on this card?
It offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 3,000, with a maximum benefit of Rs. 100 per month.

### Are there any monthly limits on cashback earnings?
Yes. The 10% shopping cashback is capped at Rs. 1,500 per month overall, with a sub-limit of Rs. 500 per merchant. The 1% general cashback on all spends is capped at Rs. 1,000 per month. The 5% travel cashback follows the same eligible partner platforms but does not have a separate high-value unlimited structure.

### Do offline spends also earn rewards on this card?
Yes, the card offers 1% cashback on both online and offline transactions, as long as the spend is eligible.
