## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Simple LTF co-branded fintech RuPay card from CSB Bank & Jupiter.

**Best For:** Jupiter app users wanting an LTF RuPay UPI credit card with 2% rewards on one primary spend category

**Not Ideal For:** Users wanting high flat cashback (check Edge+ CSB Bank or Kiwi Yes Bank)

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 8,000 – Rs. 25,000

### Pros (Good)
- Instant Jewels redemption into cash or digital gold in the Jupiter app
- Flexible 2% category selector (Shopping / Travel / Dining)

### Cons (Bad)
- Lower 0.4% base rate outside chosen 2% category (Edge+ offers higher 10%/5% brand rates)
- No airport lounge access
