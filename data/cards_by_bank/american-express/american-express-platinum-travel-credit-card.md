## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Unbeatable Rs. 4 Lakh/yr milestone card — park all jewelry/insurance/tax/general spends to hit Rs. 4L cleanly, then switch to another card.

**Best For:** Users with exactly ~Rs. 4,00,000/year in non-rewarding spends (tax, insurance, jewelry, education, rent) turning dead spends into 48k MR + Rs. 10k Taj voucher

**Not Ideal For:** Spenders above Rs. 4L (returns crash past milestone) or users needing wide Amex offline acceptance

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 33,333 (exactly Rs. 4L/yr sweet spot)

### Pros (Good)
- Targeted rewards structure backed by American Express
- 8 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits/year

### Cons (Bad)
- High joining/annual fee (Rs. 5,000 + GST) if spend thresholds aren't met
- High 3.5% (+GST) foreign currency markup — poor for overseas spends
- Check latest issuer exclusions on rent, utilities, education & wallet loads before high spends

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the validity of rewards points earned via American Express® Platinum Travel Credit Card?
+- American Express® Platinum Travel Credit Card lets you earn Membership Rewards Points on your transactions, which never expire. You can redeem the reward points earned via this credit card till the card is active. However, in case of any card cancellation, the reward points will get forfeited within 30 days of the cancellation.

### Can I convert my American Express® Platinum Travel Credit Card purchases into EMI?
+- Yes, you can convert your purchases of Rs. 5,000 or above into easy EMIs. For offline transactions, you can ask the merchant for the EMI option at the point of sale before making the purchase. To avail this option on online purchases, you can select the EMI option while checking out.

### Does American Express® Platinum Travel Credit Card allow contactless payment?
+- Yes, you can tap and pay through this credit card without entering any OTP. However, this option is available only on your purchases below Rs. 5,000. All you need to do is touch your card at the POS machine to make a transaction. Also, to check whether your card has this feature or not, you can look for contactless symbol at the front or back of your card.
