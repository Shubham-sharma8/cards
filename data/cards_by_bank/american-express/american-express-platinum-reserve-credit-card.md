## About the Card
Accor Plus membership has been added, which could be quite helpful for travellers. The card already offers Taj Epicure membership, providing benefits with Taj Group properties, along with Accor Plus membership, thus allowing the frequent travellers to save on their hotel stays. Moreover, both domestic and international lounge access with a Priority Pass membership further lets consumers travel in comfort. The card’s monthly milestone benefit of Rs. 1,000 enhances its overall value-back, despite the Rs. 50,000 spending requirement. For high spenders, meeting this threshold shouldn’t be a challenge, thus allowing them to earn benefits worth Rs. 12,000 annually. These monthly spends will also unlock the golf benefits for the cardholders. However, the card’s poor reward rate is a major drawback. Cardholders would be able to get good additional benefits, but the core value-back through rewards would be much lower compared to what you could get with other premium cards like HDFC Diners Club Black, ICICI Emeralde Private, or even with certain lower-fee options like Axis Atlas.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does American Express® Platinum Reserve Credit Card offer purchase protection?
Yes, being a premium credit card it offers purchase protection. You can get a refund for any item that is stolen due to burglary or damages or breaks due to fire, earthquake, flood, storm, cyclone, etc., within 60 days of purchase.

### Can I convert my American Express® Platinum Reserve Credit Card purchases into EMI?
Yes, you can convert your purchases of Rs. 5,000 or above into easy EMI. To avail this facility at the time of purchase, you can ask the merchant to convert the entire transaction into EMI.

### Can I earn reward points on fuel transactions via American Express® Platinum Reserve Credit Card?
No, you do not earn any points on fuel spends. However, you can avail fuel convenience fee waiver on your fuel expenses at HPCL petrol pumps. You can get a 0% and 1% convenience fee on your fuel transactions of up to Rs. 5,000 and above Rs. 5,000, respectively.

### How can I redeem the reward points earned via American Express® Platinum Reserve Credit Card?
You can redeem your Membership Rewards Points earned via this credit card against a variety of categories, such as travel, dining, accessories and more by logging in to your rewards account. Apart from this, you can also use your earned reward points to pay for your card charges, which is an added advantage.

### Does American Express® Platinum Reserve Credit Card offer lost card liability?
Yes, you can claim the lost card liability by reporting the loss or fraud within 3 working days to the American Express customer care. If the fraud is reported beyond 3 working days, then you will get a maximum liability of Rs. 1,000. Therefore, it is important to report the fraud as soon as possible to make the most out of this benefit.

### How can I apply for American Express® Platinum Reserve Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online through the official website of American Express. All you need to do is look for the required card as per its eligibility and fill out the application form. You are also required to submit relevant documents for verification. The bank will then verify your details and process your request.
