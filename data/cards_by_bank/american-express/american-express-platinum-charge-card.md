## About the Card
Lifestyle and luxury-travel status card rather than a points-earning workhorse — worth it only if you extract >Rs. 80k/yr from FHR, lounges, and hotel statuses.

## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Lifestyle and luxury-travel status card rather than a points-earning workhorse — worth it only if you extract >Rs. 80k/yr from FHR, lounges, and hotel statuses.

**Best For:** Super-Premium & domestic retail cardholders

**Not Ideal For:** Everyday reward maximizers or domestic-only spenders seeking fee waivers

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 1,50,000+

### Pros (Good)
- Unmatched hotel elite status bundle (Marriott Gold, Hilton Gold, Taj Epicure) & FHR $100 hotel credits
- Best global lounge access in India (Centurion, Delta, Lufthansa, Priority Pass)
- Metal prestige & world-class Platinum Concierge service

### Cons (Bad)
- Steep Rs. 66,000 + GST (Rs. 77,880/yr) fee with NO spend-based waiver
- Base reward rate (~1.25%) lags Infinia/EPM/Magnus Burgundy; high 3.5% forex fee
- Lower domestic offline merchant acceptance than Visa/Mastercard
