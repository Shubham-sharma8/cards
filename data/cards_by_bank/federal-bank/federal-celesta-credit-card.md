## About the Card
Federal Celesta Credit Card is a lifetime free card primarily focused on rewards and travel. Cardholders can earn **3X reward points on travel and international spends**. In addition, the card offers other travel benefits, including a **low forex markup fee of 2%** and complimentary domestic & international lounge visits, enhancing its overall value for frequent travellers. Besides travel, the card offers **2X rewards on dining** expenses and a base reward rate on other spends. With no joining fee, it also offers an **Amazon Pay e-gift voucher worth Rs. 600** as a welcome benefit, which adds to the card’s value. Overall, Federal Bank Celesta Credit Card turns out to be an **all-rounder credit card** with benefits across multiple categories such as travel, dining, shopping, entertainment, and more. It is a suitable option for consumers looking for a basic rewards credit card with all-round benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for Federal Celesta Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online through the official website of Federal Bank by submitting a duly filled application form along with the relevant documents. If you wish to apply offline, you can visit any of the nearest branches of the bank and submit the application form in person.

### Does Federal Celesta Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, this credit card offers 2 complimentary international lounge visits every year and 2 complimentary domestic lounge visits per quarter on spending Rs. 60,000 in the previous quarter.

### What is the difference between Federal Visa & Mastercard Celesta Credit Card?
Both Federal Visa and Mastercard variants offer the same benefits across travel, dining, shopping, movies, and more. However, with the Visa variant, you can avail Visa-exclusive deals and offers, whereas the Mastercard variant offers exclusive cashback offers for Mastercard holders.

### What is the annual fee for Federal Celesta Credit Card?
Federal Bank Celesta is a lifetime free credit card with no joining or annual fee. However, this is a limited-period offer, and you are advised to visit the official website for the latest updates.

### Who is eligible for Federal Bank Celesta Credit Card?
To apply for Federal Bank Visa Celesta Credit Card, your age must be between 18 and 70 years. Both salaried and self-employed individuals are eligible to apply. For detailed eligibility criteria, you can contact Federal Bank’s customer service.

### What is the limit of Federal Bank Celesta Credit Card?
The bank determines the credit limit based on several factors, such as your credit score, repayment history, monthly income, and more. You will be informed of your assigned credit limit once your application is processed and approved.
