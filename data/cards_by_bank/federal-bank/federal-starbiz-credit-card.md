## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is Federal StarBiz Credit Card?
Federal StarBiz Credit Card is a business credit card offered to Federal Bank customers who have an Overdraft (OD) or Cash Credit (CC) account with the bank. The card is linked to the existing OD/CC account instead of offering a separate credit limit.

### Who is eligible for Federal StarBiz Credit Card?
Applicants must have a Federal Bank Overdraft (OD) or Cash Credit (CC) account with a sanctioned limit between Rs. 3 Lakh and Rs. 50 Lakh to be eligible for this card.

### Does Federal StarBiz Credit Card have a separate credit limit?
No. The card uses the available limit of the linked Overdraft or Cash Credit account. It does not provide a separate credit limit like a regular business credit card.

### What types of transactions can I make using Federal StarBiz Credit Card?
The card can be used for POS, online (e-commerce), and contactless (Tap & Pay) transactions at merchants accepting RuPay and Visa cards.

### What is the daily transaction limit on Federal StarBiz Credit Card?
Cardholders can make transactions of up to Rs. 3 Lakh per day or up to the available limit in the linked OD/CC account, whichever is lower.

### What happens if the sanctioned limit on the linked OD/CC account exceeds Rs. 50 Lakh?
If the sanctioned limit on the linked Overdraft or Cash Credit account exceeds Rs. 50 Lakh, Federal StarBiz Credit Card linked to the account will be automatically cancelled by the bank after notifying the cardholder.
