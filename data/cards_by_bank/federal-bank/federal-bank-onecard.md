## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Lifetime Free option from Federal Bank suited for travel & miles users.

**Best For:** Domestic & international flyers value-optimizing airline/hotel loyalty points and lounge access

**Not Ideal For:** Low-travel users preferring straight monthly statement cashback

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 25,000+

### Pros (Good)
- Lifetime Free card with zero annual fee commitment
- Backed by Federal Bank digital card controls, EMI conversion & merchant offers

### Cons (Bad)
- No complimentary domestic or international airport lounge access
- High 3.5% (+GST) foreign currency markup — poor for overseas spends
- Check latest issuer exclusions on rent, utilities, education & wallet loads before high spends
