## Frequently Asked Questions
### What rewards does the Federal Signet Credit Card offer?
The card offers 3x rewards on electronics and apparel, 2x on entertainment spends, and 1x rewards on all other transactions, making it suitable for everyday spending categories.

### How can I get airport lounge access with this card?
Cardholders get 4 domestic lounge visits per year, with one visit per quarter available on spending at least Rs. 20,000 in the previous quarter.

### Are there any movie benefits on this card?
Yes, the card offers a buy-one-get-one free ticket on INOX movies once every quarter, helping cosnumers save on entertainment expenses.

### Does the card have any annual or joining fees?
No, the Federal Signet Credit Card is a lifetime free card with zero joining fee and no annual maintenance charges.

### What are the milestone and welcome benefits?
You get an Amazon Pay voucher worth Rs. 200 on spending Rs. 3,000 in the first month and a Swiggy voucher on quarterly spends of Rs. 20,000.
