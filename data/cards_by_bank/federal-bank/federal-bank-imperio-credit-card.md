## About the Card
In addition to rewards, the card offers useful lifestyle benefits such as **2 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits** per quarter (up to 8 per year) on spending Rs. 40,000 in the previous quarter, a **welcome Amazon Pay e-voucher worth Rs. 400** on spending Rs. 5,000 in the first month, and a buy-one-get-one INOX movie ticket offer. Cardholders also get a 1% fuel surcharge waiver across fuel stations, making it a well-rounded and decent choice for regular, everyday use.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What rewards can I earn on a Federal Imperio Credit Card?
You can earn 3X reward points on healthcare and grocery spends, 2X on utility bill payments, and 1X on all other transactions, making it suitable for everyday spending categories.

### Does this card offer airport lounge access?
Yes, the card offers 2 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per quarter, totaling up to 8 annually, subject to a minimum spend of Rs, 40,000 in the previous quarter.

### What is the welcome benefit on this credit card?
Cardholders receive an Amazon Pay e-voucher worth Rs. 400 upon spending Rs. 5,000 within the first month, providing a simple entry-level benefit for new users.

### Are there any movie-related benefits available?
Yes, you get a buy-one-get-one movie ticket offer on INOX bookings once every quarter, helping save on entertainment expenses with a capped discount benefit.

### What are the fees and charges for this card?
The card has no joining or annual fee, finance charges of 1.99% per month, and late payment fees ranging from Rs. 0 to Rs. 1,250 based on outstanding balance slabs.
