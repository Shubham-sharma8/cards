## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- 0.5% CashPoints on eligible UPI transactions above Rs. 2,000, except grocery, fuel and travel category spends
- Fee Waiver: Annual fee waived on reaching Rs. 1.5 lakh annual spends
- Fuel Surcharge Waiver: Fuel surcharge waiver up to Rs. 100 per month
- CashPoints on UPI spends only available on transactions above Rs. 2,000

### Dining Benefits
- 7.5% CashPoints on all eligible grocery spends , including spends made on popular platforms like Reliance Fresh, DMart, LuLu Hypermarket, Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, BigBasket and Flipkart Minutes

### Get This Card If
- You want a card to maximise your savings on grocery spends, including those made across leading platforms
- You prefer a credit card that offers cashback, instead of reward points, with a hassle-free redemption process
- You are looking for a card with low annual fee and easily achievable fee waiver condition
- You want a RuPay credit card to save on your UPI transactions
