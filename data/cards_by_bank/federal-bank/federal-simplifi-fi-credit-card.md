## Frequently Asked Questions
### What type of card is the SimpliFi Fi Credit Card?
SimpliFi Fi is a secured, entry-level credit card backed by a Rs. 15,000 fixed deposit. Ideal for first-time consumers who wants to start their credit journey.

### What rewards does the SimpliFi Fi Credit Card offer?
The card offers 3X rewards on weekend spends, 1X rewards on weekday spends, i.e., 2 Fi-coins per Rs. 100 spent on weekdays.

### What extra benefits does the SimpliFi Fi Credit Card provide?
Cardholders can avail up to 20% off at partner brands such as Zomato, District by Zomato, and BookMyShow on weekends, offering additional savings on dining, entertainment, and lifestyle purchases.

### Who should consider the SimpliFi Fi Credit Card?
This card is ideal for weekend spenders and partner-brand shoppers. It is also suitable for first-time consumers seeking a straightforward, no-fee rewards card focused with basic benefits.

### What are the limitations of SimpliFi Fi Credit Card?
SimpliFi Fi Credit Card offers only modest rewards on weekday spends. While it offers better value on partner brands, these offers are restricted to weekends. Also, this limits reward potential for consumers who spend primarily on weekdays or outside partner merchants.
