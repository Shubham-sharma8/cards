**Summary:** Lifetime Free option from Bank of India suited for rupay & upi users.

**Best For:** Everyday UPI Scan & Pay spenders wanting credit-line convenience on merchant QRs

**Not Ideal For:** Travel flyers seeking high-value airline/hotel miles

- Lifetime Free card with zero annual fee commitment

### Cons (Bad)

- Check latest issuer exclusions on rent, utilities, education & wallet loads before high spends
