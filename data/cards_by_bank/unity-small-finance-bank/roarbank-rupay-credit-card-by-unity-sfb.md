## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is a Roarbank RuPay Credit Card?
Roarbank RuPay Credit Card is a lifetime free credit card offered by Roarbank in collaboration with UNITY Small Finance Bank. It is designed for beginners and offers cashback along with a 2-in-1 spending feature.

### How does the cashback feature work on this credit card?
Cardholders can earn up to 20% cashback every month on any two selected categories such as grocery, fuel, dining, or movies. Categories must be chosen at the start of the month and cannot be changed mid-cycle. The maximum cashback is capped at Rs. 2,500 per month.

### What does the 2-in-1 feature of Roarbank RuPay Credit Card mean?
This 2-in-1 card allows spending through a combination of the savings account balance and the credit limit. Transactions are first deducted from the linked savings account. Once the balance is exhausted, the remaining amount is charged to the credit line.

### What is the maximum credit limit offered on Roarbank RuPay Credit Card?
The card offers a credit limit of up to Rs. 3 Lakh, depending on the eligibility and banking relationship of the applicant. The combined spending power includes both the savings account balance and the assigned credit limit.

### Who is eligible to apply for Roarbank RuPay Credit Card?
Applicants must be Indian residents and should have an active savings account with Roarbank. Since it is an entry-level credit card, it is suitable for new-to-credit consumers. However, the final approval is subject to internal assessment by the bank.

### Can I use this Roarbank RuPay credit card internationally?
Yes, you can use Roarbank credit card by Unity Small Finance Bank for international transactions. But a forex mark-up fee of 3.5% of the transaction amount would also be charged on every such spend.

### When can I withdraw the accumulated cashback using Roarbank credit card to my main account?
The cashback accrued on your Roarbank RuPay credit card spends is tracked within the Roarbank app. You can redeem this cashback by selecting the Withdraw Cashback option under the Cashback menu. The selected cashback amount will automatically transfer to the Roarbank Card account.

### What is the maximum amount of cashback you can accumulate in a month using Roarbank RuPay credit card by Unity SFB?
You can accrue a cashback worth up to Rs. 2,500 every month using your Roarbank credit card.

### What is the minimum spend to convert into EMI for Roarbank RuPay credit cardholders?
Roarbank RuPay credit card by Unity SFB offers the option to convert transactions worth Rs. 2,000 and above into EMIs.
