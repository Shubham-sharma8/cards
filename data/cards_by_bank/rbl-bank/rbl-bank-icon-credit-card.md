## About the Card
RBL Bank Icon Credit Card aims to provide appealing lifestyle benefits where cardholders can avail benefits on varied spending categories. Card users get a ‘buy one, get one’ movie offer, with up to two complimentary tickets each month. For golf enthusiasts, the card offers 12 free golf lessons annually, including complimentary green fees. Furthermore, domestic air travelers benefit from free access to airport lounges. Concierge services are also included among the card benefits for enhanced convenience, assisting with tasks like reservations and access to exclusive events.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for RBL Bank ICON Credit Card?
You can apply for RBL Bank ICON Credit Card online through the official RBL Bank website. You’ll need to fill in your details, and submit the complete application form.

### What are the pros & cons of RBL ICON Credit Card?
RBL Bank ICON Credit Card offers premium lifestyle benefits such as complimentary airport lounge access, golf privileges, and decent rewards on international and weekends dining spends. However, it comes with a high annual fee and has relatively limited reward redemption options compared to some competing cards.

### What is the joining fee for RBL Bank ICON Credit Card?
The joining fee for RBL Bank ICON Credit Card is Rs. 5,000 plus applicable taxes.

### What are the key benefits of RBL ICON Credit Card?
You get complimentary airport lounge access, golf sessions, movie benefits, milestone rewards, and accelerated points on international, travel, and dining spends.

### Does RBL ICON Credit Card offer airport lounge access?
Yes, the card offers up to 8 domestic airport lounge visits in a year, capped at 2 per quarter.

### What reward points can I earn with RBL Bank Icon Credit Card?
You earn up to 20 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on international and weekend dining, and 2 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on other categories.

### What is the interest rate on RBL ICON Credit Card?
The interest rate is typically around 3.99% per month (47.88% annually), depending on your card usage and payment history.

### Is RBL ICON Credit Card suitable for international travel?
Yes, it’s suitable for international travellers as it offers high reward points on overseas spends. However, do keep a note that it does not offer any international lounge visits.

### Who is eligible to apply for RBL Bank ICON Credit Card?
Salaried or self-employed individuals aged 21 to 60 years with a stable income and good credit history are eligible to apply.
