## About the Card
The RBL Bank Play Credit Card is a co-branded credit card with BookMyShow that **largely caters to people who frequently attend events, visit theaters, or participate in activities through BookMyShow**. However, unlike most credit cards that offer accelerated rewards on the partner brands, this card offers free tickets as a milestone spend. The card allows spending on all day-to-day transactions and offers **two free BookMyShow tickets for every Rs. 5,000**. That way, it emerges as a good choice for beginners who want to own a single credit card and are satisfied redeeming the benefits across their monthly entertainment spending.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How to get free movie tickets with an RBL credit card?
To get free movie tickets with the RBL Bank Play credit card, you need to spend Rs. 5,000 every month with the card.

### How to activate the RBL Play credit card?
You can activate your RBL Bank Play credit card in multiple ways. To activate via phone banking, you can give a missed call at 8744810111. You can also download the RBL MyCard App or set up activation through the website.

### What is the benefit of the RBL BookMyShow credit card?
RBL Bank Play credit card offers co-branded benefits with BookMyShow. Cardholders can avail two free tickets subject to spending Rs. 5,000 in a month and an additional discount on food & beverages orders.

### How can I apply for an RBL Bank Play Credit Card?
You can apply for RBL Bank Play Credit Card, at the BookMyShow app/website. Follow these steps for application, Click on Apply >> Verify your PAN >> Enter required details >> Proceed for Online KYC.

### How do I avail the welcome bonus and monthly discount offered via this card?
You can avail the welcome bonus and monthly discount offered with the BookMyShow Play Card, at the time of checkout or final payment. Here, navigate to”RBL Bank Play Credit Card Welcome Offer” under option, “Unlock offers and Promo codes”.

### Does this card offer reward points on all purchases?
No, the card offers benefits only around BookMyShow, but not on all purchases. If you frequently use BookMyShow, you can save your entertainment expenses with this card.

### Is the BookMyShow play card free?
RBL Bank BookMyShow Play Credit Card comes with an annual fee of Rs. 500, which is waived off on annual spends of Rs. 1.5 Lakh in the previous year.

### What is the interest rate for the RBL play card?
The applicable finance charges on RBL Bank Play Credit Card are up to 3.99% per month which sums up to 47.88% per annum.
