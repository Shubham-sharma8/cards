## About the Card
RBL Bank UPI Wala Credit Card is a **RuPay Select card** variant for **UPI and online** **transactions**. It comes at a low annual fee of just **Rs. 99** and is currently available only to select existing RBL credit cardholders. Its core value-back is offered in the form of its reward points, where the cardholders can earn **1 reward point for every Rs. 200 spent**. The earned reward points can later be redeemed online by logging into RBL Rewards platform. This rewards program is accompanied by a **welcome benefit in the form of cashback**.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the eligibility criteria for RBL Bank UPI Credit Card?
To be eligible for RBL Bank UPI Wala Credit Card, you must be an existing RBL credit cardholder. Further eligibility is decided as per the internal policies of the bank.

### Is RBL Bank UPI Wala Credit Card free?
No, RBL Bank UPI Wala Credit Card comes at an annual fee of Rs. 99+GST.

### Do reward points earned using RBL Bank UPI Wala Credit Card expire?
Yes, reward points earned using RBL Bank UPI Wala Credit Card are only valid up to 24 months.

### How can I redeem the reward points earned through RBL Bank UPI Wala Credit Card?
You can redeem the reward points you earn using RBL Bank UPI Wala Credit Card by logging into RBL Rewards portal and use them for shopping, e-vouchers, travel bookings, bill payments, recharges and even charity.

### Can I make offline payments using RBL Bank UPI Wala Credit Card?
You can make offline purchases using your RBL Bank UPI Wala Credit Card by opting for UPI payment mode.
