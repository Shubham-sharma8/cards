## About the Card
RBL World Safari Lite Credit Card is an entry-level travel-focused credit card designed for consumers who want basic travel benefits. It offers features such as zero foreign exchange markup on international transactions, making it suitable for overseas spending. Cardholders can earn reward points on travel-related expenses, including 5x rewards on flight and hotel bookings made through the bank’s rewards portal. Apart from this, the card also offers 4 airport lounge access yearly, both domestic and international. With a joining and annual fee of Rs. 1,000 each, along with milestone-based reward bonuses and modest redemption value, it is a suitable choice for consumers who often travel and seek basic travel perks without a high joining and annual fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the RBL World Safari Lite Credit Card?
RBL World Safari Lite Credit Card is an entry-level travel card offering zero foreign exchange markup, limited airport lounge access, and reward points on flight and hotel bookings, suitable for occasional travelers.

### What travel benefits does this card offer?
Cardholders receive one complimentary domestic and international airport lounge visit per quarter with a minimum spend of Rs. 50,000, along with zero foreign exchange fees on overseas transactions.

### How does the rewards program work?
You earn 1 Reward Point per Rs. 100 spent on eligible domestic travel and 5 Reward Points per Rs. 100 on flights and hotel bookings via the RBL Bank Rewards Portal.

### Are there milestones or welcome benefits?
The card provides a welcome gift voucher worth Rs. 1,000, milestone rewards of 5,000 points on Rs. 2.5 lakh yearly spend, and 10,000 points on Rs. 5 lakh yearly spend.

### What fees and charges are associated with the card?
The card has a joining and annual fee of Rs. 1,000 plus taxes, finance charges up to 3.99% monthly, and late payment fees between Rs. 5 and Rs. 1,300 depending on outstanding dues.
