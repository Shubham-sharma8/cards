## About the Card
RBL Bank Shoprite Credit Card is an entry-level card with a low annual fee of Rs. 500. It covers some everyday categories, like groceries, movies and shopping in its reward program, making it a suitable choice for beginners looking for multi-category benefits. Card users can save up to 5% on grocery shopping and 10% on BookMyShow movie tickets multiple times a year. Thus, a good choice if you majorly spend on these categories. However, due to a low overall reward rate, it might not be a suitable choice for people who like spending beyond the accelerated categories and are looking for a high-reward rate card for all-round benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the benefits of RBL Shoprite credit card?
RBL Shoprite credit card offers up to 20 reward points on every Rs. 100 spent, along with up to 10% off on BookMyShow movie tickets, 1% fuel surcharge waiver and spend-based annual fee waiver. You can also avail a joining bonus of 2,000 reward points using this card.

### Is RBL Shoprite credit card lifetime free?
No, RBL Shoprite credit card is not lifetime free. It charges an annual fee of Rs. 500.

### Is Shoprite credit card Rupay or Visa?
RBL Shoprite credit card is available in RuPay, Mastercard as well as Visa network variants.

### Does the RBL Shoprite credit card offers lounge access?
No, RBL Shoprite credit card does not offer any complimentary airport lounge access to the cardholders.

### Is RBL Shoprite credit card good or bad?
RBL Shoprite credit card is a good option for those looking for a low annual fee rewards credit card with accelerated savings on grocery spends and movie discount offers. However, if you wish to get a card with benefits inclined towards other spending categories like travel, dining, etc. then this one might not be the right choice for you.
