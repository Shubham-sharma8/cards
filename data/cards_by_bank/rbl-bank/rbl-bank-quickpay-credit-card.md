## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the RBL QuickPay Credit Card?
It’s an entry-level credit card with a low annual fee, designed for individuals new to credit. Offers basic rewards and limited welcome benefits for simple credit-building.

### What rewards and cashback does it offer?
Consumers can earn 1 reward point per Rs. 100 spent on eligible transactions. Get 20% cashback on your first two UPI transactions within 15 days, capped at Rs. 50.

### What are the fees and charges?
No joining fee. Annual fee is Rs. 99 + taxes. Finance charges: 3.99% p.m. Late payment fee ranges from Rs. 5 to Rs. 1,300.

### Who should apply for this card?
The card is ideal for credit beginners seeking an entry-level card with low annual fees, basic reward points, and welcome benefits. Not suitable for those wanting travel or lifestyle perks.

### Are there any restrictions on rewards?
Rewards exclude fuel, utilities, insurance, railways, real estate/rent, wallet services, and miscellaneous spends. Cashback benefits apply only to first two UPI transactions within 15 days.
