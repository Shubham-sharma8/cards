## Frequently Asked Questions
### What benefits can I get through Club Marriott annual membership on RBL Lumiere credit card?
Club Marriott membership includes up to 25% off on food and beverages for up to 10 guests at hotels in India and 20% at hotels in Asia Pacific, 20% off on the best available rate for rooms, 20% off on spa services, 15% off on “Marriott Bonvoy On Wheels” orders and complimentary add-on spouse membership for Club Marriott members.

### What are the benefits under ITC Culinaire annual membership with RBL Bank Lumiere credit card?
ITC Culinaire membership includes room upgrade to the next category for up to 3 nights, 2nd night free stay, including buffet breakfast in a standard room on BAR, up to 50% savings on food and beverage at all-day dining restaurants for up to 4 guests, 50% off on spa therapies, 20% savings on dining, up to 3% earnings of Green Points on eligible food and beverage and stays, 20% savings on laundry and spa, and 2X Green Points during birthday week

### How to transfer RBL Bank Lumiere credit card reward points to travel partner points/miles?
For points-to-miles transfer of RBL Lumiere credit card reward points, first log in to lumiere.rbl.bank.in and go to Points Transfer section. Then select your preferred hotel or airline partner and enter the number of reward points you wish to transfer. Next, submit your request by entering the OTP sent to your registered mobile number or email address.

### Can I redeem my reward points earned using RBL Bank Lumiere credit card as cashback?
No, you cannot redeem the accrued reward points through RBL Lumiere credit card against statement credit as cashback.

### Can I get a fee waiver on RBL Bank Lumiere Credit Card?
RBL Lumiere does not come with a fee waiver benefit, however some cardholders may be offered annual fee waiver based on their TRV, i.e. Total Relationship Value, with the bank.
