## About the Card
RBL World Safari Credit Card is a travel-focused card designed to offer benefits for both frequent domestic and international travellers. With **zero forex markup fee** and complimentary **lounge access worldwide** , this card is a suitable option for frequent international travellers. Besides this, cardholders can also earn **5 Reward Points on every Rs. 100 spent** on eligible retail travel spends. Apart from travel, the card offers a base reward rate of **2 points per Rs. 100 on other spends**. When it comes to spend-based benefits, this card also offers a **complimentary voucher of Rs. 10,000** from brands like MakeMyTrip, Taj Experiences and more as milestone benefit, which further enhance its value for frequent travellers. The card also offers a **MakeMyTrip voucher worth Rs. 3,000** at the time of joining, which offsets the joining fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the insurance and security benefits of RBL World Safari Credit Card?
RBL World Safari Credit Card offers complimentary travel insurance, including coverage for lost baggage, delayed flights, and passport loss. It also includes zero liability protection against fraudulent transactions in case the card is lost or stolen, ensuring peace of mind while travelling.

### What are the main travel benefits of RBL World Safari Credit Card?
This card is designed for frequent travellers and offers zero forex markup on international transactions, complimentary airport lounge access, and exclusive travel insurance. It also offers accelerated (5 points per Rs. 100) on travel spends.

### Is the fuel surcharge waiver applicable at selected fuel stations when using RBL Bank World Safari Credit Card?
No, the fuel surcharge waiver is applicable at all fuel stations on spends between Rs. 500 and Rs. 4,000, with a maximum waiver of Rs. 250 per month.

### Does RBL World Safari Credit Card offer free airport lounge access?
Yes, the card offers 8 complimentary domestic lounge visits every year (Max. 2 per quarter) on spending Rs. 35,000 in the previous quarter. It also offers complimentary Priority Pass Membership with 2 complimentary international lounge visits every year.

### Is there any foreign exchange markup fee on RBL World Safari Credit Card?
No, RBL World Safari Credit Card comes with zero foreign exchange markup fee, making it an excellent choice for international travellers since you can spend abroad without additional conversion charges.

### Are any additional charges applied on visiting airport lounges with RBL Bank World Safari Credit Card?
Once the complimentary lounge visits are exhausted, cardholders must pay $27 per visit for any additional lounge access.
