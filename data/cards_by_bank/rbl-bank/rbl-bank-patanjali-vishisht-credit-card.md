## About the Card
RBL Bank offers two co-branded credit cards in collaboration with Patanjali where RBL Patanjali Vishisht is the base variant and RBL Patanjali Swarn credit card is the premium variant. Both these cards offer accelerated value-back on co-branded spends as cashback and milestone benefits.

## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** option from RBL Bank suited for travel & miles users.

**Best For:** Domestic & international flyers value-optimizing airline/hotel loyalty points and lounge access

**Not Ideal For:** Low-travel users preferring straight monthly statement cashback

## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance
- **Welcome Benefit:** Rewards as joining bonus as compensation for the joining fee

### Milestone Benefits
- 2,000 reward points on making the 1st spend of min. Rs. 500 within 30 days from card issuance
- Rs. 500 Patanjali voucher on Rs. 2.5 lakh annual spends, subject to min. Rs. 12,000 spends on Patanjali website/store
- 2 nights/3 days Patanjali Wellness Centre stay on Rs. 4 lakh annual spends, subject to min. Rs. 15,000 spends on Patanjali website/store
- Annual fee waived off on spending a min. of Rs. 1.5 lakh in the previous year

### Get This Card If
- You are a loyal customer of Patanjali and use their products for your everyday personal and household needs
- You have another credit card to get a good value-back on your other lifestyle expenses, including apparel, travel, entertainment, dining, etc.
- You can maximise your savings by availing card benefits like milestone benefits and fee waiver by meeting the required spending conditions
