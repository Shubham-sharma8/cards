## About the Card
RBL Bank Platinum Maxima Plus Credit Card is a rewards card which also offers benefits on movies and domestic travel. You can save 2.5% in the form of rewards on spending categories like grocery, entertainment, dining and international transactions. However, all other card spends only earn a base value-back of 0.50%. The milestone bonus lets you maximise your savings through this card by offering up to 30,000 reward points on reaching annual spending threshold of Rs. 5 lakh which is achievable if you put most of your spends on this card.

## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance
- **Welcome Benefit:** Welcome benefit worth the card joining fee

### Milestone Benefits
- You can redeem the earned reward points against hotel stays, air travel and shopping vouchers available on the RBL Rewards platform
- 10,000 reward points on reaching annual spends of Rs. 2 lakh
- Additional 10,000 reward points each on reaching annual spends of Rs. 3.5 lakh and Rs. 5 lakh respectively

### Dining Benefits
- 5X Rewards: 10 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on grocery, dining, entertainment and international transactions

### Movie / Entertainment
- Buy 1 and get 1 free ticket worth up to Rs. 200 on BookMyShow, once a month
- Annual savings of up to Rs. 2,400 on movie tickets

### Get This Card If
- You want a rewards credit card and are not looking for any substantial travel benefits
- You frequently go out to watch movies and want a card with discount offers on movie tickets
- You can maximise your savings by meeting the annual spending conditions of up to Rs. 5 lakh to avail the milestone benefits
