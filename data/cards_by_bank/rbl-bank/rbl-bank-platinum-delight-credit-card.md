## About the Card
RBL Bank Platinum Delight Credit Card is an entry-level credit card offering value-back through reward points on every spend. Cardholders can earn **2X reward points on weekend spends** and a base reward rate of **2** **reward points per Rs. 100 spent on weekdays**. The accumulated reward points can be redeemed through the rewards portal against multiple categories, including travel bookings, recharges, e-vouchers, and the purchase of products & items. Apart from this, the card also offers **1,000 reward points as milestone benefit** and **4,000 reward points as welcome benefit** , which add to the card’s overall value. This card is a suitable option for consumers seeking a **card with basic reward benefits**.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for RBL Platinum Delight Credit Card?
You can apply for this card online by visiting the official website of RBL Bank and submitting a duly filled application form. Besides this, you can also apply offline by visiting any of the nearest branches of the bank and submitting the application form along with the relevant documents in person.

### Can I earn rewards on fuel spends through RBL Platinum Delight Credit Card?
No, this credit card does not offer reward points on select categories, including fuel, utilities, insurance, rent/real estate, railways, wallet loading, government services, and EMI conversion.

### How can I redeem earned reward points on RBL Platinum Delight Credit Card?
You can redeem the accumulated reward points through the rewards portal against multiple categories, including travel bookings, recharges, e-vouchers, and the purchase of products and items.

### Does RBL Platinum Delight Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
No, RBL Bank Platinum Delight Credit Card does not offer any lounge access benefits. However, you can save on your travel spends by earning up to 4 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on weekends.

### Does RBL Platinum Delight Credit Card offer renewal benefits?
No, this credit card does not offer any renewal benefits. However, you can avail an annual fee waiver on spending Rs. 2 Lakh in a year.
