## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** option from RBL Bank suited for travel & miles users.

**Best For:** Domestic & international flyers value-optimizing airline/hotel loyalty points and lounge access

**Not Ideal For:** Low-travel users preferring straight monthly statement cashback

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 25,000+

### Pros (Good)
- 5X rewards on train ticket bookings using IRCTC website/app with 1% transaction fee waiver
- Up to 8 complimentary railway lounge visits per year
- Accelerated rewards on online FASTag recharges
- Rewards redemption ratio of 1:1
- Up to 2,000 reward points as milestone bonus

### Cons (Bad)
- Rewards redemption is only limited to IRCTC bookings
- Low base reward rate of just 0.5% on most of the everyday spends, including major categories like shopping, dining, entertainment, etc.
- Capping on accelerated rewards

## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance
- **Welcome Benefit:** Welcome Benefit: 500 reward points on making a spend worth above Rs. 500 within 30 days of card issuance

### Milestone Benefits
- 1X Reward Points: 1 reward point per Rs. 200 spent on other spends
- Welcome Benefit: 500 reward points on making a spend worth above Rs. 500 within 30 days of card issuance
- Milestone Benefits: 500 reward points on reaching quarterly spends of Rs. 75,000
- Transaction Fee Waiver: Reversal of transaction charges of 1%, up to Rs. 200 per month, on train ticket bookings of up to Rs. 5,000

### Dining Benefits
- Low base reward rate of just 0.5% on most of the everyday spends, including major categories like shopping, dining, entertainment, etc.

### Movie / Entertainment
- 5X Reward Points: On train ticket bookings made on IRCTC website or mobile app, up to 1,000 reward points per month
- Redemption Option: Train ticket bookings using ‘Pay by Points’ functionality on IRCTC app or website, or against products available on the RBL Rewards platform
- Transaction Fee Waiver: Reversal of transaction charges of 1%, up to Rs. 200 per month, on train ticket bookings of up to Rs. 5,000

### Insurance Cover
- Train Cancellation Shield: Cancellation protection cover worth up to Rs. 5,000 on trains cancelled by India Railways within 24 hours of the scheduled train time

### Get This Card If
- You frequently travel using train and prefer IRCTC website or app to make your ticket bookings
- You are comfortable with redeeming all your accrued reward points against IRCTC bookings
- You want a decent value-back on FASTag recharges, but do not mind low savings on all other spends, like shopping, dining, movies, groceries, and others
