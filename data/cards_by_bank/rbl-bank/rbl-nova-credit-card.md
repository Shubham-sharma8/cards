## About the Card
RBL NOVA Credit Card is a premium offering which allows the cardholders to accrue good value-back primarily through its rewards program and travel benefits. With up to 6% savings on travel, with luxury stay gift cards and complimentary airport lounge access for card users and guests, this card can be a decent companion for travel enthusiasts. However, to make the most of the high rewards rate on travel, cardholders will have to restrict themselves to a dedicated RBL platform to make their flight and hotel bookings.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the forex mark-up fee on RBL NOVA Credit Card spends?
You are charged with a 2% forex mark-up on international spends made using RBL Bank NOVA Credit Card.

### Does RBL NOVA offer accelerated rewards with 6% back on all travel spends?
No, you can earn 12 reward points per Rs. 100 spent , i.e. 6% value-back only on travel bookings made using nova.rbl.bank.in.

### Can I take guests with me to the airport lounge for free using RBL NOVA Credit Card?
Yes, RBL NOVA Credit Card comes with 4 complimentary visits for guests every year, however this guest access is limited to airport lounges outside India.

### What is the milestone bonus that I can earn in a year using RBL NOVA Credit Card?
You can earn gift cards worth up to Rs. 40,000 as milestone bonus every year, where you get Rs. 10,000 gift card on Rs. 3 lakh quarterly spends.

### What are the benefits offered by RBL NOVA Credit Card, apart from travel and rewards?
You can get complimentary golf access, dining discount and milestone bonus as gift cards with RBL NOVA Credit Card.
