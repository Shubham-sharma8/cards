## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- On spending 3.5 lakh in a year cardholders can get 10,000 reward points
- On spending, 5 lakh in one year users can avail 15,000 reward points
- When users spend Rs. 20,000 in a month they will receive 2 free movie tickets (worth Rs. 500 each) from BookMyShow for ongoing events and movies
- If a user’s spends exceed Rs. 5 lakh annually then they can get 25,000 bonus reward points

### Dining Benefits
- When dining out on weekends users get 20 reward points for every Rs. 100 spent. Accelerated reward points are capped at 2,000 Reward points per billing cycle
- Get 5% Cashback on dining spends on weekends when you spend Rs. 5000 in a month

### Movie / Entertainment
- When users spend Rs. 20,000 in a month they will receive 2 free movie tickets (worth Rs. 500 each) from BookMyShow for ongoing events and movies
