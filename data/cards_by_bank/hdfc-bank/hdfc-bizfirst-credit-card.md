## About the Card
HDFC BizFirst is a business credit card designed for small businesses, people who are self-employed, MSMEs and entrepreneurs. The card comes at a nominal fee of Rs. 500 and is best suited for businesses that make regular spends on EMIs, utility bills, software tools and travel bookings. Consumers can save on their business travel expenses like hotels and flights by availing discounts through MMT MyBiz. Since business owners spend a huge portion of their income on such costs. This entry level business card will help you save a lot on your spends.
