## About the Card
HDFC Diners Club Privilege Credit Card is a good choice for people looking for lifestyle benefits across everyday spending categories like dining, shopping, and movies. Cardholders can earn accelerated rewards on popular brands like Zomato & Swiggy, quarterly milestone benefits from Decathlon and Marriott, and the Buy One, Get One offer on BookMyShow. The card also offers up to 10X rewards on SmartBuy, giving users a reward rate of up to 13.33% on accelerated categories and 1.33% as a base rate.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the complimentary annual memberships that come with Diners Club Privilege?
The card offers complimentary memberships of Swiggy One & Times Prime on card activation. Besides, you get a voucher worth Rs. 1,500 from Marriott, Decathlon, Barbeque Nation, O2 Spa or Lakme Salon worth Rs. 1,500 on spending over Rs. 1.5 Lakh every calendar quarter.

### What are the benefits that come with an HDFC Diners Club Privilege credit card?
HDFC Diners Club Privilege Credit Card offers great benefits on multiple lifestyle categories including dining, shopping and movies. The card comes along with card activation benefits, milestone benefits, renewal fee waiver, complimentary airport lounge access, and reward points among others.

### Is there any renewal or annual fee waiver on this card?
The card comes with an annual fee of Rs. 2,500 plus applicable taxes. However, on spending Rs. 3 lakh in a year, your next year’s renewal fee gets waived off.

### Do the reward points have any expiration?
Yes, the reward points earned via HDFC Diners Club Privilege Credit Card are valid for 2 years from the date of accumulation. You can redeem the reward points for flights and hotel bookings on SmartBuy, dining vouchers, product catalogue, or for cashback.

### Does the card offer any golf benefits?
No, the golf benefit on the card has been discontinued w.e.f August 1, 2023.

### What is a Diners Club Privilege credit card?
Diners Club Privilege credit card is offered by HDFC Bank and is targeted towards customers who have a monthly income of Rs. 35,000 and above.
