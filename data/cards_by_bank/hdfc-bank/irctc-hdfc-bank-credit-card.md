## About the Card
IRCTC HDFC Bank Credit Card is a co-branded travel credit card designed for frequent travellers who prefer to travel by train and make bookings through IRCTC. Cardholders can earn 5% value back through reward points on train ticket bookings via IRCTC, along with an additional 5% cashback on IRCTC bookings made through the SmartBuy portal. > Your credit card doesn’t have airport lounge benefits?

## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- 1% transaction charges are waived off on IRCTC website and app, capped at Rs. 1,000 in a month
- Gift voucher of Rs. 500 on activating the card within 30 days of card issuance
- Gift voucher worth Rs. 500 on spending Rs. 30,000 in a quarter
- 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000, capped at Rs. 250 in a month

### Movie / Entertainment
- Additional 5% cashback on booking IRCTC train tickets using HDFC Bank SmartBuy
- 5X rewards & 5% cashback on IRCTC train ticket bookings
- Redemption of rewards against train ticket bookings at a 1:1 ratio

- You frequently travel via train and prefer to make bookings through IRCTC
- You want to earn reward points on railway ticket bookings and other everyday spends
- You prefer redeeming accumulated reward points for train ticket bookings to maximize benefits

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does IRCTC HDFC Bank Credit Card offer an annual fee waiver?
Yes, IRCTC HDFC Bank Credit Card charges an annual fee of Rs. 500. You can avail annual fee waiver by spending Rs. 1.5 Lakh in a year.

### What is the eligibility criteria for IRCTC HDFC Credit Card?
To apply for this credit card, your age must be between 21 and 65 years. Besides this, if you are a salaried individual, you must have a monthly income of Rs. 15,000. For self-employed individuals, the annual income requirement is Rs. 6 LPA.

### Does IRCTC HDFC Bank Credit Card offer a transaction charge waiver for train ticket bookings?
Yes, you can avail 1% transaction charge waiver for ticket bookings made through the IRCTC website and app. The maximum waiver you can avail in a month is Rs. 1,000.

### How can I apply for IRCTC HDFC Credit Card?
You can apply for this card online by visiting the official website of HDFC Bank and navigating to the card page. You are required to fill out the application form and attach all the relevant documents while applying for the card. If you wish to apply offline, you can visit the nearest HDFC Bank branch and submit the application form in person.

### Can I earn reward points on UPI spends with IRCTC HDFC Bank Credit Card?
Yes, IRCTC HDFC Bank Credit Card is also available on the RuPay network. Therefore, you can link this credit card to a UPI app and earn rewards on UPI spends as well.

### Does IRCTC HDFC Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, HDFC Bank Credit Card offers 8 complimentary visits to select IRCTC Executive Lounges every year, with a maximum of 2 visits per quarter.
