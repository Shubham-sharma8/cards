## About the Card
The card offers benefits on some of the most practical everyday categories. It covers groceries with BigBasket offer, lifestyle expenditure with Flipkart and Amazon, dining with Swiggy and other departmental store purchases via Reliance Smart SuperStore. The closest competitors of this credit card are HDFC’s two other cards- Moneyback and Millennia. HDFC Moneyback is good for overall online spends but the reward rate is not impressive as compared to the Moneyback+ variant. On the other hand, HDFC Millennia is quite an impressive alternative to Moneyback+ as the rewards to cashback ratio stands strong at 1:1, though at double the fee. Moneyback+ can be a good choice for beginners looking for accelerated rewards on their frequent online spends with the partnered brands. The features are satisfactory for a low annual fee of Rs. 500 and can be easily maximized by most online shoppers.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the maximum CashPoint value I can earn with HDFC MoneyBack+ Credit Card?
As per the terms and conditions associated with the HDFC MoneyBack+ Credit Card, the card caps the maximum CashPoints that you can earn in a month. The maximum capping for transactions under 10X CashPoints on selected brands (as mentioned above) and for 5X CashPoints on EMI spends is 2,500 CashPoints.

### Can I withdraw cash using my HDFC MoneyBack+ Credit Card?
Yes, the bank allows you to withdraw cash using your HDFC Bank MoneyBack+ Credit Card. However, the bank charges a cash advance fee of 2.5% of the withdrawn amount or a minimum of Rs. 500 (whichever is higher) for withdrawing cash via your credit card. Hence, it is advisable not to withdraw cash using your credit card as it incur higher charges.

### Can I earn fuel surcharge waiver on every transaction via HDFC MoneyBack+ Credit Card?
No, you can earn a fuel surcharge waiver of 1% on your transactions between Rs. 400 to Rs. 5,000. Also, you can earn a maximum waiver of Rs. 250 in a statement cycle. This waiver can be availed on your transactions across any petrol pump in the country, wherever this credit card is acceptable.

### How can I apply for HDFC MoneyBack+ Credit Card?
You can apply for this card directly with the issuer, or with Issuer / Market by providing details on the application form available at the top of this page.

### Do I get any UPI linked benefits on the card?
Yes, HDFC Moneyback+ offers credit card linked UPI benefits on the RuPay variant of the card with 2 reward points for every Rs. 200 spent.
