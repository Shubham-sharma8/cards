## About the Card
HDFC Bank UPI RuPay credit card is a **virtual card** which you can link to your UPI ID and use it to make UPI payments through PayZapp, GPay, Phonepe, PayTM or any UPI app of your choice. Issued only to the existing HDFC Bank credit card users, this card comes with rewards benefits in the form of CashPoints on your UPI as well as other regular card transactions worth Rs. 100 and above. You can earn **up to 3% CashPoints** , depending on the spending category. This card also offers an**interest free period of up to 50 days** and the option of **Smart EMI** to its users. Best-suited for those who make most of their payments through UPI, this card is currently offered as **lifetime free card** under a limited time offer which otherwise comes with a minimal annual fee of just Rs. 99 which can easily be waived off on spending Rs. 25,000 in a year on your everyday spends. Read on to know all about the features and benefits of HDFC RuPay UPI Credit Card.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the eligibility criteria for HDFC RuPay Credit Card?
You must be an Indian resident aged 21–65 years with a good credit history. The income criteria may apply depending on the card variant.

### How can I apply for an HDFC Bank RuPay Credit Card?
You can apply online via HDFC Bank’s website or app, or visit any HDFC Bank branch to submit your application.

### What is the limit of HDFC UPI RuPay Credit Card?
The credit limit is decided by HDFC Bank based on your income, credit score, and repayment history. It varies for each individual.

### What if I change my mobile number? How can I link my HDFC Bank RuPay Credit Card on the UPI app?
If you change your mobile number, update it with HDFC Bank first. Then you can link your card to UPI using the new number for verification.

### Do I need to enable card controls on HDFC Bank RuPay Credit Card to use it for online or contactless transactions?
Yes, you must enable the respective card controls in the HDFC Bank app to allow online or contactless payments.

### Will I earn any reward points for UPI spends using HDFC Bank RuPay Credit Card?
Yes, reward points are applicable on UPI transactions made using HDFC RuPay Credit Card, as per the card’s reward program.

### What is the annual fee for HDFC RuPay Credit Card?
HDFC RuPay Credit Card is currently offered as a lifetime free card under limited time offer. Otherwise it comes with a low joining and annual fee of Rs. 99 plus applicable taxes, making it a cost-effective option for everyday spending.

### Is there any capping on the reward points that can be earned on UPI spends through RuPay Credit Cards?
Yes, you earn CashPoints on every transaction of Rs. 100 or above, but there is a monthly capping of 500 points for each category. CashPoints can be redeemed as cashback, via the rewards catalogue, or through SmartBuy.
