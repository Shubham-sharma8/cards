## About the Card
PhonePe HDFC Bank Uno Credit Card is the base variant among the two co-branded cards offered by HDFC Bank in partnership with PhonePe. It offers a **2% reward rate on select PhonePe spends** such as recharges, utility bill payments, movies, hotels, travel, and more. In addition, cardholders can earn a base reward rate of **1% on popular online brands** including Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Swiggy, Zomato, and Uber, covering major day-to-day categories like shopping, dining, travel, and entertainment.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for PhonePe HDFC Bank Uno Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card only through the PhonePe app. You will need to fill out the application form and submit the relevant documents to proceed. There is no offline or website-based application process available for this card.

### Can I link my PhonePe HDFC Bank Uno Credit Card on UPI apps other than PhonePe?
Yes, you can link your PhonePe HDFC Bank Uno Credit Card with other UPI apps such as Paytm, Google Pay, and more to make UPI payments.

### Do the reward points earned via PhonePe HDFC Bank Uno Credit Card have a validity period?
Yes, reward points earned on this credit card come with a validity of 1 year. For example, reward points earned on 10 March 2025 will expire on 10 March 2026.

### Can I earn rewards on fuel payments through PhonePe HDFC Bank Uno Credit Card?
No, this credit card does not offer rewards on select categories, including EMI transactions, fuel purchases, wallet loads, gift cards, cash advances, credit card bill payments, card fees and charges, government transactions, rent payments, education fees, insurance, investments, and jewellery purchases.

### Does PhonePe HDFC Bank Uno Credit Card offer reward redemption against cashback?
Yes, you can redeem accumulated reward points against cashback at a rate of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 1. Additionally, there is no reward redemption fee levied when redeeming against cashback.
