## About the Card
Cardholders earn value back in the form of NeuCoins, which can be redeemed across Tata Neu and its partner brands, such as Air India, BigBasket, and Westside—offering the flexibility to redeem rewards across multiple brands instead of being restricted to just one. With this card, you can earn 2% value back on all non-EMI spends at Tata Neu, along with an additional 5% NeuCoins on select Tata Neu purchases. Besides this, you can earn 1% value back on other transactions, including UPI payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does Tata Neu Plus Credit Card offer lounge access?
Yes, Tata Neu Plus HDFC Credit Card offers four complimentary domestic lounge visits each year, with a maximum of one visit allowed per quarter.

### How can the annual charges for Tata Neu Plus Credit Card be waived off?
Tata Neu Plus HDFC Credit Card charges an annual fee of Rs. 499. you can avail annual fee waiver on spending Rs. 1 Lakh in a year.

### Are there any spends on which I cannot earn NeuCoins?
NeuCoins cannot be earned on select transactions such as fuel purchases, rent payments, government-related expenses, voucher purchases, wallet loads, cash advances, outstanding bill payments, other fees or charges, and EMI transactions.

### Is Tata Neu Plus Credit Card available in the RuPay variant?
Yes, Tata Neu Plus HDFC Credit Card is available in the RuPay variant. It allows you to earn 1% value back in the form of NeuCoins on UPI spends, with a monthly capping of 500 NeuCoins.

### How to get a Tata Neu Plus credit card?
HDFC Bank is a credit card partner with Issuer / Market. If you want to apply for a Tata Neu Plus credit card, you can apply through Issuer / Market. Begin by filling out the form at the top of this page.

### What is the benefit of the Tata Neu credit card?
The Tata Neu Plus credit card offers up to 7% value back on partner Tata brands with 1% back on other spends. Cardholders can also avail complimentary lounge access, welcome, and renewal benefits.

### Who is eligible for the Tata Neu card?
The Tata Neu Plus HDFC Bank Credit Card can be availed by both salaried and self-employed individuals aged between 21 and 65 years. The minimum income requirement is Rs. 25,000 per month for salaried and Rs. 6 lakh per annum for self-employed.

### Is the Tata Neu credit card good?
Tata Neu Plus HDFC Credit Card is a good choice for beginners looking for a low annual fee card with benefits on Tata Group brands. At a fee of Rs. 499, the card offers up to 7% value back on brands like Air India, BigBasket, and Westside.

### Can I get Tata Neu Plus Credit Card against a fixed deposit?
No, Tata Neu Plus HDFC Credit Card is an unsecured credit card. To be eligible, your age must be between 21 and 65 years. For salaried individuals, the minimum income requirement is Rs. 25,000 per month, while for self-employed individuals, it is Rs. 6 LPA.

### How to close Tata Neu credit card?
If you want to close your Tata Neu Plus HDFC Bank Credit Card, you may contact HDFC Bank’s customer care at 1800 2600. Alternatively, you may visit your nearest HDFC Bank branch.
