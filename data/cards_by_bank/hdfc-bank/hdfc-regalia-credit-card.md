## About the Card
With a reward-earning rate of **2.66%** and **2:1** redemption ratio for air miles and flights and hotel bookings (via SmartBuy), HDFC Regalia Credit Card holds good value for those looking for all-round benefits, especially on travel, without paying a high annual fee. Regalia users can make full use of the reward program by redeeming rewards to save on their travel expenses. To maximize benefits, cardholders can also utilize the lounge access and low forex mark-up benefit. Though the domestic airport lounge program on Regalia Credit Card is impressive, it can be availed only on reaching the quarterly milestone of Rs. 1 Lakh. However, there is no such condition on the international lounge access for primary and add-on cardholders. Note: This credit card has been phased out. Content on this page is for information purpose only._ While HDFC Bank is no longer accepting applications for Regalia Credit Card, existing cardholders can continue using the card if they are high-spenders, travel frequently and do not wish to limit their benefits to a particular brand. However, those who wish to save on a particular brand/category or those who are loyal to an airline, hotel or a travel portal, can consider applying for co-branded cards or cards focused on specific categories.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the benefits of HDFC Regalia Credit Card?
HDFC Regalia Credit Card offers exceptional benefits around rewards and travel. With this card you get to earn 4 rewards on every Rs. 150 spent and up to 15,000 bonus reward points as milestone benefit. The earned rewards can be redeemed across flight and hotel bookings at Smartbuy, air miles conversion, products and vouchers and against cashback for up to Rs. 0.5/Up to 0.5 air miles for each reward point. Additionally, the card offers up to 6 complimentary international lounge access, 8 domestic airport lounge access and Priority Pass membership.

### Which is better Regalia or Regalia Gold?
HDFC Regalia and HDFC Regalia Gold come at a similar joining and annual fee of Rs. 2,500. However, Regalia Gold has a higher annual fee waiver criteria of Rs. 4 Lakh, in comparison to Rs. 3 Lakh required by Regalia. To find the better card amongst these, you must access your spending ability and preferences so that you can maximize benefits. HDFC Regalia Gold can be a better option for those who travel a lot, and want to own a single credit card with all round benefits like complimentary flight ticket vouchers as rewards, higher frequency on lounge access, low forex markup fee and travel memberships. The card also offers accelerated rewards on Marks & Spencers, Myntra, Reliance Digital & Nykaa. Thus, brand loyalists can easily utilize the benefits. HDFC Regalia on the other card is a suitable choice for those looking for maximum benefits in the rewards category, with features like bonus reward points, and high general reward rate not specific to any brand.

### How to redeem HDFC Regalia credit card points?
You can redeem the accumulated reward points on your HDFC Regalia credit card via HDFC Smartbuy portal or Netbanking.

### Does HDFC Regalia Credit Card charge a cash advance fee?
Yes, the bank charges a fee of 2.5% of the withdrawn amount or a minimum of Rs. 500 (whichever is higher) for withdrawing cash via HDFC Regalia Credit Card. Therefore, it is advisable not to withdraw cash using your credit card as it can also impact your credit score.

### Does HDFC Regalia Credit Card have airport lounge access?
Yes, HDFC Regalia Credit Card offers complimentary airport lounge access within and outside India. You can avail 6 complimentary international lounge visits with Priority Pass for primary and add-on member and up to 8 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits in a year (2 per quarter subject to spending Rs. 1 lakh per quarter).

### How can I check my HDFC Regalia Credit Card statement online?
You can check your credit card statement online by visiting the official website of HDFC Bank. All you need to do is log in to your account and go to ‘Cards’ and select the ‘Enquire’ option to view your statement. Apart from this, you can also use HDFC mobile banking app to download your billed statement.
