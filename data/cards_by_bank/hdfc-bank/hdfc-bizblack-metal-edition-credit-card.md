## Frequently Asked Questions
### How much money must cardholders spend with their Biz Black credit card to receive the 5X reward points?
To avail the benefit of 5X rewards, i.e. 25 reward points per Rs. 150 spent, the HDFC Biz Black cardholders must reach the minimum monthly card spends worth Rs. 50,000.

### What is the HDFC Biz Black fuel surcharge waiver?
HDFC Biz Black credit card does not offer any surcharge waiver on the fuel transactions made via the card.

### Does HDFC Biz Black provide lounge access?
Yes, HDFC Biz Black Metal credit card offers unlimited complimentary lounge access to domestic and international airport lounges. This benefit is available for both active primary card members as well as add-on card users.

### Can I get a loan on HDFC Biz Black credit card?
Yes, HDFC Bank Biz Black cardholders can get an Insta loan of any available amount within their assigned credit limit. The loan amount would be blocked against the remaining limit until repaid. Cardholders can also opt for Jumbo loan under which they can borrow a loan amount even beyond the available credit card limit without blocking any part of their credit limit.
