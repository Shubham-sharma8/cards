## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the Business Insurance Benefits of BizPower Credit Card?
The card comes with multiple insurance benefits including Fire & burglary insurance of Up to Rs. 5 Lakh, Cash in safe/transit of up to Rs. 25,000 and electronic equipment insurance of up to Rs. 50,000 among others. Card users can also get higher insurance cover benefits by choosing an insurance plan and paying the premium.

### What are the travel benefits of BizPower Credit Card?
The card offers travel benefits with MakeMyTrip on hotel and flight bookings. Additionally, consumers can redeem the accumulated reward points for airmile conversion and flights and hotel bookings via SmartBuy.

### Does HDFC Bank BizPower Credit Card provide lounge access?
Yes, the card offers complimentary domestic and international lounge access. Users get 2 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per quarter on a min spend of Rs. 75,000 in the previous quarter. For international travel, 6 complimentary visits are offered through the Priority Pass membership.

### What is the credit limit of HDFC Biz Power Credit Card?
The credit limit on HDFC Biz Power Credit Card is subjective to the consumers’ profile based on factors like income, profession, credit score, etc.

### What are the two different loans that are offered on the HDFC Bank BizPower Credit Card?
The card offers Insta loan and Jumbo loan facility. The Insta loan is offered against the credit limit, whereas the Jumbo loan is offered beyond the credit limit.

### What are the Benefits of Biz Power Credit Card?
HDFC BizPower Credit Card is a business card suitable for the self-employed. It offers benefits around travel, bill payments and shopping for gadgets and softwares.

### What is the annual fee for a Biz Power credit card?
HDFC Bank Biz Power Credit Card comes at an annual fee of Rs. 2,500.
