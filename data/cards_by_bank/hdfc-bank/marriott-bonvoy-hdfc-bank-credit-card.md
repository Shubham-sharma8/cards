## About the Card
Marriott Bonvoy HDFC Bank Credit Card is India’s first credit card co-branded with a hotel chain. Offering multiple benefits across Marriott properties worldwide, this card is ideal for Marriott loyalists.

## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Underrated keeper card: pays for itself on day 1 with the 15k-point Free Night Award + 24 spend-free global/domestic lounges.

**Best For:** Travelers wanting 12 domestic + 12 international NO-SPEND lounges AND a guaranteed annual Marriott Free Night worth >Rs. 8,000 for a Rs. 3,000 fee

**Not Ideal For:** Day-to-day cashback earners

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 0 – Rs. 25,000 (keeper hotel + lounge card)

### Pros (Good)
- Exclusive privileges on Marriott hotel bookings
- Access to both international and domestic airport lounges
- Complimentary Marriott Bonvoy Silver Elite Status
- Free Night Stays at Marriott as welcome and milestone benefits

### Cons (Bad)
- No general travel benefits
- Features are limited to co-branded hotel bookings
- Base reward rate is low
- No Priority Pass membership

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the benefit of Marriott Bonvoy Silver Elite Status?
Silver Elite is the base membership of the Marriott Bonvoy Loyalty Program. Members get 10% more points on every eligible hotel stay booking, member-only room rates, and other benefits like priority late checkout, complimentary in-room internet access, etc. Though this membership is offered on completing 10 nights per year at participating hotels, you get it complimentary with the Marriott Bonvoy HDFC Bank Credit Card.

### Is HDFC Marriott Bonvoy Credit Card worth it?
Since it is a co-branded card, it is perfect for travellers who prefer staying at Marriott Properties. However, consumers who do not wish to limit their benefits to Marriott should consider cards that extend benefits with multiple hotel chains like Axis Reserve or American Express Platinum (Charge) Card.

### How can I redeem Marriott Bonvoy Points? What is the value for each point?
Marriott Bonvoy Points can be used to book a stay at select Marriott Properties that are a part of Marriott Bonvoy and Marriott Bonvoy Moments™ experiences. The value you get out of your accumulated MB Points will vary as per the hotel, type of room, booking date, etc.

### What are Free Night Awards? How to use them?
Free Night Awards can be considered as free stay vouchers which can be redeemed at the time of booking your stay with participating Marriott properties. If you have Free Night Awards in your Marriott Bonvoy account, it will be preselected at the time of making a booking. You can also combine up to 15,000 MB Points to save more.

### Are there any terms and conditions associated with this credit card’s lounge access benefit?
No. there are no spend-based conditions applicable to the lounge access benefit.
