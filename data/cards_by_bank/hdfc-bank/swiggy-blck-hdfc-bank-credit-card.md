## About the Card
Another benefit that makes this card stand out is that, in addition to the accelerated 5% cashback, cardholders can also earn up to **19% discount** on travel bookings via **Cleartrip** and **5% discount** on spends at **Nykaa**. The card also offers exclusive golf benefits through its Mastercard Golf Program, including **12 free golf lessons** and **4 free green fees** every year. Overall, Swiggy BLCK HDFC Credit Card is a great choice for consumers seeking significant cashback on Swiggy orders and other online spends, along with lifestyle benefits like complimentary golf games.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the cashback benefits of Swiggy BLCK HDFC Credit Card?
The card offers 10% cashback on Swiggy spends, 5% cashback on select online merchants like Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa, and 1% cashback on other eligible spends. Besides this, it also offers additional discount on Nykaa and Cleartrip in addition to the accelerated cashback.

### Does Swiggy BLCK HDFC Credit Card offer any discounts on travel or shopping?
Yes, it offers up to 19% discount on Cleartrip hotel bookings, 6% on travel bookings via Cleartrip, and 5% discount on Nykaa purchases. In addition to this, it also offers additional birthday discount on Swiggy Dineout.

### Are there any lifestyle benefits with this card?
Yes, it offers 12 free golf lessons, and 4 complimentary green fees annually under the Mastercard Golf Program, plus a 50% discount on additional green fees.

### What is the difference between Swiggy Ornge and Swiggy BLCK Credit Card?
Swiggy BLCK is the premium variant of Swiggy Ornge Card. Both offer accelerated cashback on Swiggy and online spends at partner merchants, but BLCK offers higher cashback of 10% on Swiggy spends, while Ornge offers 5%. Additionally, BLCK comes with a complimentary BLCK membership for 3 months, whereas Ornge offers a Swiggy One membership for 1 year. Both cards also offer a base cashback of 1% and complimentary golf benefits under the Mastercard Golf Program.

### Who is eligible to apply for Swiggy BLCK HDFC Credit Card?
Salaried applicants must be 21 to 60 years old with a minimum monthly income of Rs. 1 Lakh. Self-employed applicants must be 21 to 65 years old with an annual ITR above Rs. 12 Lakh.

### Is this card good for online shopping apart from Swiggy?
Yes, the card offers 5% cashback on partner merchants like Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Cleartrip, and BookMyShow, making it ideal for online shopping in addition to Swiggy orders.

### What are the fees and charges of this credit card?
The card has a joining fee and annual fee of Rs. 1,000. However, it offers joining fee waiver on spending Rs. 75,000 within the first 90 days and annual fee waiver on spending Rs. 2 Lakh in a year.
