## About the Card
Cardholders can accumulate reward points and redeem them against Shoppers Stop vouchers at a 1:1 ratio—making it a suitable option for loyal Shoppers Stop customers. Additionally, welcome benefits, such as a Shoppers Stop Silver Edge Membership and a Shoppers Stop voucher worth Rs. 500, enhance the overall value of this card for loyal customers of the partnered brand. While this card is a decent fit for Shoppers Stop customers, it offers limited benefits beyond that, as it has a low reward rate of 1% on other spends and a low reward redemption value of up to 1 RP = 0.25 when redeemed against other categories like travel bookings, cashback and more. Being a co-branded card, it justifies its annual fee. However, consumers looking for an all-rounder entry-level card may consider other options, as the benefits of this card can only be maximized if you frequently shop at Shoppers Stop.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the co-branded benefits offered by Shoppers Stop HDFC Bank Credit Card?
At the time of joining, the card offers a complimentary Shoppers Stop Silver Edge Membership and a Shoppers Stop voucher worth Rs. 500. Besides this, cardholders can earn 3% rewards on spends at Shoppers Stop and redeem the accumulated rewards against Shoppers Stop vouchers at a 1:1 ratio.

### Does HDFC Bank Shoppers Stop Credit Card offer an annual fee waiver?
No, Shoppers Stop HDFC Bank Credit Card does not offer an annual fee waiver. You are required to pay an annual fee of Rs. 299 every year.

### How can I apply for HDFC Shoppers Stop Credit Card?
To apply for this credit card online, visit the official website of HDFC Bank, fill out the application form, and submit it online along with the relevant documents. You can also visit any nearest branch of HDFC Bank and contact an executive regarding the offline application process.

### Can I earn reward points on grocery spends through Shoppers Stop HDFC Bank Credit Card?
Yes, you can earn 1% reward points on every non-Shoppers Stop spend, including groceries. However, you can earn a maximum of 1,000 points per month on grocery spends.

### Does HDFC Bank Shoppers Stop Credit Card offer a fuel surcharge waiver?
Yes, you can avail 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel spends across all fuel stations. However, the maximum waiver you can earn is Rs. 250 per month on fuel transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000.

### Can I redeem reward points earned via Shoppers Stop HDFC Bank Credit Card against cashback?
Yes, you can redeem the accumulated reward points against the statement balance at a value of 1 RP = Rs. 0.20.
