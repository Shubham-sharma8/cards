## About the Card
HDFC Bank PIXEL Go Credit Card issued against an FD of Rs. 11,000. Cardholders can earn unlimited 1% cashback on eligible transactions, along with 5% cashback on SmartBuy purchases such as flights, hotels, instant vouchers, rail and bus bookings, capped at 500 CashPoints per month. The RuPay variant also offers 1% cashback on UPI spends, capped at 500 CashPoints monthly. The cashback is credited as PIXEL CashPoints in the PayZapp wallet, where 1 CashPoint is valued at Rs. 1 for voucher redemption. The card also offers additional benefits such as up to 25% discount on partner restaurants through Swiggy Dineout and a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on eligible fuel transactions.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the minimum fixed deposit required for the secured HDFC PIXEL Go Credit Card?
The secured variant can be issued against a fixed deposit starting from Rs. 11,000.

### How does cashback work on the HDFC PIXEL Go Credit Card?
Cardholders earn unlimited 1% cashback on eligible retail transactions. Additional 5% cashback is available on SmartBuy categories like flights, hotels, rail, bus bookings, and vouchers, capped at 500 CashPoints monthly. Cashback is credited as PIXEL CashPoints in PayZapp.

### Does the RuPay version offer cashback on UPI payments?
Yes, the RuPay variant offers 1% cashback on eligible UPI spends linked to the credit card. The benefit is capped at 500 CashPoints per month.

### How are PIXEL CashPoints redeemed and what is their value?
Cashback is credited as PIXEL CashPoints in the PayZapp wallet. These points can be redeemed for brand vouchers, where 1 CashPoint equals Rs. 1 in redemption value. Redemption options are available directly through the PayZapp platform.

### What fuel surcharge and dining benefits are available on the card?
The card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000, capped at Rs. 250 monthly. Consumers can also get up to 25% discount at partner restaurants through Swiggy Dineout, depending on merchant participation.
