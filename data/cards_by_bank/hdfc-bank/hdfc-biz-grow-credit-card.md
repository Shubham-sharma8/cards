## Frequently Asked Questions
### Which HDFC credit card is best Biz Grow or Biz First?
HDFC Biz Grow and Biz First - both offer decent value-backs to the cardholders. Biz First offers accelerated CashPoints on EMI, electronics, utility bills, SmartPay and Payzapp spends, while Bix Grow focuses more on business-related spends, like bill & tax payments, and MMT travel bookings among others. Both cards also offer other benefits, discounts and offers in addition to the CashPoints. However, in order to pick the better card between these two options, your individual needs and spending patterns must be considered. The card that allows you to maximise your savings without overspending would be the most-suited one for you.

### What will be the interest free credit period for HDFC Biz Grow credit card?
HDFC Biz Grow credit card offers an interest-free credit period of up to 55 days.

### What are the two types of loans available on HDFC Biz Grow Credit Card?
With HDFC Biz Grow credit card, you can avail Insta loan against your card’s credit limit. Additionally, for bigger loans, you can avail the Jumbo loan against your credit card beyond the credit limit offered on the card.

### How can a customer redeem Cash Points earned on Biz Grow Credit Card and what is the reward point value?
Cardholders can redeem reward points against cashback, SmartBuy purchases, and the product catalogue at a value of 1 Cash Point = Rs. 0.25. Additionally, reward points can be redeemed against the business catalogue at a value of 1 Cash Point = Rs. 0.30.

### How does the card offer convenience in my daily business operations?
HDFC Biz Grow is a business credit card designed for entrepreneurs and professionals. It offers accelerated rewards, including 10X Cash Points on select business spends and a base earning rate of 2 Cash Points per Rs. 150 spent on other business-related expenses. In addition, this card also offers discounts on select business-related spending needs, as mentioned on the page above, along with business insurance covers.

### How does the card protect me and my business from financial risk?
HDFC Biz Grow Credit Card comes with business insurance coverage for Fire & burglary worth up to Rs. 5 Lakh, Cash in safe/transit of up to Rs. 25,000, electronic equipment worth up to Rs. 50,000 and hospital cash worth up to Rs. 1,000 per day each for accident and illness.

### Is BizGrow credit card good or bad?
HDFC Biz Grow Credit Card can be a good option for you if you are a business owner who travels frequently and is looking for a card to save on business and travel-related spends.
