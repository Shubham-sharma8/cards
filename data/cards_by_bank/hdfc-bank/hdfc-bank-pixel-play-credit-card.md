## Eligibility Criteria
- **Occupation:** Salaried or Self-Employed

- **Min. Salaried Income:** Rs. 600,000/month
- **Self-Employed Min. ITR:** Rs. 600,000/annum
- **Min. CIBIL Score:** 730
- **Approval Notes:** The card is offered to both salaried and self-employed individuals aged between 21 and up to 65 years. Additionally, one needs to fulfill the income requirement of minimum Rs. 25,000 per month for salaried and ITR greater than Rs. 6 Lakh per annum for self-employed.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the eligibility criteria to avail this card?
The card is offered to both salaried and self-employed individuals aged between 21 and up to 65 years. Additionally, one needs to fulfill the income requirement of minimum Rs. 25,000 per month for salaried and ITR greater than Rs. 6 Lakh per annum for self-employed.

### What transactions are excluded from the cashback program?
The card does not offer any cashback on fuel, rent, EMI, e-wallet loading and government related spends.

### How do I apply for this credit card?
You can apply for this credit card via the HDFC Bank’s PayZapp app.

### Does the card offer any UPI linked credit card benefits?
Yes, with the RuPay variant of the HDFC Bank Pixel Play Credit Card, you can avail 1% cashback benefit on the UPI spends.

### Can I avail fuel surcharge waiver on this credit card?
Yes, the card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions ranging between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000, up to Rs. 250 per month.
