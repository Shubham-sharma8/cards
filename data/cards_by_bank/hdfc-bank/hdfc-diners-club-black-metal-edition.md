## About the Card
The point-to-mile conversion and redemption of rewards against flight/hotel bookings at a 1:1 ratio make this card a preferable option for frequent travellers. Complimentary international lounge access for primary and add-on members, along with a low forex mark-up fee and the facility to convert points to miles, add to its value for international travellers. HDFC Diners Club Black offers benefits similar to HDFC Bank’s other premium credit card, HDFC Infinia. While Infinia is an invite-only card that comes with a stringent eligibility criteria, individuals who want to avail similar benefits but are not eligible for it can consider applying for HDFC DC Black Credit Card, offered at a comparatively lower annual fee of Rs. 10,000.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the value of 1 HDFC Diners Club Black reward point in rupees?
The redemption value of reward points on HDFC DC Black differ across multiple redemption categories. 1 reward point = 1 Airmile/Rs. 1, if you choose to transfer the points to miles or book flights/hotels through HDFC Bank’s SmartBuy Platform. However, for redemption against product catalogue, you get Rs. 0.50 and for cash conversion, you get Rs. 0.30 for each reward point.

### What is the minimum income required to be eligible for HDFC Diners Club Black Credit Card?
The minimum income requirement is Rs. 2.5 Lakh p.m. for salaried applicants and Rs. 30 Lakh p.a. for self-employed applicants.

### How does the 10X rewards program on HDFC Diners Club Black work?
With HDFC DC Black Card, you get 10X rewards on select spends made on SmartBuy. Currently, 10X rewards can be earned on Hotel Bookings, IGP, Jockey and PharmEasy. These categories and brands can be changed at the bank’s discretion. Hence, cardholders must check the details before making a purchase.

### What is the maximum value-back I can earn with the HDFC Diners Club Black card in a year?
Cardholders can earn maximum 75,000 reward points in a statement cycle, which adds up to 9 Lakh reward points in a year.

### What is the process for redeeming HDFC Diners Club Black reward points for air miles?
You can redeem your reward points for air miles through HDFC Bank’s SmartBuy Portal. Login to the SmartBuy portal, select the airline, enter your airline loyalty program ID, enter the number of points to be converted to Air Miles and proceed. The transferred miles will be added to your airline loyalty program balance, which can be used at the time of booking your tickets through the respective airline’s platform.

### How does the golf benefit on HDFC Diners Club Black work?
With HDFC Diners Club Black Credit Card, you get 6 complimentary golf games in a quarter. You can book the same by calling their 24-hour Golf Concierge Assistance Service at Golf Concierge Assistance on the Toll free no.: 1800 118 887 (India) or on Landline – 022 42320226. Customers travelling overseas can call at Landline: 022-6171 7606.
