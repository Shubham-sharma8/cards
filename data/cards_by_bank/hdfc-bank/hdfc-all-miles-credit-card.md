## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- Cardholders can avail 1% fuel surcharge waiver on a minimum transaction of Rs. 400. The maximum cash back that can be earned is Rs. 500 per statement cycle
- First year membership fee can be waived off if user spends over Rs. 15,000 in the first 90 days of card issuance

### Insurance Cover
- Reward points can be accrued on insurance transactions. However, a maximum of 2000 points can be earned per transaction
- Users get the following insurance cover on using the credit card for 4 transactions in a month:
- Accidental Death Insurance up to Rs. 50 lakh
