## About the Card
Apart from the rewards program, PhonePe Ultimo credit cardholders get the option to avail **domestic lounge access** by meeting the quarterly spending condition. While the joining fee cost can be overcome through the welcome benefit worth the same amount, the annual fee comes with its own waiver condition. Both these conditions lean towards the lower side and hence can be met by any **average spender** too. Read on to know more about the card features and benefits in detail.

## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- Up to 8 complimentary domestic lounge visits in a year, capped at 2 per quarter on reaching Rs. 75,000 quarterly spends
- Renewal fee is waived off on spending min. Rs. 2 lakh in the previous year
- 1% fuel surcharge waiver, up to Rs. 250 per month, on fuel spends of Rs. 400 to Rs. 5,000

### Dining Benefits
- 5% Reward Points: On spends made on Myntra, Swiggy, Zomato, Amazon, Ajio, Flipkart and Uber (Capping: 500 reward points per month)

### Movie / Entertainment
- 10% Reward Points: On spends made on utilities, movies, hotels, travel, recharges, bill payments, PhonePe Pincode app, and Travel Switch category on PhonePe (Capping: 1,000 reward points per month)

### Get This Card If
- You are looking for a RuPay credit card to save on UPI transactions and get accelerated savings on popular shopping apps and platforms
- PhonePe is either your preferred payments app or you can maximise your value-back by start using this app for payments
- You want a credit card to save on spends like utilities, bill payments and recharges - categories that are often excluded
- You are looking for card with domestic airport lounge access and can meet the quarterly spend condition to avail this benefit

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Can reward points earned using PhonePe Ultimo HDFC credit card be auto-credited as cashback?
No, the earned reward points can be redeemed as cashback against statement balance by request only. You can request for cashback accrual by logging into your card account.

### Is there any cashback redemption fee applicable on HDFC Ultimo credit card?
No, no fee is charged on cashback redemption of the accrued reward points.

### Is there a minimum transaction amount for rewards earnings on PhonePe HDFC Ultimo credit card?
Yes, only the card transactions that are worth Rs. 100 or more are eligible for accrual of reward points.

### How many reward points can I earn using PhonePe HDFC Ultimo credit card?
You can earn a maximum of 2,000 reward points every month using HDFC PhonePe Ultimo credit card.

### Can I link PhonePe HDFC Bank Credit Card to other UPI apps apart from PhonePe?
Yes, you can link PhonePe Ultimo HDFC credit card to any UPI app other than PhonePe as well, including Google Pay, BHIM UPI, Paytm, CRED, etc.
