## About the Card
One thing to note is that this card earlier offered complimentary Paytm First membership as a joining benefit, which has now been discontinued. At present, despite charging a joining fee of Rs. 500, **the card does not offer any welcome benefits**. However, cardholders can avail joining and annual fee waivers on achieving the spending milestones. The card also offers **cashback of Rs. 1,200 every year as a milestone benefit** , along with other basic benefits such as a fuel surcharge waiver and dining discount. For consumers who frequently shop or pay via Paytm and are looking for an entry-level credit card with basic benefits, this card can be a suitable option.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Can I withdraw cash using Paytm HDFC Credit Card?
Yes, you can withdraw cash using your Paytm HDFC Credit Card. However, note that the cash withdrawal via this credit card is chargeable at 2.5% of the transaction amount or Rs. 500, whichever is higher.

### Can I get a new card, if I lost my HDFC Paytm Credit Card?
Yes, in case you lose or damage your credit card, you can get a replacement card by paying a fee of Rs. 100. For safety purposes, ensure informing the bank at the earliest, if your card is lost or stolen.

### What is the validity of reward points earned on my HDFC Bank Paytm Credit Card?
The reward points earned on your HDFC Paytm Credit Card are valid for a period of two years. Also, note that all rewards earned via this card are in multiples of Rs. 150.

### How can I apply for Paytm HDFC Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online either through the Paytm app or by visiting the HDFC Bank’s official website and submitting the duly filled application form. Besides this, you can also apply offline by visiting the nearest branch of HDFC Bank and submitting the application form along with relevant documents.

### Can I redeem reward points earned via Paytm HDFC Credit Card against cashback?
Yes, you can redeem the accumulated Cashpoints against cashback. However, you can redeem a maximum of 3,000 per month against cashback.
