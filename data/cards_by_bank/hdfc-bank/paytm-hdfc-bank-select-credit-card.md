## About the Card
Another feature which differentiates this card from the base variant is that it offers **8 complimentary domestic lounge visits every year** , enhancing the card’s overall value for frequent travellers. Although the complimentary Paytm First membership (previously available on both variants) has been discontinued, consumers can still save at the time of joining and renewal by availing joining and renewal fee waiver on achieving the certain spending milestone. The card also offers **gift vouchers worth Rs. 2,000 as annual milestone benefits** along with a fuel surcharge waiver and **up to 20% dining discount**. Overall, this card is a suitable option for consumers who prefer to spend online and are looking for a rewards credit card with basic travel benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does Paytm HDFC Bank Select Credit Card offer milestone benefits?
Paytm HDFC Bank Select Credit Card offers milestone benefit on spending Rs. 50,000 in a calendar quarter. On achieving this milestone, you get a gift voucher worth Rs. 500. When you are eligible for this benefit, you will be informed of the same by the bank.

### How do I get the credit card statement for Paytm HDFC Bank Select Credit Card?
You will receive your Paytm HDFC Bank Select Credit Card statement via email to your registered email address. Apart from that, you can view your statement and download it by logging into your HDFC Bank net banking account.

### Can I apply online for Paytm HDFC Bank Select Credit Card?
Yes, you can apply online by visiting the official website of HDFC Bank and navigating to the respective card page. You are required to submit the duly filled application form along with relevant documents. Besides this, you can also apply online through Paytm app by submitting the online application.

### Can I earn rewards on bill payments via Paytm HDFC Bank Select Credit Card?
Yes, the card offers 5% Cashpoints on Paytm spends, including utility bill payments. However, you cannot earn rewards on bill payments via any other app.

### Does Paytm HDFC Bank Select Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, the card offers 8 complimentary domestic lounge visits every year. You can avail a maximum of 2 visits in a quarter.
