## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for this credit card?
You can apply for the HDFC Bank Pixel Go credit card through the PayZapp app.

### Do I earn cashback on insurance transactions?
Yes, you can earn cashback on insurance payments as per the base rate of 1% on the card. However, cashpoints on insurance transactions are capped at 2,000 per day.

### What is the dining benefit on this card?
The card offers a dining discount of up to 10% via Swiggy Dineout.

### What are the benefits of HDFC Pixel Go credit card?
HDFC Bank Pixel Go credit card offers 5% cashback on spends across Smartbuy. Plus, users can earn a 1% return on retail and UPI spends. It also offers up to 10% discount on partner restaurants via Swiggy Dineout.

### Can I link my Pixel Go RuPay card to UPI apps?
Yes, you can link your HDFC Bank Pixel Go RuPay credit card to third-party apps like Gpay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc for UPI linked credit card benefits.

### What is a Pixel Go credit card?
The HDFC Bank Pixel Go Credit Card is a digital credit card with cashback benefits. It comes at a low annual fee of Rs. 250.

### How to close my HDFC Pixel Go credit card?
To close your Pixel Go credit card, you can contact HDFC Bank credit card customer care at 1800 1600 / 1800 2600.

### HDFC Pixel Play vs Pixel Go credit card?
HDFC Pixel Play and Pixel Go are digital credit cards that offer cashback benefits at a fee of Rs. 500 and Rs. 250, respectively. The base variant Pixel Go offers a straightforward cashback of up to 5% on Smartbuy and other retail spends. On the other hand, Pixel Play offers additional benefits to E-commerce merchants and popular brands. With the latter, cardholders can choose between the choice of brands to earn accelerated benefits.
