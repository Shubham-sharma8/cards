## About the Card
Apart from the value back, what sets this card apart from the base variant is that it offers complimentary domestic and international lounge visits every year. Additionally, cardholders can also redeem the reward points against air miles and travel bookings through the SmartBuy portal at a 2:1 ratio, which is good, especially considering this is a co-branded credit card. Also, similar to the base variant, this card surpasses its joining fee of Rs. 4,500 by offering a complimentary Shoppers Stop Black Membership worth Rs. 4,500 and a Shoppers Stop discount voucher worth Rs. 1,500 as welcome benefits. Consumers who frequently shop at Shoppers Stop can maximize the benefits of this card. However, if you frequently travel, you can also save on your travel spends through this card via complimentary lounge access and redemption of rewards against travel bookings.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who is eligible for Shoppers Stop Black HDFC Bank Credit Card?
To apply for this credit card, your age must be between 21 and 65 years. Besides this, if you are a salaried individual, you must have a monthly income of Rs. 70,000, and for self-employed individuals, the annual income requirement is Rs. 8.4 LPA.

### Does HDFC Bank Shoppers Stop Black Credit Card have lounge access benefits?
Yes, the card offers 16 complimentary domestic lounge visits per year, with a maximum of 4 visits per quarter, and 8 complimentary international lounge visits every year, with a maximum of 2 visits per quarter.

### How can I apply for HDFC Shoppers Stop Black Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online by visiting the official website of HDFC Bank and submitting the duly filled application form along with the required documents. Besides this, if you want to apply offline, you can visit the nearest branch of HDFC Bank and contact an executive regarding the offline application process.

### Does Shoppers Stop Black HDFC Bank Credit Card offer a fuel surcharge waiver?
Yes, you can avail 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel spends between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000. You can earn a maximum waiver of Rs. 500 per month.

### Is there any capping on reward points earned via Shoppers Stop Black HDFC Bank Credit Card?
Yes, you can earn 7% rewards on Shoppers Stop spends, with a monthly capping of 2,000 points, and 1% rewards on other spends, with a monthly capping of 2,000 points.

### Does Shoppers Stop Black HDFC Bank Credit Card offer an annual fee waiver?
No, Shoppers Stop HDFC Bank Credit Card does not offer an annual fee waiver. You are required to pay an annual fee of Rs. 4,500 every year.
