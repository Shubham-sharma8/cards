## About the Card
Cardholders can earn 2 CashPoints for every Rs. 200 spent on general business expenses and 6 CashPoints for every Rs. 200 spent on eligible business digital spends, bill payments, GST payments, and income tax payments. Accumulated CashPoints can be redeemed at a value of 1 CashPoint = Rs. 0.25. The card also includes features such as monthly spending-based milestone rewards, a welcome benefit, dining discounts through Swiggy Dineout, and a fuel surcharge waiver on eligible fuel transactions. The card comes with an annual fee of Rs. 500, which can be waived if the cardholder spends at least Rs. 50,000 during the card anniversary year. Overall, the HDFC GIGA BUSINESS Credit Card is positioned as a mid-range business credit card that combines rewards on business spends with basic lifestyle and savings benefits, making it suitable for consumers seeking an entry-level credit card to start their credit journey.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who is the HDFC GIGA BUSINESS Credit Card best suited for?
The card is primarily designed for consumers who regularly use a credit card for business-related expenses such as utility bills, tax payments, and digital transactions.

### How are CashPoints earned on business expenses?
Cardholders can earn CashPoints at different rates depending on the spending category. General business purchases earn a standard reward rate, while eligible digital payments, utility bills, GST payments, and income tax payments earn rewards at a higher rate.

### Can rewards earned on tax payments be redeemed as cash?
CashPoints accumulated through eligible spending can be redeemed against the card statement or other available redemption options. Each CashPoint carries a fixed redemption value, making it easier to estimate the actual reward earned.

### What happens if I do not meet the annual fee waiver condition?
If the required spending threshold is not achieved during the card anniversary year, the annual membership fee will be charged. Reviewing yearly spending can help cardholders understand whether they qualify for the fee waiver.

### Does the card provide benefits beyond business-related rewards?
Apart from rewards on business expenses, the card includes additional features such as spending-based milestone benefits, dining-related savings through partner programs, and a fuel surcharge waiver on eligible fuel purchases.

### Are all digital transactions eligible for accelerated rewards?
Not every digital transaction may qualify for the higher reward rate. Accelerated rewards generally apply to specific categories defined by the issuer, so cardholders should review the applicable terms to understand eligible transactions.
