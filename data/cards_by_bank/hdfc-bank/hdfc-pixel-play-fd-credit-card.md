## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the HDFC PIXEL Play Against FD Credit Card?
It is a secured credit card issued against a fixed deposit of Rs. 56,000. The card helps consumers to build or improve their credit score while availing cashback benefits on shopping, travel, dining, grocery, and UPI transactions.

### How much credit limit will I get on this card?
The card offers up to 90% of the fixed deposit amount as the credit limit. The exact limit depends on HDFC Bank’s internal policies and the FD value linked to the card application.

### Can I earn cashback on everyday spending?
Yes, cardholders earn 1% unlimited cashback on eligible spends across categories. Additional cashback of up to 5% is available on selected spending packs and partner merchants chosen through the PIXEL platform.

### What are the 5% cashback spending packs?
Consumers can select two preferred packs from categories like dining, travel, grocery, electronics, and fashion. Cashback is applicable on partner brands such as Zomato, Myntra, Uber, Blinkit, and Croma, subject to monthly caps.

### Does the card offer cashback on Amazon or Flipkart?
Yes, cardholders can choose one preferred e-commerce platform like Amazon, Flipkart, or PayZapp and earn 3% cashback on eligible spends, capped at 500 reward points per month.

### Is there any benefit on UPI transactions?
RuPay variant of the card offers 1% cashback on eligible UPI transactions. This makes the card useful for everyday QR-code payments while still earning rewards on routine spending.
