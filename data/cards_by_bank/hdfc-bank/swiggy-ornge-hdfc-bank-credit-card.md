## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the monthly cashback capping on Swiggy Ornge HDFC Credit Card?
You can earn up to Rs. 2,500 cashback every month using Swiggy Ornge HDFC credit card where 5% cashback is capped at Rs. 1,500 and 1% at Rs. 1,000 per month.

### Will I get Swiggy One membership with Swiggy Ornge Credit Card if I have an existing Swiggy membership?
Yes, all cardholders get a 12 month Swiggy One membership on card activation. If a card user is an existing member, then their membership is extended by 12 months with Swiggy One benefits.

### Why didn’t I earn any cashback on my online purchase using Swiggy Ornge HDFC Credit Card?
This can happen in case you have reached the cashback capped limit in that month or if your purchase value is less than Rs. 249. Also confirm that the online spend category is not excluded from the cashback benefit.

### Will I get the Swiggy One membership with Swiggy Ornge HDFC Credit Card every year?
No, the complimentary Swiggy One membership is only given after card activation in the first card membership year.

### Does Swiggy Ornge HDFC Credit Card give cashback on UPI transactions?
No, since Swiggy Ornge HDFC Bank Credit Card is only offered on Mastercard network, and not on RuPay, it cannot be used to pay UPI payments and hence does not offer any cashback on such transactions.

### Does Swiggy Ornge HDFC Credit Card offer any airport lounge privileges?
No, Swiggy Ornge HDFC Bank Credit Card does not offer any complimentary airport lounge access to its cardholders.
