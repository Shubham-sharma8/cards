## About the Card
The card is a good option for consumers who transact on Swiggy frequently, and spend a significant amount on the brand’s platforms every month. For instance, those who use Instamart for monthly staples and groceries or use food delivery services several times a month can easily recover the Rs. 500 annual fee and also earn decent cashback. Cardholders who spend **Rs. 15,000 every month** on Swiggy platforms can earn an **annual cashback of Rs. 18,000** from Swiggy platforms alone. Cashback is directly credited into the card statement and adjusted against the outstanding due.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is Swiggy HDFC Bank Credit Card free?
No, Swiggy HDFC Credit Card charges a joining and annual fee of Rs. 500. However, you can avail annual fee waiver on spending Rs. 2 Lakh in a year. Additionally, the card offers a complimentary 3-month Swiggy One membership upon activation.

### Can I use HDFC Swiggy Credit Card for online shopping?
Yes, you can use Swiggy HDFC Credit Card for online shopping and earn 5% cashback on select partner categories, including apparel, department stores, entertainment, electronics, home décor, and more. However, the maximum cashback you can earn in this category is Rs. 1,500 per month.

### How do I apply for Swiggy HDFC Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online by visiting the official website of HDFC Bank and submitting the duly filled application form along with the required documents. Additionally, you can apply offline by visiting the nearest HDFC Bank branch and applying in person.

### Can I use HDFC Swiggy Credit Card for EMI transactions?
Yes, you can use this credit card for EMI transactions. However, before making an EMI payment, you should check whether the respective brand or e-commerce website offers an EMI option for this card.

### Does Swiggy HDFC Credit Card offer lounge access?
No, Swiggy HDFC Bank Credit Card does not offer any lounge access benefits.
