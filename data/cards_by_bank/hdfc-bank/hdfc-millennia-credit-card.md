## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Great core HDFC starter card for building HDFC upgrade history and top-10 merchant 5% cashback, even after losing direct lounge access.

**Best For:** Young salaried users wanting an easy Rs. 1L waiver core HDFC card for Amazon/Flipkart/Swiggy/Zomato/Uber (up to Rs. 20k/mo) & HDFC sales

### Cons (Bad)

## Frequently Asked Questions
### I already have an HDFC Credit Card. Can I apply for HDFC Millennia Credit Card?
HDFC Bank allows you to have two different HDFC Credit Cards in very rare cases, as in general, it does not allow the same. If you are keen to avail HDFC Millennia Credit Card, you can put forth a card upgrade request. For more details, you can contact the HDFC Bank Customer Care.

### How can I apply for HDFC Millennia Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card through online as well as offline modes. You can apply online by visiting the official website of the bank. If you want to apply offline, you can visit the nearest branch of the bank to fill out the application form. You can also apply for HDFC Millennia Credit card, with Issuer / Market.

### What is the credit limit offered on HDFC Millennia Credit Card?
The credit limit is decided by the bank after scrutinizing your application. The bank considers various factors like credit score, repayment capacity, income and more while deciding your credit limit. You will get to know your credit limit only after applying for the credit card.

### I have just started with my new job. Will I be eligible for HDFC Millennia Credit Card?
To be eligible for any credit card, you should be employed in your current organization for at least 6 months. Also, the minimum income requirement to apply for this credit card is Rs. 35,000 per month. However, if you have a salary account with HDFC Bank, the eligibility criteria may be relaxed.

### What is the cash advance fee charged on HDFC Millennia Credit Card?
The cash advance fee charged on HDFC Millennia Credit Card is 2.5% of the withdrawn amount or Rs. 500 (whichever is higher). Therefore, you should not withdraw cash using your credit card as it comes with additional charges.

### How can I see offers on my HDFC Millennia Credit Card?
You can see all the available offers on your credit card by logging in to the HDFC net banking. All you need to do is log in to the net banking portal and click on the ‘Offers’ section. Under this section, you’ll be able to see all the available offers on your credit card.

### How can I check the status of my HDFC Millennia Credit Card?
You can check the status of your credit card through the official website of the bank. You need to click on ‘Credit Card’ under the ‘Products’ tab and then click on the ‘Track your Credit Card Application Status’ option. Enter the asked details and the status of your application will be shown on the screen.

### What is the annual fee for HDFC Bank Millennia credit card?
The HDFC Millennia credit card comes with a joining and annual fee of Rs. 1,000 + applicable taxes. However, if you spends Rs. 1,00,000 in the previous year, the renewal fee will be waived.

### Does HDFC Millennia Credit Card give cashback on UPI (RuPay variant)?
Cashback on UPI can only be availed on Rupay variant. However, HDFC Millennia Credit Card is currently not available on RuPay variant. Hence, you cannot avail cashback benefits on the same.

### Why does HDFC reject Millennia applications even with good CIBIL scores?
A good credit score cannot be the only factor to get a credit card. There are other eligibility criteria that you need to meet for the application to be approved. Your application might have been rejected due to other reasons like insufficient income, a high-debt to income ratio, too many credit card applications among others.
