## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Solid core HDFC travel stepper toward Infinia, though HSBC TravelOne now beats it for pure miles & spend-free lounges.

**Best For:** HDFC Imperia/Preferred customers climbing toward Infinia/DCB who spend Rs. 4L–8L/year incl. Myntra/Nykaa/Reliance Digital/M&S

**Not Ideal For:** Low spenders wanting no-spend domestic lounge access (cut by Rs. 60k/qtr rule in 2026)

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 35,000 – Rs. 75,000

### Pros (Good)
- 5X rewards on Nykaa, Myntra, Marks & Spencer, and Reliance Digital
- Rewards on generally-excluded categories like insurance, utilities and education
- Multiple redemption options with good redemption value
- Travel benefits like Priority Pass Membership, complimentary lounge access, flight vouchers, etc.
- Vouchers worth Rs. 16,000 in a year as milestone benefits

### Cons (Bad)
- Accelerated earning is limited to select brands only
- Low redemption value on product/voucher purchase and cash conversion
- High spending threshold to make the most of milestone benefits

## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- Up to 10X reward points on SmartBuy spends (Capped at 3,000 points per month for brand voucher purchases on SmartBuy and capped at 4,000 points per month overall, including brand vouchers)
- Product and Voucher Purchase: Rs. 0.35
- 3 complimentary domestic lounge visits per quarter on min. Rs. 60,000 spends in the last quarter
- Spend Rs. 1.5 Lakh in a quarter and get Rs. 1,500 vouchers from Marks and Spencer, Reliance Digital, Myntra or Marriott

### Dining Benefits
- Complimentary Swiggy One Membership with dining offers on credit card

### Insurance Cover
- 5 reward points per Rs. 200 spent across all categories including insurance, utilities and education
- Accidental air death cover worth Rs. 1 Crore
- Credit Liability cover of up to Rs. 9 Lakh
- Emergency overseas hospitalization of up to Rs. 15 Lakh

### Get This Card If
- You are a high-spender and can make the most of rewards as well as milestone benefits
- You frequently shop at Nykaa, Myntra, Marks & Spencer and Reliance Digital
- You want versatile reward redemption options like air miles, products, vouchers, etc.
- You value travel perks like lounge access, flight vouchers, MMT membership, etc.
- You are looking for a card that offers good value-back on both online and offline spends

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How long are my reward points valid?
HDFC Regalia Gold reward points are valid for 2 years from the date of accrual.

### How can I avail the complimentary memberships?
Once you pay the joining fee and meet other eligibility requirements, you can claim the complimentary memberships through SmartBuy (for Regalia Gold).

### Are there any restrictions on redeeming points for flights or hotels?
Points can be redeemed for flights and hotel bookings through SmartBuy, but they must be redeemed for bookings within India and with participating airlines and hotel chains. HDFC Bank regularly updates the list of partner airlines and hotels, hence consumers must check beforehand.

### What happens if I do not meet the spend requirements for milestone benefits?
If you do not meet the spend requirements for milestone benefits (like vouchers or fee waiver), you will not be eligible for those specific benefits, but you will still earn reward points on regular spending.

### Does HDFC Bank provide lounge access to add-on cardholders on the Regalia Gold Credit Card?
Add-on cardholders of HDFC Regalia Gold Credit Card can get their own Priority Pass by calling the HDFC Bank phone banking numbers 1800 1600 or 1800 2600 (toll-free) and avail complimentary airport lounge access using it.

### What is the limit for HDFC Regalia Gold credit card?
The credit limit you get with your HDFC Regalia Credit Card depends on your income, current debts, maximum limit on your existing credit cards, and several other factors. You will get to know the limit offered to you only after you receive your credit card.
