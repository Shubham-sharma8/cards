## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** option from HDFC Bank suited for fuel users.

**Best For:** Regular petrol/diesel commuters matching the card's partner pump brand (BPCL/IOCL/HPCL)

**Not Ideal For:** EV drivers or users spending under Rs. 2,000/month on fuel

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 4,000 – Rs. 15,000 fuel/month

### Pros (Good)
- Annual fee (Rs. 500) waived on spending Rs. 50,000/year
- Backed by HDFC Bank digital card controls, EMI conversion & merchant offers

### Cons (Bad)
- No complimentary domestic or international airport lounge access
- High 3.5% (+GST) foreign currency markup — poor for overseas spends
- Check latest issuer exclusions on rent, utilities, education & wallet loads before high spends
