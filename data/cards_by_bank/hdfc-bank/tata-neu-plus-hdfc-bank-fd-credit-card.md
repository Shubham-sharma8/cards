## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is HDFC Tata Neu Plus Credit Card against FD?
HDFC Tata Neu Plus Credit Card against FD is a secured credit card backed by a minimum fixed deposit of Rs. 15,000. It is ideal for new-to-credit consumers and offers rewards, lounge access, and fuel benefits at low annual fees.

### How do I earn NeuCoins with this card?
Cardholders earn 2% NeuCoins on Tata Neu and partner brand spends, 5% on Tata Neu app/website after registering for Tata NeuPass, and 1% on non-Tata brands or UPI transactions. UPI NeuCoins are capped at 500 per month.

### What travel benefits does this card offer?
The card provides 4 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year, credited quarterly after spending Rs. 50,000 in the preceding quarter. International lounge access is not included. This adds convenience for frequent domestic travelers.

### Who should consider getting this card?
This card is suited for consumers new to credit or seeking a secured card, Tata brand loyalists, and those wanting extra NeuCoins, lounge access, and fuel savings. Annual fee waivers are available with Rs. 1 lakh yearly spending.

### Are there any fees or charges associated?
The card has a joining fee and annual fee of Rs. 499 plus taxes. Finance charges are 3.75% per month (45% p.a.). Late payment fees range from Rs. 100 to Rs. 1,300, depending on outstanding amounts.
