## About the Card
6E Rewards XL IndiGo HDFC Credit Card is a co-branded travel credit card designed for consumers who prefer flying with IndiGo. Cardholders can earn up to 5% 6E Rewards on IndiGo spends and redeem them against flight bookings and 6E Prime add-ons through IndiGo at a 1:1 ratio, allowing them to save on travel expenses. Besides this, unlike many other travel credit cards, this card also offers accelerated rewards (3%) on everyday spending categories, including dining, groceries, and entertainment. The card justifies its Rs. 1,500 joining fee by offering an IndiGo flight ticket worth Rs. 3,000 along with 6E Prime add-ons worth Rs. 1,099 for services like priority check-in, complimentary meal, and seat selection. Additionally, travel benefits such as complimentary domestic lounge access (8 visits per year) and a low forex markup fee of 2.5% enhance the card’s overall value for frequent travellers. However, the lack of international lounge access might disappoint frequent international travellers. At this annual fee, many consumers would expect at least a few international lounge visits. If you are an IndiGo loyalist looking for a credit card that offers decent value back on both travel and everyday spends, this credit card can be a great choice. However, if you are not loyal to a specific airline, cards like Axis Atlas or Axis Horizon may offer better overall value.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is 6E Rewards Program?
6E Rewards is the rewards program of IndiGo Airlines through which cardholders can earn rewards on every purchase and then redeem the same to avail privileges across travel and other partner brands. Please note that the 6E Rewards Program is not a frequent flyer program.

### Where can I redeem the 6E Reward Points earned via 6E Rewards XL IndiGo HDFC Bank Credit Card?
The rewards can be redeemed only after logging into the membership program. If your mobile number and email ID are verified, you may use the reward points to make purchases through the website.

### Are 6E Rewards earned on 6E Rewards XL IndiGo HDFC Bank Credit Card valid for lifetime?
No, 6E Reward Points are valid only up to 2 years from the date of accrual.

### Is there any annual fee waiver facility on 6E Rewards XL IndiGo HDFC Bank Credit Card?
No, there is no annual fee waiver on the 6E Rewards XL IndiGo HDFC Bank Credit Card.

### How do I check the application status for the 6E Rewards XL IndiGo HDFC Bank Credit Card?
After successful application, an application reference number will be shared with you. You can use the same on HDFC credit card application status page or IndiGo’s page to see how far your application process has come.
