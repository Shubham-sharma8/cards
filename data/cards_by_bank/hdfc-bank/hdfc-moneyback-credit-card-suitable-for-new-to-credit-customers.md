## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for HDFC MoneyBack Credit Card?
+- You can apply for HDFC MoneyBack Credit Card via both online and offline modes. If you want to apply offline, then you can visit the nearest branch of the bank to fill out the application form. However, to apply online, you can visit the official website of HDFC Bank.

### What is HDFC MoneyBack Credit Card Limit?
+- The credit limit is decided by the bank on several factors, such as credit score, annual income and cardholder’s age. It is disclosed only after applying for the credit card. Once you apply for the credit card and your application is approved, the bank will communicate the credit limit to you.

### How can I redeem HDFC MoneyBack Credit Card reward points into cash?
+- You can redeem your earned reward points into cash via internet banking. Under the redemption portal after logging in, you will see an option of converting reward points into cash. You can click on that to redeem your reward points against cashback. The cash credit is usually credited within 7 working days.

### What is the interest-free period offered on HDFC MoneyBack Credit Card?
+- The bank offers an interest-free credit period of up to **50 days** on HDFC MoneyBack Credit Card. The interest-free period is offered from the date of purchase and subject to the submission of charge by the merchant.
