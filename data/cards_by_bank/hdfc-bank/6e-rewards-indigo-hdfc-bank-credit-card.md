## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is 6E Rewards Program?
6E Rewards Program has been started by IndiGo in association with HDFC Bank in which a cardholder can earn rewards on making all purchases. These reward points can be redeemed to avail special privileges across travel and other partner brands.

### Is 6E Rewards Program an alternative to the Frequent Flyer Program?
6E Rewards Program is not a frequent flyer program. You can use these reward points to get discounts on IndiGo flight tickets.

### Where can I redeem the 6E Reward Points?
You can redeem the 6E Rewards by logging into the membership program. You will have to verify your mobile number and email id to make purchases through the IndiGo portal.

### What is the validity of reward points earned under the 6E Rewards Program?
Reward Points earned under the 6E Rewards Program are valid for a duration of 2 years from the date of accrual.
