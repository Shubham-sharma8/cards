## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the IOB ZET Credit Card?
IOB ZET Credit Card is a secured credit card issued against a fixed deposit of Rs. 2,500. It is designed for consumers who are looking to start or build their credit history.

### What is the FD requirement for the IOB ZET Credit Card?
The minimum fixed deposit requirement for the IOB ZET Credit Card is Rs. 2,500. The card is issued against this deposit, and the credit limit can be up to 90% of the FD amount.

### What cashback does the IOB ZET Credit Card offer?
The card offers 2% cashback on eligible UPI transactions and up to 15% cashback on selected brands and platforms. Cashback is provided as ZET Coins and has monthly limits.

### Does the IOB ZET Credit Card offer discounts?
Yes, the card offers discounts on Swiggy, Zomato, Swiggy Instamart and BookMyShow. The discounts are subject to minimum transaction values, monthly limits and applicable terms and conditions.

### Does the IOB ZET Credit Card offer airport lounge access?
No, the IOB ZET Credit Card does not provide airport lounge access. Therefore, consumers specifically looking for complimentary domestic or international airport lounge benefits may need to consider other credit cards.

### What are the fees for the IOB ZET Credit Card?
The card has no joining or annual fee. However, finance charges are applicable at 3.25% per month, while late payment charges depend on the outstanding amount.

### What is the forex markup on the IOB ZET Credit Card?
The IOB ZET Credit Card carries a foreign exchange markup fee of 2.49%. This applies to eligible international transactions and is lower than the markup charged by many cards.
