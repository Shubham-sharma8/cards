## About the Card
Apart from rewards, this credit card also offers **8 complimentary domestic lounge visits every year**. Other benefits like fuel surcharge waiver and **up to 20% dining discount at partner restaurants** further enhance its value. Like the platinum variant, this credit card also comes with no joining or annual fees. However, despite being an upgraded variant, the Signature Credit Card offers the **same reward rate as the base variant**. The key differentiator is only the **addition of complimentary lounge access**. Overall, this card is a decent option for consumers seeking to save on LIC premium payments while availing basic travel benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply online for LIC Signature Credit Card?
You can apply online for this credit card through the official website of LIC Cards or Axis Bank. All you need to do is look out for this credit card and click on ‘Apply Now’. After this, you will be redirected to the application page. You are required to submit the application form along with relevant documents to apply for this credit card.

### Can I earn fuel surcharge waiver on every transaction via LIC Signature Credit Card?
You can earn 1% fuel surcharge waiver on your fuel transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000. This is applicable across all petrol pumps in India. However, you can earn a maximum waiver of Rs. 400 in a calendar month.

### What is the forex markup fee charged on LIC Signature Credit Card?
The foreign currency transaction fee charged on this credit card is 3.5% of the transaction amount. Therefore, this is not an ideal option for international transactions as it comes with a high forex markup fee.

### Can I get an add-on card with LIC Signature Credit Card?
Yes, you can get up to three add-on cards with LIC Signature Credit Card. Also, just like the primary card, the add-on cards come with no joining and annual fees.

### Can I withdraw cash using my LIC Signature Credit Card?
Yes, you can withdraw cash using your Axis Bank LIC Signature Credit Card. However, a fee of 2.5% of the transaction amount or a minimum fee of Rs. 500 will be charged for withdrawing cash using your credit card. Therefore, it is advisable not to withdraw cash using your credit card as it incurs higher charges.

### What is the validity of reward points earned via LIC Signature Credit Card?
The reward points earned on LIC credit cards are valid for a period of 12 months or one year. For an instance, the reward points earned on 5 Jan 2024 will be valid till 5 Jan 2025. After this date, your reward points will become null and you won’t be able to redeem them.
