## About the Card
LIC Axis Bank Platinum Credit Card is the base variant among the two co-branded credit cards offered by Axis Bank and LIC. This credit card is suitable for beginners looking for benefits around LIC premium payments. Cardholders can earn **2X (2 points per Rs. 100) on LIC premium payments** along with the **international transactions**. On other spends, the card offers a **base reward rate of 1 point per Rs. 100 spent**. While the main USP of this credit card is the **accelerated rewards on LIC premium payments** and international transactions. However, being a **lifetime free entry-level card** , the reward structure offered on this card is basic. Also, in comparison to other rewards credit cards that allow redemption across multiple channels, this card allows **reward redemption only across selected products** mentioned in the LIC credit card rewards catalogue. However, not many cards offer rewards around LIC payments. Overll, this card is a **beginner-friendly rewards credit card** with inclined benefits towards LIC payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Which credit card is best for LIC payments?
While not many cards offer benefits around LIC payments, Axis Bank offers co-branded credit cards with LIC Cards, which could be a suitable pick in this category. One such card is LIC Axis Bank Platinum Credit Card, which offers 2 reward points for every Rs. 100 spent on LIC premium payments.

### How can I redeem the reward points earned on LIC Axis Bank Platinum Credit Card?
You can redeem the reward points on this card against the products listed in the LIC rewards points catalogue. In order to redeem the reward points, you can contact customer care at 1800 419 0064.

### What is the validity of reward points offered on LIC Axis Bank Platinum Credit Card?
All reward points earned on this card are valid for one year from the date of accumulation. In case the reward points are not used in this duration, they will expire.

### How do I pay my LIC Axis Bank Platinum Credit Card bill?
You can pay your LIC Axis Bank Platinum Credit Card bill via NEFT, Visa Money Transfer, Axis Bank mobile app, or via the LIC cards website. You can also pay offline via cash payment at your nearest Axis Bank branch or via cheque/draft.

### Can I avail EMI facility with LIC Axis Bank Platinum Credit Card?
Yes, you can convert purchases of Rs. 2,500 or more into easy EMIs using this credit card. This feature helps you manage big-ticket expenses more conveniently.
