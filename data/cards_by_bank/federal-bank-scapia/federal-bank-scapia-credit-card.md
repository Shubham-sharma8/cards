## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Leading 0% forex LTF travel card alongside AU Ixigo & IDFC WOW/Wealth — note Rs. 20k/cycle spend needed for lounges in 2026.

**Best For:** International travelers wanting pure 0% forex markup on LTF card, plus domestic flyers meeting Rs. 20k/cycle spend

**Not Ideal For:** Users wanting rewards on forex spends (IDFC Wealth/AU Ixigo give rewards ON forex) or international Priority Pass lounges

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 20,000+ in travel months (for unlimited domestic lounge)

### Pros (Good)
- Zero forex mark-up fee on international spends
- Up to 20% Scapia coins on card transactions without any capping
- Unlimited domestic airport lounge access with discount on airport spends
- Complimentary RuPay card with 5% rewards on UPI transactions
- No joining or renewal charges

### Cons (Bad)
- Scapia coins redemption limited to just travel bookings on Scapia app
- Low Scapia coins redemption ratio of 5:1
- No reward points on international spends
- No complimentary international airport lounge access
