## About the Card
YES Bank’s premium credit card – Marquee is tailored to the needs of high-income individuals. Much like other premium cards, YES Marquee also offers benefits across multiple categories, with more focus on rewards and travel. With a generous sign-up bonus, unlimited lounge access, and a competitive value back of up to 4.5%, the card stands as a worthy competitor to premium all-rounder credit cards like HDFC Diners Club Black, Standard Chartered Ultimate and ICICI Emeralde Private Credit Card. Read on to know about its features and benefits, followed by a review.

## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Powerful 4.5% online / 1% forex card with great BMS BOGO, though domestic lounge spend criteria make it less attractive for light flyers.

**Best For:** High online & international spenders wanting 1% low forex, BOGO up to Rs. 800 on BookMyShow (3/mo), and unlimited international lounges

**Not Ideal For:** Low quarterly spenders wanting spend-free domestic lounges

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 50,000+

### Pros (Good)
- Impressive reward rate of up to 4.5%
- Accelerated earning on all online spends, irrespective of category/brand
- Unlimited international lounge visits for primary and add-on cardholder
- Low forex mark-up fee of 1%
- Save up to Rs. 2,400 on movie tickets per month through BookMyShow

### Cons (Bad)
- Limited reward redemption options
- Low reward redemption ratio of 4:1

- **Min. CIBIL Score:** 730

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What options do I have for redeeming YES Marquee Credit Card reward points?
You can redeem the accumulated reward points through YES Rewardz Portal for travel booking, miles conversion (with select airlines), vouchers and merchandise.

### Are there any spend-based eligibility conditions for availing lounge access on YES Marquee Credit Card?
Yes. To be eligible for the complimentary lounge access benefit, cardholders must spend Rs. 1 Lakh in the preceding quarter. Fee payments, reversals/cancelled transactions, rental and wallet transactions will not qualify for the spend threshold.

### I already have YES First Preferred Credit Card. Can I upgrade it to Marquee?
You can get an upgrade on your existing credit card depending on your eligibility and other internal policies of YES Bank. You can contact your relationship manager or speak to a customer care representative to get more clarity on this.
