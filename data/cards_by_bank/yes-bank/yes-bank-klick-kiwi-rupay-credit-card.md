## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I earn reward points with the Klick Credit Card?
You earn 2 reward points for every Rs. 200 spent on non-UPI transactions. This makes it ideal for offline shopping and daily expenses.

### What travel benefits does the Klick Credit Card offer?
The card charges a low forex markup fee of 2.75%, making it cost-effective for international transactions. Additionally, it provides convenience and rewards for overseas travel and online shopping.

### Is there a fuel surcharge waiver on this credit card?
Yes. You get a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on all fuel purchases across stations, capped at Rs. 250 per month.

### What cashback benefits are offered on UPI and online payments?
Paying with the Klick Credit Card on Kiwi gives 1.5% cashback on UPI Scan & Pay transactions and 0.5% cashback on online payments, maximizing savings on digital transactions.

### How can I apply for the Klick Credit Card?
You can apply by completing the KYC process via the Kiwi app. The card is entirely virtual, making it easy to access rewards and benefits without visiting a branch.
