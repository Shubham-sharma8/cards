## About the Card
As part of the welcome benefits, consumers can earn 500 POPcoins on activation, vouchers worth Rs. 750, a 3-month Zomato Gold membership, and a 6-month PharmEasy Plus membership. Additional features include a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000, capped at Rs. 300 per month.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for YES BANK POP-CLUB Credit Card?
You can apply for YES BANK POP-CLUB Credit Card online through the official YES BANK website or partner platforms. Alternatively, you can visit the nearest YES BANK branch and submit the required documents along with your application form.

### How can I contact YES BANK Credit Card Customer Care?
You can contact YES BANK Credit Card Customer Care by calling their toll-free helpline at 1800 103 1212 or through the number mentioned on the back of your credit card. You can also reach out via email or visit the nearest YES BANK branch for assistance.

### Who is eligible to apply for YES BANK POP-CLUB Credit Card?
Salaried or self-employed Indian residents aged between 21 and 60 years can apply for the YES BANK POP-CLUB Credit Card. Applicants should have a stable income and a good credit score as per the bank’s criteria.

### How can I earn and redeem reward points on POP-CLUB Credit Card?
You can earn reward points on every eligible spend made using your POP-CLUB Credit Card. These points can be redeemed through the POP-CLUB app.

### What are the joining and annual fees for the YES BANK POP-CLUB Credit Card?
YES BANK POP-CLUB Credit Card comes with no joining fee. However, it charges an annual fee of Rs. 399 plus applicable taxes from second year onwards.

### Can I convert my large purchases into EMIs with this credit card?
Yes, you can convert your high-value purchases into easy monthly installments (EMIs) through YES BANK’s EMI conversion facility. This helps manage your expenses conveniently with flexible tenure options.

### Does POP-CLUB Credit Card come with travel or airport lounge benefits?
Currently, YES BANK POP-CLUB Credit Card focuses more on lifestyle and entertainment benefits rather than travel perks. It does not offer complimentary airport lounge access or travel-related privileges.
