## About the Card
YES First Business Credit Card is a business-oriented credit card that is specifically designed for self-employed professionals. This card offers up to 16 reward points for every Rs. 200 spent on multiple categories and 4 reward points per Rs. 200 on select categories. Besides this, cardholders can also earn up to 5X reward points on select spends by opting for the Rewards Subscription Plan at an annual fee of up to Rs. 5,000. For frequent travellers, this card offers 8 complimentary domestic & 6 international lounge visits per year. It also charges a low forex markup fee of 1.75%, enhancing its overall value for overseas travellers. The card not only offers travel benefits but also comes with lifestyle benefits like free golf lessons, and milestone benefits of up to 20,000 reward points and more. Overall, this credit card is a decent choice for business owners looking to maximize their savings on everyday spends while availing decent travel & lifestyle benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who is eligible for YES First Business Credit Card?
YES First Business Credit Card is available to self-employed professionals between 21 and 70 years of age. Applicants must have a minimum taxable income or ITR of Rs. 10 Lakh, along with valid business and income documents.

### What kind of reward points can cardholders earn?
**Cardholders can earn up to 16 reward points on Rs. 200 spent on all categories except select categories. Additional benefits include 2X rewards on travel, dining, and vehicle rentals, and up to 5X reward points on fashion and healthcare spends through optional Reward Subscription Plans.

### Are there any golf or lifestyle privileges included in YES First Business Credit Card?
Yes, YES First Business Credit Card provides premium lifestyle benefits such as 12 complimentary golf lessons annually and 4 free rounds of green fee per year. These benefits are ideal for professionals who value leisure.

### How can cardholders get the joining and annual fees waived?
The joining fee is waived if the cardholder spends Rs. 20,000 within the first month of card issuance. The annual fee is waived on achieving total annual spends of Rs. 3 Lakh.

### Is enrolling in the Reward Subscription Plan compulsory?
No, the Reward Subscription Plan is optional. However, opting for the plan allows cardholders to earn significantly higher reward points, including up to 3X rewards on multiple categories and up to 5X rewards on fashion and healthcare spends.
