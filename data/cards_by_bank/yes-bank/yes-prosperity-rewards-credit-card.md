## About the Card
YES Prosperity Rewards Credit Card is a decent choice for consumers who want to start their credit journey with an entry-level card that offers basic benefits. The card offers **2 reward points for every Rs. 200 spent** on retail purchases and **1 reward point per Rs. 200 spent** on select categories, and additional rewards through subscription plans. Besides this, it also offers basic benefits such as a fuel surcharge waiver and insurance coverage. Overall, this credit card turns out to be a suitable option for consumers seeking a low annual fee card with benefits on everyday spends. Note:_This credit card has been discountinued. ## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for YES Prosperity Rewards Credit Card?
If you want to apply for YES Prosperity Rewards Credit Card, you can apply through both offline and online modes. Visit the official website of the bank and fill out the application form, or you can also visit your nearest YES Bank branch to fill out your application in offline mode.

### Does YES BANK Prosperity Rewards Credit Card have lounge access?
No, YES BANK Prosperity Rewards Credit Card does not offer any airport lounge access benefits, as it is an entry-level reward credit card and does not include any travel benefits apart from the low forex markup fee.

### What are the benefits of YES Prosperity Rewards Credit Card?
YES BANK Prosperity Reward Credit Card offers reward points on everyday spends. Besides this, it also comes with other benefits, such as a low forex markup fee of 3.40%, fuel surcharge waiver and joining/annual fee waiver.

### What is the annual fee for YES Prosperity Rewards Credit Card?
YES BANK Prosperity Rewards Credit Card comes with a joining and annual fee of Rs. 499 + applicable taxes, with a renewal fee waived off on spending of Rs. 1 Lakh.

### What is the difference between YES BANK Prosperity Rewards and YES BANK Prosperity Business Cards?
he major difference between YES BANK Prosperity Rewards and YES BANK Prosperity Business Credit Cards is that the Prosperity Rewards card mainly focuses on reward points and lifestyle benefits, whereas YES BANK Prosperity Business Cards focus on lifestyle and travel benefits.
