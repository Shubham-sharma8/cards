## About the Card
The card also offers **up to 8 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year** for frequent travellers. In addition, it is a lifetime free credit card, which means cardholders do not have to pay joining or annual fees. The card includes basic features such as fuel surcharge waiver and UPI payments.
