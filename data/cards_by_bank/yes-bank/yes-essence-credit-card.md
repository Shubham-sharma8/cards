## About the Card
YES Essence Credit Card is designed for consumers who frequently spend online on dining, travel, fashion, beauty and groceries. Cardholders can earn up to **12 YES Rewardz per Rs. 200** on eligible online spends across select categories and **8 YES Rewardz per Rs. 200** on other online shopping spends. The card also offers a base reward rate of **4 YES Rewardz per Rs. 200** on offline spends.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the reward rate on YES Essence Credit Card?
YES Essence Credit Card offers 12 YES Rewardz per Rs. 200 on eligible online spends across fashion, beauty, travel, dining and grocery. It offers 8 YES Rewardz per Rs. 200 on other eligible online shopping spends and 4 YES Rewardz per Rs. 200 on offline spends.

### Does YES Essence Credit Card offer airport lounge access?
Yes. Cardholders can avail 1 complimentary domestic airport lounge access per quarter by spending Rs. 25,000 or more in the previous quarter.

### What are the milestone benefits offered by YES Essence Credit Card?
Cardholders can earn 5,000 bonus YES Rewardz on spending Rs. 50,000 within the first 90 days of card setup. An additional 10,000 bonus YES Rewardz can be earned on annual spends of Rs. 1 lakh.

### Does YES Essence Credit Card offer golf benefits?
Yes. The card offers 4 green fee waivers and 12 complimentary golf lessons per year, subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

### Can the joining and annual fees of YES Essence Credit Card be waived?
Yes. The joining fee can be reversed on spending Rs. 20,000 within one month of card setup. The annual fee can be reversed on spending Rs. 1 lakh in the previous year.

### What other lifestyle benefits does YES Essence Credit Card offer?
The card offers several additional lifestyle benefits, including a complimentary ET Prime subscription with 3-month Sony LIV access, a Rs. 1,000 Myntra voucher, a women-centric health check-up during the birthday month and Buy 1 Get 1 coffee offers at select coffee outlets in malls.

### Who should get YES Essence Credit Card?
YES Essence Credit Card is best suited for consumers with who prefer to spend online and want to earn accelerated rewards while availing other benefits such as lounge access, golf games, movie offers, subscriptions and more.
