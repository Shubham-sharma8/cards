## About the Card
The YES BANK Wellness Plus Credit Card covers a distinct lifestyle category—health and wellness. The card is offered by YES BANK in collaboration with Aditya Birla Wellness Private Limited and is a premium variant of another card offered in this association—the YES BANK Wellness Credit Card. Though the type of benefits covered are similar, with the Plus variant the extent is higher. You get benefits around annual health check-ups, round-the-clock doctor or counselor helplines, in-studio or home-based workout sessions, and personalized diet plans. Based on its features around health check-ups and accelerated rewards at chemist/pharmaceutical stores, it comes in close competition with the Apollo SBI Credit Card Select, which comes at a similar fee and offers co-branded benefits with the Apollo network. If you have regular medical expenses and would also like to earn rewards on your medicine-related purchases, the YES BANK Wellness Plus card can be a good choice for you. However, if you think that you only need to go to the doctor once in a while and avail yourself of such complimentary benefits with your insurance plans, you could definitely get better options as per your spending habits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the ways for YES Bank credit card bill payment?
You can pay your YES Bank credit card bill via both offline and online modes through NEFT/IMPS/RTGS, Cash/Cheque/Demand Draft, Net Banking, YES Pay App. Existing Yes Bank account holders can also use ATM fund transfer and standing instructions facility.

### How can I generate a YES Bank credit card PIN?
To generate your credit card PIN, login to YES Bank Net Banking account or visit the nearest YES Bank ATM.

### Will I get international lounge benefits with my YES Bank Wellness Plus Credit Card?
No. YES Bank Wellness Credit Card does not offer international lounge access to the users. However you can avail domestic lounge access up to 2 visits per quarter. This is applicable for primary cardholders only.

### How to Block YES Bank Wellness Plus Credit Card, if lost?
In case your YES Bank Credit Card is lost or stolen, you need to block your card immediately. For this send an SMS with text, BLKCC to number 9840909000 via your registered mobile number.

### What is the Instant EMI facility on the Wellness Plus Credit Card?
YES Bank provides the instant EMI privilege on this credit card. Via this facility, you can convert purchases above Rs. 2,500 into easy EMIs for a repayment tenure of 3 to 24 months. The interest rate charged on the EMIs range from 12% to 15% per annum.
