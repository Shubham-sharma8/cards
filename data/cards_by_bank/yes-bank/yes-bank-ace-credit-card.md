## About the Card
YES Bank ACE Credit Card is an entry-level credit card best suited for earning reward points on everyday spends. The card offers **4X rewards (8 points per Rs. 200 spent) on online shopping**. Besides this, cardholders can earn 2 points per Rs. 200 on select merchant spends and **2X reward points on other offline spends**. What sets this card apart is that, in addition to the regular rewards program, cardholders can earn **up to 5X reward points on their spends through subscription plans**. Although these subscription plans charge an annual fee of up to Rs. 2,000, they can help you save significantly on your everyday expenses. Additional benefits like joining and annual fee reversal and a fuel surcharge waiver further add to the card’s overall value. This card is a suitable option for new to credit individuals who are looking to maximize their benefits on both online and offline spends.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### My credit score is 350. Can I apply for YES Bank ACE Credit Card?
If your credit score is above 750, then there are high chances that your application for YES Bank ACE Credit Card will be approved. But if your credit score is 350, which is considered to be poor, some issuers might reject your application.

### Is there a fuel surcharge fee on YES Bank ACE Credit Card?
No, YES Bank ACE Credit Card does not charge a fuel surcharge fee on your fuel spends between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000. You can avail a maximum waiver of Rs. 125 per month.

### How can I apply for YES Bank ACE Credit Card?
You can apply online by visiting the official website of YES Bank and submitting a duly filled application form along with relevant documents. You can also apply offline by visiting the nearest branch of the bank and submitting the application form in person.

### How much cash advance fee is charged on YES Bank ACE Credit Card?
If you have YES Bank ACE Credit Card, you will have to pay a cash advance fee of 2.5% of the transacted amount or a minimum of Rs. 300 (whichever is higher) which will be billed in the next monthly statement. The transaction fee is subject to change at the sole discretion of the bank.

### Can I make YES Bank ACE Credit Card bill payment via net banking?
Yes, if you are a YES Bank account holder, you can easily make credit card bill payment through net banking.

### I am 20 years old. Am I eligible to apply for YES Bank ACE Credit Card?
No, to apply for YES ACE Credit Card, you must be between 21 to 60 years old.

### What are the benefits of YES Bank ACE Credit Card?
YES Bank ACE Credit Card is primarily a rewards-focused credit card. It offers 3X rewards on online spends, 2X rewards on offline spends, and a base reward rate of 2 points per Rs. 200 spent on select merchant categories. In addition to this, the card comes with basic benefits such as joining & annual fee waiver, fuel surcharge waiver, and more.

### Does YES Bank ACE Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
No, YES Bank ACE Credit Card does not offer any lounge access benefits. However, it allows you to save on your online travel spends by offering accelerated reward points on online transactions.

### Is YES Bank ACE Credit Card available in RuPay or Visa?
YES Bank ACE Credit Card is available in both RuPay and Visa variants. You can choose the variant based on your eligibility & preference and earn benefits on both online & offline transactions.

### What is the minimum credit limit of YES Bank ACE Credit Card?
The credit limit for YES Bank ACE Credit Card is determined by the bank after assessing several factors, such as your monthly income, credit score, repayment history, and more. The final credit limit will be communicated to you once your application is approved.
