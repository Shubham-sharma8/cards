YES Bank Wellness Credit Card is one amongst the two health oriented credit cards that YES Bank launched with Aditya Birla Wellness Pvt. Ltd. The other variant in this league is YES Bank Wellness Plus Credit Card. You can earn a lot of benefits on self care, medical check ups, consultations and fitness sessions. ## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for YES Bank Wellness Credit Card?
This card is not sourced by Issuer / Market currently. Thereby, you can apply for YES Bank Wellness Credit Card directly at YES Bank’s official website or by visiting the nearest bank branch.

### How can I check the status of my YES Bank Wellness credit card application status?
You can easily check your application status by visiting the YES Bank Credit Card Application Status page, click here for the same. To check the application status you require information such as registered mobile number, reference or application number, date of birth and PAN number. To know more click here.

### How to make YES Bank credit card bill payment?
You can pay the YES Bank credit card bill via NEFT/IMPS, Cash/Cheque/Demand Draft, Net Banking and so on. Existing YES Bank account holders can also opt for standing instruction by linking their YES Bank savings or current account. You can check more details about YES Bank credit card bill payment here.

### Will I get airport lounge benefits with my YES Bank Wellness Credit Card?
No. YES Bank Wellness Credit Card does not offer any airport lounge access. You can get 2 complimentary domestic lounge visits with the Wellness Plus variant. Or, you can check our other travel credit cards in India or credit cards with airport lounge access to avail such benefits.

### Do I get unlimited access to fitness sessions with YES Bank Wellness Credit Card?
No, you only get 6 complimentary fitness sessions for Gym, Yoga, Zumba, etc. per month. With the Wellness Plus variant, you will get 12 complimentary sessions.
