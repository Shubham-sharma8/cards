## About the Card
Previously known as YES FIRST Exclusive** , the YES Reserv Credit Card is designed to offer all-round benefits to its users. It offers accelerated earnings on online spends and rewards on often-excluded categories like insurance and utilities, making it a solid option for high-spenders. Benefits like lounge access and low forex mark-up fee make this card a good choice for frequent travellers as well. However, beyond these benefits, the card lacks lifestyle benefits across movies and dining, which are commonly found in other mid-range cards. Read on to know the detailed features and benefits of YES Reserv followed by its review.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is YES FIRST Preferred better than YES Reserv Credit Card?
YES FIRST Preferred and YES Reserv have their own pros and cons. YES Reserv’s rewards program is more focused on online spends whereas YES FIRST Preferred offers the same reward rate across all eligible categories (except select categories where you get rewards at lower rate). Both the cards offer domestic and international lounge visits, low forex mark-up and golf benefits. However, they differ in important aspects like annual fees, eligibility criteria, and reward caps. Before choosing a card, you must see if the benefits align with your spending patterns.

### What is the credit limit on YES Reserv Credit Card?
The credit limit you get depends on several factors including your income, credit score, highest credit limit on your existing cards, and the bank’s internal policies. Your exact credit limit will be communicated only after the card is issued.

### What is YES Bank’s reward subscription plan?
In addition to the standard reward structure, YES Bank offers paid reward acceleration plans that allow cardholders to earn 2X, 3X, or even 5X rewards on their spends. These plans can be selected based on individual spending preferences, and the subscription fees vary depending on the card variant and the chosen plan.

### Is there an annual fee waiver condition on YES Reserv Credit Card?
Yes. You can get the annual fee waived off on spending Rs. 3 Lakh or more in the preceding year. However, if you cannot meet this spending condition, the annual fee will be charged and you will get 8,000 bonus reward points as renewal benefit.

### Are there any milestone benefits on YES Reserv Credit Card?
No, YES Reserv Credit Card does not offer any milestone benefits.

### How is YES Bank RESERV Credit Card?
YES Bank RESERV is an all-rounder credit card offering benefits across multiple categories such as shopping, travel, lifestyle, and more. The card offers 4X rewards on online spends, 2X rewards on offline spends, and 6 reward points per Rs. 200 spent on select categories. Additionally, travel benefits like a low forex markup fee, complimentary international and domestic lounge access make it a suitable option for frequent travellers.

### What is the forex markup fee charged on YES Bank RESERV Credit Card?
YES Bank RESERV Credit Card charges a forex markup fee of 1.75%, making it a good option for international spends.

### What are the fees for YES Bank RESERV Credit Card?
YES Bank RESERV Credit Card comes with a joining and annual fee of Rs. 2,499 + applicable taxes. However, the joining fee is waived if you spend Rs. 40,000 or more within the first 30 days. The annual fee is waived on spending Rs. 3 Lakh or more in a year.
