## About the Card
YES Bank SELECT Credit Card, **previously known as YES Prosperity EDGE Credit Card** , is a reward-focused card offering accelerated value-back on online spends. The card also offers rewards on categories that are often excluded such as utilities, education, insurance, etc. though at a much lower rate. However, while the earning rate is high, the redemption value of up to Rs. 0.25 brings down the overall value-back. Apart from its rewards program, the card extends travel and lifestyle benefits such as complimentary domestic lounge access and golf benefits. Read the detailed features and benefits of the card below, along with reviews, pros and cons.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the credit limit on YES Bank SELECT Credit Card?
The credit limit is determined basis your income, current debts, and several other internal policies of the bank. The credit limit assigned to you will be disclosed only when you receive the card.

### Does YES Bank SELECT Credit Card offer international lounge access?
No. There is no complimentary international lounge visit on YES Bank SELECT Credit Card.

### How to avail golf benefit on YES Bank SELECT Credit Card?
To book a golf game, you can call the toll free number for golf concierge – 1800-102-6263. You would not get golf lessons at all golf courses, so if you want to avail the complimentary lesson, you must choose the right golf course. Bookings must be made at least 5 days in advance. You can read the detailed terms and conditions on YES Bank’s website.

### I already have a YES Bank credit card. Can I apply for YES SELECT Credit Card?
No. Existing YES Bank credit cardholders are not eligible for this credit card.
