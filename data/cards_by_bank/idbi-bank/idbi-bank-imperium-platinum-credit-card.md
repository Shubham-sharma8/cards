## Frequently Asked Questions
### What rewards does IDBI Bank Imperium Platinum Credit Card offer?
IDBI Bank Imperium Platinum Credit Card offers 2 Delight Points per Rs. 150 spent on shopping, movies, travel and other lifestyle purchases for everyday spending.

### Is there any welcome benefit on IDBI Bank Imperium Platinum Credit Card?
IDBI Bank Imperium Platinum offers 500 Delight Points on Rs. 1,500 spend within 30 days or 300 points if spent between 31 and 90 days of activation.

### What are the fees for an IDBI Bank Imperium Platinum Credit Card?
IDBI Bank Imperium Platinum charges Rs. 499 plus taxes as joining and annual fee, which can be waived on annual spends of Rs. 75,000.

### Does this card offer fuel benefits?
Yes, the card offers 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000, capped at Rs. 300 per month.

### Who should apply for this card?
IDBI Bank Imperium Platinum Credit Card is suitable for new-to-credit individuals who are not eligible for unsecured cards, seeking basic rewards while building credit history.
