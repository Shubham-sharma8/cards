## About the Card
The card comes at a low annual fee of Rs. 199 along with a low waiver requirement of just Rs. 50,000 in a year. Considering the savings of up to 5% on fuel expenses, efficient use of the card can yield good returns to users. Benefits are also extended to other major everyday lifestyle expenses like grocery, utility bills, FASTag recharges and UPI spends. In comparison to other fuel credit cards, co-branded with HPCL like ICICI Bank HPCL Coral , this card has an edge due to these two factors: (i) it comes at the lowest possible fee, but offers a similar value-back percentage (ii) it does not keep the accelerated reward earning limited to fuel. The ICICI variant offers a lower savings of 3.5% and extended benefit to just movies and fuel surcharge waiver.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How do I use my Power credit card for UPI payments?
To use your credit card for UPI linked payments, ensure linking it to your preferred UPI app- any among BHIM, PhonPe, PayTm, Gpay, Freecharge, Slice, Cred & Mobikwik. Post linking, you will be able to scan any UPI enabled QR code and make a payment.

### What is the income eligibility criteria to apply for this card?
To apply for this card, you must have an annual income of Rs. 3 Lakh or more. Other important eligibility factors are: you must be at least 21 years old, must have a healthy credit score and acceptable credit utilization ratio.

### Do I get any complimentary lounge access on the card?
No, the base variant that is IDFC FIRST HPCL Power does not offer any lounge access. However, complimentary domestic lounge visits are a feature of the Plus variant.

### Can I pay rent using this card?
Yes you can pay rent using the IDFC FIRST HPCL Power Card, but a fee of 1% of the transaction value is applicable on such transactions. This is subject to a minimum of Rs. 249 per transaction.
