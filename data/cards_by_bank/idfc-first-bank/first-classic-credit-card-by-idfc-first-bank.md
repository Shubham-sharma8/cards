## About the Card
The IDFC First Classic Credit Card is a lifetime free credit card offering up to 10X rewards on your spends. It also offers welcome gift vouchers worth Rs. 500 and some premium privileges across categories like entertainment, lifestyle, etc. As an entry-level credit card, it is offering up to 2.5% returns, which is pretty good. It is best suited for those who are seeking a zero annual fee credit card, satisfactory rewards, low-interest rates, and good benefits. Read on to know more about this card.

## Good, Bad & Best For Whom

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for IDFC First Classic Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card on the IDFC FIRST Bank website, or you can visit your nearest IDFC Bank branch.

### What is the joining fee for IDFC First Classic Credit Card?
IDFC First Classic Credit Card is a lifetime free card with no joining/membership/annual fee applicable.

### Can I earn reward points on any online or offline transaction through IDFC First Classic Credit Card ?
Yes, you can earn up to 10X reward points on online and offline spends. But some transactions are not eligible for reward points, such as fuel spends, EMIs, and cash withdrawals.

### Which IDFC Classic Credit Card transactions can I convert into EMI?
Transactions that are over Rs. 2,500 can be converted into EMI post-purchases.

### Does IDFC First Classic Credit Card provide airport lounge access?
No, IDFC First Classic Credit Card does not provide any complimentary access to airport lounges. However, the cardholder gets 4 complimentary Railway lounge visits every quarter.
