## About the Card
IDFC FIRST RuPay Credit Card is a digital card that is issued against an existing IDFC FIRST Credit Card. Cardholders can link this card with UPI apps like Paytm, GPay, Slice, etc., and earn up to **3X reward points on their UPI spends**. The card offers 3X rewards on UPI spends above Rs. 2,000 and a base reward rate of **1 point per Rs. 150 on UPI spends below Rs. 2,000**. Additional, it also offers the base reward rate on usually excluded categories such as utility and insurance payments. Currently, existing IDFC FIRST Classic, FIRST Millennia, FIRST WOW!, FIRST Select, FIRST Wealth, FIRST Private, FIRST SWYP, Ashva, and Mayura credit cardholders can apply for this digital credit card and earn benefits on their UPI spends. Overall, existing IDFC cardholders looking for a card with decent benefits on UPI spends at an affordable annual fee, this card can be a suitable option.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for IDFC FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card?
IDFC FIRST RuPay Credit Card is offered only to existing IDFC FIRST credit cardholders. You can apply for this card online by visiting the card page on the bank’s official website and clicking on “Apply Now.” You’ll be required to submit basic details and complete the application process. Upon approval, you will receive the digital card instantly.

### Can I register IDFC FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card on the GPay app?
Yes, you can link your IDFC FIRST RuPay Credit Card with all major UPI apps, such as GPay, Paytm, Slice, Freecharge, CRED, PhonePe, Mobikwik, and more.

### How can I pay my IDFC FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card bill?
The payment for this digital credit card is combined with your primary card and reflected in the primary card’s account. You can pay your credit card bill through the IDFC FIRST Bank app, net banking, NEFT, or cheque.

### What is the credit limit offered on IDFC FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card?
The credit limit on this IDFC RuPay Credit Card is shared with your primary card. It remains the same and this card does not come with an additional or separate credit limit.

### Will I receive the physical IDFC FIRST RuPay Credit Card after application?
No, this is a digital credit card and is issued exclusively online. You will not receive any physical card.

### Which IDFC FIRST Bank Credit Cards are eligible for obtaining FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card?
Currently, select existing IDFC FIRST Bank Credit Card customers are eligible to get the FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card. The eligibility is determined by the bank based on your credit profile and card usage history.

### What are the benefits of IDFC RuPay Credit Card?
IDFC FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card offers benefits like UPI linkage for convenient payments, cashback/rewards on transactions, and seamless online usage.

### Is there any joining/annual fee for FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card?
Yes, IDFC FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card comes with a joining and annual fee of Rs. 199 plus applicable taxes.

### Are there cashback/rewards on UPI transactions with FIRST Digital RuPay Card?
Yes, you can earn reward points on eligible UPI transactions made through the FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card. The card offers 3 reward points per Rs. 150 on UPI spends above Rs. 2,000 and 1 reward point per Rs. 150 on UPI spends below Rs. 2,000.

### On which UPI apps can I register my FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card?
You can link your FIRST Digital RuPay Credit Card on popular UPI apps like BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm, provided your mobile number is registered with IDFC FIRST Bank.
