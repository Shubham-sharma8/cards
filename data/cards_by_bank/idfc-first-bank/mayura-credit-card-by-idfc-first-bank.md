## About the Card
IDFC FIRST Mayura Credit Card is a premium metal card designed to offer benefits across various categories, including travel, dining, shopping, and more. The card offers up to 10X rewards on monthly spends above Rs. 20,000. Since this card is targeted at high-income individuals, reaching this threshold may not be difficult, allowing cardholders to easily maximize their reward point earnings. However, the low redemption value of Rs. 0.25 per reward point significantly limits the actual value back—bringing it down to just 1.66%, even at the 10X reward rate, which is quite low for a card with an annual fee of Rs. 5,999. Additionally, the card does not offer any annual fee waiver, although you can earn cashback as a welcome benefit and bonus reward points on renewal, which partially compensate for the fee. On the other hand, the card offers excellent travel benefits like zero forex markup, complimentary domestic and international lounge visits, trip cancellation protection, and extensive travel insurance, making it a solid option for frequent travellers. These travel privileges, combined with lifestyle benefits such as complimentary golf rounds, free movie tickets, and rewards on shopping and other spends, make it an all-rounder credit card.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I avail complimentary airport lounge visits through IDFC FIRST Mayura Credit Card?
You can avail up to 16 complimentary domestic and 16 international airport lounge visits every year with this card. However, you can avail this benefit on spending Rs. 20,000 in the previous month.

### What is the forex markup fee on IDFC FIRST Mayura Credit Card?
IDFC FIRST Mayura Credit Card comes with zero forex markup fee, making it a suitable option for international transactions.

### How can I apply for IDFC FIRST Mayura Credit Card?
To apply for this credit card, you can visit any of the nearest branches of IDFC FIRST Bank and submit a duly filled application form in person. Alternatively, you can apply online via the official website by filling and submitting the application form digitally.

### Do reward points offered on IDFC FIRST Mayura Credit Card have an expiry?
No, the reward points earned on this credit card do not have an expiry period. You can redeem them as long as your credit card remains active.

### Does IDFC FIRST Mayura Credit Card offer an annual fee waiver?
No, this credit card does not offer an annual fee waiver. However, you can earn 7,500 reward points worth Rs. 1,875 on spending Rs. 8 Lakh in a year or 15,000 reward points worth Rs. 3,750 on spending Rs. 15 Lakh in a year.

### What is the credit limit offered on IDFC FIRST Mayura Credit Card?
The credit limit is determined by the bank based on multiple factors such as your credit score, repayment history, monthly income, and more. The assigned credit limit will be communicated to you upon approval of the card.
