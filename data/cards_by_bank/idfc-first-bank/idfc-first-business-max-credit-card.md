## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who should consider the IDFC FIRST Business Max Credit Card?
The card is ideal for small business owners, freelancers, and startups who may not qualify for unsecured business cards. Since it is backed by a fixed deposit starting at Rs. 10,000, it helps consumers build business credit while availing lifetime free card benefits and low finance charges of 12% p.a.

### How rewarding is the card for regular business spending?
Cardholders can earn 6 reward points per Rs. 200 spent on domestic online purchases and 4 reward points on offline transactions. UPI payments above Rs. 2,000 earn 3 points per Rs. 200.

### Does the card offer any travel-related benefits for business owners?
Yes, the card comes with a low forex markup fee of 1.5%, making international transactions more affordable. Consumers can also get 20% bonus reward points on hotel bookings and 10% bonus reward points on flight bookings made through the IDFC FIRST Bank mobile app.

### Are there any annual or joining charges on the card?
No, the IDFC FIRST Business Max Credit Card is completely lifetime free with zero joining and annual fees.

### Can purchases be converted into EMIs with this card?
Yes, transactions above Rs. 1,500 can be converted into easy EMIs.
