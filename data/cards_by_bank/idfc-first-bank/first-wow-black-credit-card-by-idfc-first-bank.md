## About the Card
IDFC FIRST WOW! Black is a secured card with up to **16.7% value-back on travel spends**. This card primarily caters to frequent travellers with accelerated rewards on travel spends, complimentary lounge access & zero forex markup fee. The card offers **50 bonus points** for every Rs. 150 spent on hotels & 30 bonus points for every Rs. 150 spent on flight bookings through IDFC FIRST’s mobile app. Cardholders can redeem the accumulated reward points at a value of **1 RP = Rs. 0.50** against travel bookings and save significantly on their travel spends. Besides travel, this credit card also offers accelerated reward points on other online & offline spends, along with a base reward rate on often-excluded categories, such as insurance and utility bills. Another thing that makes this card stand out is the joining benefits. At a joining fee of Rs. 750, this card offers **joining benefits worth more than Rs. 5,000** through complimentary memberships, bonus cashback, and discounts on travel bookings.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is IDFC FIRST WOW! Black Credit Card?
IDFC FIRST WOW! Black is a secured credit card primarily focused on travel & lifestyle, offering value on everyday purchases. Additionally, this card offers benefits such as a UPI-enabled RuPay virtual card, accelerated reward points on online/offline spends, making it a decent choice for consumers who want to start their credit journey with a secured card.

### Is there any annual fee for IDFC FIRST WOW! Black Credit Card?
Yes, IDFC FIRST WOW! Black Credit Card comes with a Rs. 750 annual fee + Taxes, which can be waived off on spending Rs. 1.5 Lakh in a year, making this card a cost-effective choice for the regular consumers.

### Can I convert large purchases into EMIs on this card?
Yes, transactions can be converted into EMIs at the bank’s applicable interest rate. The card also offers 5% cashback up to Rs. 1,000 on the first EMI transaction made within 30 days.

### Who is eligible to apply for IDFC FIRST WOW! Black Credit Card?
Indian residents planning to start their credit journey can apply for this credit card. You are required to open an FD account with a minimum of Rs. 20,000 to avail this credit card.

### How can I apply for IDFC FIRST WOW! Black Credit Card?
The application can be completed online through the IDFC FIRST Bank website by opening an FD account, or you can also visit the nearest branch.
