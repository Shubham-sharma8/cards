## About the Card
IDFC FIRST Ashva Credit Card is a semi-premium metal card that offers benefits across multiple spending categories, including travel, shopping, entertainment, and more. The card offers up to 10X reward points on your spends, which appears to be quite a good reward rate. However, this rate is applicable only on spends above Rs. 20,000 every month. Moreover, with a redemption ratio of 4:1 (1 RP = Rs. 0.25), the maximum value back turns out to be just 1.66%, which is relatively low for a card in this segment. Targeted towards high-net-worth individuals, this card primarily offers travel and lifestyle benefits. Apart from rewards, you get complimentary airport lounge access on spending a minimum of Rs. 20,000 in the previous month, which is easily achievable for an average spender. However, to maximize the golf benefits, you need to spend double this amount each month. On the positive side, Visa Infinite lifestyle privileges offered with this card align well with its premium positioning. These benefits enhance your travel experience with features like discounts on airport meet & greet services, personalized travel concierge, and more. Other features like zero forex markup, free movie tickets, railway lounge access, and comprehensive insurance coverage further add to the card’s overall value back on lifestyle-related expenses.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for IDFC FIRST Ashva Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online by visiting the official website of IDFC FIRST Bank and submitting a duly filled application form along with the relevant documents. Besides this, you can also visit any of the bank’s nearest branches and speak to an executive about the offline application process.

### Can I earn reward points on fuel spends with IDFC FIRST Ashva Credit Card?
No, you cannot earn reward points on fuel spends with this credit card. However, you can save up to Rs. 300 per month on fuel transactions through a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on spends between Rs. 200 and Rs. 5,000.

### Are there any exclusions for earning 10X rewards on IDFC FIRST Ashva Credit Card?
Yes, the following categories are excluded from earning 10X reward points: education, wallet loads, government payments, rent and property management/purchase transactions, utilities, insurance, EMIs, cash withdrawals, fuel, and fees & charges.

### What is the joining fee for IDFC FIRST Ashva Credit Card?
The joining and annual fee for IDFC FIRST Ashva Credit Card is Rs. 2,999. However, you can earn welcome benefits worth Rs. 2,000 by earning Rs. 500 cashback on each of the 4 transactions worth Rs. 500 or more, made within 60 days of card generation.

### Does IDFC FIRST Ashva Credit Card offer airport lounge access benefits?
Yes, the card offers up to 16 complimentary visits per year to select domestic airport lounges and spas in India, and up to 8 complimentary visits to international airport lounges per year. However, these benefits can be availed only if you spend Rs. 20,000 in the previous month.

### What is the validity of reward points earned on IDFC FIRST Ashva Credit Card?
The reward points earned on this credit card do not expire, meaning they remain valid as long as your credit card is active.
