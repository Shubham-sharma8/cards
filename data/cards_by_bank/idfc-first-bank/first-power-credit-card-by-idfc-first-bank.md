## Frequently Asked Questions
### How is IDFC FIRST Power Plus Credit Card?
The IDFC FIRST Power Plus Credit Card is a good choice for beginners who are also HPCL fuel station loyalists. The card offers up to 6.5% savings on fuel but also accelerated 5% savings on everyday transactions like groceries and utilities. However, to maximize benefits, users will have to shift their other brand preferences.

### Who is eligible for the FIRST Power Credit card?
The IDFC FIRST Power Plus credit card is offered to individuals aged at least 21 years, with a stable source of income of a minimum of Rs. 3 lakh every year and a good credit score.

### What is the cashback feature on the IDFC FIRST Power Plus Credit Card?
The cashback on the IDFC FIRST Power Plus credit card is offered in the first year under the activation benefits. On the first fuel transaction done within the first 30 days of card set-up, card users earn Rs. 500 cashback, subject to a minimum transaction value of Rs. 500.

### Is there any spend capping to earn rewards on this card?
Maximum spends capping of Rs. 12,000 per statement cycle for HPCL fuel spends; Rs. 10,000 per statement cycle on HP Pay App; Rs. 2,000 per statement cycle on grocery and utilities; Rs. 1,000 spends per statement cycle on IDFC FIRST FASTag Recharge.

### How much is the value of IDFC Power Plus card points?
The per reward value on IDFC FIRST Power Plus credit card is Rs. 0.25.

### Does the power plus credit card offer lounge access?
Yes, the card offers access to domestic airport lounges, 1 visit every quarter on minimum monthly spends of Rs. 20,000.

### What is the difference between IDFC FIRST Power and Power Plus credit card?
IDFC FIRST Power and Power Plus are two co-branded fuel credit cards. The base variant, Power offers a return of up to 5% on fuel spends, in comparison to 6.5% value-back offered on the Power Plus card.

### How to get IDFC First Power RuPay Credit Card?
IDFC FIRST Bank is a credit card partner with Issuer / Market. To apply for this card you can fill the form on the top of this page by providing your mobile number.
