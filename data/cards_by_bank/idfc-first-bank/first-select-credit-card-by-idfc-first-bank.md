## Frequently Asked Questions
### How is IDFC FIRST Select credit card?
IDFC FIRST Select credit card is a good choice for beginners seeking a lifetime free credit card with reward benefits. High spenders and frequent travellers can also maximise their value-back through uncapped rewards, along with higher savings and discounts on travel, dining and movie spends.

### What is the difference between the IDFC FIRST Wealth credit card and the FIRST Select credit card?
Among IDFC FIRST Select and IDFC Wealth, the latter is a premium variant based on the features and benefits. While both cards are lifetime free, Wealth comes with a higher income eligibility requirement of Rs. 36 Lakh per annum, in comparison to Rs. 12 Lakh per annum on Select. Wealth also offers a lower forex markup fee, a better reward rate, and other lifestyle benefits.

### What is the forex markup fee for IDFC FIRST Select credit card?
The forex mark-up fee charged to the IDFC FIRST Select credit card users is 1.99%.

### Does Select credit card have a movie offer?
Yes, IDFC FIRST Select credit card offers buy 1 and get up to Rs. 125 off offer on the 2nd movie ticket on District twice a month on spending min. Rs. 20,000 in the previous month.

### How to apply for an IDFC FIRST Select credit card?
If interested, you can apply for an IDFC FIRST Select credit card by visiting the bank’s official website or contacting the customer care.

### Who is eligible for IDFC Select credit card?
Individuals who earn an annual income of more than Rs. 12 Lakh can apply for this card.

### Does IDFC Bank FIRST Select Credit Card offer lounge access?
Yes, IDFC FIRST Select credit card offers both railway and airport lounge access. Cardholders can access 4 complimentary railway lounge visits per quarter and 1 domestic airport lounge visit per quarter. But these lounge visits are subject to monthly spends of Rs. 20,000.

### Is there an expiry date on the reward points accrued on my card?
The reward points earned using your IDFC FIRST Select Credit Card expire after 24 months.
