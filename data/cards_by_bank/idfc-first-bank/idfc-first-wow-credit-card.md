## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the rewards on IDFC FIRST WOW! Credit Card?
The card offers 4X reward points on online, offline, and international spends. For all other spends, you earn 1 reward point per Rs. 200. Insurance premiums, FASTag recharge, railway bookings, and utility bill payments also earn 1X reward points.

### How can I redeem my reward points?
Reward points can be redeemed at a rate of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 0.25. Points accumulated from eligible transactions can be used for various purchases or discounts as per the card’s reward redemption program.

### Are there any forex charges when I use this card internationally?
No, the IDFC FIRST WOW! Credit Card has a zero-forex markup fee, making it suitable for international transactions. You can use it for overseas shopping or travel without worrying about additional foreign currency conversion charges.

### Does this card provide any fuel benefits?
Yes, the card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel transactions, with savings of up to Rs. 200 per month. Over a year, you can save up to Rs. 2,400 on fuel payments while enjoying the card’s regular rewards.

### What are the fees and charges associated with this card?
This is a lifetime free credit card with no joining or annual fees. Finance charges range from 0.71% to 3.85% per month (8.5%–46.2% annually), and late payment fees are 15% of the outstanding amount, with a minimum of Rs. 100 and maximum of Rs. 1,300.

### Who is eligible to apply for this credit card?
Applicants must be 18 years or older and can be salaried or self-employed. A fixed deposit of at least Rs. 20,000 is required to get this FD-backed credit card, making it ideal for beginners or those looking for secured credit.

### What are the main pros and cons of the card?
Pros include zero-forex markup fees, 4X reward points, fuel surcharge savings, and lifetime free usage. Cons are the low reward redemption value (1RP = Rs. 0.25) and no travel perks like domestic or international lounge access.
