## About the Card
IDFC FIRST SWYP Credit Card, with an annual fee of Rs. 499, offers discounts of up to 20% on leading online portals and brands such as Domino’s, Nykaa, MakeMyTrip, Myntra, and more. However, unlike most credit cards that offer reward points on every transaction, FIRST SWYP offers rewards in the form of spend-based monthly milestone benefits. Despite this difference, cardholders can still earn a decent reward rate on their expenses. In addition to regular card spends, cardholders can also earn monthly spend-based rewards on UPI transactions. However, to unlock this feature and activate the UPI facility, they are required to get a FIRST Digital RuPay UPI Credit Card, which is issued at an additional annual fee of Rs. 199. The referral program is another unique feature of FIRST SWYP Credit Card. Under this program, cardholders can avail benefits such as annual fee waiver, cashback, bonus reward points, movie discounts, and complimentary lounge access every year, depending on the number of successful referrals. However, despite being a decent option, not every cardholder may be able to utilize this benefit effectively. Additionally, IDFC FIRST Bank has restricted the bill payment options for this card, which can be considered as a positive step towards better credit management for some users. Cardholders can either pay their total outstanding amount in full or convert eligible dues into easy EMIs, eliminating the option to revolve outstanding balances to the next billing cycle, an option that usually comes with high finance charges.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is IDFC FIRST SWYP Credit Card good for beginners or first-time card users?
Yes, IDFC FIRST SWYP Credit Card is suitable for new-to-credit users as it comes with a low annual fee and simple spend-based milestone rewards. Additionally, the restriction on revolving credit encourages better repayment discipline.

### How do milestone rewards work on the IDFC FIRST SWYP Credit Card?
Unlike regular cards, SWYP does not offer reward points on every transaction. Instead, you earn fixed reward points on crossing specific monthly spending thresholds. These milestone benefits are not incremental and only the highest eligible milestone is rewarded.

### Can I earn rewards on UPI transactions with IDFC FIRST SWYP Credit Card?
Yes, you can earn rewards on UPI spends, but only after activating the FIRST Digital RuPay UPI Credit Card by paying an additional annual fee of Rs. 199 + GST. UPI rewards are applicable only on eligible spends, excluding cash and fuel transactions.

### Does IDFC FIRST SWYP Credit Card allow partial bill payments?
No, the SWYP Credit Card does not allow partial or revolving payments. Cardholders can either pay the total outstanding amount in full or convert eligible dues into EMIs, helping avoid high finance charges.

### What is the reward point value of IDFC FIRST SWYP Credit Card?
The reward redemption value for IDFC FIRST SWYP Credit Card is Rs. 0.25 per reward point. This relatively low value makes milestone planning important to maximize overall returns.

### Are airport lounge visits included with IDFC FIRST SWYP Credit Card?
No, the card does not offer complimentary airport lounge access. However, cardholders get up to 16 complimentary railway lounge visits per year (4 per quarter).

### How does IDFC FIRST SWYP Credit Card referral program work?
Under the referral program, cardholders can earn benefits like annual fee waiver, cashback, reward points, movie vouchers, and lounge access based on the number of successful referrals made within a year, subject to bank terms and eligibility conditions.** **
