## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I redeem the reward points I earned on my IDFC Wealth card?
You can directly use the rewards points to pay for your online purchases or while shopping at a store. To pay directly with points, you can choose the ‘pay with points’ option. You can also redeem your reward points against vouchers through IDFC FIRST Bank’s net banking or mobile app.

### How do I contact the IDFC Wealth Credit Card customer care?
In case of any issues with your IDFC FIRST Wealth Credit Card, you can contact the customer care at 1800 10 888.

### Can I convert my outstanding balance into EMI?
Yes, you can convert the outstanding balance on your IDFC FIRST Credit Card in EMIs for tenure ranging between 3 to 18 months.

### Can I add IDFC FIRST Wealth Credit Card to UPI Apps?
With your IDFC FIRST Wealth Visa Credit Card, you can separately apply for a Rupay variant. The Rupay Card can be linked to UPI Apps, and on all UPI spends, you can earn 3X reward points. However, the Rupay Variant is available at an annual fee of Rs. 199.

### How is IDFC FIRST Wealth Credit Card different from IDFC FIRST Millennia and IDFC FIRST Select?
The reward rate on all 3 cards is the same. However, they differ in terms of lounge access, movie and other lifestyle benefits.
