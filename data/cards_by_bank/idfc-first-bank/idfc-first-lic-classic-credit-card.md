## About the Card
IDFC FIRST LIC Classic Credit Card is a co-branded credit option built around insurance-related and everyday spends. It offers 1 reward point for every Rs. 125 spent, 6X on LIC insurance premiums and 3X on groceries, utilities, railways, FASTag, education, and government payments. Other transactions also earn 3X rewards. Through the travel section, cardholders can get 20% bonus points on hotel bookings and 10% on flights. The card also includes 8 domestic lounge visits annually and 4 railway lounge accesses. Benefits include insurance cover up to Rs. 2 lakh, Rs. 25,000 lost card liability protection, roadside assistance worth Rs. 1,399, fuel surcharge waiver of 1% capped at Rs. 300, and no joining or annual fees.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How are reward points earned on this card?
Consumers can earn 1 reward point for every Rs. 125 spent. Cardholders can earn 6X points on LIC insurance premiums and 3X points on groceries, utilities, railways, FASTag, education, and government payments.

### What is the value of reward points?
Each reward point is worth Rs. 0.25 when redeemed. For example, 1,000 points can be redeemed for Rs. 250. Points remain valid for 3 years from the date they are earned.

### Are there any joining or annual fees?
No, the card comes with zero joining fee and zero annual fee, making it a lifetime-free credit card.

### What travel benefits does this card offer?
The card offers up to 8 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year (subject to Rs. 20,000 monthly spending in the previous month) and 4 railway lounge visits per quarter.

### What insurance and safety benefits are included?
Cardholders get personal accident insurance cover up to Rs. 2 lakh, lost card liability protection up to Rs. 25,000, and free roadside assistance worth Rs. 1,399.

### What is the interest and late payment charge structure?
The finance charge is 8.5% per month (46.2% annually). Late payment fees range from Rs. 100 to Rs. 1,300, depending on the total outstanding amount.
