## About the Card
IndiGo IDFC FIRST Dual Credit Card** is a co-branded card in partnership with IndiGo airlines. It comes in two variants: one unsecured with a joining fee of Rs. 4,999, and the other secured which does not charge any joining fee but is backed by an FD of Rs. 1 lakh or more. The secured option can be quite beneficial for new-to-credit individuals who may otherwise find it difficult to get a good travel credit card. Regardless of the variant you choose, you will be charged an annual fee from the 2nd year onwards. Both variants offer dual network benefits, with two physical cards, one from Mastercard and the other from RuPay, which share the same credit limit, statement, and a common rewards pool.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Do I get the option to choose between Mastercard and RuPay variant of IndiGo IDFC FIRST Credit Card?
No, both Mastercard and RuPay variants are given to all IDFC FIRST IndiGo credit cardholders as two separate physical cards.

### Is IndiGo IDFC FIRST Rupay Credit Card a digital card?
No, the Rupay variant of IndiGo IDFC FIRST credit card is not a digital/virtual card, but is offered as a physical card.

### Is IndiGo BluChip membership compulsory for IDFC FIRST IndiGo Credit Card?
Yes, you must enroll in the IndiGo BluChip membership program to avail the benefits of IndiGo IDFC FIRST Credit Card.

### What is the fee of the IndiGo BluChip Program membership?
You can enroll into the IndiGo BluChip Program for free. You will get the entry-level base tier status of Blu 3, which you can upgrade from if your IndiGo spends meet the qualifying criteria.

### Does the FD backed secured version of IndiGo IDFC FIRST Credit Card have any annual fee?
Though the secured FD backed IndiGo IDFC FIRST Credit Card variants does not charge any joining fee, you will have to pay an annual fee of Rs. 1,499 from 2nd year onwards.
