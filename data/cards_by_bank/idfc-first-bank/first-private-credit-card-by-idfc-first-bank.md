## About the Card
IDFC FIRST Private Credit Card is an **invite-only super-premium card** catering to high-income consumer segment. Cardholders can earn reward points on all spends, including generally excluded categories like rent, education and insurance. Unlimited lounge access, zero forex mark-up fee and complimentary hotel memberships make this card a good option for frequent travellers. Note: This is an invite-only card. Content on this page is for information only._
