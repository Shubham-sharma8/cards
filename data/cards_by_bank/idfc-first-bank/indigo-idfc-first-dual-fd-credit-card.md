## About the Card
The **IndiGo First Dual Credit Card Against FD** is a **co-branded secured credit card** issued against a Fixed Deposit (FD). This card is best suited for individuals who are new to credit or have limited credit history and may otherwise face difficulties to qualify for feature-rich travel credit cards.

## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** option from IDFC FIRST Bank suited for super-premium users and low 0% forex markup.

**Best For:** Students, New-to-Credit applicants, or low-CIBIL users building credit history via a Fixed Deposit

**Not Ideal For:** Established salaried professionals eligible for unsecured cashback/travel cards

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 5,000 – Rs. 20,000 (<30% FD utilization)

### Pros (Good)
- Up to 22 BluChips per Rs.100 on IndiGo flight booking which makes it highly rewarding for frequernt flyers
- Unlike most cards, it offers BluChip rewards even on UPI payments and some excluded categories such as rent, utilities, and wallet top-ups
- Up to 25,000 bonus BluChips annually through milestone-based spending rewards
- Zero forex markup and annual fee payment bonus credited every year
- Benefit from trip cancellation coverage that renews annually for added travel security

### Cons (Bad)
- No complimentary airport lounge access which is a notable gap for a travel-friendly card
- Rewards outside of travel spending are limited, with the little to no lifestyle perks for everyday non-travel purchases
- No annual fee waiver option regardless of spending level

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the minimum fixed deposit required to get this card?
Rs. 1 Lakh is the minimum fixed deposit required to get IDFC FIRST Dual Credit Card Against FD.

### Is there any annual fee on this card?
Yes. You will be charged Rs. 4,999 + applicable taxes from 2nd year onwards on this card. For the first year there is no annual or joining fee.

### How is IndiGo IDFC FIRST Dual Credit Card Against FD different from its regular variant IndiGo IDFC FIRST Dual Credit Cards?
The main difference between both the card lies in issued and joining fee. The FD-backed variant is a secured credit card which is issued against a fixed deposit of Rs. 1 Lakh or more and comes with zero joining fee. Whereas the regular variant is an unsecured credit card that comes with a joining fee of Rs. 4,999. However, all other features and benefits remain the same.

### Will my fixed deposit continue to earn interest after it is placed for the card?
Yes. The fixed deposit will continue to earn interest. It simply serves as a security deposit for the credit card.

### My fixed deposit is of Rs. 1 Lakh, will I get a credit limit of Rs. 1 Lakh only on my card?
Typically, limit on a secured credit card is set as a percentage of the fixed deposit. However, the exact limit is decided by the bank at the time of card issuance.
