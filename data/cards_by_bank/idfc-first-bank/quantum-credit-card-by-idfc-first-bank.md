## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is the IDFC FIRST Quantum+ Credit Card a secured credit card?
Yes. The IDFC FIRST Quantum+ Credit Card is a secured credit card backed by a fixed deposit of Rs. 10,000, which serves as collateral for the credit limit.

### Is there a limit on the cashback I can earn?
Yes. The total cashback across all eligible spending categories is capped at Rs. 500 per statement cycle, regardless of your overall monthly spending.

### What insurance benefits are included with this card?
The card provides a personal accident cover of Rs. 2 lakh and a lost card liability cover of up to Rs. 25,000, subject to the banks applicable terms and conditions.

### Which transactions earn lower cashback?
Utility bill payments, rent, jewellery purchases, wallet loads, insurance premiums, and government service payments earn 0.5% cashback instead of the standard cashback rates.

### What happens if I do not pay my credit card bill on time?
Unpaid balances attract finance charges of 3.99% per month (47.88% per annum), and a late payment fee is charged based on the outstanding amount, subject to the banks fee limits.
