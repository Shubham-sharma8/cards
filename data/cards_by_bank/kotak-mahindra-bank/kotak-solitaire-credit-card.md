## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is Kotak Solitaire Credit Card?
Kotak Solitaire Credit Card is an invite-only premium card that offers AirMiles on every spend and accelerated savings of up to 10% on travel-related expenses. The accumulated AirMiles can be redeemed for travel bookings at a 1:1 ratio. Additionally, the card offers exclusive deals and discounts at top hotels, including ITC Hotels, Elivaas Villas, and Alaya Stays.

### Is Kotak Solitaire Credit Card free?
Kotak Solitaire Credit Card is only free for Kotak Solitaire program customers, while others are charged with an annual fee of Rs. 25,000. However, no joining fee is applicable for all customers.

### Is there any capping on Air Miles redemption?
You can redeem Air Miles for up to 80% of the booking value while making flight or hotel bookings on Kotak Unbox. The same 80% limit is also applicable on non-travel redemptions like vouchers and catalogue on Kotak Unbox. However, no such limitation is applied on Kotak Air Miles to partner miles conversions.

### Can I withdraw cash using Kotak Solitaire Credit Card and are there any applicable charges?
Yes, you can withdraw cash using Kotak Solitaire Credit Card. However, it is not advisable to do so due to the additional charges which accompany a credit card cash withdrawal. Kotak Solitaire charges a one-time ATM cash withdrawal fee of 1% of the withdrawn amount. There would be no interest charges applicable till the bill payment due date of the statement cycle in which cash is withdrawn.

### What is the minimum number of Air Miles required for redemption?
You must have accrued at least 2,000 Air Miles in order to convert them into partner miles. However, if you wish to redeem them against travel bookings on Kotak Unbox or use them for other redemption options, you must have a balance of 500 Air Miles.

### Does Kotak Solitaire Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, the card offers unlimited complimentary domestic and international lounge access for both primary and add-on cardholders. In addition to this, it offers 2 complimentary guest visits per year for domestic lounges and 2 for international lounges.

### How to get a Kotak Solitaire Credit Card?
Kotak Solitaire Credit Card is an invite-only card offered to select customers of Kotak Mahindra Bank. You cannot apply for this card directly. However, you may contact Kotak’s customer service to check if you are eligible. If you meet the bank\\\\\'s criteria, an invitation will be sent to you.
