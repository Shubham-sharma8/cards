## About the Card
Designed specifically for movie buffs, PVR INOX Kotak Credit Card is a co-branded card that offers entertainment benefits like discounts on movie tickets and free movie tickets every month. With this card, cardholders can save 5% on PVR movie tickets and 20% on food and beverages at PVR cinemas. Additionally, the card also offers complimentary access to premium PVR INOX cinema lounges. The standout feature of this card is the spend-based unlimited movie tickets benefit. Cardholders can earn a free movie ticket worth Rs. 300 for every Rs. 10,000 spent in a month. Since there’s no capping on the number of complimentary tickets, the more you spend, the more you save. If you’re a movie buff who prefers watching movies at PVR, this card can help you save significantly on your entertainment spends.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How many free movie tickets can I earn through PVR INOX Kotak Credit Card?
The card offers a free movie ticket worth Rs. 300 for every Rs. 10,000 spent in a calendar month. As there is no capping on the number of complimentary tickets, you can earn unlimited free movie tickets through this milestone benefit.

### Is PVR INOX Kotak Credit Card lifetime free?
No, the card does not charge any joining fee. However, from the second year onwards, you have to pay an annual fee of Rs. 499.

### What are the benefits offered by PVR INOX Kotak Credit Card?
The card offers 5% off on PVR movie tickets and 20% off on food and beverages at PVR cinemas. In addition, you get complimentary access to PVR in-cinema lounges. You can also earn free movie tickets as milestone benefits.

### How much can I save on movie tickets via PVR INOX Kotak Credit Card?
With this card, you can save 5% on movie tickets at PVR INOX theatres or on the PVR INOX website/app. Additionally, you can save 20% on food and beverages at PVR cinemas.

### What is the eligibility criteria to apply for PVR INOX Kotak Credit Card?
To apply for this card, you must be a resident of India. Applicants between the age of 18 to 65 years are eligible to apply.

### How can I apply for PVR INOX Kotak Credit Card?
To apply for this credit card online, visit the official Kotak Mahindra Bank website and submit your application along with the required documents. Alternatively, you can apply offline by visiting any nearby branch of the bank and submitting the application form in person.
