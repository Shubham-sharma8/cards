## About the Card
Kotak Infinite is an invite-only credit card that is exclusively offered to Kotak Private Banking customers. This card offers a personalized card design along with basic rewards, travel & golf benefits. For frequent travellers, it charges a low forex markup fee of 2% along with complimentary domestic lounge visits. Besides travel, the card offers moderate golf benefits, including up to 24 free golf rounds every year, along with 2 guest visits per year. Cardholders can earn 1 point per Rs. 125 spent across all categories, which can later be redeemed against recharges, products & vouchers, and more through the rewards catalogue. Note: Kotak Mahindra Bank has discontinued _the issuance of this card for new customer_ s. The features and benefits available on this page apply only to existing credit cardholders.__

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who is eligible for the Kotak Infinite Credit Card?
Kotak Infinite Credit Card is an invite-only card, primarily for Kotak Private Banking customers. Invitations are extended to select customers based on their profile and banking relationship with Kotak.

### What are the key travel benefits of this card?
Kotak Infinite Credit Card offers a low forex markup fee of 2%, making international transactions cost-effective. Additionally, the card provides 4 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits every quarter.

### What golf-related perks does the Kotak Infinite Credit Card offer?
Cardholders can avail 2 complimentary rounds of golf per month and free tee-offs at select premium golf courses. Cardholders can also invite up to 2 guests for a complimentary round once every quarter.

### How are rewards earned and redeemed?
Cardholders can earn 1 reward point for every Rs. 125 spent on all purchases. Reward points can be redeemed through the Kotak rewards catalogue for products, gift vouchers, mobile/DTH recharges, and other available redemption options.

### Are there any fees associated with the card?
Kotak Infinite Credit Card comes with no joining or annual fee. Finance charges are 3.50% per month (42% p.a.), and late payment fees vary based on the outstanding amount, ranging from Rs. 100 to Rs. 1,200.

### Does the card provide benefits outside of travel and golf?
Yes, but they are limited. Benefits include a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 7,500 (capped at Rs. 4,500/year) and a railway surcharge waiver for bookings via IRCTC or Indian Railways (capped at Rs. 500/year).
