## About the Card
Kotak White Reserve Credit Card is a **premium metal card** offering designed to enhance the cardholder’s lifestyle through exclusive benefits. Unlike conventional rewards programs, it features **‘White Pass’ — a unique reward currency** awarded based on the annual spending milestones. This White Pass value can be used to purchase products from the White Pass catalogue or redeemed as cashback. For travel enthusiasts, the card provides a **Club Marriott membership** , with exclusive discounts on stays, dining, and spa services, alongside unlimited access to both domestic and international airport lounges. While its **forex markup of 2%** is relatively lower than the general 3.5%, it still adds to the overall cost of international transactions. Additionally, golf enthusiasts get **complimentary golf rounds** , and dedicated concierge services are available to maintain a premium lifestyle in a hassle-free manner.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does Kotak White Pass have any expiry date?
The White Pass value accrued in an anniversary year is valid for usage only till 90 days after the end of the annual anniversary cycle. The White Pass balance expires and becomes invalid after this period and is reset to nil.

### What is the forex markup charges on Kotak White Reserve Credit Card?
Kotak White Reserve comes with a foreign currency exchange mark-up of 2%.

### How can I avail White Pass worth Rs. 2.5 lakh in a year?
White Pass value of Rs. 2.5 lakh is available only when you spend Rs. 100 lakh (i.e. Rs. 1 crore) or more in a year.

### Can add-on card members get access to the Club Marriott membership?
No, the complimentary Club Marriott membership offered with Kotak White Reserve credit card is only available for the primary cardholders.

### Is there a fee waiver option for Kotak White Reserve credit card users?
Yes, you can get the annual card fee waived off by spending at least Rs. 10 lakh in an anniversary year.
