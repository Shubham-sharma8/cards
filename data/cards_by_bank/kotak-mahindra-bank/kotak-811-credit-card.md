## About the Card
Kotak 811 Credit Card is a lifetime free credit card designed for consumers who want to earn reward points on their regular online and offline purchases. It is suitable for users who frequently shop online, make daily transactions, and prefer benefits such as fuel savings and movie offers. The card offers 2 reward points per Rs. 100 spent online and 1 reward point per Rs. 100 spent offline. Cardholders can also earn 500 bonus reward points after spending Rs. 3,000 in the month of card activation. Additional benefits include a 1% fuel surcharge waiver, railway surcharge waiver, and 3 complimentary PVR tickets on achieving the annual spending milestone.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How do I earn reward points with the Kotak 811 Credit Card?
You earn 2 reward points per Rs. 100 spent online and 1 reward point per Rs. 100 spent on offline transactions. Reward points are not applicable on excluded categories such as education, fuel, rent, insurance, wallet loading, government transactions, and online skill-based gaming.

### What is the welcome benefit on the Kotak 811 Credit Card?
You receive 500 bonus reward points after spending Rs. 3,000 within the month of card activation.

### Does the Kotak 811 Credit Card offer movie benefits?
Yes. You can get 3 complimentary PVR movie tickets after spending Rs. 75,000 in a year.

### Are there any fuel or railway surcharge waivers?
Yes. The card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on eligible fuel transactions between Rs. 500 and Rs. 4,000 and a railway surcharge waiver on IRCTC and railway booking counter transactions, subject to the specified annual limits.

### Does the Kotak 811 Credit Card offer airport lounge access?
No. The Kotak 811 Credit Card does not offer complimentary airport lounge access.
