## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the eligibility criteria for Kotak UPI RuPay Credit Card?
Only the existing Kotak credit card users who are at least 18 years old or above in age are eligible to apply for Kotak UPI RuPay Credit Card.

### What is Kotak UPI RuPay Credit Card?
Kotak UPI RuPay Credit Card is a lifetime-free virtual credit card that allows the Kotak credit cardholders to make online spends, including UPI transactions.

### How can I apply for the Kotak UPI RuPay Credit Card?
Since only the existing Kotak credit cardholders are eligible for this card, they can either contact their relationship manager, if any, visit their associated Kotak bank branch or call customer care to enquire about this card.

### What if I change my mobile number, how can I link my Kotak Bank RuPay Credit Card on UPI app?
If you change your mobile number, you must update it for your Kotak Bank UPI RuPay Credit Card as well. Once it is updated, you are required to re-register your card on the respective UPI apps.

### How many transactions can I make from the linked Kotak RuPay Credit Card on UPI?
There is no restriction on the number of transactions made using Kotak RuPay credit cards.

### Can I make payment to any person or other credit card using the linked Kotak Credit Card on UPI?
No, the linked Kotak RuPay Credit Card can only be used to make payment to merchant (P2M). No Peer to Peer (P2P) or Peer to Peer Merchant (P2PM) transactions are allowed.

### Is there any spending limit on using my linked Kotak RuPay Credit card on UPI?
Generally, a UPI amount limit of Rs. 1 lakh per day (or Rs. 2 lakh per day for certain special cases) is applicable for every card.

### Will I be charged for linking or making any purchase using Kotak RuPay Credit Card on UPI?
No, Kotak RuPay Credit Card does not come with any such transaction charges.

### Will I earn any reward points for UPI spends using Kotak Bank UPI RuPay Credit Card?
Yes, you can earn 3 reward points for every Rs. 100 spent on UPI transactions.

### Is UPI facility available on Add-on Kotak UPI RuPay Credit Cards?
Kotak UPI RuPay Credit Card does not come with any add-on card facility.
