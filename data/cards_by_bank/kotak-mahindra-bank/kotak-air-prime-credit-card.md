## Frequently Asked Questions
### How is Kotak Air+ Prime different from Kotak Air+ Credit Card? Which is better?
Both Kotak Air+ and Kotak Air+ Prime credit cards come at an annual fee of Rs. 3,000 and offer similar benefits with minor differences. Kotak Air+ offers 2,500 Air Miles as joining and renewal bonus, while Kotak Air+ Prime offers 3,000 Air Miles instead. Kotak Air+ offers accelerated rewards only on flight/hotel bookings on Kotak Unbox, Kotak Air+ Prime also offers higher rewards on flight/hotel add-ons on Unbox, along with auto dealer merchants. Moreover, the Prime card variant also comes with an annual fee waiver option. So, the Prime variant is a better card option.

### How to waive the fee of Kotak Air+ Prime Credit Card?
You can waive the annual fee of Kotak Air+ Prime credit card by spending Rs. 4 lakh or more in a year.

### How many Air Miles can I earn on international spends using Kotak Air+ Prime Credit Card?
International transactions are excluded from the reward program of Kotak Air+ Prime Credit Card, so you do not earn any Air Miles on these spends.

### What are the partner airlines and hotels for Air Miles to partner miles transfer option for Kotak Air+ Prime Credit Card?
You can convert your accrued Air Miles using Kotak Air+ Prime credit card into partner loyalty points for airlines and hotels like Marriott, Accor, Air India, Air Canada, Air France, British Airways, Cathay Pacific, Etihad Airways, Qatar Airlines and United Airlines.

### How can I access Kotak Unbox platform as Kotak Air+ Prime Credit Card user?
You can access Kotak Unbox on Kotak Mahindra Bank mobile app. Login to the app and either click on Wallet or Credit Cards option and select Kotak Air+ Prime Credit Card. Then scroll to the Air Miles section to check your accrued Air Miles balance and click on Redeem option to access Kotak Unbox.
