## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does the cashback earned using Kotak Cashback+ Prime Credit Card expire?
Yes, the cashback accrued using Kotak Cashback+ Prime Credit Card has limited time validity and all the unclaimed cashback expires in 1 year.

### How to redeem cashback earned using Kotak Cashback+ Prime Credit Card?
You can redeem your cashback earned using Kotak Cashback+ Prime Credit Card by logging in to the Kotak Rewards platform. Enter your CRN and click on ‘Get OTP’ button. Enter the OTP received on your registered mobile number and email ID and click on ‘Submit’. Select ‘Credit Card’ in the ‘Card Type’ dropdown menu. Then choose Cashback+ Prime card from ‘Card Number’ dropdown menu. Enter the number of reward points/cashback you wish to redeem, accept T&C and submit.

### Why cannot I redeem my cashback worth Rs. 340 via Kotak Cashback+ Prime Credit Card?
The cashback earnings on Kotak Cashback+ Prime Credit Card can be redeemed against statements outstanding in 1:1 ratio, however the cashback can only be redeemed in multiples of 400. So, if your cashback balance is less than 400 then you can not redeem it until you reach the Rs. 400 limit.

### How can I activate my Kotak Cashback+ Prime Credit Card?
To activate your Kotak Cashback+ Prime Credit Card, log in to the Kotak mobile banking app and click on ‘Wallet’ or ‘Credit Cards’. Then click on ‘Card Controls’ and select the ‘Generate Card PIN’ option. Enter the necessary details to generate your new card PIN and activate your card. You can also activate your card via this app by updating the card controls or making a card transaction or by giving a missed call on the number 7669010000.

### Does Kotak Cashback+ Prime Credit Card offer lounge access?
No, Kotak Cashback+ Prime Credit Card does not offer any complimentary airport lounge access to its cardholders.
