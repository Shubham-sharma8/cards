## About the Card
Kotak Royale Signature Credit Card is a rewards card primarily designed to offer benefits on travel-related spends. The card offers**2X rewards** (4 points per Rs. 150 spent) on hotels, airlines & air carriers, restaurants, travel agencies, tour operators (including package tour operators), and international spends. Besides this, it offers a base reward rate of **2 points per Rs. 150 spent** on other categories. While this card offers accelerated rewards on the travel category, the overall reward rate is relatively low at 0.66% on select categories and 0.33% on other spends, primarily due to its low reward redemption value of **1 Reward Point = Up to Rs. 0.25**. Besides rewards, the card offers **complimentary domestic lounge visits** and other basic benefits such as railway and fuel surcharge waivers. Cardholders can also earn **up to 30,000 reward points as milestone benefits** on spending Rs. 8 Lakh in a year. With a moderate annual fee and an easily achievable annual fee waiver condition, this card is a suitable option for consumers looking to save on their travel spends. Note: Kotak Mahindra Bank has discontinued the issuance of this card for new customers. The features and benefits available on this page apply only to the existing credit cardholders.__

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for Kotak Royale Signature Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online through the official website of the bank. You are required to submit a duly filled application form along with relevant documents. Besides this, you can also apply offline by submitting the application form in person at the nearest branch.

### Does Kotak Royale Signature Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, this credit card offers up to 8 complimentary domestic lounge visits per year, capped at 2 visits per quarter. However, you can avail these benefits only after spending Rs. 75,000 in the previous quarter.

### Can I redeem rewards against cash through Kotak Royale Signature Credit Card?
Yes, you can redeem accumulated reward points against cash at a value of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 0.20. Besides this, you can also redeem the accumulated reward points either using the Pay with Reward Points option at the POS or redeem them against the Kotak Rewards Catalogue for products and services like shopping, mobile recharge, brand e-vouchers, movies, travel, and more.

### What is the forex markup fee charged on Kotak Royale Signature Credit Card?
Kotak Royale Signature Credit Card charges a forex markup fee of 3.5%. Therefore, it is not a suitable option for international spends. If you want to save on your international transactions, you should opt for a card with a low or zero forex markup fee.

### Can I earn rewards on fuel spends with Kotak Royale Signature Credit Card?
No, the credit card does not offer reward points on select categories, including wallet load, fuel, rent, and online skill-based gaming spends.
