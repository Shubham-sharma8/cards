## About the Card
IndiGo Kotak XL Credit Card is the **premium variant** in the co-branded partnership between IndiGo Airlines and Kotak Mahindra Bank. Unlike the existing ‘6E Rewards’ cards with the airline, these co-branded cards offer **value-back in the form of BluChips** – the reward currency under IndiGo’s BluChip Program. In addition to IndiGo spends, cardholders earn accelerated BluChips on dining and entertainment spends, enhancing the card’s overall value. Besides this, the card compensates for its joining and annual fee of Rs. 3,000 by offering a **complimentary IndiGo BluChips voucher worth Rs. 4,000** and a 6E Eats voucher. Additionally, **BluChip vouchers worth Rs. 16,000** as milestone benefits, along with **complimentary domestic lounge visits** , make this card a suitable option for frequent flyers. However, consumers are required to enrol in the BluChip program (at the Blu 3 level) to avail these benefits. Going forward, **existing 6E Rewards cardholders will be migrated to the new program** , and new cards will be issued as per the variant. Read on to know about IndiGo Kotak XL Card features and the BluChip Membership tiers in detail.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for IndiGo Kotak XL Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online by visiting the official website of the bank. You are required to navigate to the card page and submit the duly filled application form by clicking on the “Apply Now” button. Besides this, you can also visit any of the nearest branches of the bank and submit the application form in person.

### Can I use IndiGo Kotak XL Credit Card for international transactions?
Yes, you can make international transactions with this card. However, it charges a high forex markup fee of 3.5%. Therefore, it is advisable to use a credit card with zero or low forex markup fee to save on international spends.

### What is BluChip in IndiGo Kotak XL Credit Card?
BluChip is the newly launched loyalty program of IndiGo Airlines, which allows users to earn BluChips on their spends and redeem them for future bookings. The program comes with simplified tiers, lifetime validity of points, and no blackout dates.

### How can I achieve a higher tier with IndiGo Kotak XL Credit Card?
At the time of joining, you are automatically enrolled in the base tier, Blu 3. However, if you want to achieve Blu 2 tier, you are required to spend Rs. 1 Lakh in a year along with a minimum of 4 IndiGo flights. To achieve the higher Blu 1 tier, you need to spend Rs. 2 Lakh in a year and take at least 8 IndiGo flights.

### Is IndiGo Kotak XL Credit Card a replacement for Kotak IndiGo 6E Rewards XL Credit Card?
IndiGo’s 6E Rewards Program has been discontinued and replaced by the BluChip Program. Kotak Mahindra Bank has launched two new co-branded cards for IndiGo loyalists. However, existing co-branded credit cards operating under the 6E Rewards program will continue to offer benefits as per their terms and conditions until further notice.
