## About the Card
Kotak Privy League Signature Credit Card is designed for Privy League customers looking to save on their online and offline spends. The card offers accelerated rewards **(5 points per Rs. 100)** on multiple categories, including apparel, groceries, departmental stores, travel agencies, jewellery, and more, making it a practical option for everyday spends. Besides this, it offers a **base reward rate of 2 points per Rs. 100** spent on other categories. Another good thing about this card is that it comes with the option to**choose between the Shoppers or Travellers plan** , giving flexibility to cardholders to select categories as per their preferences. Besides rewards, travel benefits like **complimentary lounge visits worldwide** and a **low forex markup fee of 2%** make this card a suitable option for international travel as well. However, one important thing to note is that this card works differently as it is offered in **two variants — paid and lifetime free**. While the paid variant offers higher value-back, the free variant comes with limited travel benefits. By paying an annual fee of Rs. 2,500, consumers can maximize benefits of this card. Note: Kotak Mahindra Bank has discontinued the issuance of this card for new customers. The features and benefits available on this page apply only to the existing credit cardholders.__

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who can apply for Kotak Mahindra Privy League Signature Credit Card?
Customers who have maintained Privy League relationship with Kotak Mahindra Bank can apply for this credit card.

### Can I use Kotak Mahindra Privy League Signature Credit Card for international spends?
Yes, you can use this credit card for international spends. The paid variant charges a low forex markup fee of 2%, making it suitable for overseas expenses. However, the free variant comes with a higher forex markup fee of 3.5%.

### Does Kotak Mahindra Privy League Signature Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, the card offers complimentary Priority Pass membership along with 4 complimentary international lounge visits per year (with the paid variant). It also offers 2 complimentary domestic lounge visits every quarter at DreamFolks lounges.

### Can I earn rewards on fuel spends with Kotak Mahindra Privy League Signature Credit Card?
No, this credit card does not offer rewards on fuel and rent payments. However, you can save up to Rs. 4,500 per year on fuel spends through a 1% fuel surcharge waiver.

### Can I redeem reward points against cash with Kotak Mahindra Privy League Signature Credit Card?
Yes, you can redeem accumulated reward points against cash, as well as for mobile recharges, products, and vouchers through the rewards portal, at a value of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 0.25.
