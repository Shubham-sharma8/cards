## About the Card
Kotak League Platinum Credit Card is primarily a rewards credit card offering**accelerated benefits on select day-to-day and travel spends**. The card works on a different spend-based reward structure. Cardholders can earn 2X rewards (8 points per Rs. 150) on air carriers, travel agencies & tour operators, package tour operators, electric sales, departmental stores and durable goods, making it a suitable option for everyday expenses. For other categories, it offers a**base reward rate of 4 points per Rs. 150** spent for annual spends up to Rs. 2 Lakh, which increases to 2X rewards (8 points per Rs. 150) on annual spends exceeding Rs. 2 Lakh. Despite being a rewards credit card, it lags behind with a **maximum value-back of 0.53%** in accelerated categories and 0.26% on other spends, primarily due to its low reward redemption value**(1 RP = Up to Rs. 0.10)** , which impacts the overall value. However, additional features like welcome benefits worth the joining fee, **free movie tickets** or rewards as milestone benefits and exclusive assistance services enhance the card’s value. This card is a suitable option for consumers looking for an **entry level credit card with basic benefits**.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Can I apply online for Kotak League Platinum Credit Card?
Yes, you can visit the official website of the bank and apply online by submitting a duly filled application form along with the relevant documents. Besides this, you can also apply offline by submitting the application form at the nearest branch of the bank.

### I’ve reached the spending limit of Rs. 1.25 lakh in the last 6 months via Kotak League Platinum Credit Card. Why did I not get the PVR ticket benefit?
You can claim the free tickets within 3 months after the program period ends. After 3 months, you will not be able to get the benefit. For example, if you received your card in January and reached the spending milestone of Rs. 1.25 Lakh by July, you will have to claim the benefit by October, or else the privilege expires.

### Can I use Kotak League Platinum Credit Card for international transactions?
Yes, you can use this credit card for international spends. However, it charges a high forex markup fee of 3.5%. If you want to save on your international spends, you should opt for a credit card with a low or zero forex markup fee.

### Why did I not get the welcome voucher with my Kotak League Platinum Credit Card?
The welcome benefit is offered only with the joining fee variant. If you are getting the variant with the joining fee waived off, you would not be able to avail welcome benefit.

### Can I get an add-on credit card for my family members with Kotak League Platinum Credit Card?
Yes, Kotak Mahindra Bank credit cards come with add-on card feature wherein you can apply for an add-on card for your family members. You can also set spending limit on each add-on card and track their spends.
