## About the Card
The Kotak IndiGo Ka-ching 6E Rewards XL Credit Card is a **co-branded card for frequent travellers** who prefer flying via IndiGo airlines. The card is a premium variant of the other Kotak bank card—Kotak IndiGo 6E Rewards Credit Card. It offers exclusive travel benefits such as lounge access, priority meals, baggage assistance, a 6% reward on IndiGo spends, and an IndiGo airline ticket worth Rs. 3,000 as a welcome benefit. In addition to that, it also provides cross-category benefits across dining, entertainment, and grocery. That way, the card somehow caters to the all-rounder daily needs of customers despite being a co-branded travel credit card. Having said that, overall **this card is a good fit only for IndiGo loyalists** , and other extended benefits on the card cannot be a deciding factor, especially when the redemption also circles back to IndiGo flights. Plus, the reward rate offered for non-IndiGo expenses is very basic and not that great if you are looking for highly rewarding cards across multiple categories. **Users seeking all-round benefits can definitely find better options at a fee of Rs. 1,500**. This credit card is definitely a good option for you if you are keenly looking for travel benefits on IndiGo airline, as that way you can justify the annual fee. However, it would have been even better if the co-branded activation benefits were offered every year as a milestone or renewal benefit.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How do I redeem my 6E reward points?
All the 6E reward points that you earn on IndiGo co-branded credit cards can be redeemed on the IndiGo website to purchase IndiGo flight tickets. You can redeem your IndiGo points without any expiry.

### How do I get a Ka Ching card?
You can get the IndiGo Ka-ching 6E Rewards XL Credit Card from Kotak Bank by applying directly on the Kotak Mahindra Bank’s website, or by contacting the customer service at 1860 266 2666. Apart from that, you can also visit the nearest Kotak Mahindra Branch to get yourself the Ka Ching Card.

### How much do I save on spending Rs. 1.5 Lakh in a year on merchant spends?
The card offers a 2% return as IndiGo rewards on spending across merchants (including dining, grocery and entertainment). That way, if you spend Rs. 1.5 Lakh in this category you save around Rs. 4,500 in a year.

### What spends are excluded from rewards earning?
Some exclusions from the rewards program are rent, wallet, education and Government spends.

### What is the 6E reward in IndiGo?
6E reward is the redemption currency offered for reward points earned on IndiGo co-branded credit cards with top banks.

### What is the age limit for add-on cardholders for Kotak IndiGo Ka-ching 6E Rewards XL Credit Card?
The age limit for add-on cardholders for the Kotak Ka-Ching card is 18 years and above.
