## About the Card
Kotak Cashback+ Credit Card is a core cashback-focused card designed to reward everyday spends such as online grocery, food delivery, online entertainment and fuel expenses. Beyond accelerated spends, cardholders earn 0.5% unlimited cashback across all other eligible categories. While the card covers multiple everyday spends, low monthly cashback capping limits the overall saving potential, especially for high spenders. Read on to know about this card’s features and benefits along with a view on who should get this card.

## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** option from Kotak Mahindra Bank suited for cashback users.

**Best For:** Kotak Mahindra Bank customers seeking cashback rewards & bank-specific discount offers

**Not Ideal For:** Users focused purely on competing co-brand ecosystems or heavy airport lounge visits

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Any (LTF keeper)

### Pros (Good)
- Accelerated value-back on everyday spends like grocery and food delivery
- Covers more ground in entertainment with benefits on OTT subscriptions and gaming
- Offers 3% cashback on fuel spends – which is generally excluded from rewards programs

### Cons (Bad)
- Base rate of 0.5% is too low for a card that charges Rs. 750 annual fee
- Low capping of Rs. 500 across all accelerated spends which limit the earning potential
- No added benefits like domestic lounge access, discounts, etc.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I claim the cashback accumulated on my card?
To claim cashback on your card, you need to redeem the accumulated reward points. For this, you can visit the Kotak Rewards Platform, login using your customer ID and password and proceed to redeem. The cashback will be credited to your card account.

### What is the maximum cashback I can earn on non-accelerated categories?
There is no limit on the base cashback earning.

### What happens to the earned cashback if I cancel a transaction?
When you cancel a transaction or return a purchase, cashback earned on such transactions will be reversed.

### If I make a purchase on EMI, how much cashback will I earn?
EMI transactions are not eligible to earn cashback. If the cashback is credited before the conversion of transaction into EMIs, the earned cashback will be reversed.

### What is Kotak Mahindra Bank’s credit card customer care number?
You can reach Kotak Credit Card customer care on 1860 266 2666.
