## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is there any annual fee waiver option for Kotak Air+ Credit Card?
No, Kotak Air+ Credit Card does not come with any spend-based fee waiver option.

### Does Kotak Air+ Credit Card offer any rewards or savings on international transactions?
Kotak Air+ Credit Card does not offer any rewards or Air Miles on international transactions. Moreover, all international spends are charged with a 2% forex mark-up fee.

### How can I access Kotak Unbox platform?
You can find Kotak Unbox on Kotak Mahindra Bank mobile app. You can login to the app and either click on Wallet or Credit Cards option and select Kotak Air+ Credit Card. Then scroll to the Air Miles section to check your accrued Air Miles balance and click on Redeem option to access Kotak Unbox.

### When will the Air Miles get credited to my Kotak Air+ Credit card account?
Air Miles earned on Kotak Air+ Credit Card spends are credited within 5 days of transaction settlement.

### What is the capping on Air Miles earnings for Kotak Air+ Credit Card users?
The accelerated Air Miles are capped at 15,000 Air Miles per month. But there is no capping on base earning rate. So, cardholders will continue earning the base Air Miles rate on all spends even if they reach the capped limit for accelerated Air Miles.
