## About the Card
Apart from cashback, the card offers basic benefits like fuel and railway surcharge waivers, and the option to convert eligible transactions into EMIs. The card also comes with a **cash withdrawal limit of up to 90%** of the total credit limit. Overall, Kotak Dream Different Credit Card is a suitable option for consumers looking for a credit card with cashback on UPI and other everyday transactions.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who can apply for Kotak Dream Different Credit Card?
Kotak Dream Different Credit Card can be suitable for consumers who may not have a CIBIL score or income proof required for a regular credit card. Applicants must meet the eligibility criteria specified by Kotak Mahindra Bank.

### How much cashback can I earn on Kotak Dream Different Credit Card?
You can earn 2% cashback on eligible UPI spends. Additionally, you can earn 2% cashback on on other spends of Rs. 15,000 or more in a billing cycle.

### How does the Rs. 15,000 cashback benefit work?
The card offers 2% cashback when you spend Rs. 15,000 or more in a billing cycle. The benefit is subject to the applicable cashback terms, and cardholders can earn a maximum cashback of Rs. 500 in a billing cycle.

### Can I withdraw cash using Kotak Dream Different Credit Card?
Yes. The card offers a cash withdrawal limit of up to 90% of the total credit limit. However, cash withdrawals may attract applicable fees and finance charges, so this facility should be used only when necessary.

### Is Kotak Dream Different Credit Card suitable for first-time credit card users?
Yes. The card can be a suitable option for first-time credit card users because it does not require a CIBIL score or income proof. Responsible usage and timely bill payments can help users establish their credit history over time.
