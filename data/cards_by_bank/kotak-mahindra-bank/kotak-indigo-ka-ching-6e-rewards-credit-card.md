## About the Card
Kotak Mahindra Bank, in partnership with IndiGo airlines, offers two co-branded credit cards – Kotak IndiGo Ka-ching 6E Rewards and Kotak IndiGo Ka-ching 6E Rewards XL. Here, Kotak IndiGo 6E Rewards comes as the base variant which allows the cardholders to have accelerated savings on IndiGo bookings. The card users are offered up to 3% value-back in the form of 6E Rewards on the card spends. These earned 6E Rewards can later be utilised to make IndiGo travel bookings, hence helping to save a considerable amount on co-branded spends. In addition to the rewards program, cardholders also get exclusive joining benefits worth Rs. 2,599 which is more than triple the value of the card joining fee charged.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who can apply for this credit card?
Indian citizens who fall under the age range of 21 years to 65 years are eligible to apply for this Kotak credit card.

### Does Kotak IndiGo credit card provide complimentary airport lounge access?
No, Kotak IndiGo Ka-ching 6E Rewards Credit Card does not provide complimentary airport lounge access to its users. However, you can avail complimentary airport lounge access with its upgraded version, i.e. Kotak IndiGo Ka-ching 6E Rewards XL Credit Card.

### Do the earned 6E Rewards using Kotak IndiGo credit card expire?
Yes, the 6E Rewards you accrue using the Kotak IndiGo co-branded credit cards are only valid for redemption for up to 24 months from the date of accrual.

### What is the validity of the free air ticket and 6E Prime Add-on offered as welcome bonus to Kotak IndiGo credit card users?
The welcome benefits offered with Kotak IndiGo 6E Rewards Credit Card are only valid for 6 months from the date of issuance.
