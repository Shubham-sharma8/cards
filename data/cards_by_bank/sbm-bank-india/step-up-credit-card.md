## Eligibility Criteria
- **Occupation:** Salaried or Self-Employed
- **Age:** 21 to 65 years
- **Min. Salaried Income:** As per bank policy / relationship
- **Min. CIBIL Score:** 730
- **Approval Notes:** To be eligible to apply for Step Up SBM Credit Card, you must have and maintain a **fixed deposit of minimum Rs. 2,000** with SBM bank mobile app or website. You must also note that only the fixed deposits that are in the name of a single individual are eligible for SMB Step Up Credit Card, though nomination facility will be available for the fixed deposits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is annual fee for Step Up Credit Card?
+- This credit card does not charge any annual fee. However, it charges a joining fee depending on the FD amount. For an FD of Rs. 2,000, the joining fee is Rs. 500 plus applicable taxes. For an FD of Rs. 5,000, the joining fee is Rs. 250 plus applicable taxes and for an FD above Rs. 5,000, there is no joining fee.

### Can I apply online for Step Up Credit Card?
+- Yes, you can apply online for this credit card through Issuer / Market’s app or website. You are required to open an FD account with SBM Bank through Issuer / Market’s app or website to be eligible for this credit card.

### Can I earn rewards on fuel spends with Step Up Credit Card?
+- No, this credit card does not offer reward points on select categories, including e-wallet loading, rent payment, fuel spends, utility payments, insurance premiums and related spends, cash advances, and EMI payments.

### What is the credit limit of Step Up Credit Card?
+- SBM Issuer / Market Step-Up Credit Card is a secured credit card issued against a minimum fixed deposit of Rs. 2,000. The card offers a credit limit of up to 90% of the FD amount.

### Can I redeem earned reward points against cash with Step Up Credit Card?
+- Yes, you can redeem the accumulated reward points as cash credit at a value of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 0.25.
