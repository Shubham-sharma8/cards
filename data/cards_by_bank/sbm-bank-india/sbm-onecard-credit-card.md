## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the joining fee for SBM OneCard Credit Card?
SBM OneCard Credit Card has no joining fee. It is a lifetime-free credit card, making it an ideal choice for those looking for a no-cost card for regular spends.

### How do I earn reward points with SBM OneCard Credit Card?
Consumers can earn 1 reward point for every Rs. 50 spent on eligible purchases. Purchases under Rs. 50 earn fractional points (e.g., Rs. 25 spent earns 0.5 points). Some high-spend categories offer 5 times the points.

### Is there any fuel surcharge waiver with SBM OneCard Credit Card?
Yes, SBM OneCard offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 5,000, capped at Rs. 400 per month.

### How can I redeem the reward points?
You can redeem the reward points for past transactions or other offers. The \"Pay With Points\" feature applies to transactions made within the past two months, and points are automatically deducted.

### What are the benefits of this card?
Apart from reward points on eligible spends, SBM OneCard offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver, up to Rs. 400 per month, through which you can save up to Rs. 4,800 annually on fuel. It also has no joining or annual fees.
