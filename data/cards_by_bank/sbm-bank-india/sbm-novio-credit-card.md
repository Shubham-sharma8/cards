## About the Card
It offers value-back in the form of Novio Coins, which can be redeemed for brand vouchers via Novio app. Cardholders can earn up to **10X Novio Coins on their fuel spends** and 5X Novio Coins on other categories, including UPI transactions. Additionally, the card charges a **comparatively low forex markup fee of 2.49%,** making it a suitable option for international spends.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How does SBM novio Credit Card\\\\\'s rewards program work?
You can earn Novio Coins as value-back on their spends. The card offers up to 10 Coins per Rs. 100 spent on fuel and 5 Coins per Rs. 100 spent on other categories, including UPI transactions. These Coins can be redeemed for brand vouchers through the Novio app.

### What is the credit limit offered on SBM novio Credit Card?
The credit limit on SBM novio Credit Card is 90% of the fixed deposit amount. For example, if your FD is Rs. 10,000, your available credit limit will be Rs. 9,000.

### Are there any fees associated with SBM novio Credit Card?
This card has no joining or annual fees. However, finance charges are 3.5% per month (42% p.a.), and late payment fee is Rs. 499. The card also has a low forex markup fee of 2.49%, making it suitable for international spends.

### What is the eligibility criteria for this card?
The applicants must be Indian residents aged 18 years or above. The minimum FD required is Rs. 5,000 to apply for this credit card.

### Can I use SBM novio Credit Card for international transactions?
Yes, SBM novio Credit Card charges a low forex markup fee of 2.49%, which is lower than many standard credit cards. This makes it a practical option for international spends.

### Is SBM novio suitable for everyday spending?
Yes, the card offers rewards on everyday categories including UPI transactions and accelerated rewards on fuel spends, making it useful for day-to-day purchases while helping you build a strong credit profile.
