## About the Card
SBM ZET Credit Card** is a co-branded credit card offered against an **FD worth minimum Rs. 2,000**. This FD-backed card is available on **RuPay network** and hence can be used to make **UPI transactions** by adding it to third-party UPI apps, like PhonePe, Google pay, CRED, and others. This card does **not require any credit history, income proof or documentation** and is thus **suitable for beginners who are new to credit** and wish to**build their credit scores**. Note: This card is also available in partnership with DCB and IOB, with no joining or annual fees._

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What documents are required to apply for SBM ZET Credit Card?
No documents like income proof and others are required to apply for SBM ZET Credit Card application. However, you will have to provide PAN card and Aadhaar card details.

### Is SBM ZET Credit Card a virtual card?
SBM ZET is offered as both virtual as well as physical card. You can get the virtual card instantly after completing the application process, though the latter can take around 7 days to be delivered to you.

### What is the eligibility criteria to apply for SBM ZET Credit Card?
You must create an SBM fixed deposit of Rs. 5,000 or more to be eligible for SBM ZET Credit Card.

### Can I use SBM ZET card to make UPI payments?
Yes, since SBM ZET is a RuPay credit card, you can add it to any third-party UPI app of choice to make UPI transactions.

### Can I get SBM ZET Credit Card if my credit score is low?
Yes, SBM ZET credit card has 100% approval rate and is offered to all - new to credit customers, those with low credit score and those who already have credit cards.
