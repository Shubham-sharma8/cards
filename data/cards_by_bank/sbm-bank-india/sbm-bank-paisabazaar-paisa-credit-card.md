+- Yes. Cardholders with an FD above Rs. 10,000 earn 1.5% cashback on online spends, compared to 1% for FD amounts between Rs. 2,000 and Rs. 10,000. Offline spends earn 1% cashback regardless of the deposit value.

### Is there a limit on cashback earnings?
