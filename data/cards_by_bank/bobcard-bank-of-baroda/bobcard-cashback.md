## About the Card
BOBCARD Cashback** comes with **up to 5% cashback** benefits on card spends. It offers accelerated cashback earnings of 4% on online purchases in addition to the base cashback rate of 1%, making it a good option for frequent online shoppers. Though these accelerated **4% savings are limited to Rs. 1,500 for every month** , i.e. up to Rs. 37,500 monthly online spends, a low to average spender would still be able to maximise the benefits without much restriction. On the other hand, **offline spends come with unlimited 1% cashback**. The earned **cashback is credited to the account automatically** which makes the value-back process simpler for the card user. Read on to know in detail about the features, benefits and fee structure of BOBCARD Cashback.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the annual fee of BOBCARD Cashback Credit Card?
BOBCARD Cashback comes with an annual fee of Rs. 499. However, cardholders can also choose the monthly fee plan of Rs. 49 per month.

### Can I change the fee plan for BOBCARD Cashback?
Yes, BOBCARD allows you to change the fee plan between annual and monthly fee payments once during the Cashback credit card’s lifecycle.

### Why can I not earn 5% cashback on my online spends using BOBCARD Cashback Credit Card?
5% cashback is offered on select domestic online spends only if you have made at least 4 card transactions in that month. Moreover, this accelerated cashback is also capped at cashback of up to Rs. 1,500 for every month. You will also not earn any cashback on excluded spending categories, as mentioned on this page.

### What is the income eligibility criteria for BOBCARD Cashback?
To be eligible for BOBCARD Cashback, the salaried applicant must have a minimum net annual salary of Rs. 3 lakh and a self-employed applicant must have a min. net annual income of Rs. 3.5 lakh.

### What is the minimum age requirement for BOBCARD Cashback?
To get BOBCARD Cashback, salaried individuals must be 21 to 60 years old and self-employed must be 24 to 65 years old. However, add-on cardholders can be 18 years and above.
