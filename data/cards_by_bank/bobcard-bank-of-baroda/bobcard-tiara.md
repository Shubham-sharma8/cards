## About the Card
With benefits spanning multiple categories, BOBCARD Tiara Credit Card could be a good choice for people who seek a versatile card or those looking to upgrade from a basic card, without committing to a category-focused option. However, there are some limitations that consumers should keep in mind. Despite the card’s strong reward-earning potential, a low redemption ratio of 4:1 brings down the overall value. Additionally, the card offers a comparatively low forex mark-up fee, but it does not feature international lounge visits and domestic lounge visits are also linked to a quarterly spending of Rs. 40,000, which could be limiting for frequent travellers.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does BOBCARD Tiara Credit Card offer lounge access?
Yes, BOBCARD Tiara Credit Card offers unlimited complimentary domestic lounge visits every year, on spending Rs. 40,000 in the previous quarter.

### What are the activation benefits of BOBCARD Tiara Credit Card?
BOBCARD Tiara Credit Card offers annual memberships for Amazon Prime or Jio Hotstar and Gaana Plus, and a 3-month Swiggy One membership as welcome benefits.

### How can I apply for BOBCARD Tiara Credit Card?
To apply for Tiara credit card, you can submit an enquiry online by visiting the official BOBCARD website. Simply check your eligibility and click on the submit enquiry button on BOBCARD Tiara page. Fill the details, as required, to complete the process.

### What exclusive benefits does BOBCARD Tiara Credit Card offer for women?
BOBCARD Tiara Credit Card offers exclusive women’s health benefits, including complimentary health check-ups such as Pap Smear & Blood Tests, a Mammography package, and more.

### Can I earn reward points on fuel spends with BOBCARD Tiara Credit Card?
No, reward points are not offered on fuel transactions. However, you can save up to Rs. 250 every month through a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on fuel spends between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000.

### Can I redeem reward points earned through BOBCARD Tiara Credit Card for cashback?
Yes, you can redeem reward points as a statement credit, where 1 reward point is equivalent to Rs. 0.25.
