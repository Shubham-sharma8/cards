## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the reward rate on BOBCARD VIKRAM?
BOBCARD VIKRAM offers 5 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on movies and departmental stores, subject to a monthly capping of 1,000 reward points. Apart from this, the card offers a base reward rate of 1 reward point per Rs. 100 spent.

### How much are BOBCARD VIKRAM reward points worth?
The accumulated reward points can be redeemed against cashback at a value of Rs. 0.25 per reward point. Therefore, 100 reward points are equivalent to Rs. 25 in cashback.

### Does BOBCARD VIKRAM offer rewards on UPI transactions?
Yes. The RuPay variant of BOBCARD VIKRAM offers 1 reward point per Rs. 100 spent on eligible UPI transactions.

### What are the welcome benefits offered by BOBCARD VIKRAM?
Cardholders can get a complimentary 3-month JioHotstar subscription by making the first transaction within 30 days of card issuance. They can also earn 500 bonus reward points on spending Rs. 5,000 within 60 days of card issuance.

### Can I convert BOBCARD VIKRAM transactions into EMI?
Yes. Eligible transactions of Rs. 2,500 and above can be converted into smart EMIs for a tenure ranging from 6 to 48 months, subject to applicable terms and conditions.
