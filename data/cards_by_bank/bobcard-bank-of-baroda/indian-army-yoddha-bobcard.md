## About the Card
Indian Army YODDHA BOBCARD is exclusively for the Indian army personnel, offering a combination of rewards, travel and lifestyle benefits. Cardholders can earn 10 reward points per Rs. 100 spent at departmental stores, along with a base reward rate of 2 reward points per Rs. 100 on other categories, including UPI spends.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who is eligible for Indian Army YODDHA BOBCARD?
Indian Army YODDHA BOBCARD is designed for eligible Indian Army personnel. Applicants should check the latest eligibility requirements with Bank of Baroda before applying.

### How many reward points can I earn on departmental store spends?
Cardholders can earn 10 reward points per Rs. 100 spent at departmental stores. The accelerated reward earning is capped at 1,000 reward points per month.

### What is the value of reward points on Indian Army YODDHA BOBCARD?
Reward points can be redeemed against cashback at a value of 1 reward point = Rs. 0.25.

### Does Indian Army YODDHA BOBCARD offer airport lounge access?
Yes. The card offers 8 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year, with 2 visits per quarter, on spending Rs. 20,000 in the preceding quarter.

### What are the welcome benefits offered on the card?
Cardholders can earn 500 bonus reward points on spending Rs. 5,000 or more within 60 days of card issuance. The card also offers a complimentary 12-month Amazon Prime Membership on spending Rs. 1 Lakh within 90 days of issuance and a complimentary 12-month FITPASS PRO Membership.

### Does the card offer add-on cards?
Yes. Cardholders can get up to 3 lifetime free add-on credit cards for eligible family members, subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

### What concierge services are available with Indian Army YODDHA BOBCARD?
The card offers 24x7 concierge services for domestic and international requirements through the RuPay variant. The availability of specific services may be subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

### Is Indian Army YODDHA BOBCARD suitable for UPI payments?
Yes. The card offers 2 reward points per Rs. 100 on eligible spends across other categories, including UPI transactions. As a RuPay card, it can be linked to supported UPI apps for eligible payments.
