## About the Card
BOBCARD Eterna Credit Card is a good all-rounder offering which provides decent value-back on multiple categories. Its reward structure comes with accelerated benefits on travel, online shopping, dining and international spends. Here, though all these spends accrue 5X rewards, travel spends made via BOBCARD SmartDeal let you further earn up to 10X extra rewards. However, when it comes to international spends, even though you get a value-back of 3.75%, you must also take in account the forex mark-up fee of 2% which makes the overall savings on such spends drop to around 1.75%.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the documents required to apply for Eterna and other BOBCARD Credit Cards?
The applicant must have a recent passport size photograph, along with proofs of identity, address and income while applying for BOBCARD Credit Cards.

### What is the benefit of BOBCARD Eterna card?
BOBCARD Eterna credit card offers travel and reward benefits. Cardholders can avail unlimited domestic lounge access and a low forex markup fee of 2%. Additionally, it offers accelerated rewards on dining and travel. To add more to the lifestyle privileges, cardholders can avail buy-one-get-one offer on District, and rewards on all their spends.

### Which payment network does BOBCARD Eterna Credit Card use?
BOBCARD Eterna Credit Card uses MasterCard payment network for its transactions.

### I earn Rs. 10 lakh per annum. Can I apply for BOBCARD Eterna Credit Card?
BOBCARD Eterna Credit Card is given to those applicants who earn Rs. 12 lakh p.a. or more. However, your chances of approval may increase if you have maintained a good relationship with the bank. The final decision to accept or decline the application rests with the bank.

### What is credit limit and how much credit limit will be given to me on BOBCARD Eterna Credit Card?
Credit Limit is the maximum amount of money that you can spend using the credit card. The credit limit extended to you is dependent on your income, previous credit limit, credit history and so on.

### Does the add-on card of BOBCARD Eterna have a separate credit limit?
No, the add-on cardholder will share the credit limit that was assigned to the primary cardholder.

### Is the Eterna card suitable for international transactions?
The card offers accelerated rewards on international transactions and a low forex markup fee of 2%, making it a suitable choice for international spends.
