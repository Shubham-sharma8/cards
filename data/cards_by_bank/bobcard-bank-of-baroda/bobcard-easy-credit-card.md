## Frequently Asked Questions
### Can I get a BOBCARD Easy Credit Card even if I do not pay taxes?
Yes, you can get this card even if you do not fall in a tax bracket that requires tax payment. However, you must meet the eligibility criteria set by the bank in order to avail this card.

### Is BOBCARD Easy Credit Card available on both Visa and RuPay platforms?
Yes, BOBCARD Easy Credit Card is available on Visa, Mastercard and RuPay platforms, giving cardholders the flexibility to choose as per their preference and acceptance.

### Who is eligible to apply for BOBCARD Easy Credit Card?
Salaried or self-employed Indian residents aged between 21 and 60 years for salaried and 24 and 65 years for self-employed applicants with a stable income and good credit history are eligible to apply for BOBCARD Easy Credit Card. The minimum income requirement details are mentioned on the page above.

### What are the charges for international transactions on BOBCARD Easy Credit Card?
A foreign currency markup fee of 3.5% of the transaction value is applicable on all international spends made with BOBCARD Easy.

### How do I increase my BOBCARD Easy Credit Card limit?
To increase your credit limit, you can place a request with the bank by providing income proof or maintaining a strong repayment history, as the bank reviews your eligibility before approving a higher limit.
