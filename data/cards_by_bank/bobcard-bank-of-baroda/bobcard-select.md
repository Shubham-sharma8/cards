## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the documents required to apply for BOBCARD Select and other BOBCARD Credit Cards?
The applicant must have proof of identity, address and income, along with a recent passport size photograph, while applying for BOBCARD Credit Cards.

### Do I get accelerated rewards on all travel spends using BOBCARD Select?
No, cardholders can get extra rewards on flight and hotel bookings made using BOBCARD SmartDeal platform only. All other travel spends outside this platform will earn the base reward rate of 1 Reward point per Rs. 100 spent.

### What is the capping limit on 5X rewards earnings using BOBCARD Select?
5 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on online and dining spending categories have a combined capping of 1,000 reward points per month. After reaching this limit, these category spends would accrue 1 reward point per Rs. 100 spent.

### Is there any cashback benefit on BOBCARD Select?
The reward points that you earn on the spends made using BOBCARD Select Credit card can be redeemed as cashback.

### Is BOBCARD Select available for self-employed?
Yes, self-employed applicants between the age of 24 and 65 years and a net annual income of more than Rs. 4.8 lakh are eligible to get BOBCARD Select Credit Card, subject to other internal policies of the card provider.
