## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does BOBCARD HPCL ENERGIE Credit Card offer lounge access?
Yes, cardholders can get 1 complimentary domestic lounge visit every quarter, subject to meeting the quarterly spend threshold of Rs. 20,000.

### How much fuel surcharge waiver can I get on BOBCARD HPCL ENERGIE Credit Card?
Fuel surcharge waiver is capped at Rs. 100 per statement cycle.

### Are there any renewal benefits on the card?
No. The card does not offer any renewal benefits. However, it comes with an easy annual fee waiver condition of Rs. 50,000 per year.

### Can I convert my transactions into EMIs?
Yes. BOBCARD HPCL ENERGIE Credit Card offers Smart EMI Option wherein you can convert transactions above Rs. 2,500 into EMIs of 6 months to 48 months. The rate of interest will be communicated at the time of sourcing.

### What are the terms and conditions related to the movie benefit on BOBCARD HPCL ENERGIE Credit Card?
You get 25% off up to Rs. 100 when booking movie tickets via District App. However, you must book at least 2 tickets to avail this benefit. You can get this discount only twice a month.
