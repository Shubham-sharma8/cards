## About the Card
BOBCARD Etihad Guest Credit Card offers **3 Etihad Guest Miles for every Rs. 100 spent at Etihad** and 1 Mile per Rs. 100 on non-Etihad spends. Cardholders can redeem the accumulated Miles only through the Etihad platform, making it a suitable option for Etihad loyalists. Besides this, a**low forex markup fee of 1%** , 8 domestic & 4 international lounge visits per year, enhances its value for frequent travellers. Another standout feature of this card is the milestone benefit. Cardholders can earn **more than 15,000 Miles every year** and save significantly on their travel spends by redeeming them against flight bookings. The card also offers a complimentary **Etihad Guest Silver Tier membership** and 2,500 Miles as welcome benefits. Overall, BOBCARD Etihad Guest Credit Card offers great value for moderate spenders and frequent flyers of Etihad who can easily reach spending milestones to maximize travel benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is BOBCARD Etihad Guest best used for?
BOBCARD Etihad Guest Credit Card is best for frequent travellers who often fly with Etihad Airways. It offers higher Etihad Guest Miles on Etihad Airways, a low 1% forex markup, and airport lounge access, making it suitable for international and travel-focused spending.

### How can I earn Etihad Guest Miles with this card?
Cardholders can earn 3 Etihad Guest Miles for every Rs. 100 spent on Etihad Airways. For other domestic and international spends, the card offers 1 Mile per Rs. 100. Additional Miles can also be earned through monthly, quarterly, and annual milestone benefits.

### What are the lounge access benefits on this card?
The card offers 8 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year, with a limit of 2 per quarter. It also offers 4 complimentary international lounge visits annually, capped at 1 per quarter.

### Does the card offer any welcome or joining benefits?
Yes, the card offers 2,500 Etihad Guest Bonus Miles on spending Rs. 10,000 within the first 60 days. Cardholders also get a complimentary Etihad Guest Silver Tier membership on completing their first transaction within six months of card issuance.

### Who is eligible to apply for this credit card?
Indian residents aged between 25 and 60 years can apply for this card. Salaried applicants should have a minimum annual income of Rs. 18 Lakh. Both salaried and self-employed individuals are eligible, subject to the BOBCard’s approval norms.
