## About the Card
Standard Chartered Super Value Titanium is an entry-level credit card primarily offering benefits across fuel and utility spends. It is among the few credit cards in India that offer benefits on fuel without being in co-branded partnership with a fuel refinery. Value-back on primary categories is offered in the form of direct cashback, with separate capping on each category. On other spends, cardholders earn reward points. At an annual fee of Rs. 750, Standard Chartered Super Value Titanium Credit Card is slightly costlier than other cards in this category. Read on to know about its features and benefits and a comparison with other fuel and entry-level cards.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Can I transfer my outstanding balance on any other bank’s credit card?
Yes, Standard Chartered Super Value Titanium credit card provides the facility of ‘Balance Transfer’ through which you can transfer your outstanding balance on any other bank’s credit card. The card allows transferring other credit card balance of up to Rs. 5 Lakh, at an interest rate of 0.99% for first six months.

### What are the documents required to apply for Super Value Titanium credit card?
To apply for Standard Chartered Super Value Titanium credit card, you need to provide government approved photo ID proof like Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID card, etc. You will also have to provide address proof and income proof documents.

### Will I be charged anything extra if I use my card overseas?
Yes. All transactions made in any foreign currency will attract a 3.5% forex mark-up fee. GST will also be applicable on the fee amount.

### Can I get an add-on card for my son?
Yes. You can get an add-on card on the Super Value Titanium Credit Card. However, minimum age of the add-on cardmember should be 18 years.

### How can I convert my outstanding bills into EMIs?
You can convert your outstanding amount into EMI through net banking or mobile app. Minimum spends of Rs. 2,000 can be converted. Tenure ranges from 3 months to 60 months and the interest rate starts from 0.99% per month.
