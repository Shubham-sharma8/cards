## Frequently Asked Questions
### Which transactions are not eligible for cashback?
You can earn cashback on online spends and offline spends except fuel, cash withdrawal and returns and exchange transactions.

### Is there any fee charged on the redemption of cashback?
No, there is no fee charged on the redemption of cashback earned via the Standard Chartered Smart Credit Card.

### Is the extended interest-free credit period also applicable on cash transactions?
No, the extended interest-free credit period of 90 days is not applicable on cash transactions done on the Standard Chartered Smart Credit Card. Cash transactions are charged at an interest rate of 3.75% per month.

### Can I avail the interest-free credit period on Loan against credit card, EMI and balance transfer facility?
No, the interest-free credit period is only applicable on retail transactions done via this credit card. The payments for these services- Loan, EMI and balance transfer are serviced separately.

### Are there any transactions thresholds for earning cashback ?
There are no minimum transaction value or minimum spends requirement for earning cashback.

### What are the benefits of Standard Chartered Smart Credit Card?
Standard Chartered Smart Credit Card is a cashback card that offers 2% cashback on online spends and 1% cashback on offline spends. In addition, the card offers an interest-free period of up to 90 days and a low-interest EMI facility.

### What is the annual fee for Standard Chartered Smart Card?
Standard Chartered Smart Credit Card charges a joining and annual fee of Rs. 499 plus applicable taxes. However, the annual fee can be waived off by spending Rs. 1.2 Lakh or more in a year.

### Does Standard Chartered Smart Card provide lounge access?
No, Standard Chartered Smart Credit Card does not offer domestic or international lounge access benefits.
