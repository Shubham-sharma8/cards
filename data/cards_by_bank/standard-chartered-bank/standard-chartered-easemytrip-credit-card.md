## About the Card
Standard Chartered EaseMyTrip Credit Card is a co-branded travel credit card that offers benefits to its users in the form of instant discount on travel bookings made through EaseMyTrip, along with rewards benefits and complimentary lounge access. With this credit card, users can avail direct discount of up to 20% on domestic and international travel bookings on the platform without any minimum spending threshold. Moreover, travel benefits are not just limited to EaseMyTrip as the cardholders can earn accelerated rewards on spends made at standalone airline and hotel websites or apps as well, along with a base reward rate of all other spends. These rewards points can be redeemed on the bank’s 360 Rewards platform against a variety of options.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How many rewards can I earn on EaseMyTrip bookings via Standard Chartered EaseMyTrip Credit Card?
You can earn 2 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on EaseMyTrip bookings using your Standard Chartered EaseMyTrip Credit Card.

### Can I waive the annual fee of Standard Chartered EaseMyTrip Credit Card?
No, there is no fee waiver option for Standard Chartered EaseMyTrip cardholders.

### How to redeem Standard Chartered EaseMyTrip Credit Card reward points? Can I redeem them on EaseMyTrip?
The accrued reward points are redeemable via the Standard Chartered 360 rewards portal where 2 Reward Points = Rs. 0.50. However, you cannot redeem them directly on the EaseMyTrip portal.

### What benefits does Standard Chartered EaseMyTrip Credit Card offer on EaseMyTrip?
You get an instant discount of up to 20% on EaseMyTrip using this card on hotel, flight and bus ticket bookings. You can also earn 2 reward points on every Rs. 100 spent on this platform.
