## About the Card
Standard Chartered Platinum Rewards Credit Card is an entry-level card primarily designed to offer benefits on dining and fuel spends. Cardholders can **earn 5X reward points** (5 points per Rs. 150) **on dining and fuel** , **without any monthly capping**. While many cards either exclude fuel from their rewards program or limit the accelerated rewards, this card’s unlimited accelerated rewards give it an edge over other entry-level cards. For other spends, the card offers a **base reward rate of 1 point per Rs. 150** spent. Cardholders can redeem the accumulated reward points against gift vouchers and products & items through the rewards catalogue at a value of **1 RP = Rs. 0.25**. While the card does not charge any annual fee, it comes with a **joining fee of Rs. 250** , which gets waived off on spending Rs. 500 within the first 30 days, an easily achievable threshold even for low spenders. Overall, this card is a suitable option for consumers whose monthly spends are largely focused on fuel and dining.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is Standard Chartered Platinum Rewards Credit Card good?
Yes, Standard Chartered Platinum Rewards Credit Card is a good option for those seeking decent rewards without paying an annual fee. With this card, you can earn 5X rewards on fuel and fine dining without any monthly capping, along with base rewards on other spends.

### Can I transfer my Standard Chartered Platinum Rewards Card’s outstanding balance to another bank’s credit card?
Yes, Standard Chartered Platinum Rewards Credit Card provides the facility of ‘Balance Transfer’ through which you can transfer your outstanding balance on any other bank’s credit card.

### Will I be charged if I use my Standard Chartered Platinum Rewards Card overseas?
Yes, this card charges a forex markup fee of 3.5%. Therefore, it may not be the best choice for international transactions. If you frequently spend abroad, you can consider opting for a card with a low or zero forex markup fee.

### How can I apply for Standard Chartered Platinum Rewards Card?
You can apply for this card online by visiting the official website of Standard Chartered Bank. You are required to fill out the application form, and if eligible, your card may be issued instantly.

### How can I redeem reward points earned through Standard Chartered Platinum Rewards Card?
You can redeem your accumulated reward points by logging into Standard Chartered\'s net banking portal and choosing from a variety of options such as gift vouchers, items, and more.
