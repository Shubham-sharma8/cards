## About the Card
Standard Chartered Beyond is an invite-only credit card that is exclusively offered to Priority Banking customers. The credit card is designed to offer premium benefits, such as unlimited lounge access, golf games, complimentary hotel stays, and more. The card offers **3.33% savings on everyday spends** and **2% savings on select excluded categories** , such as utilities, insurance, government spends, and more. Cardholders can redeem the accumulated reward points at a **1:1 ratio** via the 360 Rewards catalogue. Apart from rewards, the card also offers premium travel and lifestyle benefits, such as a low forex markup fee of 2%, **unlimited global lounge access** , complimentary golf games, and exclusive **benefits at ITC Hotels, Elivaas Villas** , and other premium hotels.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who can apply for Standard Chartered Beyond Credit Card?
Standard Chartered Beyond Credit Card is an invite-only card offered exclusively to Priority Banking customers. Applicants must be between 18 and 70 years of age and can be salaried or self-employed. Since it is invite-based, eligible customers are selected by the bank.

### How can I redeem reward points?
Cardholders can redeem their reward points through the 360 Rewards catalogue at a value of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 1. You can use these points for travel bookings, vouchers, products, and more. This 1:1 redemption ratio enhances the overall value of the card.

### What is the credit limit offered on Standard Chartered Beyond Credit Card?
The credit limit on Standard Chartered Beyond Credit Card is not fixed and varies from one customer to another. It depends on factors such as income, credit profile, and banking relationship with Standard Chartered. Since this is an invite-only card, it generally comes with a high credit limit. The exact limit is communicated at the time of card issuance.

### Are there any restrictions on the Buy One Get One offer on movie tickets?
Yes, the BOGO offer on movie tickets comes with certain conditions. The benefit is available on BookMyShow when you purchase a minimum of two tickets. The discount is capped at Rs. 1,000 per month. Additionally, this offer can be availed only once per month.

### What dining benefits are available on Standard Chartered Beyond Credit Card?
Cardholders can avail up to 25% discount on dining bills via EazyDiner at partner restaurants. The discount is capped at Rs. 2,500 per month and does not require a minimum transaction value. Additionally, users get access to an exclusive reservation platform with curated dining experiences. This makes it a good option for those who frequently dine at premium restaurants.

### Does this card offer an annual fee waiver?
Yes, the card offers an annual fee waiver, but it is linked to FUM (Funds Under Management) criteria. Cardholders need to maintain the required FUM with the bank to qualify for the waiver. The threshold is relatively high, making it suitable for affluent customers. If the criteria are not met, an annual fee of Rs. 25,000 is applicable.

### What travel benefits does Standard Chartered Beyond Credit Card offer?
The card offers a wide range of premium travel benefits, including unlimited domestic and international lounge access with complimentary guest visits. Cardholders also get a low forex markup fee of 2%, making it suitable for international spends. Additional benefits include complimentary hotel stays and exclusive privileges at ITC Hotels and Elivaas Villas. It also offers 15% cashback on duty-free spends, capped at Rs. 1,500 per month.
