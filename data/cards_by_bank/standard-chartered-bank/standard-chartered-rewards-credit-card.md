## Benefits, Milestones & Insurance

### Milestone Benefits
- 1X Rewards: 1 reward point on every Rs. 150 spent on insurance and government spends
- Bonus Rewards: Additional 4 reward points per Rs. 150 spent above Rs. 20,000 monthly retail spends
- Card renewal fee is reversed on spending a minimum of Rs. 1.5 lakh during the previous year
- 1% fuel surcharge waiver of up to Rs. 400 per month, on fuel spends of Rs. 400 to Rs. 2,000

### Dining Benefits
- No significant benefit on other lifestyle-related spending categories like dining, movies, travel, etc.

### Movie / Entertainment
- No significant benefit on other lifestyle-related spending categories like dining, movies, travel, etc.

### Insurance Cover
- 1X Rewards: 1 reward point on every Rs. 150 spent on insurance and government spends

### Get This Card If
- You want a basic rewards credit card which also offers domestic airport lounge benefits
- You can maximise your rewards earnings by becoming eligible for additional 4 rewards points feature by spending more than Rs. 20,000 in a month
- You prefer to redeem your reward points for offers, vouchers and products available in the bank’s rewards catalogue over statement credit and hence can get a higher value-back using them

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Why am I not able to redeem the reward points against Standard Chartered Rewards Credit Card outstanding balance?
You must have at least 1,000 Standard Chartered reward points to initiate their redemption against your credit card statement.

### Is there any redemption fee levied on my Standard Chartered credit card reward points?
Yes, a rewards redemption fee of Rs. 99 is charged whenever you redeem your Standard Chartered credit card rewards.

### Where can I check my earned reward points using the Standard Chartered rewards credit card?
You can check your reward points balance that you have earned via transactions done using the Standard Chartered Rewards card either on the monthly credit card statement or on the 360 Rewards portal.

### Is there a capping on reward points earning on Standard Chartered Rewards Credit Card?
Yes, the bonus 4X reward points earned on monthly retail spends above Rs. 20,000 are capped at 2,000 reward points per month.
