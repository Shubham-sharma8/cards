## About the Card
Standard Chartered Ultimate is a premium credit card offering **3.33% value-back** across multiple spending categories, matching top premium cards like **HDFC Diners Club Black** and **HDFC Infinia**. It also provides notable travel benefits, including **5% cashback on duty-free purchases** and a **low forex mark-up of 2%**. Up to **12 complimentary rounds annually** at premier golf courses in India further add to its benefits. However, the card falls short on **lounge access** , offering **12 free visits per year** with a **spend-based condition**. Additionally, its **lifestyle perks** in categories like **dining and entertainment** are limited. Despite lacking on additional benefits, the card’s core rewards program is strong. It stands out for its broad reward coverage, including utilities, insurance, and education—categories often excluded by other cards. If you are a high-spender and do not seek air mile conversion and prefer redemption through vouchers and merchandise, this card can be a great fit.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the additional benefits on Standard Chartered Ultimate credit card?
The additional benefits offered on Standard Chartered Ultimate credit card include digital payment solutions, online banking, and conversion of transactions above Rs. 5,000 to EMI. Apart from that, the card also offers dial-a-loan facility for a loan up to Rs. 5 Lakh for tenures ranging between 12 to 60 months.

### Can I transfer my outstanding balance on any other bank’s credit card with Ultimate credit card?
Yes, Standard Chartered Ultimate credit card provides the facility of ‘Balance Transfer’ via which you can transfer your outstanding balance on any other bank’s credit card. You can transfer card balance up to Rs. 5 lakh to Standard Chartered Ultimate Credit Card. However, this may vary as per your credit limit.

### Will I be charged if I use my card overseas?
Yes, a foreign exchange mark-up fee of 2% (plus 18% GST) will be applicable on all transactions made in foreign currency.

### When are the bonus reward points credited to my card account?
The bonus 6,000 reward points on card activation are credited within 60 days of receiving of joining fee payment by the issuer.

### What are options to redeem my reward points?
Accumulated reward points can be redeemed for vouchers and merchandise purchase from Standard Chartered Bank’s 360° Rewards Portal.
