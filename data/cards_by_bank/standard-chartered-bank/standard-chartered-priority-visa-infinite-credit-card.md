## About the Card
Standard Chartered Priority Visa Infinite Credit Card is primarily designed to offer benefits around travel and lifestyle. Cardholders can earn**accelerated reward points on international and retail spends** , making it a suitable option for frequent travellers. Additionally, travel benefits like up to **24 complimentary international** and **16 domestic lounge visits** per year further enhance the card’s value. However, only Priority Banking customers are eligible for complimentary international lounge access, while non-Priority customers receive only the Priority Pass membership. It’s also important to note that this card is **lifetime free only for Priority Banking customers** , and non-Priority Banking customers have to pay both joining and annual fees. Another drawback is the **high forex markup fee of 3.5%** , especially considering that this card focuses on international travel. This can significantly reduce the value of accelerated rewards on international spends. Overall, this card is a **suitable option if you maintain a Priority Banking relationship** with the bank and want to avail decent travel benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for Standard Chartered Priority Visa Infinite Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card through the bank’s official website. You need to fill out an application form and attach the required documents to apply online for this card. However, if you want to apply offline, you can visit any of the nearest branches of the bank and contact an executive.

### I don’t have an account with the bank. Can I apply for Standard Chartered Priority Visa Infinite Credit Card?
Yes, you can apply for this credit card. However, if you are not a Priority Banking customer, your approval will depend on the bank’s eligibility criteria, and you will be required to pay both joining and annual fees. Additionally, you must be between 21 and 65 years of age and have a stable monthly income to be eligible for this credit card.

### What is the credit limit offered on Standard Chartered Priority Visa Infinite Credit Card?
The bank decides the credit limit only after scrutinizing your application. Your credit limit depends on various factors, including monthly income, repayment capacity, credit score and more. Therefore, you will get to know about your credit limit only after successful verification.

### Can I withdraw cash via Standard Chartered Priority Visa Infinite Credit Card?
Yes, you can withdraw cash using your credit card through any ATM. However, the bank will charge a cash advance fee of 3% of the cash withdrawal amount or a minimum of Rs. 300. Therefore, it is advisable not to withdraw cash using a credit card as it incurs high charges.

### How can I check my Standard Chartered Priority Visa Infinite Credit Card application status?
You can check your credit card application status by visiting the bank’s official website. All you need to do is visit the application status page on the official website and enter the application number to check your status. Apart from this, you can also call Standard Chartered Credit Card customer care number and ask the representative about your status.
