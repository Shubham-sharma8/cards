## Good, Bad & Best For Whom
**Summary:** Lifetime Free option from Canara Bank suited for rupay & upi users.

**Best For:** Everyday UPI Scan & Pay spenders wanting credit-line convenience on merchant QRs

**Not Ideal For:** Travel flyers seeking high-value airline/hotel miles

**Recommended Monthly Spend:** Rs. 10,000 – Rs. 35,000 on UPI & retail

### Pros (Good)
- Lifetime Free card with zero annual fee commitment
- RuPay network supports Scan & Pay merchant UPI transactions

### Cons (Bad)
- No complimentary domestic or international airport lounge access
- High 3.5% (+GST) foreign currency markup — poor for overseas spends
- Check latest issuer exclusions on rent, utilities, education & wallet loads before high spends
