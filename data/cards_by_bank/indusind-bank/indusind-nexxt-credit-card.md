## About the Card
IndusInd Nexxt Credit Card offers **1 reward point per Rs. 150 spent** across all categories. Cardholders can redeem the accumulated reward points against cashback, Airmiles, products, and vouchers through the rewards catalogue or via the Pay with Points option. In addition, the card offers **free movie tickets** on bookings made through BookMyShow, along with other basic benefits. While the card does not charge an annual fee from the second year onwards, it comes with a **joining fee of Rs. 10,000**. However, it offers **complimentary vouchers** from top brands like Montblanc, The Postcard Hotel, Amazon, Flipkart, and more as welcome benefits. Overall, IndusInd Nexxt is a rewards credit card with basic benefits and is suitable for consumers who want to save on their online and offline spends through reward points.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for IndusInd Nexxt Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online by visiting the official website of IndusInd Bank. You’ll need to fill out the application form and submit the required documents. Alternatively, you can apply offline by visiting any of the bank’s branches and submitting the form in person.

### Can I use IndusInd Nexxt Credit Card for international transactions?
Yes, you can use this credit card for overseas transactions. However, it comes with a forex markup fee of 3.5%, which is relatively high. If you are looking to save on international spends, consider a card with zero or low forex markup fees.

### How many free movie tickets can I get in a month with IndusInd Nexxt Credit Card?
You can avail Buy 1 Get 1 Free offer on movie ticket bookings through BookMyShow. A maximum of 2 free movie tickets per month can be availed, with a capping of Rs. 200 per ticket.

### How is IndusInd Nexxt an interactive credit card?
IndusInd Nexxt Credit Card is an interactive card equipped with buttons that allow you to choose your preferred mode of payment—Credit, EMI, or Rewards, at the time of purchase.

### What is the annual fee of IndusInd Nexxt Credit Card?
This card does not charge any annual fee from the second year onwards. However, it does come with a one-time joining fee of Rs. 10,000.
