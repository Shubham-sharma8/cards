## About the Card
IndusInd Bank Duo Card is India’s first debit-cum-credit card. This card comes with two EMV chips, allowing cardholders to use it as a debit or credit card as per their requirement. Cardholders can also earn **1 reward point for every Rs. 150** spent through this card. Besides this, the card offers other benefits such as free **movie tickets worth Rs. 3,000** every year, **20% discount on Zomato & BigBasket**, 2% cross currency markup fee waiver, and more, which further enhances the card’s overall value. Consumers looking for a **dual-functionality card with basic benefits** can apply for this card. However, it might not be the best option for those seeking a credit card that offer category-specific benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for IndusInd Bank Duo Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card through both offline as well as online mode. To apply online, you have to visit the official website of IndusInd Bank and fill out the application form. However, consumers, who want to apply offline, can visit the nearest branch of IndusInd Bank.

### Can I earn reward points on debit card transactions?
Yes, IndusInd Bank Duo Card lets you earn reward points on transactions via both credit and debit card. However, the reward points offered on the first transaction through the debit card are much better than the reward points offered on credit card transactions.

### What is the interest-free credit period offered on this credit card?
The interest-free period offered on this credit card is up to 50 days. However, this interest-free period is not applicable if the total amount due is not paid in full.

### Can I withdraw cash using IndusInd Bank Duo Credit Card?
Yes, you can withdraw cash using your IndusInd Bank Duo Credit Card. However, the bank will charge a cash advance fee of 2.5% of the withdrawn amount. Therefore, it is advisable not to withdraw cash using a credit card.

### Can I earn reward points on fuel transactions?
No, you cannot earn reward points on fuel transactions. However, you can avail a fuel surcharge waiver of 1% across all petrol pumps in the country. This waiver can be availed only on transactions between Rs. 500 and Rs. 3,000.

### What is the over limit charge for IndusInd Bank Duo Credit Card?
The over limit charges for IndusInd Bank Duo card is 2.5% of the over limit amount. However, the bank charges a minimum over limit charge of Rs. 500.** **
