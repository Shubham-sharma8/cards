## About the Card
The IndusInd Legend Credit Card is a**rewards and travel-focused card, offering accelerated earning on weekend spends and a good redemption value on transferring points to air miles**. It can be a good option for frequent travelers as the forex mark-up charge is low (1.8%), and the accumulated rewards can be transferred to partner airline loyalty programs in a 1:1 ratio. Complimentary movie tickets, a dedicated concierge service, auto-assist, and complimentary insurance add to the card’s benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is the IndusInd Legend card lifetime free?
Yes, IndusInd Bank Legend is a lifetime free credit card.

### When do the reward points on the IndusInd Legend credit card expire?
The reward points you earn through the IndusInd Bank Legend credit card never expire.

### How to get free movie tickets on the Legend credit card?
The card offers a Buy One Get One benefit on bookings via BookMyShow with savings of up to Rs. 200 in a month. To avail this benefit, log on to www.bookmyshow.com.

### Who can apply for this credit card?
Indian citizens who are over 18 years of age but below 65 years of age can apply for this credit card. Also, customers must have a clean credit history.

### Does IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card offer lounge access?
No, with effect from 7 March, 2025 the complimentary lounge access benefit on the card has been discontinued.

### How can we contact the customer care of IndusInd Bank?
You can contact the customer care of IndusInd Bank for issues related to credit cards at 1860 267 7777.

### How to redeem Legend credit card points?
You can redeem the accumulated reward points on the IndusInd Bank Legend credit card against cash credit (1 RP = Rs. 0.5), KrisFlyer Miles(4:1), or for non-cash redemption/Pay with Points (1 RP = up to Rs. 0.75).

### How to activate an IndusInd Legend credit card?
You can activate your IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card through the bank’s official website or the mobile banking app. Alternatively, you can contact the IndusInd Bank Credit Card Customer Care.

### How to close the IndusInd Legend card?
To close the IndusInd Bank Legend Credit Card, you can contact the bank’s customer care at 1860 267 7777.
