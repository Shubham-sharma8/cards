## About the Card
IndusInd Platinum Credit Card is a rewards credit card currently available on the Visa and RuPay networks. Essentially a reward-focused card, IndusInd Platinum offers value back across all transactions in the form of reward points, which can be redeemed against various options, including air miles, cash credit, or products/vouchers from their rewards catalogue. Although promoted as a rewards and lifestyle credit card, IndusInd Platinum struggles to establish itself as a rewarding option, especially in a market where several entry-level cards come with extensive benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the benefits offered by IndusInd Bank Platinum Credit Card?
IndusInd Bank Platinum Credit Card is an entry-level credit card that offers basic benefits, including 1.5 reward points for every Rs. 150 spent, complimentary vouchers as welcome benefits, and 1% fuel surcharge waiver.

### Can I earn rewards on UPI spends with IndusInd Bank Platinum Credit Card?
Yes, with IndusInd Bank Platinum RuPay Credit Card, you can earn reward points on both UPI and non-UPI transactions. You will earn 2 reward points on every Rs. 100 spent through UPI and 1 reward point for every Rs. 100 spent on non-UPI transactions.

### How can I apply for IndusInd Bank Platinum Credit Card?
To apply for this credit card online, visit the official website of IndusInd Bank, fill out the application form, and submit the required documents. Alternatively, you can apply offline by visiting your nearest IndusInd Bank branch and submitting the application form in person.

### Can I earn reward points on fuel spends with IndusInd Bank Platinum Credit Card?
No, IndusInd Bank Platinum Credit Card does not offer reward points on fuel transactions. However, you can avail a 1% fuel surcharge waiver across all petrol pumps in India on transactions between Rs. 500 and Rs. 3,000, with a monthly capping of Rs. 100.

### What are the travel benefits offered by IndusInd Bank Platinum Credit Card?
IndusInd Bank Platinum Credit Card does not provide exclusive travel benefits. However, you can earn reward points on every spend and redeem them for air miles at a rate of 400 reward points = 100 KrisFlyer Miles, allowing you save on travel expenses.

### Does IndusInd Platinum credit card have lounge access?
No, the IndusInd Platinum credit card does not offer any lounge access benefit.

### Is the IndusInd Platinum Rupay credit card good?
The IndusInd Platinum RuPay card offers basic reward benefits. It can be a suitable choice for beginners who are new to credit and are looking for a lifetime free credit card.
