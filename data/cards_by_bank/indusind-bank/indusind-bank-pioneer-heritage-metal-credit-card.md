## About the Card
The IndusInd Bank Pioneer Heritage Metal Credit Card is a premium category card largely offered to high-net-worth customers, especially affluent Indian professionals and entrepreneurs. With this card, one can avail benefits around multiple lifestyle categories like travel, wellness, golf, entertainment, etc. However, in comparison to other premium cards, at a high annual fee the reward rate on this card is significantly low, especially for domestic transactions. On the other hand, a zero forex markup fee and decent rewards on international transactions mark it as a suitable choice for international travelers who can maximize the reward benefits via miles conversion. Thus, the card can be a good choice for those who already own a rewards credit card but want another card from the premium category.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Does the IndusInd Bank Pioneer Heritage Credit Card come with contactless technology?
Yes, the IndusInd Bank Pioneer Heritage Credit Card comes with contactless technology.

### What can I do if my IndusInd Bank Pioneer Heritage Metal Credit Card is misplaced or stolen?
In case of loss or misplacement of the credit card, you can call the IndusInd Bank credit card customer care and report the case after which your card will be blocked and a new one will be dispatched to your registered address.

### What does the feature of ‘Total Protect’ entail on the IndusInd Bank Pioneer Heritage Metal Credit Card?
With the Total Protect feature, you will be insured for a sum up to the credit limit of your card. It covers unauthorised transactions if your card is lost or misplaced and counterfeit fraud on your credit card.

### Is there an interest-free period on the credit card?
Yes, an interest-free period of up to 50 days is given on the IndusInd Bank Pioneer Heritage Metal Credit Card.

### What benefits are offered with the complimentary LoungeKey membership?
Complimentary LoungeKey membership includes access to over 850 lounges in more than 400+ airports over 120 countries worldwide, guest visits (chargeable at USD 27 per person, per guest), access to business facilities (subject to availability and at the discretion of lounge operator) – email, internet, phones, fax machines, and even conference space in some lounges.
