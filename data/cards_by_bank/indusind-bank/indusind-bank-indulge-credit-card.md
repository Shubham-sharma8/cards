## About the Card
IndusInd Bank Indulge Credit Card is a premium card with a pure **22K gold inlay** and **no pre-set spending limit**. It offers benefits across multiple categories such as dining, travel, lifestyle, and entertainment, making it an all-rounder option for high spenders. While the card caters to various categories, its **reward rate is comparatively low at 1.5%** , especially when compared to similar cards like HDFC Diners Club Black (3.33% value-back) and ICICI Emeralde Private (3% value-back). However, the card somewhat compensates for the lower earning rate by offering reward redemption at a 1:1 ratio (1 RP = Rs. 1) against cashback. Besides rewards, the card offers limited travel benefits, such as a **low forex markup fee of 1.8%** and **16 complimentary international lounge visits** per year. Whereas, other cards with a similar annual fee offer unlimited international lounge visits worldwide, which gives them an edge for frequent travellers. However, the Indulge Card enhances its overall value with lifestyle benefits like **unlimited complimentary golf games** & lessons,**free movie tickets** , and dining discounts. Overall, IndusInd Bank Indulge Credit Card is a reasonable option for high spenders who are looking for a card with premium lifestyle benefits.

### What is the eligibility criteria to apply for IndusInd Bank Indulge Credit Card?
To apply for this credit card, you must be at least 21 years old. If you are a salaried individual, your annual income should be Rs. 36 Lakh or more. For self-employed individuals, the required annual income is Rs. 42 Lakh or more.

### How can I apply for IndusInd Bank Indulge Credit Card?
You can apply online by visiting the official website of IndusInd Bank and submitting a duly filled application form. Alternatively, you can also apply offline by visiting the nearest IndusInd Bank branch in person.

### Does IndusInd Bank Indulge Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, the card offers 16 complimentary international lounge visits per year (maximum 4 per quarter) through Priority Pass.

### What is the credit limit offered on IndusInd Bank Indulge Credit Card?
IndusInd Bank Indulge Credit Card comes with no pre-set spending limit. However, each transaction’s approval is subject to your eligibility and IndusInd Bank’s internal policies.

### Does IndusInd Bank Indulge Credit Card offer free movie tickets?
Yes, you can avail the Buy 1 Get 1 offer up to 3 times per month on ticket bookings via BookMyShow. You can save up to Rs. 700 on the second ticket. Additionally, the card also offers an extra Rs. 50 discount on selecting the F&B option.

### Can I redeem reward points earned via IndusInd Bank Indulge Credit Card against cashback?
Yes, you can redeem accumulated reward points against cashback at a value of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 1.
