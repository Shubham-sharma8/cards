## About the Card
EazyDiner IndusInd Bank Platinum Credit Card is the base variant of the co-branded credit cards IndusInd Bank offers with EazyDiner—the other one being EazyDiner IndusInd Bank Signature Credit Card. While both cards are designed to enhance the dining experience through complimentary EazyDiner Membership and additional discounts at 2,000+ partner restaurants, the base variant is a more suitable option for beginners seeking a lifetime free credit card with dining benefits. For a zero annual fee, the card offers exceptional benefits for those who dine out several times in a month. Cardholders can save up to 50% on their dining bills and can earn extra via 2x EazyPoints, redeemable against free meals and hotel bookings. Note that the primary features on the card are centered around dining with limited benefits on other categories. Thus, it cannot be the sole card to rely on to derive overall savings on your lifestyle. However, it can be a good add-on to your wallet to save on your dining expenses if you have other existing credit cards to cover up for where the card lacks. The good part, however, is that it covers some often excluded categories in its rewards program, like rent, utility, insurance, and government services.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the credit limit on this credit card?
The card offers a minimum credit limit of Rs. 30,000, which may exceed as per the consumer’s profile.

### Is EazyDiner membership offered to add-on cardholders?
No, the EazyDiner membership on the card is offered only to primary credit cardholders.

### How much can I save via the dining discount on PayEazy?
The card offers an additional discount of 20% off up to Rs. 500 on payment of dining bills via PayEazy. This is valid for up to three times every month, implying a savings of Rs. 18,000 every year. This is valid on dine-in and take-away as well.

### Does the card offer any benefits on fuel?
The card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions ranging between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000. However, fuel transactions are excluded from rewards program.

### What transactions are not considered for milestone and renewal benefits?
Transactions such as ATM cash withdrawals, fees, interest and charges are not considered for calculation of these benefits.
