## About the Card
IndusInd Platinum Aura Edge Credit Card is an entry-level credit card that offers benefits primarily through its rewards program. It comes with a unique reward structure by offering points across four different plans—**Home, Shop, Travel, and Party** —each catering to different spending categories. Cardholders have the flexibility to choose a plan based on their lifestyle and maximize their rewards accordingly. The card offers a reward rate of up to **4 points per Rs. 100 spent on everyday categories** such as groceries, travel bookings, and departmental stores. However, for other spends, the base reward rate is quite low at **0.5 points per Rs. 100 spent**. The card allows reward redemption across multiple options, including cash credit, conversion into air miles (limited to KrisFlyer Miles), and ‘Pay with Points’ at partner merchants. However, one limitation is that cardholders must select a rewards plan at the time of activation and can only change it after 6 months, which may be inconvenient for some users. Apart from the rewards program, the card also offers**complimentary vouchers of top brands** as welcome benefits, a fuel surcharge waiver, and insurance coverage, further enhancing its value. Overall, this is a basic entry-level credit card best suited for consumers who want to maximize their everyday spends through reward points.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for IndusInd Platinum Aura Edge Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card through our Platform by filling out the application form. Besides this, you can also apply online by visiting the official website of the bank. All you need to do is look for this credit card and submit the application form. You can also visit any of the nearest branches of the bank to apply for this card in person.

### Are there any fuel benefits offered on IndusInd Platinum Aura Edge Credit Card?
Yes, this credit card offers a fuel surcharge waiver of 1% on your fuel expenses. However, this is applicable only on your transactions between Rs. 500 and Rs. 3,000.

### What should I do if I lose my IndusInd Platinum Aura Edge Credit Card?
As soon as you notice the loss of your card or a fraudulent transaction, you must inform the bank by calling on the customer care number – 1860 267 7777. The representative will ask a few questions for confirmation and block your card.

### How can I check the application status of my IndusInd Platinum Aura Edge Credit Card?
You can check the application status of your card by visiting the official website of the bank. You are required to enter the application number to track your status. Besides this, the bank also sends a text on your registered mobile number regarding your application status.

### What is the credit limit offered on IndusInd Platinum Aura Edge Credit Card?
The credit limit varies from applicant to applicant. The bank decides your credit limit after scrutinizing your application form. Your credit limit is decided based on your income, payment history, debt and more. Therefore, you will get to know about your credit limit when you receive your credit card.

### What is the forex markup fee charged on IndusInd Platinum Aura Edge Credit Card?
IndusInd Platinum Aura Edge Credit Card charges a forex markup fee of 3.5% on your foreign currency transactions.
