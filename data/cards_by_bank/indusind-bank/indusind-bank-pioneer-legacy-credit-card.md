## About the Card
IndusInd Bank Pioneer Legacy Credit Card is an all-rounder card offering benefits across multiple spending categories, including travel, shopping, entertainment and more. Cardholders can earn **2X reward points on weekends** and a base reward rate of **1 point per Rs. 100 spent on weekdays** across all categories. The accumulated reward points can be redeemed against cash at a 1:1 ratio. Besides this, the card is a suitable option for frequent travellers as well, with benefits like a **low forex markup fee of 1.8%** and complimentary **domestic & international lounge visits**. In addition to this, lifestyle benefits, including complimentary golf games, **free movie tickets** , and exclusive concierge services, enhance the card’s value. Overall, this card is a suitable option for consumers seeking an **all-rounder card with no annual fee**. However, the card charges a joining fee of Rs. 5,000, which is waived off on spending Rs. 50,000 within the first 90 days.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is IndusInd Bank Pioneer Legacy Credit Card worth it?
IndusInd Bank Pioneer Legacy Credit Card is an all-rounder credit card offering benefits across multiple categories. If you are ready to pay a joining fee of Rs. 5,000, you can save significantly on your travel as well as day-to-day spends. This card is a decent option if you prefer to put most of your expenses on your credit card.

### What is the joining fee for IndusInd Bank Pioneer Legacy Credit Card?
The card charges a joining fee of Rs. 5,000, which gets waived off if you spend Rs. 50,000 within the first 90 days of card issuance. However, if you do not meet this spending criteria, a joining fee of Rs. 5,000 will be charged.

### What are the reward benefits offered on IndusInd Bank Pioneer Legacy Credit Card?
This credit card offers 2 reward points per Rs. 100 spent across all categories on weekends and 1 reward point per Rs. 100 spent on weekdays. Besides this, you can also earn 0.70 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on usually excluded categories, including utility bills, insurance payments, government services, education, and rental payments.

### Can I redeem accumulated rewards against cashback with IndusInd Bank Pioneer Legacy Credit Card?
Yes, you can redeem the accumulated reward points against cash credit at a value of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 1.
