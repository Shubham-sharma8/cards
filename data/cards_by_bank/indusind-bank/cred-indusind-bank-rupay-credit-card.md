## About the Card
CRED IndusInd Bank RuPay Credit Card** is an **entry-level co-branded card** that offers accelerated value-back on **online and co-branded spends**. Moreover, with **5% value-back** on UPI spends made through CRED app, this credit card manages to hold its place among the cards offering the highest savings on UPI transactions. The rewards on card spends are credited in the form of CRED Points which can later be redeemed against travel bookings or bill payments on CRED app in 1:1 and 2:1 redemption ratios, respectively.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Are there any reward redemption charges applicable on CRED IndusInd Bank RuPay Credit Card?
No, you can redeem the earned CRED Points on CRED app without paying any redemption fee within 24 months of accrual.

### Can I use CRED IndusInd RuPay Credit Card on UPI apps other than CRED?
Yes, you can use CRED IndusInd RuPay Credit Card on non-CRED UPI apps like PhonePe, Google Pay, etc. However, you will not accrue any CRED reward points on those transactions. CRED Points on UPI spends are only credited if you use CRED platform to make those payments.

### Is there any fee waiver condition on CRED IndusInd Bank RuPay Credit Card?
No, there is no fee waiver facility available on this IndusInd Bank credit card. Even though there is no joining fee charged, you will have to pay an annual fee of Rs. 499 plus taxes.

### Can I use CRED IndusInd Bank RuPay Credit Card for international purchases?
Yes, you can make international purchases using this co-branded credit card and earn 1 CRED Point on every Rs. 100 spent. However, a forex mark-up fee of 3.5% will also apply on such spends.

### Do the accrued CRED Points expire?
Yes, the earned CRED Points expire after 24 months of their accrual.
