## About the Card
Indus Solitaire Credit Card is designed for high spenders seeking premium travel and lifestyle benefits. With welcome benefits like **complimentary EazyDiner Prime, Taj Epicure, and Moneycontrol memberships** , this card caters to a range of everyday categories, including dining, travel, and entertainment. One of the standout features of this card is the**zero forex markup fee** , making it a suitable option for international spends. Additionally, up to **16 domestic and international lounge visits annually** enhance its value for frequent travellers. While the card offers decent travel and lifestyle benefits, it falls short with **a low reward rate of 0.5%**. When compared to cards like HDFC Infinia and ICICI Emeralde Private, which offer reward rates of up to 3.33%, unlimited lounge access, and golf benefits, Indus Solitaire lags behind due to its low reward rate and limited travel & lifestyle benefits. However, it’s important to note that **Mastercard variant** holders can maximize the card’s overall value with exclusive travel and lifestyle benefits, such as **Club ITC membership, complimentary golf games and lessons, access to premium airport services** , and more.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What are the activation benefits offered by Indus Solitaire Credit Card?
The card offers 5,000 bonus reward points on spending Rs. 1 Lakh within the first 30 days of card issuance. In addition, it offers complimentary memberships of Taj Epicure, EazyDiner Prime, and Moneycontrol Pro, offering exclusive benefits such as discounts on stays, complimentary meals, and more.

### How can I apply for Indus Solitaire Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card online by visiting the official IndusInd Bank website. You are required to fill out the application form and submit the required documents. Alternatively, you can apply offline by visiting your nearest IndusInd Bank branch and submitting the application form in person.

### What is the annual fee for Indus Solitaire Credit Card?
Indus Solitaire Credit Card charges an annual fee of Rs. 10,000. However, this fee can be waived off on spending Rs. 10 Lakh or more in a year.

### Does Indus Solitaire Credit Card offer lounge access benefits?
Yes, the card offers 4 complimentary domestic on a min spend of Rs. 5 lakh in the previous quarter and 4 international lounge visits per quarter, summing up to 16 visits in a year.

### Can I redeem reward points accumulated via Indus Solitaire Credit Card against cashback?
Yes, you can redeem accumulated reward points against cash credit at the rate of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 0.50.

### Does Indus Solitaire Credit Card offer any benefits on movie tickets?
No, the card does not offer direct benefits like free or discounted movie tickets. However, you can earn reward points on movie ticket purchases and redeem them across various categories as per your preferences.
