## Frequently Asked Questions
### Is lounge access on the EazyDiner Signature credit card spend-based?
No, the domestic lounge access benefits on the card are not spend-based.

### How much can I save on movie tickets?
You can save a maximum of Rs. 200 per ticket, thus a total of Rs. 400 every month.

### Is there any capping on fuel surcharge waivers?
The card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver of up to Rs. 250 per month on transactions ranging between Rs. 500 and Rs. 3,000.

### What is the value of the complimentary EazyDiner membership?
The card offers complimentary EazyDiner membership worth Rs. 3,550. The membership price may change.

### How to apply for an IndusInd Bank EazyDiner Signature Credit Card?
To apply for the IndusInd Bank EazyDiner Signature Credit Card, you can contact the issuer directly through their website or visit the nearest branch.
