## About the Card
IndusInd Bank Crest Credit Card is a premium, invite-only card issued by the bank to high-income consumers who are looking for luxury, convenience, and rewards across travel, lifestyle, and entertainment benefits. The consumer can avail of **8 complimentary domestic and international airport lounge** benefits, along with a free LoungeKey membership. IndusInd Crest Credit Card offers **2.5 points per Rs. 100 spent on international spends** and **1 point per Rs 100 spent on domestic spends**. This card also offers **3 golf rounds and lessons per month** with VIP access to premium courses through the TPC Network. Additionally, this card offers decent value on movie tickets, and cardholders can get **20% off on BookMyShow** event and theatre booking, **buy-1-get-1-free movie tickets** up to 3 per month. Additional benefits include dining discounts at select restaurants and ITC hotels, fuel surcharge waiver, personal accident insurance up to Rs. 25 lakh, and 24/7 Crest Concierge & Auto Assist services, making it a complete lifestyle and travel credit card. IndusInd Crest Credit Card is a suitable option for consumers who want to opt for a credit card that combines luxury, convenience and rewards such as domestic and international airport lounge access, golf benefits, premium dining benefits and reward points.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Who is eligible for the IndusInd Crest Credit Card?
This is an invite-only credit card. Eligibility is determined by IndusInd Bank based on internal criteria such as income profile, relationship value, and creditworthiness.

### What are the lounge access benefits offered?
Cardholders can avail 2 complimentary international and 2 domestic airport lounge visits per quarter on a min. spend of Rs. 5 Lakh in the previous quarter, totaling up to 8 annual visits, along with a complimentary LoungeKey membership.

### How does the reward points system work?
Cosnumers can earn 2.5 reward points per Rs. 100 on international spends and 1 point per Rs. 100 on domestic spends, redeemable at approximately Rs. 0.75 per point.

### What golf benefits are included with the card?
The card offers 3 complimentary golf rounds and 3 lessons per month, amounting to 36 rounds annually, with access to select premium golf courses through partner networks.

### What are the fees and annual charges?
The joining fee is Rs. 75,000 plus taxes, and the annual fee is Rs. 10,000 plus taxes, which may be waived on annual spending of Rs. 10 lakh.

### Are there any movie or entertainment benefits?
Cardholders can avail 20% discount on BookMyShow bookings and buy-one-get-one-free movie tickets, capped at Rs. 700 per ticket, up to 3 times per month.

### Does the card offer dining-related benefits?
Consumers can avail discounts at select partner restaurants and ITC hotels, including up to 25% off on food and beverages and additional offers under Club ITC programs.
