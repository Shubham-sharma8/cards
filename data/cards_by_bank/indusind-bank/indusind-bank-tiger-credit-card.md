## About the Card
IndusInd Bank Tiger Credit Card is a lifetime free card offered in collaboration with Tiger Fintech (A Bajaj Capital Group company). The card is designed for consumers who want to save on their spends through reward points. With this card, you can **earn up to 6X rewards (6 points per Rs. 100 spent)** across all categories. However, the card works differently with the spend-based reward structure. On annual spends of up to Rs. 1 Lakh, the card offers a reward rate of 1.2%; however, on annual spends above Rs. 5 Lakh, this **reward rate increases to 7.2%**. Besides the rewards program, the card also offers decent travel benefits, including a **low forex markup fee of 1.5%** (a benefit usually offered by premium credit cards) and complimentary international & domestic lounge visits. Also, other benefits like**complimentary golf games/lessons, free movie tickets and unlimited fuel surcharge waiver** add to the card’s overall value.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I apply for IndusInd Bank Tiger Credit Card?
You can apply for this credit card through online and offline modes. To apply online, you can visit the official website of the bank and fill out the application form. Besides this, you can also apply offline by visiting any of the nearest branches of the bank and submitting a duly filled application form.

### Can I use my IndusInd Bank Tiger Credit Card for overseas transactions?
Yes, you can use your IndusInd Bank Tiger Credit Card for foreign currency transactions. This credit card is suitable for international transactions as it charges a low forex markup fee of 1.5%.

### Can I redeem my IndusInd Bank Tiger Credit Card reward points against cashback?
Yes, the bank allows you to redeem your earned reward points against a variety of categories, including cash credit, Airmiles and products from the rewards catalogue. You can redeem the reward points against cash credit at a value of 1 Reward Point = Rs. 0.40.

### How can I check the application status of my IndusInd Bank Tiger Credit Card?
You can check the application status of your credit card through the official website of the bank. All you need to do is open the tracking page and enter the application number. Besides this, the bank also informs you about your current application status through text messages or email.

### Does IndusInd Bank Tiger Credit Card offer a fuel surcharge waiver?
Yes, you can avail a fuel surcharge waiver of 1% on your transactions between Rs. 400 to Rs. 4,000. This is applicable across all petrol pumps in the country.

### Does IndusInd Bank Tiger Credit Card offer any golf benefits?
Yes, with IndusInd Tiger Credit Card, you can avail one complimentary golf game or lesson in a quarter, which sums up to 4 complimentary games or lessons in a year.
