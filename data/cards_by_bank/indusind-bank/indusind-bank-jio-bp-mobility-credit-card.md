## Frequently Asked Questions
### How can I avail complimentary roadside assistance service using IndusInd Jio-bp Mobility+ Credit Card?
You can avail the free roadside assistance service with IndusInd Bank Jio-bp Mobility+ Credit Card by calling on the 24X7 toll free number 18005713981.

### Can I earn reward points on all fuel spends using IndusInd Jio-bp Mobility+ Credit Card?
No, you can earn reward points, i.e. Smiles, only on fuel purchased at Jio-bp fuel stations using this IndusInd Bank fuel credit card.

### Can I make international purchases using IndusInd Bank Jio-bp Mobility+ Credit Card?
Yes, you can use IndusInd Jio-bp Mobility+ Credit Card for international spends by enabling international transactions. However, take note that a forex mark-up fee of 3.5% will also apply on such transactions.

### How can I check my earned Smiles (reward points) balance using IndusInd Jio-bp Mobility+ Credit Card?
You can check your accrued Smiles balance by either sending ‘Hi’ to 83560 17100 on WhatsApp using your registered mobile number or on the My Jio-bp App.

### What is Jio-bp loyalty program?
Jio-bp loyalty program is called Jio-bp 4ever Loyalty Program under which you can earn loyalty reward points called Smiles on every spend made at Jio-bp fuel stations, including spends on fuel, convenience store, wildbean cafe, etc. These Smiles can be redeemed at Jio-bp fuel stations up to 36 months from their accrual.
