## About the Card
IndusInd Bank Avios Visa Infinite Credit Card is a premium credit card designed for frequent travellers. **Unlike other co-branded airline credit cards, this card is associated with not one but two popular airlines** —Qatar Airways and British Airways. As per your preference of airlines and travel locations, you can earn accelerated rewards and benefits. Beyond co-branded airline benefits, the card also offers general travel benefits like a low forex mark-up fee (at selected destinations), complimentary Priority Pass membership, and bonus Avios points via renewal and milestone benefits. The Avios card stands as a strong competitor due to its co-branded privileges on multiple airlines. Card users owning this card for their preferences towards Qatar and British Airways is understood. But if you are sticking to this card for its multiple airline benefits, it is better to find an airline-agnostic card that can offer a better count of travel partners to redeem your points.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What does the Qatar Airways Gold Membership Offer?
Gold members of Qatar Airways Privilege Club can enjoy priority check-in and boarding, extra baggage allowance, complimentary seat selection, access to Business Class lounges at select airports, meet-and-assist services when traveling through Hamad International Airport, 15% off on shopping and dining at Qatar Duty Free when paying with Avios, and much more. As of now, the Gold membership on the card is discontinued.

### Does the card offer any travel insurance covers?
Yes, the card offers multiple travel insurance covers. You get a lost baggage cover insured of Rs. 1 Lakh; delayed baggage, lost ticket and missed flight connection covers of Rs. 25,000 each and loss of passport cover for sum insured of up to Rs. 50,000.

### When do I avail the complimentary Gold membership of Qatar Airways?
The complimentary Qatar Airways Gold membership was offered after 45 days of the joining fee payment. However, the benefit is now discontinued.

### How do I contact IndusInd Bank for more details on the card?
You can contact the issuer at their 24‑Hour phone banking number: 1860267 7777. Alternatively, you can email your concerns at premium.care@indusind.com.

### How many bonus reward points can I earn in a year?
You can earn a maximum of 36,000 bonus Avios in a year- 18,000 after first Rs. 8 Lakh spent in a year and next 18,000 after second Rs. 8 Lakh spent in a year.
